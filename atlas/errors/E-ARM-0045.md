## Symptom

Under admittance control the tool buzzes or bounces on a hard surface and is steady on a soft one.

## Cause

The force measurement's delay and the inner loop's lag are inside the loop, and a stiff surface has a high gain. A virtual damper of 100 N s/m was unstable on the stiff surface (0 for steady) and steady on the soft one (1); 1500 gave steady on both.

## Fix

Raise the virtual damper to about the surface's stiffness over the inner loop's bandwidth, or use impedance control for stiff surfaces.

```cpp
rc::arm::AdmittanceConfig a;
a.dv = 1500.0;
```
