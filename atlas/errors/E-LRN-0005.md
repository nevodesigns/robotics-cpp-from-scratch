## Symptom

A policy fitted as a straight line steers too gently near the centre and asks for more than the car can give far from it, though it was fitted to good demonstrations.

## Cause

The expert's turn stops growing at the car's limit, and a line cannot stop growing. With 8.4 percent of the actions at the limit, the best line had weights of -1.044 and -1.576, smaller than the expert's, and an error of 0.01816 that no line removes.

Least squares gives the best line, which is not the same as a good policy: it is wrong in different ways at the two ends of the range.

## Fix

Fit only the range where the expert is linear, clip the line's output as the expert's is clipped, or use a learner that can bend (38-03).

```cpp
const double turn = rc::learn::clip(rc::learn::linear_act(p, s), task.max_turn);
```

The share of the actions at the limit (saturated_share) says beforehand how much of this to expect.
