## Symptom

A station that has been running for days reports every robot lost at once after the time is corrected, or never reports one lost after the clock was put back.

## Cause

The wall clock can be changed by a time service, a person or a daylight rule. An age worked out from it can be negative or very large. Measured: a time before the last sign was an age of **0**, and the age after an hour was **3600000** with no overflow; the monitor uses a monotonic clock.

## Fix

Use a clock that only goes forward, and clamp:

```cpp
long long age(long long now_ms) const { return now_ms > last_ ? now_ms - last_ : 0; }
```
