match: a wall and a post become lethal cells, as many as their size says

## Symptom

A path that is clear on the map scrapes a wall or a post.

## Cause

The map had the obstacles and not the vehicle: the planner moved a point. Inflation by the inscribed radius gives every cell within 0.25 m of a post the cost 253.

## Fix

Inflate by the vehicle's inscribed radius and plan the centre only through cells below 253.

```cpp
rc::perceive::inflate(master, robot_radius, inflation_radius, scale);
```
