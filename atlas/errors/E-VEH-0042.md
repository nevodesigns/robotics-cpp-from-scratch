match: noise on flat ground is roughness, and the step shows at its edge
match: the layer is free on smooth ground, rises with the slope, and is lethal on the steep slope and at the step

## Symptom

A kerb or a step is in no cell's slope, and stops the vehicle.

## Cause

A plane fitted over 3 by 3 cells smooths a step away: a step of 5 cm read as a slope of 14.0 degrees and a roughness of 0.0144 m, both inside the limits.

## Fix

Compare neighbouring cells as well as fitting a plane, and use the steepest rise. The rise between neighbours was 26.6 degrees, which made 2 cells a row lethal.

```cpp
const double share = std::max(std::max(slope, max_gradient) / max_slope, roughness / max_roughness);
```
