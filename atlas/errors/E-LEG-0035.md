## Symptom

The attitude estimate is good while walking and jumps or drifts as soon as the robot trots.

## Cause

The plane through the feet was used whatever the gait. A plane needs three points. In a trot only two feet are down, and the "plane" through them is undetermined. In the walk the feet took the error to 0.40 degrees; in the trot they cannot be used at all.

## Fix

Use the feet only when three or more are down, and fall back to the gyro and the accelerometer otherwise: 1.02 degrees in the trot.

```cpp
const bool feet_valid = feet_down >= 3;
rc::legged::attitude_update(f, gx, gy, acc, feet_roll, feet_pitch, feet_valid, dt);
```

A gait with fewer feet down needs a better IMU, since it has less help from the legs.
