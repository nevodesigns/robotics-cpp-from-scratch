## Symptom

A sensor that is turning near due south is placed pointing north for one cycle, and everything it sees is flipped.

## Cause

Headings repeat every full turn, and a plain average of 170 and -170 degrees is 0, the opposite way. The blend takes the difference, wraps it to a half turn and goes part of the way: 180 degrees. After every composition the heading is wrapped, so 3.0 rad turned by 3.0 rad is -0.283, the same direction.

## Fix

Wrap the difference before blending.

```cpp
r.theta = wrap_angle(a.theta + wrap_angle(b.theta - a.theta) * f);
```
