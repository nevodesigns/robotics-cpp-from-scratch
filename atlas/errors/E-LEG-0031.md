## Symptom

During a change of gait one leg suddenly swings almost a whole cycle the wrong way, dragging or kicking, while the others change smoothly.

## Cause

A phase offset was moved toward its target by plain subtraction. A phase is a point on a circle: 0.95 and 0.05 are 0.1 apart, not 0.9. Moved the long way, the offset runs backward through nearly the whole cycle.

## Fix

Wrap the difference into the range minus a half to a half before limiting it, and wrap the offset back into 0 to 1 afterwards. Starting at 0.95 with a target of 0.05, the offset then went forward through the wrap.

```cpp
double cur[4], target[4];
rc::legged::gait_offsets(rc::legged::kTrot, target);
rc::legged::step_offsets_toward(cur, target, 0.2, dt);   // the short way round, at most 0.2 cycles a second
```

A difference of exactly half a cycle can go either way. A real gait avoids being there.
