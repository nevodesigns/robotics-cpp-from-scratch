title: A Jacobian for the flange used for the tip
match: the tip drifts when I command a velocity of the gripper
match: the Jacobian is for the flange but I control the fingertips
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Commanded in terms of the fingertip, the tool moves as if the point being controlled were the flange.

## Cause

The Jacobian was worked out at the flange. A tool offset of 0.15 m changes the linear part and not the angular part.

## Fix

Work the Jacobian at the point being controlled, with the tool offset.

```cpp
jacobian(table, q, base, trans(0, 0, tip_length), table.convention);
```
