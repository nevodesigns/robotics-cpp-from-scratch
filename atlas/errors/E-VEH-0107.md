title: The shortest path over bad ground
match: the vehicle goes straight through the sand when there is a firm way round
match: the planner takes the shortest path and the battery is flat
match: with no weight on energy the shortest path goes through the sand, with weight it takes the ford
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-09-planning-over-rough-ground
---

## Symptom

The vehicle crosses sand or mud by the shortest way, slowly and at a high cost to its battery.

## Cause

The planner minimised distance only: 16.0 m and 90 J, where 31.2 m gave 66 J.

## Fix

Add a weight on energy per metre to the price of a step, chosen for the mission.

```cpp
cfg.energy = 1.0;
```
