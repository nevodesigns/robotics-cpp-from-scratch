## Symptom

Adding a term that pushes the joints towards the middle of their limits makes the tool move.

## Cause

The term was added straight onto the step. Without the projection it was felt as 1.244 m/s; through the null space, 0.00000000.

## Fix

Project the secondary motion through the null space of the Jacobian before adding it to the step that tracks the tool.

```cpp
null_space_motion(j, want, lambda);
```
