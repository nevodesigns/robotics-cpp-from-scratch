## Symptom

Settings are changed (speed, route) and the job still fails.

## Cause

A camera good to only 12 mm fails at requirement 4, which no speed setting can fix.

## Fix

Look at which requirement failed first: design ones (stopping, payload, grasp) need a different robot, settings ones (battery, speed, people) a different job.

```cpp
first_fail <= 4 means a design limit
```
