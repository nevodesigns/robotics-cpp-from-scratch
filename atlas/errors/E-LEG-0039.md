## Symptom

A check of balance says the robot is fine because its centre of mass is over the feet, and it tips over as it speeds up or brakes.

## Cause

Only the centre of mass was checked. A body that accelerates presses on the ground somewhere else: behind its centre of mass by h over g times the acceleration. For a body 0.27 m high, 3 m/s2 forward moved that point 83 mm back. Balance needs the zero moment point, not the centre of mass, inside the feet.

## Fix

Check the zero moment point against the support, and limit the acceleration so that it stays inside: with four feet, 6.90 m/s2 forward and 5.45 sideways.

```cpp
double z[2];
rc::legged::zmp_from_com(com, 0.27, accel, z);
if (rc::legged::support_margin(feet, on_ground, z) < 0.0) limit_accel();
const double a_max = rc::legged::max_accel(feet, on_ground, com, 0.27, dir);
```
