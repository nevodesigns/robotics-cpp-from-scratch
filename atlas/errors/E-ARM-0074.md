## Symptom

The sensor sees the thing and the arm goes on along its plan.

## Cause

The arm acted on the plan and not on the prediction: it was -0.112 m from the obstacle (through it), against 0.031 m for one that stopped.

## Fix

Stop when a meeting is predicted within the horizon (at least the time to stop), and look again next tick.

```cpp
if (ttc >= 0.0) hold = true;
```
