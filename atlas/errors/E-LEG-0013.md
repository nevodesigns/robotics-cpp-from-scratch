## Symptom

The foot touches down and slides a little before it grips, or the contact sense chatters at every landing.

## Cause

The foot was still moving when it reached the ground. The swing must end at rest; then the ground moves under the foot (the stance) and the leg loads instead of skidding.

## Fix

End the swing with zero speed relative to the body and start the stance from there.

```cpp
rc::legged::swing_peaks(p0, p1, h, t_sw, &v_peak, &a_peak);   // the end speeds are zero by construction
```
