## Symptom

Acceleration that was right at the start of the day is poor by the end of it.

## Cause

The battery's internal resistance makes the bus sag under load. A battery of 0.5 ohm cut the standing acceleration from 4.54 to 3.32 m/s2.

## Fix

Plan the acceleration with the sag, and keep a margin for the end of the charge.

```cpp
const double bus = bus_voltage(drive_train, speed);
```
