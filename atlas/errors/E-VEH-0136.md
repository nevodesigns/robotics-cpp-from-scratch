## Symptom

Every part passes its own test and the vehicle still does the wrong thing when two of them act at once.

## Cause

Nothing said who wins. Here the speeds are joined by the least of them (the mode's cap, the mission's, the field's), and the field is applied before the unsticking ladder; the day was 4 legs of 4 with 0 contacts.

## Fix

Write the one rule that joins the limits and the order the layers act in, and test it on a day with events that meet.

```cpp
v = combine_speed(wanted, mode_cap, field_result, creep);
```
