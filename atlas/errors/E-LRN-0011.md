## Symptom

The error falls for a few steps and then shoots up, or becomes NaN, and the weights are enormous.

## Cause

Each step was larger than the curvature of the error allows, so it overshot the minimum by more than it started from, and the next step overshot further. With a step of 1.0 the weights grew without bound; 0.02 brought the test error to 0.00130, and 0.001 was still at 0.00243 after 20 epochs.

## Fix

Try a few step sizes, each about three times the last, on the validation set, take the largest that is stable, and stop training if the error is not finite.

```cpp
if (!std::isfinite(rc::learn::mlp_mse(net, scale, validation))) stop_and_lower_the_step();
```

Scaled inputs (38-01) let one step size suit every weight; unscaled ones make the band of good steps narrow.
