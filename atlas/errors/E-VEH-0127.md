## Symptom

The vehicle goes out with a check failed, because a person pressed continue.

## Cause

Every failure could be overridden, so a failure was a nuisance. Here a block (6 of the 10 checks) cannot be overridden, and an override of a warning needs a reason that is kept.

## Fix

Decide which checks are blocks and which are warnings, refuse overrides of blocks, and require and record a reason for the others.

```cpp
const Decision d = decide(results, overrides);
```
