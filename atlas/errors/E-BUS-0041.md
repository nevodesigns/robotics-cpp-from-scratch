id: E-BUS-0041
title: Threads added to a single exclusive group
match: two threads do nothing for callbacks that share one exclusive group, and the extra thread sits idle
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-14-one-thread-or-many
---

## Symptom

A second thread was added to the executor to fix a latency problem and the latency is exactly the same.

## Cause

A mutually exclusive group never runs two of its callbacks together, whatever the thread count. With everything in one such group and two threads the worst wait was 45, the same as one thread; with a reentrant group it was 0, at the price of the callbacks now being able to run at once.

## Fix

Separate the callbacks into groups, or make the group reentrant and protect what its callbacks share.

```cpp
simulate(tasks, {false}, 2, 5000);   // reentrant
```
