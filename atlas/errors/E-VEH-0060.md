## Symptom

After correcting for the lever arm the position still wanders when the robot turns.

## Cause

The correction uses the heading. With it 5 degrees wrong the residual was 0.044 m, the lever times the error.

## Fix

Shorten the lever, and improve the heading, before relying on the correction.

```cpp
// residual = |lever| * heading_error
```
