## Symptom

A robot given the same speed on both tracks curves away on uneven ground.

## Cause

The slips differ from side to side. Slips of 10 and 20 percent turned a straight command by 44.1 degrees in 10 s.

## Fix

Hold the heading with feedback from a gyro or a compass, and treat the track command as a request.

```cpp
const double correction = heading_pid.update(target, measured);
```
