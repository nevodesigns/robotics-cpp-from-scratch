title: A heat budget worked out for standing
match: the robot ran fine standing and the knees overheated trotting
match: the thermal check passed standing and failed walking
match: the stance torque is the weight's share through the Jacobian, and two feet down give twice four
match: the rms torque over a cycle lies between the swing and the stance torque and heat goes as its square
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 34-06-how-hot-the-knees-get

## Symptom

The knees are fine while the robot stands and overheat a few minutes into a trot.

## Cause

The heat budget was worked out for standing. Standing on four feet the knee gave 4.33 N m; in a trot only two feet are down, so each carries twice as much, 8.65 N m in the stance. Heat goes as the square of torque, so the trot's knees settled at 28.1 K above the air against 14.0 K standing.

## Fix

Work out the heat from the root mean square torque over the gait cycle actually used.

```cpp
double tau[3];
rc::legged::stance_torques(leg, 0.30, 12.0, 2, tau);                         // two feet down: 8.65 N m
const double rms = rc::legged::cycle_rms_torque(std::fabs(tau[2]), 0.3, 0.5);   // 6.12 N m
const double rise = rc::legged::winding_rise(knee, rms);                       // 28.1 K
```
