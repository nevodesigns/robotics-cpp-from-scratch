title: A beam that grew in length and not in section

## Symptom

A larger machine of the same design is far less accurate than the small one.

## Cause

The sag goes as the cube of the length: twice the span gave 8 times the sag (50 micrometres at 1.0 m).

## Fix

Increase the section of the beam with the length, or support it, and work the sag out before building.

```cpp
beam_sag(load, span, E * I);
```
