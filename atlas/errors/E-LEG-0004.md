## Symptom

The torques for standing are right on the left legs and wrong on the right, or a foot force estimated from the motors has the wrong sign sideways on one side.

## Cause

The Jacobian was derived for one side and used for both. The sideways offset has the side's sign, and the column of the lean joint depends on it. A Jacobian checked only on a left leg passes and is wrong for a right one.

## Fix

Build the Jacobian from the same geometry, side included, and check it against small moves of each joint on both sides.

```cpp
double j[3][3];
rc::legged::leg_jacobian(right, q0, q1, q2, j);   // right.side = -1
// check: (leg_fk(q + dq) - leg_fk(q)) / dq against each column
```
