## Symptom

A model is tuned until a run looks right and the numbers inside it mean nothing.

## Cause

The efficiency is a ratio that can be measured. A turn on a model of 0.8 read back as 0.800.

## Fix

Measure it from a turn: the body's turn over the turn the wheels ask for.

```cpp
const double eff = turn_efficiency_from(left_m, right_m, track, dtheta);
```
