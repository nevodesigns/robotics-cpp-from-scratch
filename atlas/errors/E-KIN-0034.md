## Symptom

An orientation error flips sign or direction when the tool is almost upside down from its target, or a task is declared done with the tool the wrong way round.

## Cause

The axis is read from the antisymmetric part divided by the sine of the angle, and at a half turn the sine is zero. The symmetric part holds the axis there. And convergence of a pose is two conditions. Measured: a half turn about x was **pi** along x; pi less 1e-6 about +y and -y gave y = **3.14159** and **-3.14159**; a pose with position error **0** m and rotation error **0.1** rad was not converged.

## Fix

Use the symmetric part near a half turn, and test both errors:

```cpp
return e.position_norm() <= position_tolerance && e.rotation_angle() <= rotation_tolerance;
```
