## Symptom

The robot turns on the spot on the test floor and digs in on soft ground.

## Cause

A pivot needs mu m g L / (4 B) from each track (32.7 N here), and on soft ground the slip to give it (6.8 percent for the sand) is large enough to dig the track in.

## Fix

Compute the thrust and the slip a pivot needs before planning one, and prefer an arc on soft ground.

```cpp
const double need = pivot_thrust_tracked(mass, mu, length, gauge);
```
