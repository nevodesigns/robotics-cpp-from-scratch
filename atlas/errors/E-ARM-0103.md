## Symptom

A SCARA refuses a point that is plainly within its reach, or reaches it with the elbow swung into a fixture.

## Cause

Only one elbow was tried. Each point the two links can reach has two solutions, elbow left and elbow right (q2 is plus or minus the same arccosine), and the joint limits often forbid one of them. Over the lesson's grid, 159 of the 272 pairs of a point and an elbow were inside the limits: many points can be reached one way only.

## Fix

Try both elbows, keep the ones inside the limits, and choose between them by where the arm is and where it goes next (the smaller move, or the side away from the fixtures).

```cpp
double q1 = 0.0, q2 = 0.0;
const bool ok = rc::arm::scara_ik(arm, x, y, +1, q1, q2) || rc::arm::scara_ik(arm, x, y, -1, q1, q2);
```

Swapping elbows in the middle of a job is a long move through the straight pose, so plan the elbow for a whole sequence, not for each point.
