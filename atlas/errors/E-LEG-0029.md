## Symptom

At a slow trot the robot falls sideways in the middle of a stance; at a faster trot it is fine. Slowing down, the usual remedy, makes it worse.

## Cause

The stance was longer than the time the body takes to fall from where it is. A stance of 0.25 s was short enough, with a margin of 0.7, for a body 10 mm off the line and too long for one 30 mm off. A slower trot has a longer stance, so it gives the body more time to fall.

## Fix

Check the stance against the time to fall, with a margin, before each step. A taller body falls more slowly (0.50 s for 0.40 m against 0.41 s for 0.27 m), and a shorter stance leaves less time to fall.

```cpp
if (!rc::legged::trot_stance_ok(0.25, offset, rate, 0.06, 0.27, 0.1)) gait.shorten_stance();
```

A trot that is stopped in the middle is falling, which is why a trot cannot simply be paused.
