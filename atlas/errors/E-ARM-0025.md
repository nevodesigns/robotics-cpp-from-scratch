## Symptom

The arm is accurate to a few millimetres in repeatability and a few millimetres out in where it goes.

## Cause

The table is the arm as drawn. A simulated tolerance of 0.3 degrees and 1 mm put the tool 3.39 mm from the table's prediction; calibrated, 0.100 mm.

## Fix

Measure the tool's place at many poses with something better than the arm and fit the table.

```cpp
calibrate(nominal_table, measurements, 1e-9, 12);
```
