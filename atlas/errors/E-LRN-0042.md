## Symptom

A random search makes no progress: either every try is worse than the best (it never moves) or the best jumps about and does not settle.

## Cause

The step sets how far each try moves from the best gains. Too large, and nearly every try lands somewhere useless, so nothing is kept; too small, and a try differs from the best by less than the noise of the score. The step of 2.0 here took the score from 36.9 to 500.0 in 300 tries.

## Fix

Try a few steps over a factor of ten, and shrink the step as the score nears its limit.

```cpp
for (double step : {0.5, 2.0, 8.0}) report(search_with(step));
```

The same trade is the learning rate of 38-04, in a search without a gradient.
