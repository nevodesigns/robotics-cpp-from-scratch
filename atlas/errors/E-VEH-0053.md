title: A track that lives for ever, or dies at the first miss
match: the vehicle stops for an obstacle that has gone
match: the person is a new object every time they pass behind a post
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-09-tracking-things-that-move

## Symptom

Either ghosts stay in the list of objects, or an object that is hidden for a moment is forgotten and treated as new.

## Cause

The life of a track needs rules. A target hidden for 2 frames kept its id and one hidden for 8 had been dropped.

## Fix

Confirm a track after a few hits, coast it through short gaps and drop it after a few misses, and choose the numbers for the scene.

```cpp
if (tracks_[i].misses > cfg_.max_misses) drop();
```
