id: E-VEH-0108
title: A risk that is only blocked or free
match: the planner crosses a risky patch that it could go round
match: the planner treats risky ground like safe ground
match: a patch of risk that is not blocked is crossed unless risk has a weight
match: blocked cells are never crossed, and a goal that cannot be reached is not found
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-09-planning-over-rough-ground
---

## Symptom

The path goes over loose or rocky ground that is not blocked, when firm ground was a short way round. The ground was marked as risky but not as blocked.

## Cause

Only blocked cells were avoided. A patch of cost 200 was crossed, at a worst cell cost of 200.

A planner that knows only blocked or free treats every free cell as equally good. A cell of cost 200 is free and is crossed by the shortest path as readily as one of cost 0.

## Fix

Add a weight on the cell's cost over 255 to the price of a step, and keep blocked cells as a hard no. With a weight of 5 the worst cell crossed cost 0.

```cpp
rc::drive::OffroadWeights w;
w.risk = 5.0;
```
