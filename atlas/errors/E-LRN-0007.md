## Symptom

The network's error falls at first and then stalls well above what the data allows, or falls more slowly than it should. Changing the step size does not help much.

## Cause

The hidden layer's gradient missed the derivative of tanh, the factor 1 - tanh squared. For units near zero that factor is close to one and the mistake is small; for units that are saturated it is close to zero and the wrong gradient pushes them as hard as any other. One such slip made the gradient differ from the finite difference by 0.123, where the right one agreed to better than one part in a million.

The chain rule needs every link: the output weight, the activation's derivative, the input. Leaving out any one gives a direction that is roughly downhill, which is why training half works.

## Fix

Write the backward pass from the forward pass link by link, and check it against finite differences.

```cpp
const double d = e * m.w2[i] * (1.0 - z[i] * z[i]);   // the tanh derivative
g.w1[2 * i] = d * x[0];
```

A gradient that is roughly right is the worst kind: it trains, so nobody looks.
