title: A speed limit that does not depend on the bend
match: the vehicle slides in the tight bend of a path that it drives well elsewhere
match: one top speed for every curvature
match: past the edge the tyres let go, and the speed at which that happens is the one the limit gives
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The same top speed is fine on the long curves and a slide in the tight ones.

## Cause

The limit is sqrt(mu g / kappa): 1.81 m/s on this gravel circle, and more on a gentler one.

## Fix

Let the speed limit be a function of the curvature, from max_speed_for_curvature.

```cpp
const double v = std::min(v_cruise, max_speed_for_curvature(cell, heading, curvature));
```
