match: the foot contact flickered on a soft floor

## Symptom

The contact state flickers on and off while a foot grazes the ground, and the gait reacts to every flicker.

## Cause

One threshold was used for both on and off. A force hovering around it crosses it on every reading: with the force at 8 N plus or minus 2 and one threshold at 8, the state changed 10 times in 0.4 s.

## Fix

Use a higher force to go on and a lower one to go off, and require a run of readings before believing a change. With 8 N on and 4 N off it changed 0 times.

```cpp
rc::legged::ContactParams p;      // f_on 8, f_off 4, need 3, blank 0.06
rc::legged::ContactState s;
const bool down = rc::legged::contact_update(s, force, since_lift, p);
```

The run of 3 readings delays a touchdown by 12 ms at 500 Hz. That is the price of not reacting to one noisy reading.
