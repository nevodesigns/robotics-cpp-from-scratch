## Symptom

After many turns the heading is wrong by the same proportion of the total turned.

## Cause

The geometric track was used. A track 3 percent too small made a full turn read 11.1 degrees wrong.

## Fix

Calibrate the effective track from a measured turn, and use that.

```cpp
WheelOdometry odo(effective_track, scale);
```
