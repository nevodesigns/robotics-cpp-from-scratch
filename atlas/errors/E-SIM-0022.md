## Symptom

The comparison reports a lag of one sample on traces that are not shifted, or reports a small rms error while one sample in the trace is badly wrong.

## Cause

An offset between traces pulls a shift judged by plain error one sample away, so each shift is judged by the variance of the differences: the 0.05 offset gave a lag of 0, with an error, largest difference and bias of 0.0500. A single sample out by 1.0 in 200 gave a largest difference of 1.0 and an rms error of only 0.0707.

## Fix

Judge the shift by variance and keep the largest difference in view.

```cpp
double variance = ss / count - mean * mean;
```
