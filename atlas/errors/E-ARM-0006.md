## Symptom

When the tool points straight up or down the roll and yaw of the pose swing wildly for no movement.

## Cause

At a pitch of a quarter turn roll and yaw turn about the same axis: a rotation a hair away differed by 57 degrees in roll.

## Fix

Do not filter, limit or interpolate the three angles; use the rotation (or a quaternion) and take the angles only for display.

```cpp
rpy_from_rotation(r);
```
