title: A delay left out, and a timeout so short that the stop gets turned off
match: a delayed link makes the robot go on obeying old commands, so the stop comes later by the delay times the speed
match: a lossy link makes a short timeout stop the robot for nothing, hundreds of times an hour, and a timeout of ten heartbeats almost never does
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-06-a-robot-you-drive-from-a-chair
---

## Symptom

The robot stops every few seconds on a poor link, and the operator asks for the stop to be switched off.

## Cause

A link delay of 100, 300 and 1000 ms put the stop 0.100, 0.290 and 1.000 m later at 1 m/s. At 20 percent loss a timeout of 0.1 s gave 1198 false stops in an hour and 52.1 percent of the hour stopped; 0.2 s gave 101, 0.3 s gave 3 and 0.5 s none.

## Fix

Set the timeout from the distance and the loss, and plan for the delay.

```cpp
const double chance = chance_all_lost(loss, heartbeats_in_timeout);
```
