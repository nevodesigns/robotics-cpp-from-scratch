match: lost beams are written as the maximum range
match: a scan of a room becomes a point for every beam, and the points are where the wall is
match: beams that saw nothing, were too near or too far, or are not numbers give no points
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A ring of phantom obstacles sits at the sensor's maximum range, or a gap in a scan is filled with a wall.

## Cause

A beam that returned nothing was kept as a range. Drivers report a lost beam as infinity, NaN, zero or the maximum range, and every one of those, taken as a point, is an obstacle that is not there. With a tenth of the beams lost the scan should give 325 points, and the lost beams none.

## Fix

Keep only beams whose range is a number between the sensor's limits, and watch the share of valid beams: a falling share is a dirty window or rain.

```cpp
if (!(r == r) || r < scan.min_range || r > scan.max_range) continue;   // NaN fails r == r
```
