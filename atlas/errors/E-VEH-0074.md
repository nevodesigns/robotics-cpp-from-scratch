## Symptom

The heading is good for the first minutes and drifts more and more after, especially as the day warms. It is a cold start on a warm day.

## Cause

The bias was read once, at power-up, and it wanders. Read once, the heading ended 9.9 degrees out after 300 s.

A gyro's bias changes with temperature, and the electronics warm for the first part of a run. A bias read at power-up is right only at power-up.

## Fix

Read the bias at every stop, and decide how often the robot must stop from how fast the bias wanders: with a stop every 60 s the error was 4.6 degrees, every 10 s, 0.8.

```cpp
bias.update(gyro_z, still.update(left, right, accel), dt);
```
