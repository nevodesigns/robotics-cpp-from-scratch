## Symptom

After calibration the feet are still out by a few millimetres or more.

## Cause

A link was the wrong length (10 mm), which no offsets explain: with measurements free of noise a residual of 3.6 mm was left where there should have been none. (Noise of 2 mm in each axis leaves 3.4 mm on its own, so a link error of this size is only seen if the noise of the jig is known.)

## Fix

Compare the residual with the measurement noise, and if it is much larger, find what else is wrong in the geometry.

```cpp
if (rms > 0.004) recalibrate();
```
