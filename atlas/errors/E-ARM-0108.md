title: A delta asked to work in the whole box
match: the delta could not reach the corners of the cell
match: the working area was a dome and not a box
match: the platform is put back where it was asked, from the angles the inverse kinematics gave
match: the three arms are symmetric: a point on the axis gives equal angles, and the reach is a dome
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 32-02-three-arms-and-one-plate
---

## Symptom

Points in the corners of the cell, which looked inside the delta's range, are refused. The layout was drawn as a rectangle under the robot.

## Cause

A delta's reach is not a box but a dome that narrows toward the bottom. Of a grid of 486 points in a box, 386 were reachable; at a height of -0.40 m the platform could go 0.310 m out from the axis, and less lower down.

Each arm reaches a sphere's worth of points round its elbow, and the platform must be inside all three; the intersection is a dome, widest a little below the base and narrowing with depth. A box drawn round the dome's widest part has corners outside it.

## Fix

Lay the work out in the widest part of the dome, and check every point of the work with the inverse kinematics before fixing the layout.

```cpp
double th[3];
for (const Point& p : work_points)
  if (!rc::arm::delta_ik(robot, p.x, p.y, p.z, th)) refuse(p);
```

The dome is small for the robot's size; that is the price of 0.78 kg of moving parts against 18 kg for a six axis arm of a similar reach.
