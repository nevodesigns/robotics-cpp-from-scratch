## Symptom

When the tool points straight along the arm, joints 4 and 6 swing against each other for no motion of the tool.

## Cause

Joint 5 is at zero: joints 4 and 6 turn about the same axis and only their sum is fixed.

## Fix

Plan the orientation so joint 5 stays away from zero, or take the other wrist solution of the closed form, and keep the singular value above a floor.

```cpp
q[4] = 0.5;
```
