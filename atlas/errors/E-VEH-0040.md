match: the vehicle slides on a slope its motors can climb
match: the slope limit does not include the grip or the balance
match: what the vehicle can climb is the smallest of its motors, its tyres and its balance
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The map says a slope is passable and the vehicle slips on it, or leans over. The slope is gravel or grass.

## Cause

The limit was taken from the motors alone. The motors could climb 25.6 degrees and the gravel held 22.3. The slope limit is the least of three: what the motors can start on, what the tyres grip, and where the vehicle tips sideways.

Three things limit a slope: the motors' torque, the tyres' grip, and the vehicle's balance. The motors are only one. On a loose surface the grip runs out first, and the vehicle slides with the motors not even at their limit.

## Fix

Take the least of the three, with the grip of the surface under the vehicle.

```cpp
const rc::perceive::VehicleLimits lim = rc::perceive::vehicle_limits(drive, surface, track, cg_height);
```
