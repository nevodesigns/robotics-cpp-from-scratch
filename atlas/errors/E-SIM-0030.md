## Symptom

The transcript test fails every morning because the device's time of day is in every reply, and the fix that was applied lets it pass whatever the device says.

## Cause

Some bytes are meant to differ and a mask names them. Without a mask the same session at two times of day first differed at message 1 byte 2; with bytes 2 to 5 of replies masked there was none. The mask covers only its direction (a change to a request was still caught) and only its bytes (a reading one byte past the masked time was caught at message 1, byte 6).

## Fix

Mask exactly the bytes that must differ, in the direction they are in.

```cpp
Mask{Direction::from_device, 2, 4}
```
