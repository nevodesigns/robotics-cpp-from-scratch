## Symptom

After a change the arm is less accurate than before, and the error depends on the move.

## Cause

The model's masses were off: 10, 20 and 40 percent too heavy gave 0.00467, 0.00853 and 0.01455 rad, and 40 percent too light 0.03469.

## Fix

Keep the model in step with the arm (the payload of 29-02, a measured mass), and keep feedback with enough gain to cover what is left.

```cpp
scaled_model(model, factor);
```
