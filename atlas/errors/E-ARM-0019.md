## Symptom

A term added to push the joints toward the middle of their limits makes the tool move.

## Cause

The term was added straight onto the step. Any joint motion that is not in the Jacobian's null space moves the tool: unprojected it was felt as 1.244 m/s at the tool.

## Fix

Project the secondary motion through the null space before adding it. Projected, it moved the tool 0.00000000 m/s.

```cpp
const std::vector<double> dq_null = rc::arm::null_space_motion(j, want, lambda);
```
