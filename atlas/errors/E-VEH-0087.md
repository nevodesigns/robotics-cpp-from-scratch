## Symptom

The vehicle drives a long loop to reach a point half a metre behind it.

## Cause

A Dubins path goes forward only. To reach a point just behind, it must turn right round: the loop was 10.3 times as long as the distance.

## Fix

Allow reversing where the vehicle may back up (Reeds-Shepp paths, or reverse motions in the search), or accept the loop and say why.

```cpp
// forward only: a loop of about ten times the distance
// with reversing: back up 0.5 m
```
