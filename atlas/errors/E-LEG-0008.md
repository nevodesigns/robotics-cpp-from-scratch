## Symptom

The tilt from the feet wanders by about a degree though the legs are measured well.

## Cause

With 5 mm of noise in the feet's places the roll was uncertain by 0.92 degrees (four feet) and 1.29 (three): the feet are only about 0.4 m apart.

## Fix

Combine it with the IMU (35-04), weighting each by its noise, and use four feet when there are four.

```text
four feet average more noise out than three
```
