## Symptom

After a change to the arm, it is less accurate than before, and the error depends on the move.

## Cause

The model is wrong and is trusted. Computed torque is only as good as its masses: 10, 20 and 40 percent too heavy gave rms errors of 0.00467, 0.00853 and 0.01455 rad, and 40 percent too light 0.03469.

## Fix

Keep the model in step with the arm (the payload of 29-02, a measured mass), and keep enough feedback gain to cover what the model leaves.

```cpp
const rc::arm::ArmModel m_test = rc::arm::scaled_model(model, 1.2);   // test what 20 percent costs
```
