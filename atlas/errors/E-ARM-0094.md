## Symptom

The cell makes fewer parts an hour than its specification.

## Cause

The cycle was worked out from the moves at the rated speed (3.68 s) and left out the hand (0.8 s) and the slow approaches (1.07 s) and every speed cap.

## Fix

Add every piece of the cycle, with the limits that apply, and measure it.

```cpp
cycle_time(params, override);
```
