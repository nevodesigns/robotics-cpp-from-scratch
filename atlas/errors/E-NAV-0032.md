title: A frontier found in the wrong place
match: a frontier is a free cell next to one nobody has seen, and a wall or an unseen cell is not
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 16-12-where-to-look-next
---

## Symptom

The rover is sent to a cell it cannot stand in, or a wall beside the unknown is chosen as somewhere to explore, and the planner fails.

## Cause

A frontier is a free cell with an unknown cell directly beside it. A wall is known, and an unknown cell cannot be stood in, so neither is one. On a 30 by 20 map with the left two thirds seen, there were 20 frontier cells, all of them in the column x = 19.

## Fix

Test the cell itself and then its four neighbours.

```cpp
if (!grid.inside(x, y) || grid.classify(x, y) != Cell::free) return false;
```
