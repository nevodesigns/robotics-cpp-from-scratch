id: E-VEH-0045
title: A speed limit with no idea how far the ground is seen
match: the vehicle cannot stop before a hole it has just seen
match: the stopping distance is more than the distance to the ground line
match: a hole of 0\.4 m is seen by the scans that fall on it, and the first one sees it as far ahead as the ground line was
match: the speed to stop before ground first seen at a distance falls with the latency and rises with the braking
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-06-holes-and-drops
---

## Symptom

The vehicle sees the hole in time to know about it and not to stop for it.

## Cause

The ground is first seen 2.86 m ahead and the vehicle needs room for its latency and its braking: the fastest it could go was 2.49 m/s.

## Fix

Take the speed limit from the distance at which the ground is first seen.

```cpp
const double v = max_safe_speed(distance_to_ground_line, margin, decel, latency);
```
