## Symptom

A robot that drives fast has a position that is always a little behind where it is, by speed times a delay.

## Cause

The fix was used as the present. A delay of 0.25 s at 2 m/s left a steady bias of 0.49 m.

## Fix

Stamp measurements with the time they were taken and compare them with the state of that time.

```cpp
const Correction c = delayed_correction(history, fix.stamp, fix.x, fix.y, gain);
```
