## Symptom

The base turns with the arm held out to one side and rolls over toward the arm.

## Cause

A base is usually narrower than it is long (here a half track of 0.22 m). An arm out to the side moves the centre of mass toward the wheels on that side, so a turn toward it has less to hold the base down. With 2 kg in the hand reaching sideways, the limit on turning toward that side fell to 4.22 m/s2 from 7.92 tucked.

## Fix

Tuck the arm, or slow the turn, when the arm is out to the side.

```cpp
const rc::mobile::TipLimits t = rc::mobile::tip_limits(base, com, 0.25, 0.22);
const double v_turn = std::sqrt(t.left_turn * radius);   // the sideways limit for this pose
```
