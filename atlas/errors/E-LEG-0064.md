## Symptom

With a large push the command is poor and the body recovers badly.

## Cause

A projected gradient method with a fixed number of iterations (300) was used without checking that it had converged; for a horizon of 15 steps it needed 300 iterations.

## Fix

Check the change between iterations, warm-start from the last solution, and fall back to the fixed law if it has not converged.

```text
check the step size at the last iteration
```
