## Symptom

A learning controller quickly finds something that works and stays with it, though something clearly better was available.

## Cause

It only learns the value of moves it makes, and its greedy choice keeps it making the ones that worked. With no random moves it reached the large goal 1 times in 2000 episodes and settled for the small one, returning 1 where 4 was possible; with a tenth of the moves random, 2 times and 1.

## Fix

Explore on purpose: start the values optimistic, or give a bonus to rarely tried moves, and check how much of the task the learner has actually visited.

```cpp
std::vector<int> visits;
rc::learn::q_learning_visits(task, 2000, 0.1, 0.95, 0.0, 20.0, seed, visits, nullptr);
```

A good-enough result found early is the most common way learning by trial disappoints.
