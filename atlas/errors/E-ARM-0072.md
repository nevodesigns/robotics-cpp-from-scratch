## Symptom

The arm clears everything and the part it is holding does not.

## Cause

The planner used the arm alone: a part of 0.25 m took a pose from 0.090 m clear of an obstacle to -0.090 m.

## Fix

When a part is picked up, plan with a longer tool and a fatter last link, and bump the version.

```cpp
held_arm(radii, tool_length, part_length, part_radius);
```
