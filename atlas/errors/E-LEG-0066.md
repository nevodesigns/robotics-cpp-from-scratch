## Symptom

A glitch in a controller drives a joint at full torque into a stop or into the floor.

## Cause

Nothing between the controller and the motor limited the command: a command of 40 N m was cut to 20 by the limit.

## Fix

Clip every command to the peak the motor may give at the last stage before the motor, outside the controller.

```cpp
limit_torque(cmd, tau_peak);
```
