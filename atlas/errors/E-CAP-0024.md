title: A hazard with no scenario, in a green suite
match: a suite with the link scenario left out still passes, and the report puts the unacceptable hazard in the list of what is not covered
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-08-a-test-report-you-can-read
---

## Symptom

The suite is green and the worst hazard of the log has never been exercised.

## Cause

Without the link scenario every remaining scenario passed and suite_green was true, and the report read G2 unacceptable NOT COVERED, with not covered: G2, G3, G5, G6, G7.

## Fix

Print the uncovered hazards, worst first, in the report.

```cpp
hazard_coverage(hazards, scenarios, rows);
```
