match: lifting a leg with the body centred puts the centre of mass on an edge, and shifting it gives a margin

## Symptom

Every lift makes the whole body rock, and sometimes a foot goes down early.

## Cause

With the body centred the centre of mass falls on the diagonal edge of the triangle: the margin was 0.000 m.

## Fix

Shift the body over the middle of the three feet that stay down before lifting.

```cpp
crawl_margin_shifted(feet, lifted, &shift);
```
