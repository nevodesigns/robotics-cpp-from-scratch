id: E-VEH-0039
title: A gap judged by its width and not the vehicle's
match: the planner finds a route through a gap that the vehicle does not fit
match: a gap of 0.40 m for a vehicle of 0.5
match: a gap between two posts is open to the centre of the robot only if it is wider than twice the radius, and the inflation shows it
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-04-costmap-layers
---

## Symptom

A route goes between two posts, and the vehicle stops against one. The gap was checked against the robot's width by hand.

## Cause

The gap was judged wider than a point, not wider than the vehicle. With an inscribed radius of 0.25 m, a gap of 0.60 m left 2 free cells and one of 0.40 m left 0.

Inflation already says whether the robot's centre can pass. A second check of the gap's width against a number kept elsewhere can disagree with the map, and the planner trusts the map.

## Fix

Let the inflation decide, and never compare a gap with a width the planner keeps on the side.

```cpp
const bool open = master.at(i, j) < rc::perceive::kCostInscribed;
```
