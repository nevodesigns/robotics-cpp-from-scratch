## Symptom

The vehicle follows the path into an obstacle that lies on it.

## Cause

The controller was given no grid, so its score had no cost for cells: 15 poses in blocked cells, against 0 with the grid.

## Fix

Pass the cost grid to the controller, so blocked cells are in the score of every rollout.

```cpp
mppi_control(model, cfg, pose, path, idx, &grid, st);
```
