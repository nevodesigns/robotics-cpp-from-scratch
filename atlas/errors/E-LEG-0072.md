## Symptom

A walk is released because the robot can go fast enough, and then fails in the field on a slope, under a load or in a crowd.

## Cause

Speed was the only requirement checked. The quadruped job check has six, in order: speed against what the legs allow (1.60 m/s), terrain (the least slope limit, 27.8 degrees, and the step the servos can swing, 0.050 m), the push a catching step can answer (0.31 m/s), the knees with the load (39.5 K against 60 K) and getting up, the battery's range against 1.2 times the route (957 m at 0.6 m/s), and the speed the nearest person allows (3.48 m/s at 3 m).

## Fix

Write the job and the robot down as numbers and check all six before releasing it.

```cpp
rc::legged::Job job;
job.speed = 0.6;
job.payload = 1.0;
job.route = 500.0;
const rc::legged::JobVerdict v = rc::legged::check_job(robot, job);
if (!v.released) explain(v.first_fail);
```

A walk of 0.6 m/s with 1 kg over 500 m passed all six. Passing is a screen and not a proof: the real robot still has to be tried.
