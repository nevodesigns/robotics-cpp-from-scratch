## Symptom

When the operator lets go of the stick or the link drops, the vehicle goes on at the last speed.

## Cause

The last request was held. With a timeout the vehicle went 1.99 m after the commands stopped (0.5 s) and 4.99 m with 2.0 s.

## Fix

Treat a request older than a timeout as zero, ramp down at the braking limit, and count the timeout in the stopping distance.

```cpp
if (t - last_request > timeout) target = 0.0;
```
