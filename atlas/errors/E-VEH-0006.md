## Symptom

A longer vehicle is steadier at speed and turns badly on the spot, tearing the ground and heating its motors. The chassis was lengthened for a bigger payload bay.

## Cause

The chassis was made long for stability. In a pivot the front wheels slide at atan(L / B) to their rolling direction: 50.2 degrees for a wheelbase of 0.30 and a track of 0.25, more as the chassis gets longer.

In a pivot each wheel moves round a circle at an angle to the way it rolls. The longer the chassis relative to its width, the larger that angle, so the more the wheels slide and the more force it takes.

## Fix

Choose the shape for the work: short and wide to pivot, long and narrow to go straight.

```cpp
const double angle = rc::vehicle::front_slip_angle(wheelbase, track);
```
