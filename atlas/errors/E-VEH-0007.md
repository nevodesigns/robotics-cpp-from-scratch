## Symptom

The car runs wide at the tightest bend of a path that looks fine on the screen.

## Cause

The path asked for a circle smaller than the car can drive, L over the tangent of the largest steering angle: here 0.742 m. The steering sat at its limit and the car drove a bigger circle.

## Fix

Give the planner the smallest turning radius and refuse curvatures beyond it.

```cpp
const double min_r = rc::vehicle::min_turning_radius(car);
```
