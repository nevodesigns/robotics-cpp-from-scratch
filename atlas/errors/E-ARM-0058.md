## Symptom

A planner built on a grid or a map of free space cannot be used for a six joint arm.

## Cause

The configuration space has six dimensions and its obstacles have no simple shape: the only thing available is a collision query.

## Fix

Use a sampling planner (RRT) that finds the free space by asking the collision model.

```cpp
rrt(problem, start, goal);
```
