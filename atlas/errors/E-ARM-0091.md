id: E-ARM-0091
title: A protective distance with the person taken as still
match: the arm stopped too close to the person
match: the safety distance was only the robot's braking distance
match: the protective distance is the sum of its terms, and grows with the robot's speed
match: a robot that brakes at the protective distance stops before the person, and one that leaves out a term does not
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 31-06-staying-far-enough-from-a-person
---

## Symptom

The arm stops, as designed, and the person is touched or nearly. The cell passed its review with a calculation of the robot's braking.

## Cause

The protective distance counted only the robot's stopping distance, with the person taken as standing still. The person walks on during the reaction and the braking. With a person at 1.6 m/s, 100 ms of reaction, 5 m/s2 of braking and 17 cm of intrusion and uncertainty, a robot at 1.0 m/s needed 0.85 m, and even one at rest needed 0.33 m.

Between the person entering the sensed zone and the robot stopping, there is the sensing and reaction time and the braking time. During both, the person keeps walking toward the robot, and the robot keeps moving during the reaction. A distance that counts only the robot's braking leaves all of that out.

## Fix

Add the person's travel over the reaction and stopping times, the robot's travel before braking, the intrusion and both uncertainties.

```cpp
const double d = rc::arm::protective_distance(ssm, v_robot);
```
