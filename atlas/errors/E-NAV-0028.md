## Symptom

A path that bears gently to the left near the edge of the plot is counted as having a violent turn, and the turn count is off by exactly the ones that cross west.

## Cause

Headings from atan2 jump from about pi to minus pi at due west, so a 45 degree change from west to south west is a difference of 315. A difference over pi must be taken as 2 pi minus it. With it, west then south west counted as 45 degrees and was one turn at a threshold of 0.5 and none at 1.0.

## Fix

Wrap the change.

```cpp
if (change > pi) change = 2.0 * pi - change;
```
