## Symptom

The arm shudders at the start and end of each move and the gearboxes are noisy.

## Cause

A trapezoid of speed steps the acceleration from zero to its limit in no time: a jerk of 4000 rad/s3 over a millisecond, against a limit of 20. The S curve takes 1.220 s for the move the trapezoid does in 1.000 s.

## Fix

Limit the jerk (an S curve) and accept the small extra time.

```cpp
scurve_plan(distance, V, A, J);
```
