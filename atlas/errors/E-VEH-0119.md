## Symptom

The vehicle clips a thing that is on the arc of its turn but not in front of it.

## Cause

The field was a rectangle in front. A thing on the arc of a turn of radius 2 m at 40 degrees was outside it and inside the swept field.

## Fix

Sweep the field along the arc of the turn (the curvature from the steering or the turn rate).

```cpp
in_swept_field(x, y, length, half_width, curvature);
```
