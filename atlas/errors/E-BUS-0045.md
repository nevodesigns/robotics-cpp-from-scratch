title: The latest transform used for an old scan
match: a scan stamped a moment ago is placed with where the robot was then, and using where it is now puts every obstacle out by speed times delay
match: a frame not in the tree, two trees that do not meet, and a time outside what was reported are each refused for their own reason
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-15-where-is-it-in-which-frame
---

## Symptom

The map has walls that are double, or smeared along the robot's path, and the smear is longer when the robot is faster.

## Cause

A scan is a statement about a moment. With the robot at 0.6 m/s and a scan used 0.5 s after its stamp, a post was placed at x = 2.60 with the transform at the stamp and 2.90 with the latest, out by 0.30 m, speed times delay. A lookup must also refuse what nobody reported: an unknown frame, two separate trees, or a time outside the reports.

## Fix

Look up the transform at the scan's stamp, and treat a refusal as no answer.

```cpp
if (tree.lookup("map", "laser", scan_stamp, t) == LookupStatus::ok) place_scan(t);
```
