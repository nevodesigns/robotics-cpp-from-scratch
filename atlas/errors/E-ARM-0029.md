## Symptom

A simulated arm let fall swings back higher than it started, or settles sooner than it should with no friction in the model.

## Cause

A first order step. Integrating the dynamics by Euler's method adds or removes energy every step: after a second at a step of a millisecond the energy was out by 0.03723 of itself, against 0.000000000496 with a fourth order step.

## Fix

Use a higher order integrator, or a much smaller step, and test the energy of a free fall with no friction: it must stay constant.

```cpp
rc::arm::step_dynamics(model, q, qd, tau, 0.001, true);   // fourth order
```
