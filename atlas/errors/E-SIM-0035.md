## Symptom

The controller is tuned in simulation and misbehaves on the bench, and the cause is a difference in the two backends that was never looked for.

## Cause

Both backends answer to one interface and start with the plant at 0. With no delay and no loss the bench's run was the simulation's: an rms difference of 0.0000, a largest of 0.0000 and a shift of 0, and both passed the criteria of 0.3, 1.0 s and 0.02. When they differ in this condition the difference is a bug in a backend and not a property of the machine.

## Fix

Test that two backends set up to be the same are the same.

```cpp
RC_CHECK(compare(sim.position, bench.position, 10).rmse < 0.05);
```
