title: Poses for the sweep from a source that is too slow
match: deskewing makes the cloud worse
match: the poses at the start and the end of the sweep are the same pose
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Deskewing changes nothing, or makes the cloud worse, because the two poses are not the poses at the start and the end of the sweep.

## Cause

A pose at 1 Hz is the same pose for the start and end of a sweep, so the interpolation does nothing. The interpolation needs the poses within 0.1 s of each other, such as odometry.

## Fix

Take the poses from the wheel odometry or an inertial sensor at the rate of the scan or faster.

```cpp
deskew(scan, odometry.at(sweep_start), odometry.at(sweep_end));
```
