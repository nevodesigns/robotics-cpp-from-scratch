## Symptom

The robot falls over when told to sit while it is walking.

## Cause

The sit was acted on at once; the machine must stay in the walk until it has slowed to a stop (here from 0.6 m/s) and then lower.

## Fix

Wait for the walk to stop before lowering.

```cpp
if (in.want == kWantSit && in.stopped) lower();
```
