## Symptom

After smoothing, the path takes a different route from the plan, through places the planner never checked.

## Cause

The data weight was small and the smooth weight large: the path moved 0.63 m from the plan, where a light smoother moved 0.04 m.

## Fix

Keep the data weight high enough that the deviation is within the clearance the planner left, and check the result against the grid.

```cpp
cfg.data_weight = 0.5; cfg.smooth_weight = 0.1;
```
