## Symptom

The localisation usually works and sometimes settles in the wrong place.

## Cause

With 50 particles the robot was lost in 10 of 10 runs; with 3000, in 0.

## Fix

Use enough particles to have some near the truth at the start, and more for a bigger map.

```cpp
MonteCarlo mc(map, 3000, seed);
```
