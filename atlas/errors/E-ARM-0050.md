## Symptom

A payload within the rating cannot be held at arm's length. A payload the arm lifts easily folded is dropped at full reach.

## Cause

The rating was taken from the best pose. A payload's torque grows with its distance from the joint: the shoulder could hold 2.50 kg with the arm out and 10.00 kg folded.

A mass's torque on the shoulder is its weight times its horizontal distance from the shoulder. At full reach that distance is largest, and so is the torque, so the payload the shoulder can hold there is least.

## Fix

Work the payload out for the worst pose of the task, from the arm's own torque and the payload's through the Jacobian.

```cpp
const double kg = rc::arm::max_payload(model, q_worst, tool, limits, rated);
```
