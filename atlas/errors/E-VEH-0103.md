id: E-VEH-0103
title: A stuck detector that cries wolf, or sleeps
match: the vehicle is called stuck while it is crawling
match: the vehicle is called stuck while it is waiting at a stop
match: a vehicle commanded to stop is never called stuck
match: a vehicle crawling slower than the threshold is called stuck: the threshold must sit under the slowest crawl
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-08-when-the-vehicle-is-stuck
---

## Symptom

The vehicle starts to back up or spin in the middle of a slow, correct crawl, or at a stop line.

## Cause

The distance threshold (0.1 m in 3 s) was above the crawl: a vehicle moving at 0.02 m/s was flagged after 3.0 s. A vehicle commanded to stop was never flagged (0) because the test needs a command to move.

## Fix

Test only while a speed above a floor is commanded, and set the distance under the slowest crawl the vehicle is meant to do.

```cpp
cfg.min_disp = 0.5 * slowest_speed * cfg.window;
```
