## Symptom

A list on screen is out of date until something forces it to rebuild, though the model reports the right row count when asked.

## Cause

A view learns of a change only from a signal, and `beginInsertRows` and `endInsertRows` are what emit it. A model that changes its container without them is correct to every query and wrong to every view. Measured: the silent model had **2** rows and its `Repeater` **1** item; the good one had 2 and 2.

## Fix

Put the change between the calls:

```cpp
beginInsertRows(QModelIndex(), row, row);
values_.push_back(value);
endInsertRows();
```
