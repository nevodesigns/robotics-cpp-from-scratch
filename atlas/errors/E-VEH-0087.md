## Symptom

The vehicle drives a long loop to reach a point half a metre behind it.

## Cause

A Dubins path is forward only: the loop was 10.3 times as long as the distance.

## Fix

Allow reversing (Reeds and Shepp paths) where the vehicle may back up, or accept the loop.

```cpp
// forward only: a loop; with reversing: back up 0.5 m
```
