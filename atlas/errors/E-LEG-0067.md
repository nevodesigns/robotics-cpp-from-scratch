## Symptom

A robot working on a hot day suddenly stops dead in the middle of a task when a motor reaches its temperature limit, often somewhere awkward.

## Cause

Heat was handled only at the limit: full power until the knee is at its thermal limit, then off. The safety layer derates instead. Below 0.8 of the limit the torque is full, between 0.8 and 1.0 it is scaled down in a straight line, and at 1.0 the layer asks for off. Knees at 0.9 of their limit were derated to 0.50 of the torque and at 0.95 to 0.25.

## Fix

Scale the torque down as the heat rises, and refuse to start movements that need full torque (36-02 refuses to rise above 0.8).

```cpp
rc::legged::SafetyIn in;
in.heat = knee_heat_share;            // 0.9 gives scale 0.50
const rc::legged::SafetyOut s = rc::legged::safety_decide(in, rc::legged::SafetyLimits());
tau = rc::legged::apply_scale(tau, s);
```

A derated robot walks slowly and weakly. That is the point: it can still get somewhere safe.
