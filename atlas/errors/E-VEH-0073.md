## Symptom

After a robot is lifted and put down the heading is off by a fixed amount for the rest of the run.

## Cause

The wheels were stopped while the robot was carried, and the gyro was read as the bias. The wheels alone said still for 176 of 200 samples; with the accelerometer, 0.

## Fix

Also require the accelerometer to be quiet for a window.

```cpp
return hi - lo <= accel_limit_;
```
