## Symptom

A replayed or learned motion speeds up and slows down in places the person did not.

## Cause

The samples' times were assumed to be one step apart. With a nominal 30 Hz the longest gap in a good episode was 0.039 s because of jitter.

## Fix

Store the time of every sample and resample onto a steady grid before use.

```cpp
Episode steady = resample(raw, 30.0);
```
