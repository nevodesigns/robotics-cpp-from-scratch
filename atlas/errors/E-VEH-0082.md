## Symptom

The car overshoots every corner of a planned path, or the controller saturates its steering and loses the path.

## Cause

The planner knew cells and not the car. A corner of the grid path needed a curvature of 15.7 per metre and the car's limit is 1.35; the hybrid path's largest was 1.35.

## Fix

Plan with the motion of the car: search poses, and drive arcs it can make.

```cpp
const HybridResult r = hybrid_astar(grid, start, goal, cfg);
```
