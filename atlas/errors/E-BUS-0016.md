id: E-BUS-0016
title: A message sent once before anyone listened
match: a topic that is not remembered gives a late subscriber nothing of what came before it
match: a map published once is seen by every node that starts later only if the topic is remembered
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-06-the-subscriber-that-arrived-late
---

## Symptom

The robot works when the nodes are started in one order and has no map, or no description, when they start in another.

## Cause

A message published once, before a subscriber exists, is gone on an ordinary topic. A map published at 0 s was seen by 0 of 3 nodes that started at 1, 5 and 9 s, and by 3 of 3 once the topic was transient local with a keep of 1.

## Fix

Mark once-only topics as remembered.

```cpp
bus.advertise("map", Durability::transient_local, 1);
```
