## Symptom

Feet skid at the start of each stance.

## Cause

The swing ended at speed and the ground then moved under the foot: it landed at 0.000 m/s here and at 0.80 m/s with a straight up-and-down path.

## Fix

End the swing at rest and let the stance load the leg.

```cpp
stance_position(td, v, t, out);
```
