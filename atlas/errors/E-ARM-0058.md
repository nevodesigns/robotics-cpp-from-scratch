## Symptom

A planner built on a grid or a map of free space cannot be used for a six joint arm: the grid would not fit in memory.

## Cause

The configuration space has six dimensions and its obstacles have no simple shape. A grid of 100 values per joint is 10 to the 12 cells, and nothing gives the shape of the free space; the only thing available is a collision query at a pose.

## Fix

Use a sampling planner (RRT) that finds the free space by asking the collision model one pose at a time.

```cpp
const rc::arm::PlanResult r = rc::arm::rrt(problem, start, goal);
if (!r.found) report(r.iterations);
```
