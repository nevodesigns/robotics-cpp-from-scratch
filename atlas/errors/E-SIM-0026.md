id: E-SIM-0026
title: A plant that holds its last command for ever
match: with a watchdog the emulator stops acting on a command that has gone stale, and without one it keeps going
match: when the link dies the controller's last command is the one the plant would keep acting on, which is why the watchdog exists
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-09-the-bench-link
---

## Symptom

The controller program crashes or the cable comes out, and the motor on the bench carries on at full speed until it hits something.

## Cause

A held command is right for a tick and wrong for a second. One command of 6 and then silence left the plant at 0.720 and speed 0.000 with a watchdog of 5 ticks and at 10.920 and speed 12.000 without. With the target stepped to -1 and the link dead for a second, the plant ended at -21.85 holding its last command and -1.45 with the watchdog.

## Fix

Give the plant side a watchdog that falls back to a safe command.

```cpp
if (watchdog_ > 0 && tick_ - last_command_tick_ > watchdog_) used = 0.0;
```
