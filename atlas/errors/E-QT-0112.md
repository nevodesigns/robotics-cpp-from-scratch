## Symptom

A station prints `invalid arguments` and a usage text, and the operator reads the whole usage to find that the typo was in the third option.

## Cause

An error with no name of the option and no value sends the reader to look for it. A message that quotes the option and the text it was given is read once. Measured: **11** bad lines were refused and **11** messages named what was wrong, including the unknown option `hots` and the missing value after `--host`.

## Fix

Say the option, the rule, and what was typed:

```cpp
*err = QStringLiteral("--port must be a whole number from 1 to 65535, not ") + parser.value(port);
```
