## Symptom

The estimated velocity of a person jumps by a metre a second, though they walk steadily.

## Cause

The velocity was the difference of two positions. Two positions 5 cm in error over 0.1 s differ by 0.7 m/s: the difference had an error of 1.073 m/s.

## Fix

Estimate position and velocity together with a filter that uses all the past. The filter's error was 0.122 m/s.

```cpp
rc::perceive::kf_predict(f, dt, accel_var);
rc::perceive::kf_update(f, zx, zy, meas_var);
```
