## Symptom

A vehicle out on a job reboots for an update, and the job is lost, or the vehicle is left with half a release and a flat battery. The update was pushed to the fleet on a schedule.

## Cause

Nothing checked the vehicle's state before updating. A vehicle that was moving, in a mission and at 20 percent charge was refused for 3 reasons once the checks were in.

A vehicle in a mission has state that a reboot loses, and a vehicle with little charge may not finish the update. The update server cannot see either unless the vehicle checks before accepting.

## Fix

Refuse an update unless the vehicle is parked, has no mission and has enough charge, and list every reason, not only the first.

```cpp
const std::vector<rc::drive::Refusal> why = rc::drive::check_update(on_board, bundle, policy);
```
