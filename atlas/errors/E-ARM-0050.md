## Symptom

A payload within the rating cannot be held at arm's length.

## Cause

The torque of the payload grows with the distance: the shoulder held 2.50 kg with the arm out and 10.00 kg folded.

## Fix

Work out the payload for the worst pose of the task, from the arm's own torque and the payload's through the Jacobian.

```cpp
max_payload(model, q, tool, limits);
```
