title: Gains tuned in a simulator with no delay
match: the leg shook on the real robot though it was perfect in simulation
match: the controller rang as soon as it ran on the hardware
match: a delay and a lag in the actuator make a joint that settled in the simulator ring on the real one, and a joint with too much delay does not settle at all
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 36-07-what-changes-on-the-real-robot

## Symptom

The gains that gave the quickest, cleanest step in simulation make the real joint ring or oscillate without settling.

## Cause

The simulator had no delay and no lag, so a stiff loop looked best there: gains of 40 and 3.0 settled in 0.24 s with 0.2 percent of overshoot. Add 20 ms of delay and a 10 ms lag and the same gains overshot 23.5 percent and never settled. A delay is phase lag at the frequency where the loop crosses, and a quick loop crosses high, where the same delay costs more.

## Fix

Put the delay and lag in the simulator before tuning, and prefer gains with margin over the quickest step.

```cpp
rc::legged::JointSim j;
j.kp = 40.0;
j.kd = 3.0;
j.delay = 0.020;
j.lag = 0.010;
const rc::legged::StepResult r = rc::legged::simulate_step(j, 0.5, 2.0, 0.001);   // overshoot 0.235, never settles
```

The gentle gains of 10 and 1.5 settled in 0.53 s without the delay and still settled with it.
