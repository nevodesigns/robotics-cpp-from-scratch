## Symptom

A small car at full lock scuffs its front tyres and turns less than the model says.

## Cause

Both front wheels were pointed at one angle. On a circle the inner wheel is nearer the centre and must turn more: at the smallest circle the two should be 5.8 degrees apart.

## Fix

Steer each wheel at right angles to its line to the centre of the circle (Ackermann).

```cpp
double inner = 0.0, outer = 0.0;
rc::vehicle::ackermann_angles(car, radius, inner, outer);
```
