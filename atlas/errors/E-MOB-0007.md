## Symptom

The base parks where the target is just within reach, and a small stopping error puts it out of reach.

## Cause

The place was chosen at the edge of the ring, often because it was the nearest. A place in the middle of the ring has the whole tolerance on both sides; one at the edge has none on one side.

## Fix

Score the margin in the ring as well as the travel, so the planner prefers the middle unless travel matters more. With the margin weighted most, the base was put 0.36 m away with a margin of 1.00, the middle.

```cpp
rc::mobile::ParkParams p;
p.w_margin = 1.0;     // 1 in the middle of the ring
p.w_travel = 0.05;
const rc::mobile::ParkChoice c = rc::mobile::choose_park(m, ring, target, current, obstacles, n, p);
```
