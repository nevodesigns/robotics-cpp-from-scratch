## Symptom

The foot meets a bump, goes on along its path and the leg is jammed or the body stumbles.

## Cause

The swing was not told the foot was down: a bump of 3 cm is met before the swing ends and the swing must stop.

## Fix

Stop the swing the moment the contact sense says the foot is down, and plant it there.

```cpp
reach_down_step(search, contact, dt, rate, max_extra);
```
