## Symptom

An interface item has no width, or no colour, or is at the wrong place, and every test passes: nothing was printed and nothing was refused.

## Cause

A value computed in QML that is wrong but valid, such as a division of zero by zero, is not an error to the engine. Measured: `width: 0 / 0` loaded with **0** failures, and the width was **0**.

## Fix

Ask for the value, and assert on it:

```cpp
RC_CHECK_NEAR(ui.read(root, "width").value.toDouble(), 320.0, 1e-9);
```
