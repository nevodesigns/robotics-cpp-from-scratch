## Symptom

Things line up while the robot is steady and smear while it turns.

## Cause

Two sensors' clocks were never compared, and one is behind the other. A lag of 40 ms at 1 rad/s is 2.3 degrees.

## Fix

Estimate the lag from two signals of the same motion and correct the stamps. The shift that matched the gyro and the wheels was found as 0.12 s.

```cpp
const int lag = rc::locate::estimate_lag(wheel_rate, gyro_rate, max_lag);
```
