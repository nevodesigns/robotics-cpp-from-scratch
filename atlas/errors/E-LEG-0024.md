## Symptom

The robot stands firmly and tips as soon as it lifts a foot.

## Cause

The check was made with four feet down; lifting one leaves a triangle, and a centred body has a margin of 0.000 m in it.

## Fix

Work out the margin for the feet that will be on the ground, for each leg to be lifted.

```cpp
crawl_margin(feet, lifted, com);
```
