## Symptom

Walking over a small bump, a foot lands early, keeps pushing down and lifts that corner of the body. The robot rocks or its body tilts at every obstacle a few centimetres high.

## Cause

The swing was played out to its end whatever the contact sense said. A 3 cm bump is met before the swing finishes, so the foot is already on the ground while the swing is still commanding it downward, and the rest of the swing drives it into the bump.

## Fix

Stop the swing the moment contact is believed (34-07's contact sense, with its hysteresis and blank) and let the leg take its share of the weight there. On the bump of the lesson no reaching down was needed at all: an extension of 0.000 m.

```cpp
if (contact.on) {              // from the contact sense of 34-07
  swing.stop();
  ground[i] = rc::legged::update_ground(ground[i], foot_z, 0.2);
}
```

Early contact is information as well: it says the ground is higher there, which the ground estimate should learn.
