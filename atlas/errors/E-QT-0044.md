## Symptom

A filter, a ramp or a distance integrates with `dt = 0.01` once per tick, and after a stall it is behind: the ramp is late, the integral is short, the robot has gone less far than it thinks.

## Cause

The count of ticks is not the elapsed time. Every tick the loop could not deliver is time that passed and was not counted. `ticks * period` is a lower bound.

Measured over 400 ms with one 100 ms block, in three runs: **30 or 31** ticks of 10 ms counted as **300 or 310** ms, **90 to 100** ms short of the real time. The grid's last slot said **400** ms.

## Fix

Use the clock for time and the ticks only for when to look:

```cpp
const double dt = slot_dt;      // from the slot numbers, (slot - last_slot) * period
```
