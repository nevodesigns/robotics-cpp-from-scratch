match: the joint oscillates at high frequency in light poses
match: gains tuned for the heaviest pose are good there and ring at the lightest; gains that follow the inertia are good at both

## Symptom

A joint buzzes or oscillates in the poses where the arm is light. The buzzing starts as the arm folds and stops as it stretches.

## Cause

The velocity loop's bandwidth is Kv over J. With the gains for the heaviest pose it was three times too high at the lightest, past what the loop's delay allows: a step there overshot 13909338 percent (it blew up), against 0.0 at the heaviest.

The velocity loop's bandwidth is its gain over the inertia. Gains for the heaviest pose give a bandwidth three times higher when the inertia is three times lower, and the loop's delay, which does not change, eats the phase margin until it oscillates.

## Fix

Scale Kv with the inertia. With gains that followed it the step overshot 0.0 percent at both ends.

```cpp
rc::arm::CascadeGains g;
g.kp_vel = inertia * wv;
```
