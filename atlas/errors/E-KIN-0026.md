## Symptom

A tool that is nearly on its heading turns almost a full circle to correct it, or a controller oscillates when the heading passes plus or minus pi.

## Cause

Headings are angles, and an angle repeats every turn. Subtracting two of them as numbers gives an answer that can be a full turn out. Measured: 3.14 against -3.14 was **6.28** as a difference and **-0.00318531** the short way round.

## Fix

Wrap the difference into one turn:

```cpp
inline double heading_error(double target, double actual) { return rc::math::wrap_angle(target - actual); }
```
