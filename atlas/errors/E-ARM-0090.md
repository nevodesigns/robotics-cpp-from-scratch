## Symptom

The peg is stuck on the chamfer and pushing harder does not move it. It is worst on dry, clean steel or with rubber on the peg.

## Cause

The chamfer's slope is inside the friction cone. If the tangent of its angle is not more than mu, no push has a sideways part left over after friction, and more force only presses harder. At a friction of 1.2 on a 45 degree chamfer the peg could not slide (0) however soft the hand.

On a slope, the push down has a part along the slope and a part into it. Friction resists the first with mu times the second. If the slope is shallower than the friction angle, friction wins whatever the push, and the peg stays.

## Fix

Lower the friction (a little oil) or make the chamfer steeper, and back off before trying again.

```cpp
if (!rc::arm::can_slide(hole)) change_the_part_or_the_surface();
```
