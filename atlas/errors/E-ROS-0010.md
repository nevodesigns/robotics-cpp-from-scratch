## Symptom

The robot keeps acting, confidently, on data that stopped arriving a minute ago, and every node reports that it is running.

## Cause

A node can be active and produce nothing. The sensor stopped at 5.00 s with its last scan at 4.91 s: the controller's watchdog stopped the arm at 5.12 s, the supervisor saw the stall at 5.17 s, a deadline of 0.25 s after the last output, and restarted the sensor through its life cycle, and the arm moved again at 5.21 s with no move command in between. A healthy graph gave 99 moves and no events.

## Fix

Give every important output a deadline and every node a watchdog of its own.

```cpp
supervisor.watch("sensor", sensor_life, 0.25, true, {});
```
