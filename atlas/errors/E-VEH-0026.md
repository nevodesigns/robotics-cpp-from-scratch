## Symptom

A difference is explained, written up, and wrong.

## Cause

The first explanation was scrubbing wheels. It predicted a short turn from rest too, and the friction made no difference. The turns were 1.01, 0.74 and 0.85 of those asked.

## Fix

List the candidates, and run an experiment that separates them before writing any of them down.

```cpp
// vary one thing: friction, time since the drive, whether it moved at all
```
