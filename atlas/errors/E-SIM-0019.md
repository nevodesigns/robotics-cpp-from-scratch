## Symptom

Two parts of a simulation given seeds 1 and 2 show a family resemblance in their noise, and a statistical check finds correlation that is not meant to be there.

## Cause

A seed is a starting point and starting points that are close must not give streams that are alike. A mixer that works flips about half the output bits when one input bit changes: 32.1 of 64 on average. Seeds derived from the names motor0, motor1, motor2 and so on differed in 32.1 of 64 bits between neighbours.

## Fix

Mix the master seed, and mix a hash of the name into it.

```cpp
return mix64(mix64(master) ^ h);
```
