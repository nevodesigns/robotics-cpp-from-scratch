## Symptom

The planner sees a wall that slopes, or the floor as a wall, depending on how the sensor is mounted.

## Cause

The points were used in the sensor's frame. A sensor tipped down 5 degrees sees a vertical wall as leaning: in the vehicle's frame the same wall had heights from -0.19 to 0.69 m.

## Fix

Put the points into the vehicle's frame with the measured mount before anything uses them.

```cpp
const auto in_vehicle = rc::perceive::transform_points(rc::perceive::scan_to_points(scan), mount);
```
