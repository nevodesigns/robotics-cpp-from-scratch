## Symptom

A thin obstacle shows in the scan only now and then at a distance, though the sensor's quoted range is far greater. It is a post, a pole or a person's leg at a distance.

## Cause

The range was quoted for a large target. At one degree between beams the points are 0.175 m apart at 10 m, and a post of 0.3 m is hit by two beams only up to 17.2 m.

A lidar's beams fan out with a fixed angle between them, so the gap between neighbouring points grows with range. A thin thing far away falls between beams. A range quoted for a large flat target says nothing about a post.

## Fix

Plan the stopping distance from the range at which the narrowest thing that matters is hit at least twice.

```cpp
const double seen_to = rc::perceive::max_range_for_width(0.3, angle_inc, 2);   // 17.2 m at 1 degree
```
