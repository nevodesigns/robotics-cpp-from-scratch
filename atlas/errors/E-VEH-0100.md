## Symptom

The vehicle is at full speed when it reaches a bend, and slides or overshoots.

## Cause

The speed was capped at the bend and not before it: going into a bend of radius 1 m the capped speeds asked for a deceleration of 20.0 m/s2, where the limit is 1.5.

## Fix

Add the backward pass: no point faster than one the vehicle could still brake from to reach the next (1.50 m/s2 with it).

```cpp
v[i] = std::min(v[i], std::sqrt(v[i + 1] * v[i + 1] + 2.0 * a_dec * ds[i]));
```
