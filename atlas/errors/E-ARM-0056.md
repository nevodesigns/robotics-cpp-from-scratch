## Symptom

The planner is slow, and most of its time goes on motions nowhere near anything.

## Cause

Fixed small steps look at every part of a motion alike. An edge far from obstacles took 201 poses at a fixed step; conservative advancement crossed it in 2, because a large clearance allows a large step.

## Fix

Let the step grow with the clearance: the arm cannot move more than its sweep bound per radian, so a clearance d allows a step of (d less the margin) over that bound.

```cpp
const double du = (d - margin) / full_sweep;   // what edge_conservative does at each step
```
