## Symptom

When the receiver drops to a plain fix the estimated position jitters and wanders, though the robot is steady.

## Cause

The fixes were all given the noise of a fixed fix. On the single fixes the rms error was 2.81 m, against 0.67 m when they were weighed by level.

## Fix

Give each fix the noise of its level, and none to a fix of none.

```cpp
const double sigma = fix_sigma(fix.fix);
```
