title: A connection that nothing ends
match: a scope keeps its connections and ends them together, by clear and by being destroyed
match: a connection with a receiver ends with it, and one with none does not until it is ended
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 11-10-the-handler-that-outlived-its-item

## Symptom

An object that was destroyed is called by a signal, or a lambda that captured a local runs after the local is gone. It crashes rarely, in code that was fine.

## Cause

A connection to a function with no receiver object has no owner, and lasts as long as the sender does. Measured: a connection with a receiver ended when the receiver was deleted (**1** call in all); one without ran until `clear` ended it, and a scope that was destroyed ended its lambda with it.

## Fix

Keep what `connect` returns, and end it with the scope:

```cpp
scope.add(QObject::connect(&sensor, &Sensor::reading, [&tally] { ++tally.hits; }));
```
