title: A mode that flaps
match: the vehicle speeds up and slows down as a sensor drops in and out
match: the degraded mode changes every few seconds
match: a requirement that flaps is not followed up and down
match: a worse requirement is taken at once, and a better one one step at a time after the dwell
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The vehicle lurches between speeds as a marginal sensor drops out and comes back.

## Cause

The mode followed the requirement directly. It changed 60 times in 120 s.

## Fix

Go down at once; go up only after a dwell in which the requirement stayed better, and one step at a time. With a dwell of 10 s the manager changed the mode 1 time.

```cpp
rc::drive::ModeManager manager(policy);
const rc::drive::Mode m = manager.update(now, required);
```
