## Symptom

The vehicle turns back at what it thinks is the right moment and runs out of charge on the way home.

## Cause

The way home was taken as flat. It turned back 16.8 m out and was 25 J short, because the sand in between cost more; planned over the ground it turned back at 12.0 m and had 71 J spare.

## Fix

Plan the way home over the terrain and take its energy, at every decision.

```cpp
const double home_wh = route_wh(terrain, plan_offroad(...).points, power);
```
