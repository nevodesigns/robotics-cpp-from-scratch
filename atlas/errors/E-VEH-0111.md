## Symptom

Each new plan starts behind the vehicle, which turns back to join it, is then off the path, and plans again, over and over. The planner takes a second or two and the vehicle is moving.

## Cause

The plan was begun from the pose at the time of the request, and the vehicle moved on while it was made. The new path started 1.16 m behind it, past the off-path threshold, so it planned again: 83 plans in 90 s, and it never arrived.

A plan that starts where the vehicle was when it asked starts behind where it is when the plan arrives. The follower sees the vehicle off the path, which is a reason to plan again, and the loop never ends.

## Fix

Begin the plan from where the vehicle will be when it arrives: the path, advanced by the speed times the planning time. From the predicted pose the plan started 0.06 m away and the vehicle arrived in 20.1 s.

```cpp
const rc::drive::CarPose from = rc::drive::predicted_pose(pose, path, idx, speed, planning_time);
```
