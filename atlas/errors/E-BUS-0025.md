## Symptom

A robot opens its gripper when it was told to close, or two requests swap results, when two calls are in flight at once.

## Cause

Answers arrive in any order, so they carry the number of the call they belong to. Two calls answered in the other order each got their own answer, and a call with no answer was sent at 0, 10 and 20 and timed out at 30, so that nothing waits for ever.

## Fix

Number every call and match by the number.

```cpp
const auto found = calls_.find(response.id);
```
