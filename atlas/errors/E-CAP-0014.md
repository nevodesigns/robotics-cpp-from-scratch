## Symptom

The geofence works in every test and the robot leaves the field with the fence reporting that it is inside.

## Cause

With no buffer the robot left a corridor of 3 m after 9.6 m and went on to y = -18.3 m believing itself on the axis. With an assumed drift of 0.05, 0.10, 0.20 and 0.40 m a metre it stopped after 20.1, 10.1, 5.1 and 2.6 m, the truth outside for the first two; the real drift at the edge was 0.156 m a metre and growing.

## Fix

Assume the drift for the longest distance without a fix.

```cpp
stop_buffer(stopping_distance, drift_per_metre, metres_since_fix);
```
