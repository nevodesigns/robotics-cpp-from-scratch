## Symptom

With a load in the hand the base tips when it stops, at a deceleration that was safe empty.

## Cause

The payload was not counted. With 5 kg in the hand at full reach, the braking limit fell to 5.19 m/s2, below the 5.89 m/s2 the wheels grip on a dry floor: the base tips before it skids.

## Fix

Include the payload in the mass model at the tool, and cap the braking at the tipping limit as well as the grip.

```cpp
rc::mobile::MassModel masses;
masses.payload = 5.0;   // kg at the tool
rc::mobile::com_world(m, base, q, masses, com);
```

From 1.0 m/s the loaded base needed 0.096 m to stop at that limit.
