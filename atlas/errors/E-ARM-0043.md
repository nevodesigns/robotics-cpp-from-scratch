## Symptom

The arm presses on a surface with a force that depends on where the target was put, and breaks things when the target is a little too low.

## Cause

Under impedance the force is the stiffness times the distance of the target past the surface: 4.02, 9.75 and 19.00 N for 200, 500 and 1000 N/m with 2 cm.

## Fix

Choose the stiffness for the force you want (force = stiffness times depth) and put the target the right depth past the surface.

```cpp
const double k[6] = {2000, 2000, 500, 10, 10, 10};
```
