## Symptom

A move works in every test and the arm overheats a few minutes into a production run.

## Cause

Once is not all day. What warms the winding is the rms heat over the cycle: run back to back with no wait, the steady rise was 118 K, against a limit of 75; with a wait of 4 s it was 27 K.

## Fix

Work out the steady rise for the cycle as it will be run, and add a wait or slow the move until it is under the limit.

```cpp
const double rise = rc::arm::duty_rise(model, move, motor, joint, 4.0);
```
