match: one leg hit its end stop on a tilt
match: the leg lengths at home are all equal for a symmetric hexapod, and a raise lengthens them
match: a raise and a tilt need a range of leg lengths

## Symptom

As the platform tilts, one leg hits the end of its stroke and the motion stops short.

## Cause

The leg lengths for each pose were never compared with the stroke. A platform's reach is limited by its legs' travel, not by joint angles: a raise of 50 mm with a tilt of 10 degrees needed legs between 0.366 and 0.410 m, a spread that a short stroke does not cover.

## Fix

Compute the six lengths for every pose on the path and check each against the stroke before sending the path.

```cpp
double len[6];
rc::arm::stewart_lengths(hexapod, pose, len);
for (double l : len) if (l < l_min || l > l_max) refuse(pose);
```
