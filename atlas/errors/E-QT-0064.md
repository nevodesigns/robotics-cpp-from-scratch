## Symptom

A fault shows at 7 seconds in the terminal and at 6 in the window, and the two charts do not agree about when it happened.

## Cause

Each view mapped time to its columns by its own rule, or took its window from a different place. Measured: a spike at sample 7000 of 10000 was column **35** of 50 in the text and column **140** of 200 in the picture, **0.71** and **0.70** of the way across, and the column was **119** pixels tall.

## Fix

Put the window in the model and map every sample into a column of the same range of time:

```cpp
decimate(samples_, t0_, t1_, columns)
```
