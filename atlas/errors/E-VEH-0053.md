match: a detection is a tentative track, three in a row confirm it, and one false detection is never believed
match: a short gap is coasted through and the id kept, a long one drops the track and the target gets a new one
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Ghosts stay in the list of objects, or an object hidden for a moment is forgotten and comes back as new.

## Cause

The life of a track had no rules, or the wrong ones. A target hidden for 2 frames should keep its id; one gone for 8 should be dropped.

## Fix

Confirm a track after a few hits, coast it through short gaps, drop it after a few misses, and choose the numbers for the scene's frame rate and occlusions.

```cpp
if (tracks_[i].misses > cfg_.max_misses) drop(i);
```
