## Symptom

Tested at the ideal parking spot the arm always reaches; in use, with the base stopping a little short or long, it sometimes does not.

## Cause

Only the ideal place was tested. A base stops with an error, and the question is how far from the ideal it may stop and still reach. Half the ring's width is that tolerance: 36.0 cm at the table's height and 31.5 cm at a target 0.20 m lower.

## Fix

Compare the base's stopping accuracy with the ring's tolerance, with a factor for safety (the job check of 33-10 asks for three times the stopping sigma).

```cpp
const double tol = rc::mobile::park_tolerance(ring);   // 0.36 m
if (tol < 3.0 * stop_sigma) refuse("the base does not stop well enough");
```
