## Symptom

On a straight the vehicle weaves from side to side, or on a curve it cuts inside the path.

## Cause

The lookahead was too short for the speed (0.1 m: 9 crossings against 2 at 1.5 m), or too long for the curve (2.0 m: strayed 0.149 m against 0.015 m at 0.6 m).

## Fix

Make the lookahead a number of seconds ahead of the speed, within a range that suits the tightest curve on the path.

```cpp
cfg.lookahead = std::max(0.6, 1.0 * speed);
```
