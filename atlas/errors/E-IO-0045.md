## Symptom

After a reconnect, a command appears to be outstanding for ever, and `ask` refuses
every new one. Or a command that was sent before the cable came out is answered by
something that arrived after it. Or every timestamp from the device is a few seconds
out, for ten minutes.

## Cause

The session is the thing that owns the conversation, and a conversation belongs to a
connection. Three things carried across a reconnect hold state that is no longer true.

**A request in flight.** Its answer was on the old connection and will never come. It
sits in `Waiting` until its attempts run out, and in the meantime `begin` refuses a
new one. Worse, it is resent onto a link where the device has been power cycled and
its reply cache is empty, so a command the device already did is done again.

**The sequence tracker.** A device that restarted counts from zero again and the
tracker reports a phantom loss, or calls everything late.

**The clock map.** The fit was made before a step in the device's clock and averages
the old samples in with the new for ten minutes. Lesson 08-12 measured a map kept
across a five second restart at **5011 ms** out.

Here, a device off for five seconds and rebooted at thirty seconds, with the session
starting its map again on the new connection, was within **12.0 ms** for the next
twenty. The request that was in flight when the cable came out was counted as
abandoned and the state went back to idle, so the next one could be sent.

## Fix

On a new connection, start the whole conversation over, and count what was lost:

```cpp
if (requester_->state() == RequestState::Waiting) ++abandoned_;
link_.reset(new Link(port_, storage, 1e12));
requester_.reset(new Requester(*link_, timeout, attempts));
tracker_ = SequenceTracker<std::uint16_t>();
unwrapper_ = TickUnwrapper<std::uint16_t>();
clock_.reset();
```

Counting the abandoned request is the part that is easy to leave out. The caller asked
for something and it may or may not have happened, and a robot that cannot tell what
it asked for and did not get told about is a robot to be careful with.
