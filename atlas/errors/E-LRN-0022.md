## Symptom

A learned policy with an excellent test error is released and fails in use: in states it was never shown, on a bad sensor reading, or the next time someone tries to retrain it.

## Cause

Only one of six questions was asked. The test error says how well the network answers single questions in the demonstrated states. It says nothing about driving from the job's starts, how much the guard does, a NaN input, the actuator limit, or whether the policy can be made again.

## Fix

Write the job as numbers and run every check, in order, and report the first that fails with all the figures.

```cpp
const rc::learn::PolicyVerdict v = rc::learn::check_policy(task, job, candidate, train, test);
if (!v.released) explain(v.first_fail);
```

The DAgger policy passed all six: test error 0.00022 against the line's 0.01240, success 98 percent, handover 3.2 percent.
