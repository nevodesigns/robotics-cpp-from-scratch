## Symptom

Two uses of a macro in one file fail to compile, reporting a redefinition of a
generated function or variable name.

## Cause

The macro pasted __LINE__ directly, so the preprocessor joined the literal text
rather than the number it stands for, and every use produced the identical name.
Pasting suppresses expansion of the operands, which is a genuine wart rather than
a subtlety you should have known.

## Fix

Two levels of macro. The outer one expands its arguments, and the inner one does
the pasting:

```cpp
#define MINI_CONCAT_INNER(a, b) a##b
#define MINI_CONCAT(a, b) MINI_CONCAT_INNER(a, b)
```

Now MINI_CONCAT(mini_fn_, __LINE__) becomes mini_fn_42. Use __COUNTER__ instead
where the compiler supports it and two macros must be able to share a line.
