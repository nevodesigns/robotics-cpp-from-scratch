## Symptom

Next to a long wall the vehicle thinks the ground is vertical, and the floor is an obstacle.

## Cause

The wall held more points than the ground and the search took the biggest plane: 90 degrees. Allowing 30 degrees found the ground at 0.2.

## Fix

Refuse planes tilted beyond what the vehicle can drive on.

```cpp
if (tilt_degrees(plane) > max_tilt_deg) continue;
```
