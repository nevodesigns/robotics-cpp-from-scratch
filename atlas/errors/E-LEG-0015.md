## Symptom

The legs are cool standing and the knees overheat in a trot.

## Cause

Standing has four feet sharing the weight and a trot two: the knee gave 4.33 N m standing and 8.65 N m in the stance, and heat goes as the square (14.0 K against 28.1 K).

## Fix

Work out the heat for the gait's own number of feet down, using the rms over the cycle.

```cpp
cycle_rms_torque(tau_stance, tau_swing, duty);
```
