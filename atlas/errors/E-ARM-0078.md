## Symptom

A grip that held every part on the bench drops some on the line. The rate of drops rises through the shift as the pads collect dust.

## Cause

The friction was taken from a clean, dry pad. Friction is the part of the grip that is not known, and on the line pads get dusty, oily or worn. At half the friction the payload the hand can hold falls from 16.0 kg to 8.0 kg.

The force a pad can resist is the friction times the squeeze, and the friction is the one number in that product that is not measured on the line. Oil, dust and wear lower it, and every lowering is a smaller hand.

## Fix

Use a lower friction than the sheet's and a safety factor, and test with the real surfaces in the real state.

```cpp
rc::arm::Gripper hand;
hand.mu = 0.5 * sheet_mu;   // a dirty pad is a smaller hand
const double m_max = rc::arm::max_payload(hand, hand.f_max, accel, 1.5);
```
