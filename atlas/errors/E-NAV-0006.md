id: E-NAV-0006
title: A filter's variance read as a statement about its accuracy
match: an innovation is a residual divided by what was expected of it
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The estimator reports a covariance that looks healthy while the robot is
visibly, measurably in the wrong place. A monitoring page shows a confident
number, an operator trusts it, and the fault is found some other way entirely.

Or the reverse: somebody uses the reported deviation as an accuracy figure in a
report, and it is out by an order of magnitude in either direction.

## Cause

A filter's variance is computed from the variances it was given. It is a
statement about the model, and it says nothing about whether the model is true.

The same filter fused with fixes of six different ages, over forty seconds:

| fix age | actual error | what the filter claimed |
|---|---|---|
| 0.000 s | 0.0325 m | 0.0376 m |
| 0.080 s | 0.0782 m | 0.0376 m |
| 0.150 s | 0.1449 m | 0.0376 m |
| 0.300 s | 0.2930 m | 0.0376 m |
| 0.600 s | 0.5920 m | 0.0376 m |

The claim is identical in every row, including the one where the estimate is
sixteen times worse than it says. Nothing was wrong with the arithmetic. The
arithmetic was answering a different question: given these variances and this
motion model, how uncertain should I be? The measurements being late is not in
that question, and neither is a wheel slipping, a beacon reflecting, or a
variance somebody guessed once and never revisited.

## Fix

Treat the reported variance as what it is, and get your accuracy figure from
somewhere that can be wrong.

**Compare against truth where you can.** A surveyed point, a fiducial at a known
place, a second sensor that fails differently. Anything the filter did not
already use.

**Watch the innovations.** The residual between prediction and measurement,
divided by the deviation the filter itself predicted, should sit near one. Well
above it means the filter is more wrong than it believes, and it is the only
check available that uses no outside information. Its limits are in
`E-NAV-0007`.

**Publish both numbers.** A page that shows the reported deviation beside the
measured innovation statistic is honest. One that shows the reported deviation
alone invites exactly the mistake above, and operators are right to believe what
a screen tells them.

Never report a filter's own covariance as an accuracy specification. It is a
consistency figure, and it is only meaningful once something outside the filter
has confirmed that its inputs describe reality.
