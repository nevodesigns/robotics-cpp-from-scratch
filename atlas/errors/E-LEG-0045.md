## Symptom

The robot is cleared for slopes up to the angle its feet can grip, and on a slope well short of that it stalls with a leg at full stretch or tips over.

## Cause

Only friction was checked. Three things limit a robot on a slope, and the lowest gives first: grip (31.0 degrees at a friction of 0.6, 16.7 when wet at 0.3), tipping over the downhill feet (35.1 degrees for a centre of mass 0.27 m high), and the legs' reach for a body kept level (27.8 degrees with 0.10 m spare up and 0.12 m down). For these legs the reach is the limit.

## Fix

Take the least of the three, and let the body tilt with the slope if the reach is the one that limits.

```cpp
const double a = std::min({rc::legged::max_slope_friction(mu),
                            rc::legged::max_slope_tip(0.19, 0.27),
                            rc::legged::max_slope_level(0.19, 0.10, 0.12)});   // 27.8 degrees here
```

A wet floor changes the answer to friction, 16.7 degrees, which is why the limit has to be worked out for the surface.
