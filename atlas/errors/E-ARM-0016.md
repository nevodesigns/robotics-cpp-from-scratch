## Symptom

An arm that works in most poses misbehaves in a few: it cannot move one way, or moves that way at an enormous joint speed.

## Cause

Nobody measured how near a singularity the pose was. The smallest singular value was 0.172 in a generic pose and fell smoothly to zero as joint 5 straightened (0.1337, 0.0313, 0.00317).

## Fix

Compute the smallest singular value for every pose that is planned, and keep it above a floor.

```cpp
smallest_singular(jacobian(table, q, base, tool, table.convention));
```
