## Symptom

Every foot is a centimetre or two from where it should be, consistently, and the body's tilt from the feet reads a few degrees off on flat ground.

## Cause

The joint zeros were taken as true. A servo's zero is wherever the horn went onto the spline, a tooth one way or the other: offsets of a few degrees. Offsets of 2, -3 and 4 degrees put the foot 14.6 mm from where it was commanded.

## Fix

Calibrate: put the leg in a dozen varied poses, measure where the foot is, and fit the offsets. They were found as 1.95, -3.16 and 4.19 degrees, leaving 3.4 mm, which was the measurement noise.

```cpp
double delta[3], rms = 0.0;
if (rc::legged::calibrate_offsets(leg, q_cmd, p_meas, 12, 30, delta, &rms)) apply_offsets(delta);
```
