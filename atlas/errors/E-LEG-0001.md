## Symptom

The foot lands a few centimetres to the side of the place asked for, and left and right legs differ.

## Cause

The sideways offset of the hip (0.08 m) was left out of the geometry, or used with the same sign on both sides.

## Fix

Put the offset in with the side's sign (+1 left, -1 right) in both directions of the kinematics.

```cpp
g.side = -1; // a right leg
```
