## Symptom

The robot tries to step out of a fall, fails, and lands harder than if it had gone soft, with its legs stiff and half extended.

## Cause

It tried to recover when there was not enough time. A recovery step here needs 0.30 s. The quick fall had 0.22 s and should have gone soft; the slow fall had 0.38 s and could recover. A robot that tries and then goes soft too late gets the worst of both.

## Fix

Decide once and early: recover if the time to the ground is longer than the step needs, go soft if it is shorter.

```cpp
const int act = rc::legged::fall_action(theta, omega, 0.27, 0.30, warn, warn_rate, fallen);
// kFallNone, kFallRecover (1), kFallSoft (2), kFallen (3)
```

Re-deciding every tick lets the answer flip between recovering and going soft in the middle of the fall.
