## Symptom

The planner grows a large tree and takes many iterations to hit the goal.

## Cause

One tree has to find the goal, a small target in a large space: RRT used 58 nodes on average against 23 for RRT-Connect, and found a path in 3 of 6 runs against 6.

## Fix

Grow a tree from each end and join them (RRT-Connect).

```cpp
rrt_connect(problem, start, goal);
```
