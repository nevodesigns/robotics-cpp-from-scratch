## Symptom

The arm shudders at the start and end of each move and the gearboxes are noisy.

## Cause

A trapezoid of speed steps the acceleration from zero to its limit at once: a jerk of 4000 rad/s3 over a millisecond, against a limit of 20. The step excites the arm's resonances.

## Fix

Limit the jerk with an S curve, and accept the small extra time: 1.220 s for the move the trapezoid does in 1.000 s.

```cpp
const rc::arm::SCurve s = rc::arm::scurve_plan(distance, V, A, 20.0);
```
