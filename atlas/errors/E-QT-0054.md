## Symptom

A chart panel has a toolbar as tall as the plot. Resizing the window makes all three bigger together, and the plot is never as large as it should be.

## Cause

A layout with no fixed sizes and no stretch factors has no way to prefer one widget. Measured: three plain widgets in 300 pixels were **100**, **100** and **100**.

## Fix

Fix what must not change, and give the plot a stretch factor:

```cpp
toolbar_->setFixedHeight(30);
layout->addWidget(plot_, 1);
```
