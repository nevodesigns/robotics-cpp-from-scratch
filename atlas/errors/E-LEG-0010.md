## Symptom

The robot falls over if the gait is stopped in the middle of a step.

## Cause

A trot has 2 feet down at a time, which cannot hold a body still; a walk with a duty of 0.75 has 3.

## Fix

Bring the feet to a standing pose (all four down) before stopping a trot, as 36-02's state machine does.

```cpp
count_stance(gait, t) >= 3
```
