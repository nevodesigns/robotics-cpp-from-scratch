match: a body falls away from the line of its feet sooner the further off it starts, and later the taller it is

## Symptom

A trot that works with the body centred falls when the body is a little off to one side.

## Cause

The time to fall falls quickly with the offset: 0.41 s at 10 mm and 0.22 s at 30 mm, and with some speed away 0.15 s.

## Fix

Keep the centre of mass over the line of the stance feet with the balance control of 35-05.

```cpp
trot_stance_ok(t_stance, x_off, v_off, L, h, 0.7);
```
