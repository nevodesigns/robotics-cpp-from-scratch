## Symptom

Every step lands with a thump that shakes the body.

## Cause

The foot went up and down in straight lines: it landed at 0.80 m/s where the smooth path landed at 0.000 m/s.

## Fix

Use a path that has zero speed and acceleration at lift-off and touchdown.

```cpp
swing_position(p0, p1, h, tau, out);
```
