## Symptom

The planner finds no way between two obstacles that are farther apart than the vehicle is wide.

## Cause

A fixed radius made large enough for the far wall joined two posts at 2 m into 1 object.

## Fix

Keep the radius no bigger than the point spacing at the range the points were seen. The grown radius kept the posts as 2.

```cpp
const auto objects = rc::perceive::cluster_points(points, 0.05, 1.5 * angle_inc, 1);
```
