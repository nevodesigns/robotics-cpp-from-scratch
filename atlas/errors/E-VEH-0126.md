## Symptom

A stretch of the log is missing and the report says the run was fine.

## Cause

Nothing looked at the time between rows. A jump of 20 s is a finding of its own, and the other tests, run across it, say nothing about what happened in it.

## Fix

Report every gap longer than a limit, and treat what happened in it as unknown.

```cpp
if (t[i] - t[i - 1] > cfg.max_gap) report_gap();
```
