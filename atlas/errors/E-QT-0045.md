## Symptom

A sensor line parses most of the time and fails now and then, with half a line in the log: "temp 21" and then ".5". It happens more when the system is busy or the baud rate is high, and never in a test that feeds the parser a whole string.

## Cause

A `QIODevice` gives you what has arrived, and `readAll()` or `read(n)` has no notion of a line. A message can be cut anywhere, and the cut moves from run to run. Splitting each chunk at the newline and taking every piece as a line makes a line whose cut falls inside it into two pieces, neither of which is the line.

Measured: 33 bytes of two lines, read 7 at a time, were 3 chunks. Splitting each chunk gave **4** pieces for 2 lines, and the first line was never whole.

## Fix

Keep the part that has not ended, and join the next chunk to it:

```cpp
line_.append(c);                    // not yet a line
if (c == '\n') { /* line_ is complete: hand it over, then line_.clear() */ }
```
