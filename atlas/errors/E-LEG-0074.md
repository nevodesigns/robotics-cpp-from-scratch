## Symptom

Settings are changed (speed, route) and the walk still fails.

## Cause

30 kg of load fails at requirement 4, the knees and getting up, which no speed or route setting can fix.

## Fix

Look at which requirement failed first: the robot's own (knees, battery, legs) needs a different robot, the job's (speed, ground, push) a different job.

```cpp
first_fail >= 4 means a robot or site limit
```
