title: A sliding joint taken for a turning one, or the zero of a joint forgotten
match: a SCARA arm, two joints that turn and one that slides, is x and y from the angles and z as the height less the slide
match: a prismatic joint slides along z and does not turn, a missing variable is zero, and an offset of the zero shifts a revolute joint
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 13-12-the-table-that-describes-an-arm

## Symptom

The tool of a SCARA arm moves sideways when the vertical axis moves, or the arm is turned by a quarter turn at its zero.

## Cause

A prismatic joint adds its variable to d and leaves theta alone, and a revolute one the other way round. The zero of a revolute joint is a fixed offset in theta. Measured: **243** configurations of a SCARA table matched the closed form; a slide of 0.5 on an offset of 0.2 gave z = 0.7 and no turn.

## Fix

Add the variable to the right letter:

```cpp
const double theta = row.theta + (row.prismatic ? 0.0 : q);
const double d = row.d + (row.prismatic ? q : 0.0);
```
