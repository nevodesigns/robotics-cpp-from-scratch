## Symptom

A map made from a turning robot is smeared, with walls doubled or bent.

## Cause

Every beam was placed from the pose at the start of the sweep, though the vehicle moved during it. A straight wall ahead was 0.119 m rms from straight with the vehicle turning at 1 rad/s.

## Fix

Place each beam in the world from the pose it was fired from, interpolating the pose through the sweep. Deskewed, the wall was 0.0000 m from straight.

```cpp
const auto cloud = rc::perceive::deskew(scan, start_pose, end_pose);
```
