## Symptom

The car overshoots every corner of a planned path, or the controller saturates its steering and loses the path.

## Cause

The planner knew cells and not the car. A grid search joins cells by straight steps, and its corners have a curvature no car can drive: one corner of the grid path needed 15.7 per metre, where the car's limit is 1.35.

## Fix

Plan with the car's motion. Hybrid A* searches poses and expands them by arcs the car can make, so its path is drivable by construction: its largest curvature was 1.35.

```cpp
const rc::drive::HybridResult r = rc::drive::hybrid_astar(grid, start, goal, cfg);
assert(rc::drive::max_curvature(r.path) <= 1.0 / turn_radius + 1e-9);
```
