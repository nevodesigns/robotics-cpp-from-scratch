## Symptom

The sampling controller's tracking is rough and varies from run to run. It varies with the seed.

## Cause

Too few rollouts to find a good one: a mean error of 0.152 m with 8 samples, against 0.077 m with 100.

A sampling controller is only as good as the best of its samples. With few, the best one is often poor, and which one is poor depends on the random draw.

## Fix

Raise the samples as far as the processor allows at the control rate, and test with several seeds.

```cpp
rc::drive::MppiConfig cfg;
cfg.samples = 300;
```
