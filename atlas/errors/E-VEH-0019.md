## Symptom

After many turns the heading is wrong by the same share of the total turned.

## Cause

The track width was taken from a ruler. Tyres contact the floor over a patch, and the effective track differs from the geometric one: a track 3 percent too small made a full turn read 11.1 degrees wrong.

## Fix

Calibrate the effective track from a measured turn, and use that.

```cpp
rc::vehicle::WheelOdometry odo(effective_track, scale);
```
