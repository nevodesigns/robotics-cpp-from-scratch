## Symptom

The tool cannot be put pointing down at the edges of a table that the data sheet says is in reach. The corners of the table are refused with the tool pointing down.

## Cause

The orientation and the joint limits were left out. With the tool down the arm reached 96 percent of the table with no limits and with limits of plus and minus 120 degrees, and 78 percent with plus and minus 60.

Reaching a point with the tool down uses up degrees of freedom on the orientation, leaving fewer for position, and the joint limits cut further. The task's own question, at each point, with the limits, is the only one that answers it.

## Fix

Put the task to the arm before the cell is built: the pose at each point, the limits and the tool offset.

```cpp
const double share = rc::arm::table_coverage(table, tool, x0, x1, y0, y1, z, 0.05);
```
