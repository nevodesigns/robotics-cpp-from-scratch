## Symptom

Some poses work and some send the knee the wrong way: the leg folds forward like a bird's, hits the body, or the foot lands in the right place with the leg in a pose the robot cannot hold.

## Cause

The inverse kinematics has two answers for every reachable place, the knee bent one way or the other, the plus and minus of an arccosine. A quadruped's knees bend backward, so the robot must always take that one. Taking whichever sign the code happened to produce, or mixing them between legs, gives poses that reach the place and are wrong. The two knees gave the same foot to 0.000000000 m: the foot alone cannot tell them apart.

## Fix

Choose the knee's sign once, for every leg, and pass it in.

```cpp
double q[3];
if (!rc::legged::leg_ik(leg, x, y, z, -1, q)) refuse();   // -1: the knee bent back, always
```
