## Symptom

When the base turns while the arm holds a pose, the hand drifts sideways, and a controller that should keep the hand still on a line does not. It shows only when the base turns; driving straight, the hand stays on its line.

## Cause

The hand's velocity was computed from the arm's joints and the base's forward motion, and the base's turn was left out. A turning base sweeps the hand round the base's centre and turns it.

A turn of the base moves the hand as the base's rate times the lever from the base's centre to the hand, sideways, and turns the hand's orientation by the same rate. Leave that column out of the Jacobian and the controller never sees the motion, so it cannot correct it.

## Fix

Use the whole-body Jacobian, with a column for driving and one for turning as well as one for each joint: eight columns for a differential drive base and a six joint arm.

```cpp
const rc::mobile::WbJacobian j = rc::mobile::wb_jacobian(m, base, q);   // columns: drive, turn, six joints
```

A differential drive cannot slide sideways, which is why there are two base columns and not three.

The whole-body Jacobian is the arm's Jacobian in the world, plus one column per base rate; test it against a finite difference of the hand's pose.
