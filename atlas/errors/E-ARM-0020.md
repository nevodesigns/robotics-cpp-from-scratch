title: An arm that sits against a limit with a spare joint
match: the arm hits a joint limit while a redundant joint is unused
match: the seven joint arm behaves like a six joint arm
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A seven joint arm stops against a joint limit and the extra joint is not used.

## Cause

Nothing used the freedom. With the null space term the margin went from 0.086 to 0.156 over 6 s with the tool held; without it, it stayed at 0.086.

## Fix

Use the null space to steer the joints toward the middle of their limits (or away from a singularity), at a rate the joints can take.

```cpp
self_motion_run(table, q, base, tool, k, kp, seconds, dt, lambda);
```
