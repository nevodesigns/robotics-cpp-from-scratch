id: E-NUM-0004
title: Motion in the wrong direction
match: expected .*(x|y).* to (be|equal)
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 00-04-first-motion

## Symptom

The robot moves along the wrong axis, or turns the wrong way. Facing along y and
driving forward changes x instead.

## Cause

Cosine gives the x component of a direction and sine gives the y component.
Swapping them rotates every motion by ninety degrees. The other common version is
passing degrees to a function that expects radians, which scales every angle by
about fifty seven.

## Fix

Check that x uses cos and y uses sin. Confirm every angle is in radians: the
standard library trigonometry functions accept nothing else. Convert with
degrees times pi divided by 180 at the boundary, once, and keep radians
everywhere inside.
