## Symptom

A crawling robot tips over as it lifts a leg, though the stability check said it had plenty of margin.

## Cause

The margin was measured against the polygon of all four feet. With a foot in the air the support is the triangle of the other three, and the centre of mass that was well inside the rectangle may be on or beyond the edge of the triangle. A robot standing centred, with its hips 0.19 m fore and aft and its feet 0.15 m out, had a margin of 0.000 m in each of the four triangles: on the diagonal, with nothing to spare.

## Fix

Measure the margin against the feet that will be on the ground during the step, not the feet that are on it now.

```cpp
double feet[4][2];
rc::legged::default_feet(0.19, 0.15, feet);
const double m = rc::legged::crawl_margin(feet, lifted, com);   // 0.000 m for a centred body
```

The same mistake in a trot is worse: two feet make a line, and a line has no inside at all (35-02).
