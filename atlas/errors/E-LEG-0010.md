title: A trot taken for a gait that can stand still

match: a walk always has three feet down, a trot two, and a short duty has a flight

## Symptom

A trotting robot told to stop in the middle of a stride falls over, though a walking robot stops fine.

## Cause

A trot was treated as a gait that can stand still. A walk with a duty of 0.75 always has 3 feet down, and three feet can hold a body still. A trot with a duty of 0.5 has 2, which cannot: it balances only by moving.

## Fix

Stop a trot by bringing the legs to a stance with all four down, or by changing to a walk first.

```cpp
rc::legged::GaitTiming walk;
walk.duty = 0.75;   // always three feet down: may stop at any point
// a trot (duty 0.5) is brought to four feet down before it stops
```

A duty of 0.35 has a flight phase, no foot down for 30 percent of the period, which is a run and is not built here.
