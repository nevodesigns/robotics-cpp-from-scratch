## Symptom

The arm always reaches a pose with the elbow up, which takes it the long way round and overloads a motor, though the other way was quicker and lighter.

## Cause

There are two ways of reaching most poses, and they differ in the angles to move through, in the limits they meet and in what the motors hold. A planner that takes the first it finds ignores all three. Measured: the two ways for (2.2, 0.3, 1.2) asked **0.765126** and **0.732123** of the limits; from near each, the plan took that one; with the limits tightened to between them, the one that could be held; an ok plan took **1.21006** s.

## Fix

Consider both, drop those that are not legal or not holdable, and take the quickest:

```cpp
if (move.duration < best_duration) { best_duration = move.duration; plan.solution = ...; }
```
