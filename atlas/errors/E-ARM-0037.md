## Symptom

A friction compensation tuned at a working speed is wrong at low speed and at rest. It fits at the speed it was tuned at and is out at low speeds and reversals.

## Cause

Friction was taken as one number. It has a Coulomb part, a Stribeck part (higher at breakaway, falling with speed) and a viscous part: here a peak of 0.0559 N m at 0.017 rad/s, a dip to 0.0503 at 0.05 rad/s, and 0.0550 at 1 rad/s.

Friction is highest just as the joint breaks free, falls as speed rises (the Stribeck effect), then rises again with viscous drag. A single number is right at one speed only.

## Fix

Model all three, smooth the sign over a small speed, and identify them from runs at several speeds.

```cpp
const double tf = rc::arm::friction_torque(fric, v);
```
