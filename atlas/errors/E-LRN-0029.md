## Symptom

A learned or computed policy takes a route that is short and risky, or long and timid, and nobody chose that.

## Cause

The discount was left at a customary value. It weighs the near future against the far, and so it trades the length of a route against the risks along it. With gamma 0.80 the policy went round and reached the goal on 95 percent of runs; with 0.95 it took the corridor beside the pits and reached it on 54 percent.

## Fix

Choose the discount for the task: compute the policy for a few values and look at what each does, the share of bad ends included, before fixing one.

```cpp
for (double gamma : {0.8, 0.9, 0.95, 0.99}) report(rc::learn::outcome_stats(g, rc::learn::greedy_policy(g, rc::learn::value_iteration(g, gamma, 1e-10, nullptr), gamma), 4000, seed));
```

If the answer must not depend on it, the risk belongs in a constraint, not in the discount.
