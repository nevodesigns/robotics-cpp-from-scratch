## Symptom

The vehicle stands in front of an obstacle that was not on the map when it planned, or the path keeps changing and the vehicle makes little progress.

## Cause

Never replanning gives the first: it never arrived. Replanning every 0.2 s gave the second: 86 plans, arriving in 21.4 s.

## Fix

Replan for reasons (a changed goal, a blocked path, being off the path, an old plan), with a cooldown. For reasons it made 5 plans and arrived in 20.9 s.

```cpp
const rc::drive::ReplanReason why = rc::drive::should_replan(cfg, now, last_plan, pose, path, idx, grid, goal_changed);
```
