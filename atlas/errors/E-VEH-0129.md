## Symptom

A vehicle with exactly enough energy is refused, or one with a little too little is let go.

## Cause

The comparison was `>` for `>=` (or the other way). The energy check passes at 5.00 Wh for a mission of 4.50 and a reserve of 0.50 and fails at 4.99.

## Fix

Write what the rule says (at least the mission plus the reserve) and test at the edge from both sides.

```cpp
ok = soc >= mission + reserve;
```
