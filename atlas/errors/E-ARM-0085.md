## Symptom

The arm stops, or reports contact, in free space, at some poses and speeds.

## Cause

The threshold was below what the gravity model gets wrong: with gravity 5 percent out, 0.05 N m gave 1 false alarms and 0.8 N m gave 0.

## Fix

Set the threshold above the model's error over the whole workspace, found by running free motion, and a margin on top.

```cpp
det.thr = 1.5 * worst_free_residual;
```
