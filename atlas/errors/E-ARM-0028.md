## Symptom

The model's torques do not hold the arm still, or hold it in some poses and not in others.

## Cause

A sign or an arm of a cross product wrong in the recursion. The checks that catch it: the torque of gravity against the slope of the potential energy (matched to 0.00000001 N m), and a rod held out by m g L over 2 (3.924 N m).

## Fix

Test the model against things that are true (the slope of the energy, a rod by hand) before using it.

```cpp
gravity_torques(model, q) ~ d(energy)/dq;
```
