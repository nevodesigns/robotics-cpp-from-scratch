## Symptom

Two estimates of the same reach volume disagree, or the order of two robots flips between runs. The difference between two robots changes sign when the seed changes.

## Cause

The volume is found by sampling, and its error shrinks only as the square root of the number of points: sixteen times the points gave a quarter of the error. With too few points the noise is larger than the difference being claimed.

A sampled volume is a share of a box, and the share has binomial noise: its standard error falls only as the square root of the number of points. A claim of a few percent needs enough points that the noise is well under that.

## Fix

Report the standard error with the volume, and use enough points that it is well below the differences you compare. With 40000 points the SCARA's estimate was good to about 0.4 percent.

```cpp
double se = 0.0;
const double v = rc::arm::workspace_volume(test, box, 40000, seed, &se);
```
