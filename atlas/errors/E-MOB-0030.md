## Symptom

A failed job is answered by changing a setting, and it keeps failing.

## Cause

The failure was a design limit. Parking is a matter of the floor plan; stopping accuracy, payload and the camera are the robot's design; battery, travel speed and people are the job's settings. When the first failure is a design one, no setting helps.

## Fix

Read which kind the first failure is before acting, and change the robot or the site for a design limit.

```cpp
const bool design = v.first_fail >= 2 && v.first_fail <= 4;
if (design) escalate("the robot cannot do this job as built");
```
