## Symptom

A test passes and it is not the test that was written: a typo in a time, a number cut short, or a line skipped, and nothing complained.

## Cause

A hand typed file has mistakes of every kind. A script of 14 lines had 11 bad lines, found in one parse and reported with their numbers (2 3 4 5 6 8 9 10 11 12 14), and left the scenario empty. A number is the whole word: 1.5e is an error and not 1.5. A script with no end, with two, with events after its end, or with nothing in it was refused.

## Fix

Report every error with its line, read numbers whole, and leave nothing behind on failure.

```cpp
if (end != word.c_str() + word.size()) return false;
```
