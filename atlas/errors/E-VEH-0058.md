title: A degree of longitude taken as a degree of latitude
match: east west distances are too long by a third
match: the cosine of the latitude was left out
match: a degree of latitude is 111 km, and a degree of longitude is that times the cosine of the latitude
match: the local frame and the fix convert back and forth
match: the flat approximation is good to centimetres at a kilometre and falls off with distance
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 25-02-gnss-and-the-lever-arm

## Symptom

The robot's path east and west is stretched compared with its path north and south.

## Cause

A degree of longitude is shorter by the cosine of the latitude. At 52 degrees it is 68458 m, against 111195 m for latitude.

## Fix

Multiply the longitude difference by the cosine of the latitude of the origin.

```cpp
east = kEarthRadius * std::cos(f.lat0 * kDegrees) * (lon - f.lon0) * kDegrees;
```
