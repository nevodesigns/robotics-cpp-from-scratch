title: A line test that slips between touching walls
match: a line may not slip between two walls that only touch at their corners
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A shortened path goes diagonally through what looks like a wall, between two obstacles, and the rover stops or scrapes.

## Cause

A diagonal step moves on both axes at once and passes between two other cells. The line's own cells can all be free while those two are walls. With walls at (4,5) and (5,4), the diagonal from (3,3) to (6,6) crosses only (4,4) and (5,5), both free. The test must also require the two cells passed between to be free; with it the line was called blocked.

## Fix

Check both neighbours of a diagonal step.

```cpp
if (move_x && move_y && (!cell_is_free(grid, x - step_x, y) || !cell_is_free(grid, x, y - step_y))) return false;
```
