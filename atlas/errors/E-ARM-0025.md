## Symptom

The arm repeats to a fraction of a millimetre and goes a few millimetres from where it is told.

## Cause

The table is the arm as drawn, and the real one is built to tolerances. A simulated tolerance of 0.3 degrees and 1 mm put the tool 3.39 mm from the table's prediction.

## Fix

Measure the tool's place at many poses with something better than the arm, and fit the table to the measurements. Calibrated, the error was 0.100 mm.

```cpp
const rc::arm::CalibResult c = rc::arm::calibrate(nominal_table, measurements, 1e-9, 12);
```
