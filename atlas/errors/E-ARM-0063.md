## Symptom

A path from a stored roadmap runs into something that was put in the cell after the roadmap was built.

## Cause

The roadmap says what was free when it was built. After a box was added, 34 percent of its edges were no longer free, and queries returned paths with 12 bad edges.

## Fix

Check the edges a query uses against the scene as it is now, or rebuild the roadmap, whenever the scene may have changed.

```cpp
if (rc::arm::edge_free_fraction(problem_now, roadmap) < 1.0) rebuild_or_recheck();
```
