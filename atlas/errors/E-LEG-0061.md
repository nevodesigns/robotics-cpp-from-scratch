## Symptom

Over a hole the leg reaches down and down and the body follows it in.

## Cause

The search had no limit: a hole of 15 cm deeper than the 10 cm the leg may reach was reported only because the search stops.

## Fix

Stop the search at the leg's reach, call it a hole, and bring the foot back and step elsewhere.

```cpp
if (search.hole) lift_and_step_elsewhere();
```
