## Symptom

A robot held low for a while goes limp as its motors cut out.

## Cause

At 0.20 m the knee torque is much higher and the windings settled at 44.3 K in a trot; a heavy robot reached the limit in 25 s.

## Fix

Stand as high as the task allows and work out the time to the limit for any held crouch.

```cpp
time_to_limit(motor, torque);
```
