## Symptom

A controller is learned by thousands of trial runs, and a controls engineer then designs an equally good one from the equations in an afternoon.

## Cause

Learning by trial uses none of what is known about the system; it pays for every piece of knowledge with runs. Random search took 6000 runs to find gains for a pole on a cart whose equations have been published since 1983.

## Fix

Design from the equations where they are known and trusted (phase 14), and use learning to tune what the model gets wrong or where no model exists.

```cpp
// start the search from the designed gains, not from zero
```

Starting a search from a designed controller is often the best of both.
