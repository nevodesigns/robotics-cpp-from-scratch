## Symptom

The vehicle's battery is flat long before the range worked out from the motors.

## Cause

The energy counted only the work at the wheels. The computer and sensors drew their power all the time: 79 percent of a metre of tarmac at 1 m/s (10.1 J).

## Fix

Add the idle power times the time the route takes, and remember that a slower vehicle runs the computer for longer.

```cpp
joules = work / efficiency + idle_w * (length / speed);
```
