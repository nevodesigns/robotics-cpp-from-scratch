## Symptom

After an update the vehicle turns too little, or too much, and every path is followed wide.

## Cause

The bundle carried another vehicle's calibration. A track of 0.40 m taken for 0.25 m makes it turn 1.60 times too little.

## Fix

Bind a calibration to a vehicle id, refuse a bundle with another's, and let a bundle carry none.

```cpp
if (!b.calibration_for.empty() && b.calibration_for != v.id) refuse();
```
