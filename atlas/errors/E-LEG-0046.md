id: E-LEG-0046
title: A knee limit not counted
match: the leg could not fold enough to put the foot on the step
match: the planner said the step was reachable and the knee hit its stop
match: the highest step by the legs' geometry is greater the further ahead the foot is put \(the knee folds less\), and a tighter knee limit lowers it
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 35-08-slopes-and-steps
---

## Symptom

A step height that the geometry allows makes the knee hit its stop as the foot is lifted onto it.

## Cause

The highest step was found from the leg's lengths alone. The knee folds as the foot comes up under the hip, and a real knee has a limit. The geometric answer of 0.229 m, for a foot 0.10 m ahead of the hip with the body 0.27 m up, assumes the knee may fold as far as needed; a tighter knee limit lowers it.

## Fix

Include the knee's limits in the search for the highest step.

```cpp
const double s = rc::legged::max_step_height(leg, 0.10, 0.27, knee_lo, knee_hi);
```

Straight under the hip, where the knee folds most, the same leg reaches only 0.168 m.
