## Symptom

Odometry that was calibrated in the workshop turns wrongly outdoors.

## Cause

The effective track depends on the floor. For an efficiency of 0.78 on a track of 0.16 m it is 0.2051 m, not 0.16.

## Fix

Calibrate on the floor in use, and keep one value for each floor.

```cpp
const double track = calibrate_effective_track(runs);
```
