title: A chart painted once for every sample
match: a repaint is a paint every time, and an update is one paint however many there are
match: expected after_repaints
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 10-11-ten-thousand-updates-one-paint

## Symptom

The interface stutters when the sensor is fast, and profiling shows thousands of paint events a second, most of them drawing the same picture.

## Cause

`repaint()` paints now, every call. A sensor that calls it for each sample paints as fast as the sensor produces. Measured: 100 `repaint()` calls were **100** paints, and 100 `update()` calls were **1**; 10000 `add_sample` calls were **1** paint after the loop ran.

## Fix

Call `update`, and let Qt paint once when the loop is free:

```cpp
update(column_rect(column, kHeight));      // not repaint()
```
