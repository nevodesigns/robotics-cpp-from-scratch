## Symptom

The arm reaches a target at one height and not at another from the same base place.

## Cause

The ring depends on the height of the target in the arm's frame: the tolerance was 36.0 cm at -0.10 m and 31.5 cm at a height 0.20 m lower.

## Fix

Work out the ring for the target's own height (and tool orientation).

```cpp
reach_annulus(m, z_in_arm_frame, 0.01);
```
