## Symptom

At a low speed the joint moves in jerks, and tracks a slow ramp badly.

## Cause

The controller supplied the friction through its error and integral, which wind up and break free: 0.00000 rad mean error at 0.03 rad/s, against 0.00000 with the friction fed forward.

## Fix

Add the friction torque for the reference velocity (not the measured one) as a feedforward.

```cpp
c.friction_ff = true;
```
