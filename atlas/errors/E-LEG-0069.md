## Symptom

A controller that worked on the development robot fails on the second robot built, or on the same robot after a servo was replaced.

## Cause

It was tested on one plant. No two robots are the same: masses, delays and lags differ. The lesson drew 100 joints at random, with the inertia within 30 percent of nominal, a delay from 0 to 30 ms and a lag from 0 to 15 ms. Stiff gains settled on 46 percent of them and gentle gains on 97 percent.

## Fix

Test against a spread of robots, not one, and judge a controller by the share that work.

```cpp
rc::legged::JointSim nominal;
nominal.kp = 10.0;
nominal.kd = 1.5;
const double share = rc::legged::success_rate(nominal, 0.3, 0.030, 0.015, 100, 7);   // 0.97 for these gains
```

The spread to use is the one the real robots have, which means measuring more than one of them.
