## Symptom

After a pose is inverted, the tool has the right orientation and is in the wrong place. It shows the first time a pose has to be turned round: a camera's pose in the base frame, inverted to give the base in the camera frame.

## Cause

The inverse transposed the rotation and left the translation alone. The inverse of a rotation R and a translation p is R transposed and minus R transposed times p: the translation has to be turned back too. A pose 0.5 m from the origin came back 0.35 m out; the correct inverse was right to 0.0000000000000002.

A pose is a rotation and a translation applied in that order. Undoing it means undoing the translation first and then the rotation, so the translation has to be expressed in the rotated frame. Transposing the rotation alone undoes only half.

## Fix

Transpose the rotation, and take the translation as minus the new rotation times the old translation. Test by composing a pose with its inverse and checking for the identity.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Pose inv = rc::arm::inverse(pose);
// compose(pose, inv) is the identity to rounding
```
