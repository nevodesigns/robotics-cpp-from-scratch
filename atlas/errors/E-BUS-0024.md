id: E-BUS-0024
title: A queue that serves old data as if it were new
match: a queue of messages that expire hands over only those still good, and counts the ones it threw away
match: a consumer that is always behind serves older and older data, until a lifespan puts a ceiling on how old
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-08-too-late-to-be-true
---

## Symptom

A controller reacts to something that happened seconds ago, and it is hard to see because every message looks like any other.

## Cause

A queue serves its oldest message and a consumer that is slower than the publisher falls further behind. Published every 10 ms and taken every 20 ms for ten seconds, the data served was 5.00 s old with no lifespan, 0.50 s with a lifespan of 0.5 s and 0.10 s with 0.1 s, at the cost of 450 and 490 expired of 500.

## Fix

Give the queue a lifespan and count what expires.

```cpp
ExpiringQueue poses(0.1);
```
