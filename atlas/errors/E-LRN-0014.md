## Symptom

The cloned policy recovers from small errors and not from large ones: near the edge of its range it acts too gently, or the wrong way, though the expert would have managed.

## Cause

The demonstrations never visited those states, so the network was never taught what to do there, and its output is whatever its form happens to give. From wider starts the expert succeeded on 98 percent and the clone on 87 percent; its failed runs spent 100 percent of their steps more than three spreads from the demonstrated states, its good ones 12 percent.

## Fix

Get demonstrations of the states the learner reaches (38-06), and put a guard round the policy that hands over when the state is unseen (38-07).

```cpp
const rc::learn::Rollout r = rc::learn::rollout(task, &policy, seen, e, h, 300);
if (r.unseen > 0) log_unseen_states(r);
```

Counting unseen steps in every test run says how much of the policy's behaviour was never taught.
