id: E-LEG-0057
title: A rise attempted with no check of the torque
match: the robot tried to stand with a heavy payload and stalled
match: the knees could not lift it from a crouch
match: the knee's torque is greatest at the bottom of the rise and falls as the legs straighten
match: a 12 kg robot can rise, a slow rise uses more of the knee's thermal life than a fast one, and the heaviest bodies are limited by torque and by heat
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 36-03-getting-up
---

## Symptom

The robot with a payload tries to stand and the knees stall at the bottom.

## Cause

The torque at the bottom of the rise (5.9 N m for 12 kg) was never compared with the peak (20 N m); a 65 kg robot cannot rise at all.

## Fix

Work out the rise plan for the mass actually on board and refuse if the torque is over the peak.

```cpp
rise_plan(g, mass, motor, h0, h1, seconds, 40).torque_ok;
```
