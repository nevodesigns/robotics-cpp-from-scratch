match: extrapolating by the velocity at the stamp
match: when the robot stops during the delay, moving the fix on by the velocity puts it a quarter of a metre beyond the robot, and the history does not
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The delay is allowed for, and the error comes back whenever the robot speeds up or turns.

## Cause

The fix was moved on by the present velocity, which holds only if the velocity was constant over the delay. When the robot braked to a stop during it, the fix was put 0.25 m beyond the robot.

## Fix

Compare with the state at the stamp, from a history, instead of extrapolating the measurement. From the history the fix was 0.000 m out.

```cpp
rc::locate::StateHistory history;
history.add(state_now);   // every tick; delayed_correction looks up the stamp
```
