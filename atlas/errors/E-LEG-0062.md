## Symptom

Over rough ground the body's height jumps with every stone.

## Cause

The estimate followed each landing in full; with a gain of 0.2 one landing moved it 6.0 mm and ten landings 26.8 mm.

## Fix

Move the estimate toward each landing by a gain and let the body follow the mean of the feet that are down.

```cpp
est = update_ground(est, touched, 0.2);
```
