## Symptom

The arm goes through the whole pick and place and nothing was moved.

## Cause

Each step ran because the one before had been commanded, not because it had worked. With a check, a miss was retried (1 retry, 48 ticks).

## Fix

After every step that can fail, check what was sensed before going on.

```cpp
if (holding(width, obj, tol)) enter(m, kPickLift);
```
