## Symptom

The vehicle was stuck for a while in the middle of the day and the report says it went well. The report was written from the mission's own summary.

## Cause

Nothing read the day's log. The doctor of 27-05, run over it, found 5 findings, one of them the snag; the stop for the person was correctly not a finding.

The mission knows whether each leg was reached. It does not know that it was stuck for a minute on the way, because it was not watching for that. The log has the rows that say so.

## Fix

Read every day's log for the faults of 27-05 and put the findings in the report.

```cpp
r.findings = rc::drive::analyse_log(rows, rc::drive::DoctorConfig());
```
