## Symptom

The robot carries the arm's rated load and tips over when it has to stop quickly.

## Cause

The arm was rated 5.0 kg and the base with an emergency stop of 0.20 m from 1.5 m/s could carry 4.3 kg: the binding limit was number 1.

## Fix

Take the least of the arm's, the tipping and the traction limits, and test the emergency stop with the load.

```cpp
payload_budget(m, base, q, masses, arm_model, limits, rated, drive, req).best;
```
