title: An event delivered twice, never, or not at time zero
match: stepping through a scenario delivers every event once and none twice, and an event on a step boundary is delivered by the step that ends there
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A push is applied two steps in a row, or the first target is never set and the robot sits at zero, and only when the event falls on a step boundary.

## Cause

The step from t0 to t1 takes the events after t0 and up to and including t1. Stepping 10 ms for 11 s delivered each of 6 events once, at steps 1, 250, 400, 600, 700 and 1000. An interval that starts at zero leaves out an event at time zero: events_between(0, 0.01) gave nothing and events_between(-1, 0.01) gave it.

## Fix

Use a half open interval, starting the first one before zero.

```cpp
double t0 = -1.0;
```
