title: A speed with no field set
match: the vehicle goes faster than any of its field sets was designed for
match: the protective field is too small at high speed
match: a device with a few field sets uses the smallest that was designed for the speed, and refuses a speed it has no set for
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 27-03-a-field-that-moves-with-the-vehicle
---

## Symptom

At a speed above the largest field set the vehicle is protected by a field that is too short.

## Cause

The device has sets for 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 m/s and the vehicle was at 2.1: no set is big enough. (At 1.1 the set for 1.5 is 1.46 times longer than needed, which is the price of the safe choice.)

## Fix

Select the smallest set designed for at least the speed, and slow the vehicle if there is none.

```cpp
if (select_field_set(speed, design) < 0) speed = design.back();
```
