id: E-BUS-0006
title: A queue that keeps the wrong end of the data
match: a full queue that keeps the newest loses the oldest, and counts them
match: a full queue that keeps what it has loses the newest instead, and the choice decides which data survives
match: a depth of zero is a depth of one, and each topic has its own queues
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-02-a-queue-for-each-listener
---

## Symptom

A controller drives on a position that is seconds old while newer ones were thrown away, or a log has a gap at its start where the first readings were dropped.

## Cause

When a queue is full one message goes, and which one is a choice. Depth 3 and five messages: dropping the oldest took 3 4 5, and dropping the newest took 1 2 3, with 2 lost either way.

## Fix

Choose the rule from what the data is for.

```cpp
bus.subscribe("pose", 1, Overflow::drop_oldest);      // control
bus.subscribe("log", 10000, Overflow::drop_newest);   // recording
```
