## Symptom

A car-like vehicle drives round and round a waypoint without ever coming within its tolerance. It is a car-like vehicle and a waypoint just to one side of its path.

## Cause

The tolerance was finer than the car can hit. A car cannot turn on the spot: with a smallest radius of 0.742 m and a tolerance of 0.10 m, it was still circling after 40 s.

A car cannot turn tighter than its smallest radius. A target close beside it, inside that circle, can only be reached by going round, and if each pass misses by more than the tolerance it goes round for ever.

## Fix

Set each waypoint's tolerance to at least what the vehicle can do, for a car about its smallest radius, or give the waypoint a heading to arrive with. With 0.70 m it was there in 1.8 s.

```cpp
rc::drive::Waypoint w;
w.tolerance = 0.7;   // about the smallest turning radius
```
