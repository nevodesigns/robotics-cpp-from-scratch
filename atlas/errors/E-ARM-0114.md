## Symptom

Positions are out by an amount that grows with the distance from the beam's centre, and the beam turns slightly.

## Cause

A wide beam driven from both ends had its two ends out of step, so it racked. Ends 0.2 mm apart on a span of 1.0 m put 60 micrometres of error at a lever of 0.3 m: the angle times the lever.

## Fix

Measure both ends and correct the difference, or drive one end as master and the other as follower, and check the ends after homing and after any stall.

```cpp
const double e = rc::arm::rack_error(0.0002, 1.0, 0.3);   // 60 um
```
