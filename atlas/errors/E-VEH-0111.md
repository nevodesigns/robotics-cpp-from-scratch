## Symptom

When a new plan arrives its start is behind the vehicle, which turns back to join it, is then off the path, and plans again, over and over.

## Cause

The plan was begun from the pose at the time of the request, and the vehicle moved on while it was made: the new path started 1.16 m behind it, past the off-path threshold, so it planned again (83 plans in 90 s, never arrived); from the predicted pose it started 0.06 m away and arrived in 20.1 s.

## Fix

Begin the plan from where the vehicle will be when it arrives (the path, advanced by speed times the planning time).

```cpp
const CarPose from = predicted_pose(pose, path, idx, speed, planning_time);
```
