## Symptom

The robot walks well with a payload and cannot get up if it falls.

## Cause

Rising needs more than walking: the heaviest body that can rise was 40.6 kg by torque and 24.8 kg by heat in 6 s.

## Fix

Choose the payload so that the robot with it can still rise from a crouch.

```cpp
max_rising_mass(g, motor, h0, h1, seconds, true);
```
