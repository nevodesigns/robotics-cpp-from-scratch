## Symptom

A simulated motor responds exactly as fast as the real one in one test and visibly differently in another, and nobody knows which number is right.

## Cause

A first order motor reaches 63 percent of its final speed after one time constant: gain 2 and time constant 0.1 s with a command of 3 gave 3.804 of 6.0 (63.4 percent) after 0.1 s and 5.961 after 0.5 s. The time constant is a number that can be measured on the real motor, and a model that keeps the contract with the wrong one is still wrong.

## Fix

Measure the time constant on the real motor and put it in the model.

```cpp
SimMotor motor(gain, tau, limit);
```
