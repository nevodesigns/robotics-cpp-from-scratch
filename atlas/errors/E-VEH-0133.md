## Symptom

A vehicle that is out on a job reboots for an update, and the job is lost, or the vehicle is left with half a release and a flat battery.

## Cause

Nothing checked the state. A vehicle that was moving, in a mission and at 20 percent charge was refused for 3 reasons here.

## Fix

Refuse an update unless the vehicle is parked, with no mission and enough charge, and list every reason.

```cpp
const auto why = check_update(on_board, bundle, policy);
```
