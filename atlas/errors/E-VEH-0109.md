## Symptom

The vehicle stands in front of an obstacle that was not on the map when it planned, or the path keeps changing and the vehicle makes little progress.

## Cause

No replanning gives the first (it never arrived). Planning every 0.2 s gave 86 plans against 5 for the reasons, and arrived in 21.4 s against 20.9 s.

## Fix

Replan for reasons (goal, blocked, off the path, old), with a cooldown.

```cpp
should_replan(cfg, now, last_plan, pose, path, idx, grid, goal_changed);
```
