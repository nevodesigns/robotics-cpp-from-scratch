## Symptom

The arm is reported as colliding with itself in every pose, even at home. It is reported at every pose, including home, the moment self collision is turned on.

## Cause

Every pair of capsules was asked, including links that share a joint. Those always touch at the joint, so the check is always negative.

Two links joined at a joint overlap there by construction: the capsules of neighbours meet at their shared end. Asking that pair always finds contact. The arm folding back on itself is a real collision, but between links further apart.

## Fix

Leave out the pairs that share a joint (an allowed collision matrix): with four capsules, only 3 of the 6 pairs are asked. Rely on the joint limits for the arm folded on itself. With the flange brought down beside the base the self distance was then -0.120 m, a real collision.

```cpp
for (std::size_t i = 0; i < caps.size(); ++i)
  for (std::size_t j = i + 2; j < caps.size(); ++j)   // skip the neighbour
    d = std::fmin(d, rc::arm::capsule_capsule(caps[i], caps[j]));
```
