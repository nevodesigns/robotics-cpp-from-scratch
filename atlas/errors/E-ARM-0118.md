## Symptom

The robot is installed and part of the work is out of its reach.

## Cause

The work's area was never put to the robot's own reach test from where it would be mounted. A SCARA over the middle of the work reached 0.48 of it (it cannot reach under itself) and 1.00 mounted beside it; on a wide table the delta reached 0.33, the Stewart platform 0.05 and the arm 0.89.

## Fix

Write the work's area down and check every point of a grid over it with the family's reach test, from the mounting place planned.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Verdict v = rc::arm::check_family(robots, family, task);
if (!v.area_ok) report(v.coverage);   // the share reached
```
