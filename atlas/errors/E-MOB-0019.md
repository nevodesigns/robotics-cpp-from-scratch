## Symptom

Grasps succeed for objects near the base and fail for objects at full reach.

## Cause

The heading's error was added as if it were a position error; a degree at 0.9 m is 16 mm and the budget came to 25.6 mm.

## Fix

Multiply the angle errors by the distance from the base's centre to the hand before combining.

```cpp
hand_sigma(sigmas, lever);
```
