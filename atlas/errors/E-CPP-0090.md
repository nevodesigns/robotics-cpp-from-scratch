## Symptom

An operator lowers the speed limit while the robot is moving fast. Nothing happens until the next drive command, and the robot carries on at the old speed.

## Cause

The limit was stored and used only when a drive command arrived. The speeds the wheels already had were never looked at again.

Measured: driving at 0.8 and -0.4, then a limit of 0.5, left the wheels at **0.5 and -0.4**: the one above the limit was brought down and the one below it was left alone. A drive of 3 and -3 afterwards gave 0.5 and -0.5, and a stop left the limit at 0.5.

## Fix

Setting a limit clamps what is happening, as well as what comes next:

```cpp
void operator()(const SetLimit& s) const {
  state.limit = s.max_speed;
  state.left = clamp_speed(state.left, state.limit);
  state.right = clamp_speed(state.right, state.limit);
}
```
