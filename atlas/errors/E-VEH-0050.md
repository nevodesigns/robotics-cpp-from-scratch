platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Objects on the ground are placed too near or too far, increasingly so with range, and the error changes when the vehicle brakes.

## Cause

The pitch was assumed. A pitch 1 degree wrong put row 140 37 percent out in range, and a height 10 percent wrong 10 percent.

## Fix

Calibrate the height and the pitch with the vehicle at rest on flat ground, and correct the pitch for the vehicle's own tilt.

```cpp
pixel_to_ground(cam, measured_height, calibrated_pitch + vehicle_pitch, u, v, x, y);
```
