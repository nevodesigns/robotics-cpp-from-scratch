## Symptom

The robot keeps announcing that the room has changed when it has not, or a person walking by sends it replanning every time, or it never sees a door close.

## Cause

Noise puts a few beams a cell away from the map. Share of 250 unchanged scans called different: 0.800, 0.484, 0.072, 0.004 and 0.000 at thresholds of 0.01, 0.02, 0.04, 0.08 and 0.15, and of closed-door scans 1.000, 1.000, 1.000, 1.000 and 0.012. A single odd scan is one person walking past.

## Fix

Pick the threshold between the noise and the change, and want it for several scans.

```cpp
ChangeDetector detector(0.05, 3);
```
