## Symptom

A predictive controller that works in simulation is jittery or unstable on the robot.

## Cause

The model was exact (the same as the simulation's); on a real body the legs' springs, the delay and the estimate of the push make it wrong, and the prediction is only as good as the model.

## Fix

Test the controller against a model that is wrong (a different height, a delay) and keep the horizon short.

```cpp
mpc_zmp(x, v, h_assumed, dt, n, bound, qx, qv, r, iters, nullptr);
```
