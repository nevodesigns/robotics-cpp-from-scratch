## Symptom

The robot runs out of battery on a route it was rated for. The shortfall grows with the load and with routes that stop and start.

## Cause

The range was quoted for the empty base. Rolling resistance grows with mass: with 10 kg aboard the range fell from 15509 m to 13838 m. The loss is modest here because most of the energy goes to 15 W of idle electronics; a base that moves more loses more.

Range is energy over energy per metre. A heavier robot costs more per metre to roll and more to accelerate, and the electronics' idle power is paid per second however slowly the robot goes.

## Fix

Quote the range for the loaded mass and the speed of the route.

```cpp
const double r = rc::mobile::range_m(drive, base_mass + payload, speed);
```

A range quoted for the job's own payload and speed is the only one that answers the question.
