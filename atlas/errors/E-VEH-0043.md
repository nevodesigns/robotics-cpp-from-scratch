## Symptom

The vehicle falls into a ditch or off an edge, and the log shows nothing in its way.

## Cause

A hole returns no points, so a robot that avoids only what it sees cannot see it. With the sensor 0.25 m up and tipped 5 degrees, the ground was expected 2.86 m ahead.

## Fix

Expect the ground, and treat missing ground as an obstacle: a beam longer than expected, or one that returned nothing, is a hole.

```cpp
const double expected = rc::perceive::expected_ground_range(0.25, pitch, azimuth);
if (rc::perceive::classify_beam(measured, expected, margin) == rc::perceive::Beam::hole) mark_lethal();
```
