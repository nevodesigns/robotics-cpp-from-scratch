## Symptom

At the moment of contact the force spikes far above what it settles to. The steady force is fine; the first moment is not.

## Cause

A stiff controller meets the surface with its momentum and its stiffness at once: a peak of 68.6 N at 2000 N/m against 27.6 N at 200.

At contact the tool's momentum is stopped by the surface, and a stiff controller adds its own force at once. Both together make a spike far above the force the spring settles to.

## Fix

Approach slowly, soften the impedance for the approach, and stiffen after contact if the task needs it.

```cpp
const rc::arm::PressResult r = rc::arm::press(model, q0, target, tool, 200.0, dz, surface, seconds, dt);
```
