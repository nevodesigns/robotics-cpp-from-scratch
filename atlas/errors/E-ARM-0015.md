id: E-ARM-0015
title: A solver that does not know it failed
match: the arm moves toward an unreachable target and stops without a message
match: the iteration returns its last guess as an answer
match: a seven joint arm reaches the pose and does not need to be at the joint angles that made it
match: a pose out of reach is stopped by the limit of steps, with the tool as near as it got
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 28-05-iterating-to-a-pose
---

## Symptom

For a target that cannot be reached the arm goes to the last guess and nothing says it is not there.

## Cause

The result was the last joint angles, with no flag. A pose out of reach ended 0.64 m away, with ok false and the limit of steps used.

## Fix

Return a result with ok, the iterations, and the error left, and let the caller refuse.

```cpp
if (!r.ok) refuse(r.pos_err, r.rot_err);
```
