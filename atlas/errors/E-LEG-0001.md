## Symptom

The foot lands a few centimetres to the side of where it was sent, the same amount every time, and more on one side of the robot than the other.

## Cause

The sideways offset of the hip's pitch axis from its lean axis was left out of the kinematics. On the typical leg it is 0.08 m, and a left leg has it to the left and a right leg to the right. Without it every foot is placed as if the thigh hung straight from the lean axis, which it does not.

## Fix

Keep the offset in the leg's geometry, with the side's sign, and use the same geometry in both directions.

```cpp
rc::legged::LegGeom left;
left.l0 = 0.08;
left.side = 1;                     // -1 for a right leg
const rc::legged::FootPos p = rc::legged::leg_fk(left, q0, q1, q2);
```

A point nearer the lean axis than the offset cannot be reached at all, and the inverse kinematics refuses it.
