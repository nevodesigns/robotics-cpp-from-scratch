## Symptom

After a wheel spins on loose ground, the heading is wrong for the rest of the run. It follows a patch of gravel, a wet floor, or a wheel lifted over a bump.

## Cause

The wheels' turn rate was used to correct the gyro without a check. A wheel that slips reports a turn the body did not make; a slip that halved the turn for 2 s left the wheels alone 3.1 degrees out.

A fused heading trusts the wheels to correct the gyro's bias. A slipping wheel reports a turn that did not happen, and a filter that takes every measurement turns that false turn into a bias, which then corrupts every heading after.

## Fix

Gate the difference between wheels and gyro against what the estimate expects, and refuse what is too far from it. With the gate the slip was refused and the heading kept.

```cpp
if (innovation * innovation <= gate * gate * s) update();
else ++refused;
```
