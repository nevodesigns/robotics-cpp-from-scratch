## Symptom

The cell is laid out to the reach on the data sheet, and in places the arm can get to, it cannot do the job.

## Cause

Reach is the outside of the shape. At table height the farthest the tool could reach in any orientation was 0.86 m and with the tool down 0.74 m, and the dexterous poses (smallest singular value of 0.1 or more) were 62 percent of the reachable volume.

## Fix

Design round the dexterous region and the tasks (tool down, a given orientation), not the reach.

```cpp
sample_reach(table, base, tool, n, seed, 0.1, grid);
```
