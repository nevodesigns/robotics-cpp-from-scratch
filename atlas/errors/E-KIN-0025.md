## Symptom

An arm is mounted on a wall, or its elbow is given a stop, and the cell is laid out assuming it lost half the floor. Either the layout wastes space the arm
could use, or it puts a task where the arm turns out not to reach.

## Cause

Neither limit costs what it appears to. With the shoulder confined to the top half, the second link can still swing back over the shoulder's own side
and the arm reaches points below it. Measured: **1.7591 of 2.5129 m^2, 70.0 percent**, and not 50.

An elbow limited to bend one way loses nothing, since the two poses for a point are mirror images of each other and only one is needed: reachable **2.5129 of
2.5129**, usable 2.1763 of 2.1763. It does lose the choice between them, which matters to a path (lesson 13-09) and not to a map.

## Fix

Measure the area under the real limits and do not estimate it.

```cpp
const WorkspaceMap wall = map_workspace(l1, l2, wall_mounted_limits, 0.0, 0.005);
```
