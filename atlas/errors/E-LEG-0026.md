match: the body shifted the wrong way before the lift
match: the robot leaned toward the leg it was lifting
match: a body shifted the wrong way is outside the triangle, and a shift toward the middle is inside

## Symptom

The crawl is fine for the hind legs and the robot tips forward every time a front leg lifts, or the other way round.

## Cause

The body was shifted the same way for every leg. Which way to shift depends on which leg is going up: toward the side that stays down. Shifted 5 cm forward with a hind foot lifted, the margin was 0.031 m; the same forward shift with a front foot lifted moves the centre toward the corner that is leaving, out of the triangle.

## Fix

Shift toward the centroid of the triangle that remains for that particular leg.

```cpp
double tri[3][2], c[2];
rc::legged::support_triangle(feet, lifted, tri);
rc::legged::centroid(tri, 3, c);       // a different point for each lifted leg
body.move_to(c);
```
