## Symptom

A recorded motion jumps across a place where frames were lost, and what is learned from it jumps too.

## Cause

20 frames in a row were lost, making a longest gap of 0.70 s, and interpolation drew a straight line across it: the motion in the gap is a guess.

## Fix

Check every episode's longest gap, duration and idle fraction at the time of recording, and refuse the ones over the limits. The episode with the hole was refused (0); the good one was kept (1).

```cpp
if (!rc::arm::check_episode(e, 0.02, 0.2, 2.0, 0.3).ok) discard();
```

A bad demonstration is cheaper to find when it is recorded than after a model has learned from it.
