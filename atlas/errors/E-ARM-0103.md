## Symptom

A point that is within reach is refused because the solution found sat outside a limit.

## Cause

Each point has two elbows, and only one may be inside the limits (159 of 272 point and elbow pairs were reachable).

## Fix

Try both elbows and keep the ones inside the limits; choose by what is next.

```cpp
scara_ik(arm, x, y, +1, q1, q2) || scara_ik(arm, x, y, -1, q1, q2);
```
