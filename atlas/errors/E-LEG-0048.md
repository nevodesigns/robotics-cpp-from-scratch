## Symptom

The robot starts a recovery that cannot finish, or reacts after it has hit the ground.

## Cause

Nothing worked out how long the fall would take: 0.22 s for a tilt of 23 degrees at 3.0 rad/s, against 0.30 s for a step.

## Fix

Step the pendulum from the tilt and the rate and compare the time with what the recovery needs.

```cpp
time_to_ground(theta, omega, h, 5.0);
```
