## Symptom

A controller works in simulation and misbehaves on the machine, and the cause is a difference in the simulated machine that nobody wrote down.

## Cause

A fake keeps promises or breaks them, and a program can say which. A SimMotor broke none of six rules (reset, rest, saturation, symmetry, determinism, consistency). Five careless plants each broke the one that fitted: no clamp broke saturation, reporting the previous position broke consistency, an unrepeatable one broke determinism, one that forgot its speed broke reset, and one that favoured a direction broke symmetry.

## Fix

Run the contract against every plant, simulated or real.

```cpp
const auto broken = check_contract(make_plant);
```
