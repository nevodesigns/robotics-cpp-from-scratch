## Symptom

The scenario runs, nothing fails, and the control gets slowly worse over a month because the only check is a person looking at a plot.

## Cause

A scenario is a test when it ends in pass or fail and a failure says what failed. Against criteria of 0.3, 1.0 s and 0.02 the good run had no failures, a sensor 60 ms late failed overshoot, settling and final error on every step, and a stuck sensor failed settling and final error on all steps and overshoot on the second. The failures name the step and the criterion in step order.

## Fix

Run the scenario in a test and fail on a non empty verdict.

```cpp
RC_CHECK(verdict(analyse_steps(trace, changes, 0.02), criteria).empty());
```
