title: A phase that does not wrap

## Symptom

After the first cycle a leg stays in one state or the gait stops.

## Cause

The phase was not reduced to the range 0 to 1 once it passed 1: with the offsets added, the phase of a leg is the fraction of the period, wrapped.

## Fix

Subtract the floor of the phase after adding the offset.

```cpp
p -= std::floor(p);
```
