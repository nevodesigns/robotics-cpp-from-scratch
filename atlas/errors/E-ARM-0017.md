## Symptom

Following a straight line that passes near the base, joint 1 is commanded to spin faster than it can.

## Cause

An undamped step: 26.1 rad/s for a line 0.01 m from the axis at 0.25 m/s; with damping that rises as the singular value falls, 2.0 rad/s.

## Fix

Damp by the smallest singular value (and say what the damped pass costs: 0.0448 m off the line).

```cpp
adaptive_lambda(smallest_singular(j), eps, lambda_max);
```
