## Symptom

The robot is stable with the arm stowed and tips when it brakes or turns with the arm out.

## Cause

The limits were worked out for the stowed arm: braking 11.01 m/s2; with the arm reaching it is 8.88 m/s2.

## Fix

Work out the centre of mass for the pose, every cycle, and set the limits from it.

```cpp
com_world(m, base, q, masses, com); tip_limits(base, com, hw, ht);
```
