## Symptom

The arm stops, or reports contact, in free space at some poses and speeds. It is worst in poses where gravity loads the joints most, or at the start of fast moves.

## Cause

The contact threshold was below what the model gets wrong. The residual is what the model does not explain, and in free motion that is the model's own error: with gravity 5 percent out, a threshold of 0.05 N m gave 1 false alarm (declared before the wall was reached) and one of 0.8 N m gave 0.

The residual is the measured torque less the model's. In free motion it should be zero, and anything left is the model's error: a mass a few percent out, friction left out, an acceleration term missing. A threshold below that error fires in free space.

## Fix

Find the largest residual in free motion over the whole workspace and set the threshold above it with a margin.

```cpp
rc::arm::ContactDetector det;
det.thr = 1.5 * worst_free_residual;
```

A threshold too high feels only hard contact; a better model lets it come down.
