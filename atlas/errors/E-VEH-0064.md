## Symptom

Scan matching works at low speed and gives nonsense after a quick turn.

## Cause

The method finds the nearest fit, which is right only from near. It converged up to 0.5 rad of rotation error and not from 0.8.

## Fix

Start from the odometry, or from a prediction, and check the result against it.

```cpp
const IcpResult r = icp(scan, previous, odometry_guess, rounds, max_pair, tol);
```
