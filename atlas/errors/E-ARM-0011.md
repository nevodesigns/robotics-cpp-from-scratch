title: A solver that returns something for an unreachable pose
match: the arm moves to somewhere near when the target is out of reach
match: the inverse kinematics does not say the pose is unreachable
match: a pose out of reach has no solution, at the very edge the elbow up and down are one, and a straight wrist has fewer
match: the closed form is for arms whose last three axes meet, and the UR5 is not one of them
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 28-04-eight-ways-to-reach-a-pose

## Symptom

Given a target out of reach the arm stretches towards it and stops, with no complaint.

## Cause

The solver clamped what had no solution (the cosine of the elbow past 1) instead of saying so. A pose 1.5 m away gave 0 solutions here.

## Fix

Return an empty list and let the caller decide; handle the edge (the elbow straight) and the singular wrist as the special cases they are.

```cpp
if (S > 1.0 + 1e-12) continue;
```
