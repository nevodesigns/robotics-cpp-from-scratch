## Symptom

A fast robot's position is always a little behind where it is, by its speed times a delay. It is worst at speed and does not show at rest.

## Cause

The fix was stamped with the time it arrived and used as the present. A delay of 0.25 s at 2 m/s left a steady bias of 0.49 m.

A fix is the position when the receiver measured it, which is some fraction of a second before it arrives. Used as the present position, it is behind by the speed times that delay.

## Fix

Stamp measurements with the time they were taken, and compare each with the state at that time.

```cpp
const rc::locate::Correction c = rc::locate::delayed_correction(history, fix.stamp, fix.x, fix.y, gain);
```
