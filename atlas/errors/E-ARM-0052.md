## Symptom

The planner says a path is clear and the arm touches something.

## Cause

The capsules are smaller than the links (a motor, a cable, a wide gripper): the radii here are 4 to 8 cm and have to be measured.

## Fix

Take the radii from the arm's model or a measurement and add a margin; a model a little too big costs a little reach, one too small costs a collision.

```cpp
arm_capsules(table, q, radii, tool_length);
```
