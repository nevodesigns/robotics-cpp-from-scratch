## Symptom

After a sideways push the robot takes a step that lands under its body, and it carries on falling over the new foot.

## Cause

The foot was put down under the body. A body moving away from its support comes to rest only over the capture point, `x + v * T` with T the square root of h over g. A push of 0.30 m/s on a body 0.27 m high (T = 0.166 s) put the capture point 5.0 cm out. A foot nearer than that is passed over.

## Fix

Put the foot at or beyond the capture point.

```cpp
const double xi = rc::legged::capture_point(x, v, 0.27);   // 0.050 m for a push of 0.30 m/s
```

And the capture point moves while the foot is in the air, which is the next entry.
