## Symptom

The simulated arm accelerates the wrong way for a torque, or blows up after a few steps.

## Cause

The mass matrix is not a mass matrix. It must be symmetric (here to 0.000000000000) and positive in every direction (it was for 60 of 60 random vectors): a link given an impossible inertia, or a frame left out, makes it neither, and solving with it gives nonsense.

## Fix

Check symmetry and positivity for a few poses whenever the link parameters change.

```cpp
const auto M = rc::arm::mass_matrix(model, q);
// for random x: x^T M x > 0, and M[i][j] == M[j][i]
```
