## Symptom

A foot stepping toward a hole reaches down, keeps reaching, and the leg stretches until the body tips into the hole after it.

## Cause

The search for the ground had no limit. A foot that lands where the ground should be and finds nothing must reach down, but only so far: here 10 cm below the planned place. A step down of 5 cm was found with an extension of 0.060 m in 0.24 s; a drop of 15 cm, deeper than the limit, was reported as a hole after 0.40 s, when the foot had gone as far as it may.

## Fix

Bound the reach, and treat reaching the bound with no contact as a hole: do not load that leg, bring it back, and step elsewhere.

```cpp
rc::legged::ReachState s;
while (!s.done && !s.hole) rc::legged::reach_down_step(s, contact.on, dt, 0.25, 0.10);   // 0.25 m/s, at most 0.10 m
if (s.hole) { unload(i); step_elsewhere(i); }
```

The other three feet carry the body while one leg is withdrawn, which is 35-01's support polygon at work.
