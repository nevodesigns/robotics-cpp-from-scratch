match: a release that passes is confirmed at its first boot, and one that fails is rolled back to the old slot

## Symptom

A release passes its own health check, and turns out to drive the wrong way or to have lost its calibration.

## Cause

Nothing measured what it does on the vehicle. A health check that asks the software whether it is running cannot see a wheel wired backwards.

## Fix

Run a short drive on the new software and confirm the release only if the measured numbers pass; a number that is not a number fails. A wheel wired backwards failed 1 check of the self test, and a lost calibration failed 1.

```cpp
if (rc::drive::post_update_selftest(measured, limits).empty()) confirm(boot);
else roll_back(boot);
```
