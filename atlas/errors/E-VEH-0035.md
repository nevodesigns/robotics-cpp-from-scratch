## Symptom

A wall in the scan is not among the objects, and appears only when the vehicle gets close. The wall was far from the vehicle.

## Cause

At 11 m the points are 0.19 m apart, and a fixed radius of 0.15 m joined none of them: 0 objects in the wall.

Points on a far wall are further apart than the clustering radius, so no two are joined and each is too small to count as an object.

## Fix

Let the radius grow with the beam spacing at the range of the points. With a grown radius the wall was 1 object.

```cpp
const double limit = base + gain * std::max(ri, rj);   // inside cluster_points
```
