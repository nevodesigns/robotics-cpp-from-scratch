## Symptom

The arm is commanded to a pose and ends at the right point with the wrong heading, or the right heading at the wrong point.

## Cause

The third angle must make the sum of the three equal the heading, with the first two taken from the solution of the wrist. A solution that sets the third angle to the heading itself is right only when the first two sum to zero. Measured: **2498** solutions of reachable poses were all within 1e-9, in position and in heading; **209** of 360 headings at (1.5, 0).

## Fix

Set the third angle from the other two:

```cpp
q3 = rc::math::wrap_angle(target.phi - q1 - q2);
```
