## Symptom

The body bobs up and down over a gravel path, rising for every stone a foot happens to land on, or it lags far behind a real change such as a ramp.

## Cause

Either every landing was taken as the new ground height (it follows every stone), or none was (it never follows a ramp). The estimate of the ground under each foot should move toward where the foot touched by a gain. With a gain of 0.2, one landing on a 3 cm bump moved it 6.0 mm and ten landings 26.8 mm.

## Fix

Move each foot's ground estimate by a gain, and set the body's height from the mean of the feet that are down. With two feet on a 3 cm rise and two on the level, the body followed the mean by 15 mm.

```cpp
est[i] = rc::legged::update_ground(est[i], touched_z, 0.2);
const double h = rc::legged::body_height_from(nominal, est, down);
```

The gain is a trade between steadiness and keeping up. A slope steeper than the gain can follow shows up as the body sinking toward the uphill feet.
