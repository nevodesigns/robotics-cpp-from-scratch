## Symptom

The marks are found on one side of the frame and the other half of the frame is marked as well.

## Cause

One number cannot be a statement about lighting that differs from place to place. Measured on a scene lit from the left: one threshold of 121 found 0.582 of the marks and marked 0.446 of everything else, and a local threshold of radius 12 found 1.000 and marked 0.000. The window matters: radius 1 found 0.317, radius 16 found 1.000 and radius 100 found 0.577 and marked 0.405.

## Fix

Compare each pixel with its neighbourhood, with a window bigger than the thing and smaller than the change in the light.

```cpp
const auto marks = adaptive_threshold(frame, 16, 20);
```
