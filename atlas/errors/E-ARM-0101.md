## Symptom

What is learned from the demonstrations waits before it starts and after it ends.

## Cause

Each episode had 1 s of standing still at each end, and the task was 2.8 s.

## Fix

Trim the stretches in which the leader was not moving, and keep a little padding.

```cpp
trim_idle(episode, 0.02, 3);
```
