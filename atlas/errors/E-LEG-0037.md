## Symptom

After a hard push the controller asks a foot to pull on the ground; the forces it sends add to more than the weight, and the body is pushed up as well as rolled.

## Cause

The balance law asked for a moment the feet cannot give. A foot can only push, so the largest roll moment is the weight times the half width: 17.7 N m. Beyond it the least norm solution has a negative force. Cutting negative forces to zero, the obvious repair, leaves forces that add to more than the weight.

## Fix

Scale the moment down, keeping its direction, to the largest the feet can supply, and report that it was limited.

```cpp
double f[4];
const bool met = rc::legged::foot_forces(feet, on_ground, W, mx, my, f);   // false when it had to scale
if (!met) consider_a_step();
```

When the feet's moment is spent, the answer is a step (35-07).
