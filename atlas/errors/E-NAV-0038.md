title: A belief that stopped adding up to one
match: a belief always sums to one, and one with nothing left in it goes back to knowing nothing
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 16-14-which-door-am-i-at

## Symptom

The shares at each place are all too small, or an empty belief produces NaN everywhere and the most likely place is always zero.

## Cause

A belief is a distribution and must sum to 1 after every step. When every place has been ruled out the sum is zero, and dividing by it fills the belief with NaN. The belief goes back to 1/24 each, an entropy of log2 24 = 4.58 bits.

## Fix

Normalise after every update and treat a zero or non finite sum as knowing nothing.

```cpp
if (!(sum > 0.0) || !std::isfinite(sum)) { for (double& v : p_) v = 1.0 / p_.size(); return; }
```
