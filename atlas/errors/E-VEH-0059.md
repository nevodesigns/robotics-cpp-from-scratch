## Symptom

A robot that turns on the spot has a track that circles, and a speed that is not zero.

## Cause

The antenna is a lever arm from the reference point. A lever of 0.5 m made the antenna move 0.50 m/s for a turn of 1 rad/s and wander 1.00 m.

## Fix

Subtract the lever turned by the heading before using the position.

```cpp
antenna_to_reference(ax, ay, heading, lever, rx, ry);
```
