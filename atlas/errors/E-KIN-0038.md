## Symptom

An arm that is nearly straight jerks to a wild configuration for a small move of the tool, or the angles become infinite or not a number.

## Cause

The plain step divides by the determinant of the Jacobian, which is nearly zero near a singularity. A step of 24 thousand radians for a tenth of a metre is the result. Measured: the plain step was **24496.8** rad and the damped step **0.000163301** rad.

## Fix

Damp the solve, and limit the step:

```cpp
a[r][c] = sum + (r == c ? lambda * lambda : 0.0);   // J J^T + lambda^2 I
if (length > max_step) scale = max_step / length;
```
