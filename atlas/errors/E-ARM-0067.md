title: A path with no clock
match: the planner returns poses and the arm has no idea how fast to go between them
match: the path was sent to the joints at a constant speed
match: over a path no joint's speed, acceleration or jerk passes its limit, and the limiting joint is close to it
match: halving the speed limits nearly doubles the time of a long move and changes a short one much less
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 30-06-putting-the-path-in-time
---

## Symptom

A planned path is sent to the arm without timing, or at one speed, and some joints are asked for more than they can give.

## Cause

Joint limits become limits along each segment through the direction: the smallest of each joint's limit over its share. Over the path every joint stayed within its limits, and the limiting one reached 0.727, 1.000 and 1.000 of them.

## Fix

Time every segment from the limits of the joints it moves.

```cpp
plan_trajectory(path, limits);
```
