## Symptom

A walk is released because the robot's top speed is enough, and it fails on the site.

## Cause

The speed is one of six requirements: a walk of 0.6 m/s with 1 kg passed all six, and 30 kg failed at 4 while the speed was fine.

## Fix

Run every requirement against the walk and release only if all pass.

```cpp
check_job(robot, walk).released;
```
