## Symptom

The car clips the outside wall at a turn in a corridor wider than the car.

## Cause

The outer front wheel sweeps a circle of 0.904 m at the smallest radius: the lane is 0.062 m wider than the car.

## Fix

Size a turn by the swept radius of the outer front wheel, not by the width of the body.

```cpp
const double outer_edge = swept_radius_outer(car, radius);
```
