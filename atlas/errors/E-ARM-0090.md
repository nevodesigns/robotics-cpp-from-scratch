## Symptom

The peg stuck on the chamfer is pushed harder and does not move.

## Cause

With a friction of 1.2 on a 45 degree chamfer the slope is inside the friction cone (0): no push has a sideways part left over.

## Fix

Lubricate, or make the chamfer steeper, and back off before trying again.

```cpp
if (!can_slide(hole)) change_the_part_or_the_surface();
```
