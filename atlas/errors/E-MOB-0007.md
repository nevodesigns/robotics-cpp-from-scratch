## Symptom

The arm reaches the target from the planned place and fails if the base stops a centimetre short.

## Cause

The place was chosen only for being reachable; with the margin as the weight, the base was put in the middle of the ring (margin 1.00).

## Fix

Keep a minimum margin inside both edges of the ring and score the margin.

```cpp
p.min_margin = 0.05; p.w_margin = 1.0;
```
