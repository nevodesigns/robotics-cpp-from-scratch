## Symptom

The tracked robot turns on the spot on the test floor and digs in on soft ground. The test floor was concrete.

## Cause

The pivot was planned without its thrust. A pivot needs mu m g L / (4 B) from each track, 32.7 N here, and on soft ground the slip to give it (6.8 percent in the sand) is enough to dig the track in.

A tracked pivot drags both tracks sideways, needing thrust that grows with friction and track length. On soft ground, getting that thrust needs a large slip, and a slipping track digs.

## Fix

Work out the thrust and the slip a pivot needs before planning one, and prefer an arc on soft ground.

```cpp
const double need = rc::vehicle::pivot_thrust_tracked(mass, mu, length, gauge);
```
