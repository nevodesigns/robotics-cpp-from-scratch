title: Gains tuned in a simulator with no delay

match: the controller rang as soon as it ran on the hardware
match: a delay and a lag in the actuator make a joint that settled in the simulator ring on the real one, and a joint with too much delay does not settle at all
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 36-07-what-changes-on-the-real-robot

## Symptom

A leg that was smooth and quick in simulation rings or shakes on the hardware.

## Cause

The simulation had no delay or lag: gains of 40 and 3.0 settled in 0.24 s there and overshot 23.5 percent and never settled with 20 ms of delay and 10 ms of lag.

## Fix

Put the measured delay and lag into the simulation before tuning, and tune for a margin, not for the quickest.

```cpp
j.delay = measured_delay; j.lag = measured_lag;
```
