## Symptom

The window freezes for as long as the robot takes to plan, the stop button does not respond, and the station reports the link lost, though the link is fine.

## Cause

A slot runs in the thread that runs the event loop, and nothing else runs in that thread until it returns: no repaints, no clicks, no timers, no reads. Measured: a plan of 200 ms in a handler left a gap of **210** ms between ticks of a 10 ms timer; the same plan in a runner left **11** ms.

## Fix

Hand the work to another thread and take the result by signal:

```cpp
watcher_.setFuture(QtConcurrent::run(plan_work, steps, step_ms));
```
