title: Treating the joints as independent when they are not
match: the arm follows a fast move badly though each joint is tuned
match: the tracking error grows with the speed of the move
match: with an exact model computed torque follows a fast move far more closely than a PD controller with gravity
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 29-05-using-the-model-to-move
---

## Symptom

Each joint is tuned well alone, and the arm lags fast moves, more the faster they are. The lag grows with speed and with how much the other joints move.

## Cause

The joints were treated as independent. The full mass matrix and the velocity products couple them, and to a single joint loop the coupling is a disturbance that grows with speed: the joint PD with gravity had an rms error of 0.04293 rad on a fast move.

In a fast move, the other joints' accelerations and velocities put torques on each joint through the mass matrix and the velocity products. A loop that knows only its own joint sees them as disturbances, corrected only after they cause an error.

## Fix

Compute the torque from the model for the wanted acceleration (computed torque) and add feedback on the errors. Its rms error was 0.000056948 rad.

```cpp
const auto tau = rc::arm::computed_torque(model, q, qd, q_ref, qd_ref, qdd_ref, kp, kv);
```
