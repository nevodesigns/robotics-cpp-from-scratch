## Symptom

The operator holds full speed at a wall that the vehicle's own sensor sees, and the vehicle drives into it. The operator was looking at the wrong screen.

## Cause

Nothing stood between the request and the wheels. The vehicle did as it was told, and the governed one stopped 0.50 m short of the obstacle where the ungoverned one went 58.7 m past where it had been.

A teleoperated vehicle that passes the operator's request straight to the wheels has no safety of its own. Its sensor sees the obstacle and nothing uses what it sees.

## Fix

Cap the speed at what stops within the sensed distance less a margin, whatever is asked.

```cpp
target = std::min(target, rc::drive::stop_speed(obstacle - margin, latency, decel));
```

The governor caps the speed only near obstacles; elsewhere the operator has full control.
