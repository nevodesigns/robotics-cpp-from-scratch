id: E-ROS-0002
title: A node that leaves its callbacks behind
match: a node that is destroyed takes its subscriptions and timers with it, and nothing is called into it afterwards
match: a node is created and destroyed again and again with the world still working, and an invalid name does nothing instead of throwing
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-17-the-seam-where-ros-plugs-in
---

## Symptom

The robot crashes a few messages after a node is reconfigured or restarted, in a callback that belongs to something that is gone.

## Cause

A subscription is a function the bus will call, and it captures the component that made it. A node destroyed with its subscription still on the bus is called into on the next message. An in-process node unsubscribes its tokens and leaves the world: after destruction, nodes alive 1, subscribers 0 and 0 calls into it in 5 s and 10 messages. 200 nodes created and destroyed left 1 node and 1 subscriber.

## Fix

Undo every registration in the destructor.

```cpp
for (TopicBus::Token t : tokens_) world_.bus.unsubscribe(t);
```
