## Symptom

Told to sit while walking, the robot lowers itself and falls on its face. At a slow walk it sometimes gets away with it, which is why it is found late.

## Cause

The sit was acted on at once. Lowering takes the legs out of the gait, and a body moving at 0.6 m/s with no gait under it keeps going while its support folds. The machine has to stay in walk until the walk reports that it has stopped, then go to stand, and only then lower.

## Fix

Let the walk finish before the lowering begins. In the lesson, a sit asked at 0.6 m/s kept the machine in walk until the robot had slowed to a stop.

```cpp
case rc::legged::kDWalk:
  if ((in.want != rc::legged::kWantWalk || !in.battery_ok) && in.stopped)
    rc::legged::dog_enter(d, rc::legged::kDStand);   // and stand -> lowering on the next tick
  break;
```

The same rule applies to a battery that runs out while walking: slow down and stop, then lower, rather than lower where it stands.
