## Symptom

On a route with fixes every 100 m, the robot is several metres out before each one. The spacing of the fixes was set at a round number of metres.

## Cause

The spacing was chosen by feel, as if dead reckoning's error grew in proportion to the distance. It grows faster, because a heading error turns into a growing sideways error: 0.22 m after 10 m and 7.07 m after 100, 31.6 times for ten times the distance.

A heading error makes a sideways error that grows with distance, and the heading error itself grows. Together the position error grows faster than in proportion to the distance, so a spacing chosen for short runs is too long for long ones.

## Fix

Work out the bound from the odometry's noise and choose the spacing of the fixes from it.

```cpp
rc::locate::DeadReckoning dr(noise);
// drive the route in simulation; the distance at which dr.radius95() reaches the task's limit is the spacing
```
