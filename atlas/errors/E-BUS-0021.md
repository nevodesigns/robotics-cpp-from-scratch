## Symptom

The two nodes do not connect and nobody can tell which of the several settings is wrong.

## Cause

A deadline and a lease need the offer to be no longer than the request, and an offer of none cannot meet a request for one. A yes or no hides which of five properties differed. A camera offering best effort at 1 s, against a request for reliable, transient local, 0.2 s, gave three named reasons, one for each of reliability, durability and deadline.

## Fix

Return every reason, not just the first.

```cpp
c.reasons.push_back("deadline: offered period is longer than requested, or none offered");
```
