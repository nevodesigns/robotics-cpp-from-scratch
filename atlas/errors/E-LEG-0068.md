## Symptom

The emergency stop cuts the power and the robot falls and breaks something.

## Cause

Off was applied while standing: the layer must give soft first (2 standing) and off only when down (3); a silent link for 2.0 s asked for off as well.

## Fix

Turn off while standing into soft, lower the robot, and then cut the power; tell the operator the robot will go down.

```cpp
if (mode == kSafeOff && in.standing) mode = kSafeSoft;
```
