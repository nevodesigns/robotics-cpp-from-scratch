## Symptom

At a speed near the switch point the robot changes gait again and again.

## Cause

One speed was used to change up and down: a speed hovering at 0.5 m/s changed gait 175 times in 400 readings, and 0 with a gap between 0.3 and 0.7.

## Fix

Change up above one speed and down only below a lower one.

```cpp
gait_for_speed(v, current, 0.6, 0.4);
```
