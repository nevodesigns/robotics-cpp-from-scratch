## Symptom

The vehicle arrives close to the goal but not lined up, and a dock or a gate is missed by a few centimetres or degrees.

## Cause

The search keeps one pose to a coarse cell and stops within a tolerance (0.3 m, 20 degrees in 26-01), so it ends 0.073 m from the goal pose.

## Fix

Finish with a Dubins path from the last pose to the goal pose; it ends exactly (within 0.000000000 m over 200 pairs).

```cpp
const Dubins tail = dubins_shortest(last, goal, r);
```
