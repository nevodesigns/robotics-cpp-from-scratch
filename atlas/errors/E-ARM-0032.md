## Symptom

The arm is accurate empty and sags when it holds something.

## Cause

The model has the arm and not the payload. The compensation supplies the arm's own weight, and the controller's error supplies the payload's: 2 kg left an error of 0.0646 rad.

## Fix

Put the payload in the model (its mass and where it is) when it is picked up, from a weighing or the part's data.

```cpp
const rc::arm::ArmModel loaded = rc::arm::with_payload(model, 2.0, where);
```
