title: A phase that does not wrap
match: one leg stuck in swing after the first cycle
match: the gait stopped after one period
match: a leg is in stance for the duty and in swing for the rest, and phases wrap round
match: a leg's offset is added to its phase, which only shows when the offset is not a half
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 34-04-down-and-up
---

## Symptom

After a while of walking the gait goes wrong: a leg stays in swing too long, or the phases drift apart.

## Cause

A leg's phase was allowed to grow without wrapping. The phase is the fraction of the cycle a leg has done, from 0 up to but not including 1; stance is the phase below the duty. A phase of 1.25 compared with a duty of 0.5 says swing when the leg should be a quarter of the way into stance.

## Fix

Wrap the phase every time it is computed.

```cpp
double p = t / g.period + g.offset[leg];
p -= std::floor(p);   // 0 to 1
```
