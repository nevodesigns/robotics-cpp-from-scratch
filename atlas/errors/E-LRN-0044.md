## Symptom

A policy gradient learns, but slowly and erratically, and needs far more runs than expected.

## Cause

Each run's score was weighted by its whole return. When every return is large and of one sign, most of that weight is common to all runs and cancels only on average, leaving noise. Over the first 50 steps the estimates' spread was 29.2 without a baseline and 13.7 with the batch's mean return subtracted.

## Fix

Subtract a baseline from the return: the batch's mean at least, a learned value of the state better.

```cpp
const double g = rc::learn::pg_estimate(task, k, sigma, batch, true, r);   // baseline on
```

The baseline does not change what the estimate tends to, because the score's mean is zero; it only removes noise.
