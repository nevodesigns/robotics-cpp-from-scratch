## Symptom

The peg presses on the lip and the force rises until the cell faults, though the part is within a millimetre or two.

## Cause

A stiff hand cannot give sideways: at 2 mm off it needed 279 N against 50 N; a soft one needed 0.8 N.

## Fix

Make the hand soft sideways and stiff along the hole, with a chamfer on the part.

```cpp
push_to_centre(hole, offset, k_side);
```
