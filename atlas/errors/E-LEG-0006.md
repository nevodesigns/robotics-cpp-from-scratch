title: A foot sent along a straight leg
match: the knee spun when the foot was told to go straight down
match: joint speeds exploded near full leg extension
match: a foot velocity needs joint rates that grow as the leg straightens, and a straight leg has none
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A foot asked to move along the line of a nearly straight leg goes nowhere, or the joint rates it asks for are huge.

## Cause

Solving the Jacobian for joint rates near a straight leg divides by a determinant close to zero. At full stretch there are no joint rates that move the foot along the leg at all.

## Fix

Check the determinant before solving and refuse near the singularity.

```cpp
double rates[3];
if (!rc::legged::joint_rates(j, foot_vel, rates)) stay_bent();   // false near a straight leg
```
