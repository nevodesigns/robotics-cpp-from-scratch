## Symptom

Collecting data on the learner's path damages the robot or its surroundings, or the collection is stopped because it is too risky.

## Cause

DAgger needs the learner to drive and make its mistakes, since those are the states to label. On a real robot those mistakes are real, and the expert, often a person labelling afterwards, cannot prevent them.

## Fix

Collect in a simulator where one exists, or with a safety driver and a guard (38-07) that takes over before a mistake becomes damage, and label from the recording.

```cpp
const double turn = guard.active ? fallback_turn : rc::learn::learner_act(policy, e, h);
```

A person labelling a state they did not drive into labels worse than they drive; expect noisier labels in exactly the hard states.
