## Symptom

The robot takes a step in response to a push and falls over it anyway.

## Cause

The foot was put at the body's place and not at the capture point: the capture point was 5.0 cm out for a push of 0.30 m/s.

## Fix

Put the foot at or beyond the capture point, x plus v times the time constant.

```cpp
capture_point(x, v, h);
```
