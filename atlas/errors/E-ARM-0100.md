## Symptom

A replayed or learned motion speeds up and slows down in places the person did not. Training on the episodes gives a policy that moves unevenly.

## Cause

The samples were taken to be one step apart. Messages never arrive at a steady rate: an episode recorded at a nominal 30 Hz for 5.0 s had 151 samples and a longest gap of 0.039 s, from jitter in the arrival times.

The messages that make up an episode arrive with jitter, so the time between samples varies. Code that uses the sample index as time stretches the motion where samples are close and squeezes it where they are far apart.

## Fix

Store the time of every sample and resample onto a steady grid before the episode is used.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Episode steady = rc::arm::resample(recorded, 30.0);
```
