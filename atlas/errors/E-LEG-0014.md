## Symptom

At speed the feet drag along the ground in the swing, or the knee sags.

## Cause

The joint rate needed grows as the swing time falls: 4.7 rad/s over 0.25 s and 9.4 rad/s over 0.125 s, against 8 rad/s the servos give.

## Fix

Work out the joint rates of the path before using it and lengthen the swing until they fit.

```cpp
swing_peak_joint_rate(g, p0, p1, h, t_sw, -1, &peak);
```
