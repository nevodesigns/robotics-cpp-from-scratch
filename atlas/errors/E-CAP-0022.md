title: A report that says only how many tests passed
match: a hazard is covered when a scenario names it and all of them pass, failing when one of them fails, and has no test when none names it, and low ones are left out
match: the rover with its safety layer on passes all five scenarios, and the report says that four of the eight hazards have no test at all
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-08-a-test-report-you-can-read
---

## Symptom

The project has a hundred passing tests and nobody can say which of the hazards of the log has ever been tried.

## Cause

Five scenarios all passed and covered G1 and G4 (by S2), G2 (by S4) and G8 (by S5); G3, G5, G6 and G7 had no scenario. The summary read: 5 of 5 scenarios passed; 4 of 8 hazards covered by passing scenarios; not covered: G3, G5, G6, G7.

## Fix

Tie each scenario to the hazards it is evidence for.

```cpp
scenario.hazards = {G1, G4};
```
