## Symptom

The hand closes fully and the machine goes on to carry nothing.

## Cause

Closed was taken as holding; a hand closed on nothing has a gap near zero and one closed on the part has its width.

## Fix

Compare the gap with the part's width within a tolerance.

```cpp
holding(in.width, m.obj_width, m.tol);
```
