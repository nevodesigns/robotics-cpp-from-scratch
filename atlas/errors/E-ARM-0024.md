## Symptom

The tool cannot be put pointing down at the edges of the table, though they are within the reach.

## Cause

The orientation and the joint limits were left out: with the tool down and no limits the arm reached 96 percent of the table; with limits of plus and minus 120 degrees 96 percent, and with plus and minus 60 degrees 78 percent.

## Fix

Ask the task (the pose at each point, the limits, the tool offset) for the cell before it is built.

```cpp
table_coverage(table, tool, x0, x1, y0, y1, z, 0.05);
```
