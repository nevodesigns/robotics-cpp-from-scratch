id: E-SIM-0009
title: A fit of a trace that cannot give one
match: a trace that cannot be fitted is refused and not turned into numbers
match: from a clean step the fit finds the gain and the time constant the motor was built with
match: the model's step response is zero at the start, 63 percent after one time constant and the final speed in the end
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-03-measuring-the-motor
---

## Symptom

A motor that never moved, or a step recorded after it had already reached speed, gives a gain and a time constant like any other, and nobody notices.

## Cause

A fit needs the rise. It was refused for fewer than 10 readings, traces of different lengths, a zero command, a motor that never moved, and a speed already at its final value from the first reading. A clean step of 3 gave gain 1.9991 and time constant 0.0989 s for a motor built with 0.1: the simulated motor steps by Euler at 2 ms, and its effective time constant is about half a step shorter.

## Fix

Refuse what cannot be fitted.

```cpp
if (!f.ok) report_bad_trace();
```
