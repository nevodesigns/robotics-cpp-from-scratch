## Symptom

The camera on the hand was meant to make grasps certain, and they still miss now and then. The misses come on the far parts of the work, where the camera is furthest away.

## Cause

The camera's error was taken from its data sheet at a range other than the one it works at. A depth camera's error grows with range; the number to use is its error at the working range. A camera good to only 12 mm there made the job check fail at requirement 4.

A depth camera's error grows with range, often with its square. A data sheet usually quotes the best range; the grasp budget needs the error at the range the camera actually looks from.

## Fix

Measure the camera's error at the range it will work at, and use that in the grasp budget.

```cpp
const double sigma = rc::mobile::sigma_after_servo(camera_error_at_working_range, 0.0001);
if (rc::mobile::grasp_probability(sigma, tol) < 0.99) refuse();
```

Measuring it is a matter of putting a known part at the working range and reading it many times.
