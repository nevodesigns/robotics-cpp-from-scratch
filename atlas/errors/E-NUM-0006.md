## Symptom

Asked to turn from 179 degrees to minus 179 degrees, the robot rotates almost a
full circle instead of two degrees.

## Cause

Subtracting two headings gives the arithmetic difference, not the shortest
rotation. Angles wrap, so a difference of 358 degrees and one of minus 2 degrees
describe the same physical turn, and the raw subtraction reports the useless one.

## Fix

Subtract, then wrap the result into the range minus pi to pi. That is exactly
what shortest_turn does. Any code that compares, averages or interpolates
headings needs the same treatment.
