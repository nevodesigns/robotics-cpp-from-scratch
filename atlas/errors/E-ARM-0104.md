## Symptom

The arm is slow and shaky at the far side of the work area, or straight under the base.

## Cause

The planar Jacobian's determinant l1 l2 sin q2 is 0.0000 when the arm is straight and 0.0506 at a right angle.

## Fix

Lay the task out so that q2 stays well away from 0 and pi.

```cpp
scara_det(arm, q2);
```
