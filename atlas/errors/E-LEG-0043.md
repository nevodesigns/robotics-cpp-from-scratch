title: A step aimed at the capture point of the push
match: the recovery step landed short because the body kept falling during the swing
match: the step was aimed where the body was going at the start
match: the capture point is where the body would come to rest, and grows exponentially if nothing is done
match: the foot must land beyond the capture point of the landing time, and the push that can be answered falls with the swing time
match: a foot landed at the target leaves the capture point inside it, and one landed short leaves it outside
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 35-07-taking-a-step-when-pushed

## Symptom

A step that was aimed well at the moment of the push lands short.

## Cause

The capture point moves away as e to the t over T during the swing: the foot had to land 24.5 cm out and not 5.0 cm.

## Fix

Aim at the capture point of the landing time, with a margin.

```cpp
step_target(xi0, t_land, h, reach, margin);
```
