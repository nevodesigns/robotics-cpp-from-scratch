## Symptom

Smooth goals give a joint that moves in tiny jumps, and the tool in jumps of a millimetre.

## Cause

The servo has 4096 steps to a turn: 0.00153 rad, 1.07 mm at 0.7 m. A finer goal is rounded to the nearest step.

## Fix

Plan with the step in mind (a tolerance of at least a step on each joint) and do not expect smoother motion than the encoder allows.

```cpp
quantise(angle, 4096);
```
