## Symptom

A trotting robot falls when its timing slips by a fraction of a second, though a calculation of how long it had seemed to leave plenty of room.

## Cause

The time to fall was worked out as if the body moved away from the line of its feet at a constant speed. It does not: it is an inverted pendulum, `x'' = (g / h) x`, so the further out it is the faster it goes. With a time constant of 0.166 s for a body 0.27 m high, a fall starts slowly and then takes off. From 10 mm off the line it took 0.41 s to move 6 cm, and from 30 mm only 0.22 s.

## Fix

Use the pendulum's own solution, which has a closed form for the time to reach a distance.

```cpp
const double T = rc::legged::pendulum_time(0.27);                   // 0.166 s
const double t = rc::legged::time_to_move(0.010, 0.0, 0.06, 0.27);   // 0.41 s; 0.22 s from 0.030 m
```

A negative answer means the body is moving back toward the line fast enough to stop before it gets there.
