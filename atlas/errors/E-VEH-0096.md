## Symptom

The controller's tracking is rough and varies from run to run.

## Cause

Too few rollouts to find a good one: a mean error of 0.152 m at 8 samples against 0.077 m at 100.

## Fix

Raise the samples as far as the processor allows at the control rate, and test with several seeds.

```cpp
cfg.samples = 300;
```
