title: A Jacobian for the flange used for the tip
match: the tip drifts when I command a velocity of the gripper
match: the Jacobian is for the flange but I control the fingertips
match: a tool offset changes the linear part of the Jacobian and not the angular part
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 28-03-how-the-tool-moves-when-the-joints-do
---

## Symptom

Commanded in terms of the fingertip, the tool moves as if the point being controlled were the flange.

## Cause

The Jacobian was worked out at the flange. Moving the point out along the tool changes the linear part of the Jacobian (a turn of the wrist now sweeps the tip) and leaves the angular part alone; with a tool of 0.15 m that difference is large.

## Fix

Work the Jacobian out at the point being controlled, with the tool offset.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Jacobian j = rc::arm::jacobian(table, q, base, rc::arm::trans(0.0, 0.0, tip_length), table.convention);
```
