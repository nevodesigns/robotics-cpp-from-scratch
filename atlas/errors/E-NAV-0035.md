title: A scan scored with every beam counted and no tolerance
match: a perfect scan fits the map exactly from where it was taken, and fits worse the further the pose is from there
match: beams that found nothing are left out, not counted as misses
match: with noise on the ranges a tolerance of zero cells loses half the beams, and one or two cells loses almost none
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 16-13-am-i-where-the-map-says
---

## Symptom

The true pose scores low, the search chooses a pose that is clearly wrong, or a scan with a lot of open space scores worse than one in a closet.

## Cause

A beam with no return says nothing about walls and must not be counted as a miss. A noisy range will not land on exactly an occupied cell, and with 3 cm of noise a tolerance of zero cells scored 0.492 at the true pose against 0.951 at one cell and 0.984 at two. A perfect scan scored 1.000 at its pose, and 0.787, 0.656, 0.639 and 0.607 at 5, 10, 20 and 40 cm off.

## Fix

Leave out beams with no return and allow a cell or two.

```cpp
if (b.range <= 0.0 || b.range >= no_return) continue;
```
