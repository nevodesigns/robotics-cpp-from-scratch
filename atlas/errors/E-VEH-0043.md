## Symptom

The vehicle falls into a ditch or off an edge and the log shows no obstacle in its way.

## Cause

A hole returns no points. With the sensor 0.25 m up and tipped 5 degrees the ground was expected 2.86 m ahead, and a beam that returned nothing there was a hole.

## Fix

Expect the ground, and treat ground that is missing as an obstacle.

```cpp
if (classify_beam(measured, expected, margin) == Beam::hole) mark_lethal();
```
