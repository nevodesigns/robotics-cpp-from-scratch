## Symptom

The simulation passes and the bench does not, and nobody can say whether the simulation is nearly right or far off.

## Cause

Against a bench with a link delay of 2 ticks each way, 5 percent loss and noise 0.003 (two runs of it 0.0053 apart), a simulation with no delay was 0.1901 rms away. With a sensor delay equal to the link's round trip, 4 ticks, it was 0.0772 away; at 8 ticks 1.2860. The rest is the loss. The same configuration gave identical runs and another seed a different one.

## Fix

Measure the gap against a recorded bench run and put the known delay in the simulation.

```cpp
const auto m = compare(sim.position, bench.position, 0);
```
