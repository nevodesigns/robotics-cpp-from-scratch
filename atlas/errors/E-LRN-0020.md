## Symptom

A learned policy that works in testing fails in use the first time the robot is somewhere unusual, and nothing takes over.

## Cause

The policy was trusted everywhere, though it was only taught some states. The clone of 38-05 succeeded from the wide starts on 87 percent; behind a guard that handed over to a plain controller in unseen states it succeeded on 98 percent, handing over on 12 percent of the steps.

## Fix

Keep the scale of the training data with the policy, call states beyond a threshold unseen, and hand over to a hand-written controller there.

```cpp
rc::learn::Guard guard;
guard.seen = policy.scale;
guard.z_max = 3.0;
```

Log every handover: the share of steps the network did not drive is a measure of how much of the job it was not taught.
