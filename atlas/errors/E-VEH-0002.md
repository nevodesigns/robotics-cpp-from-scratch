## Symptom

After a loop the robot believes it is home and is metres away.

## Cause

Losses the model did not have. With a slip of 2 percent and an efficiency of 0.9 a square of 10 m sides ended 4.64 m from the start.

## Fix

Put the losses in the model, calibrate them, and do not trust wheel odometry over long distances without another source.

```cpp
DiffSlip model(r, track, slip, efficiency);
```
