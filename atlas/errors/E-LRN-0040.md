## Symptom

Gains found by a search score well in its own reports and less well in a separate test or on the hardware.

## Cause

Every try was scored on the same few starts, which makes tries comparable, and a search that keeps the better score can fit those starts. The found gains balanced for 500.0 steps on their own starts; the check that matters is on starts never used, where they managed 497.9.

## Fix

Score tries on fixed starts, then test the result on many starts from another seed, and report that number.

```cpp
const double held_out = rc::learn::balance_score(model, gains, 100, other_seed, 500);
```

It is the split by episode of 38-01, for a learner that has no dataset.
