title: A circle of infinite radius
match: going straight is not a circle of infinite radius
match: expected this to be true: std::isfinite(end.x)
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A robot told to drive in a straight line, with both wheels at the same speed, jumps to a position that is not a number, and every position after that is not a number either.

## Cause

The exact step follows a circle of radius forward / turn. For a straight line the turn is zero, the radius is infinite and the calculation divides by zero. Even a turn that is only very small gives a huge radius
and an answer that is the difference of two nearly equal numbers, which is rounding noise.

## Fix

When the turn is too small for a radius to mean anything, go straight:

```cpp
if (std::fabs(turn) < 1e-9) {
  next.x = start.x + forward * dt * std::cos(start.theta);
  next.y = start.y + forward * dt * std::sin(start.theta);
  next.theta = start.theta;
  return next;
}
```
