## Symptom

A friction compensation tuned at a working speed is wrong at low speed and at rest.

## Cause

Friction has Coulomb, Stribeck and viscous parts: a peak of 0.0559 N m at 0.017 rad/s, a dip to 0.0503 at 0.05 rad/s, and 0.0550 at 1 rad/s.

## Fix

Model all three, smooth the sign over a small speed, and identify them from a run at several speeds.

```cpp
friction_torque(model, v);
```
