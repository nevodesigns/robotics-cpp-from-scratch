title: A point that was never checked against the joint limits
match: the tip is where the angles say, a point gets the elbow branch that fits the limits, and a point out of reach or out of the limits gets none
match: a loop through the points has a pose for each and a timed move for each leg, and the first point that cannot be reached is named
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-03-an-arm-that-picks-and-places
---

## Symptom

The arm drives into a joint limit in the middle of the cycle, or the solver picks the elbow that cannot be reached.

## Cause

With the elbow held to [-2.4, 0.5] the point (0.5, 0.15) took the elbow up branch (q2 -1.48); with it held to [-0.5, 0.5] there was none, and (1.0, 0.5) was out of reach. A loop with the point 3 out of reach was not ok, with bad point 3, found before anything moved.

## Fix

Check each point against the limits before the cycle starts.

```cpp
const ArmPlan plan = plan_cycle(cell, points);
```
