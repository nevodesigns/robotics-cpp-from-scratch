title: A controller that holds the arm up with its error
match: the arm sags at its target pose
match: there is a steady error at the shoulder under gravity
match: a PD controller that has to hold the arm up with its error sags by the torque over the gain, and adding gravity takes it away
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 29-02-holding-the-arm-up
---

## Symptom

The arm stops short of its target, further in poses where it is stretched out, and more at the shoulder than at the wrist.

## Cause

The controller supplies the weight through its error: the droop was 0.1113 rad, the weight over the gain 0.1093.

## Fix

Add the torque of gravity of the model as a feedforward (error 0.000000000 rad with it).

```cpp
pd_torque(model, q, qd, target, gains, true);
```
