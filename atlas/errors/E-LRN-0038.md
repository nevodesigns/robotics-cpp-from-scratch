## Symptom

Training curves show the reward climbing steadily, and the robot's actual performance on the job is poor or getting worse.

## Cause

The behaviour was judged by the reward it earned, and the reward is only a stand-in for what was wanted. A gamed reward is earned in full: the bonus learner's reward grew with every step while it never arrived.

## Fix

Measure the job itself (here, steps to the goal) separately from the reward, and look at the behaviour, not only at the numbers.

```cpp
const int steps = rc::learn::steps_to_goal(task, Q, 200);   // the job, not the reward
```

When the reward rises and the job measure does not, read the reward function before anything else.
