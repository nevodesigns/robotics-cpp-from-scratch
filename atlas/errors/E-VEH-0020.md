## Symptom

Wheel odometry closes every loop and the robot is nowhere near where it believes. The odometry was judged by whether it closed its own loop.

## Cause

Odometry was judged by its own closure. The ideal parameters have no slip and no loss of turn, and the robot has both; consistent is not correct.

Odometry that uses the ideal parameters is consistent: drive a loop and it says you came back. That says nothing about the real robot, which slipped and turned short; only a measurement of where it really ended does.

## Fix

Close a loop on the real floor and measure how far off it ends. The calibrated parameters put the robot within 5 cm of the truth.

```cpp
rc::vehicle::WheelOdometry odo(track / efficiency, 1.0 - slip);
```
