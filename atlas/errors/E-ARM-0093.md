## Symptom

A cell that was checked on paper lets the arm get closer to a person than it should. The paper check used the sensor's quoted response time.

## Cause

The reaction time was left out or taken too small. In a simulation of a person walking at the robot, the robot that braked at the full distance came to rest 0.17 m from them; one that left out the reaction time came to rest at -0.09 m (they met), and one that left out the person's motion at -0.31 m.

The reaction time is the whole chain, from the person being in the zone to the brakes acting: the sensor's cycle, the safety controller's, the drive's. Each term in the distance is multiplied by the person's speed, so a short time on paper is a short distance in the real cell.

## Fix

Measure the whole chain on the real robot, sensor to processing to brake, and keep every uncertainty term.

```cpp
rc::arm::SsmParams ssm;
ssm.t_r = measured_reaction_time;
```
