## Symptom

The estimated reach is smaller than the arm can do, and bigger each time the sample is made bigger. The volume grows every time the sample is made larger.

## Cause

A point near the edge of the reach is reached by few combinations of angles, and the sample was not many to a cell. In cells of 0.10 m, 2 thousand poses gave 1.081 m3 and 200 thousand 2.958 m3; in cells of 0.05 m, 200 thousand was still rising (2.033 to 2.554).

Points near the edge of the reach are reached only by narrow ranges of joint angles. A random sample of joints lands there rarely, so the edge cells are found last, and a small sample misses many of them.

## Fix

Raise the sample until the volume stops growing, keep many samples to a cell, and say how many were used.

```cpp
rc::arm::sample_reach(table, base, tool, 200000, seed, 0.0, grid);
```
