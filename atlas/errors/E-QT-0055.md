## Symptom

The window can be dragged until the chart is a thin line, or until it is gone and only the toolbar is left.

## Cause

A widget with no minimum size has none, and a layout takes its own minimum from the minimums of what is in it. Measured: with a plot of at least 120 by 80 the panel asked for **120 by 130**, and asked to be 60 by 40 it was **120 by 130**.

## Fix

Give the plot a minimum, and let the layout carry it up to the window:

```cpp
plot_->setMinimumSize(120, 80);
```
