## Symptom

Near full stretch the leg starts to jerk, or a small command to the foot makes the joints move violently.

## Cause

A straight leg is a singularity. At full stretch (0.42 m for two links of 0.21 m) the foot cannot move further along the leg, and close to it the joint rates for a small foot motion grow without bound: a foot speed of 0.5 m/s straight down needed 3.4 rad/s at the knee at 0.30 m and 15.5 rad/s at 0.40 m.

## Fix

Keep the knee bent in normal use, and treat the straightness (the sine of the knee angle) as a limit to stay above.

```cpp
if (rc::legged::straightness(q2) < 0.2) limit_reach();
```

The same straight leg is the strongest for standing, which is the trade: strong where it cannot be controlled, controllable where it costs torque.
