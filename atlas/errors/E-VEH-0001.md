## Symptom

Every turn is short by the same fraction on a particular floor. Carpet and grass make it worse than a hard floor.

## Cause

The turn was asked of the arithmetic and not of the floor. The ideal model turns the body by r (wr - wl) / b, but tyres scrub and give, so the body turns less than the wheel difference says. At a turn efficiency of 0.8, a commanded quarter turn was 72.0 degrees.

The ideal model assumes each wheel rolls without slipping sideways. Real tyres deform and scrub in a turn, especially wide ones on a grippy floor, and the body turns less than the difference of the wheel speeds says.

## Fix

Put a turn efficiency in the model, measure it on the floor in use, and ask for the turn the floor will give.

```cpp
const double w = eff * r * (wr - wl) / b;
```
