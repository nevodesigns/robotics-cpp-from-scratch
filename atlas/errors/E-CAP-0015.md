title: A beacon that is trusted after it has died

## Symptom

The robot's position quietly goes bad after the beacon fails, and nothing says that it has.

## Cause

Fixes every 5 s that died at 20 s left 3 fixes and an error of 13.75 m at 30 m against 2.86 m for a beacon that lived. With fixes every 5 s and an assumed drift of 0.20 the robot went 8.7 m before it stopped, against 5.1 m without them.

## Fix

Count the metres since the last fix, and stop when the buffer is used up.

```cpp
since_fix += distance;
```
