## Symptom

After the window is dragged or a breakpoint is hit the program freezes for a long time, then does it again, and never gets back to real time.

## Cause

A long frame owes many steps, and running them takes longer than the time they stand for. A frame of 5 s with at most 10 steps allowed ran 10 steps, gave up 4.90 s and the next frame owed 1. A smaller step is also a more accurate simulation, at more work: the energy error of a spring was 200.000, 50.000, 22.222, 10.526 and 2.020 at 0.05, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005 and 0.001 s.

## Fix

Limit the steps per frame, and count what is dropped.

```cpp
FixedStepper stepper(0.001, 50);
```
