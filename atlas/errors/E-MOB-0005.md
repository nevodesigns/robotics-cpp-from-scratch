## Symptom

The base parks for a low shelf using the reach it has for a table, and the arm comes up short. It shows when the robot goes from a table to a floor or a low shelf in the same mission.

## Cause

The ring was worked out at one height and used at another. The arm must bend down to reach a target far below its mount, which shortens its reach: the outer radius was 0.72 m at a target 0.10 m below the mount and 0.63 m at one 0.20 m lower.

The ring is a section of the arm's reach at one height. Going down, the arm spends some of its length on the drop, and less is left for going out, so the ring shrinks. A planner that keeps one ring for every target parks too far away for the low ones.

## Fix

Work out the ring for the height of each target, relative to the mount.

```cpp
const double dz = target[2] - m.mount.p[2];
const rc::mobile::Annulus ring = rc::mobile::reach_annulus(m, dz, 0.01);
```

Rings can be worked out once for a few heights and kept in a table; a target between two heights takes the smaller.
