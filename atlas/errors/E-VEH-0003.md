## Symptom

The model is tuned until a run looks right, and the numbers inside it mean nothing.

## Cause

The loss was assumed, not measured. The turn efficiency is a ratio that can be measured directly: from a model built with 0.8, a turn read back 0.800.

## Fix

Measure it from a turn: the body's measured turn over the turn the wheels asked for.

```cpp
const double eff = rc::vehicle::turn_efficiency_from(left_m, right_m, track, dtheta);
```
