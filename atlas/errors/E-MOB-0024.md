## Symptom

The base drives with the arm out and tips over, or the arm reaches while the base moves and misses.

## Cause

The plan, which was wrong, was trusted; the interlock held a faulty plan at 0 unsafe ticks where 15 happened without it.

## Fix

Apply a simple interlock to every command, separate from the plan, and make it stop the robot when the plan is wrong.

```cpp
c = guard(c, in, &blocked);
```
