title: A chain of frames composed the wrong way
match: a point seen by the laser is placed in the map by composing the links on the way up the tree and down
match: between two reports a frame is where it would have been, so a robot driving at a steady speed is found at the midpoint
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Every obstacle is in the wrong place, by an amount that depends on where the robot is and which way it faces, and it is right at the origin.

## Cause

Transforms do not commute: first b then a is not first a then b. The lookup finds the nearest common frame, works out each side from there and combines them with an inverse. An obstacle 1 m ahead of the laser came out at (12.00, 6.30) in the map, the arm in the laser's frame at (-0.4, 0.2), and a base at 0 and 0.6 m was found at 0.3 at the midpoint.

## Fix

Compose in the order of the tree, and test with a pose that is not the origin.

```cpp
out = compose(inverse(common_from_target), common_from_source);
```
