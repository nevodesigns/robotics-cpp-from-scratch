match: the arm cannot follow a straight line near its shoulder
match: the smallest singular value falls as the wrist straightens
match: the damping is none well away, and rises to its maximum at the singularity
match: drawing a line past the shoulder axis, an undamped step turns joint 1 hard and a damped one does not, at a price in accuracy
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Following a straight line that passes near the base, joint 1 is told to spin faster than it can.

## Cause

The resolved rate step had no damping. A line 0.01 m from the axis at 0.25 m/s asked 26.1 rad/s of joint 1.

## Fix

Damp by an amount that rises as the smallest singular value falls. Joint 1 then asked 2.0 rad/s, at the cost of leaving the line by 0.0448 m near the axis; say so.

```cpp
const double lambda = rc::arm::adaptive_lambda(rc::arm::smallest_singular(j), eps, lambda_max);
```
