title: A step time built up by adding
match: events at the same time arrive together in the order they were written, and a time that is built up by adding is one step late where one that is multiplied is not
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-05-a-script-for-the-test-day

## Symptom

Every scenario event on a round number happens one step late, and the effect looks like a mysterious delay in the plant.

## Cause

Adding 0.1 ten times gives 0.9999999999999999, short of 1.0, so the step that should end at 1.0 does not take an event there. The event at 1.0 s with steps of 0.1 s arrived at step 11 when the time was built up by adding, and at step 10 as the step number times 0.1. Events at one time arrived together in the order they were written.

## Fix

Work each step's time out from the step number.

```cpp
const double t1 = steps * dt;
```
