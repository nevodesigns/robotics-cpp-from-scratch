## Symptom

The calibration repeats well and is consistently off by about a percent. All the calibration spins went the same way.

## Cause

It used spins in one direction. The gyro's bias adds to the heading of every spin the same way and is taken into the track: 0.31 percent.

The gyro used to measure the turn has a bias. A spin one way adds the bias times the time to the measured angle; a spin the other way subtracts it. Using both directions cancels it.

## Fix

Calibrate with spins in both directions and use their sizes, which cancels the bias: 0.000 percent.

```cpp
runs.push_back(spin_left);
runs.push_back(spin_right);
```
