## Symptom

Every grasp is off by a steady amount that grows with how far the arm reaches.

## Cause

The arm's mount on the base was measured in position and its turn was taken as zero; a mount turned by a degree puts the hand 15.7 mm out at 0.9 m.

## Fix

Calibrate the mount's offset and turn together (a fit like 31-03's), not by a ruler.

```cpp
arm_base_in_world(m, base);
```
