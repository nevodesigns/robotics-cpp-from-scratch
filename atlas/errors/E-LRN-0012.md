## Symptom

A network trained for longer, or made larger, has a better training error each time and does worse in use.

## Cause

It over-fitted: with more weights than the data can pin down, training went on fitting the noise of the training samples after it had learned the task. On 30 noisy samples the training error fell from 0.04970 to 0.00012, while the validation error was lowest, 0.0280, at epoch 346 and ended at 0.0448.

## Fix

Measure a validation set of other episodes after every epoch, keep the best weights, and stop when it has not improved for a patience of epochs.

```cpp
const rc::learn::TrainResult r = rc::learn::train_with_stopping(net, scale, train, validation, 3000, 0.05, 200, seed);
```

More demonstrations are the other cure, and 38-06 shows how to get them where they help most.
