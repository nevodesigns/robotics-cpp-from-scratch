## Symptom

Picks are right near one part of the cell and increasingly wrong farther from the calibration points.

## Cause

A rotation error of 1 degree is 10.5 mm at 0.6 m: the angle is multiplied by the reach.

## Fix

Calibrate over the whole working volume and check the error at its far corners, not only at the middle.

```cpp
check the residual at the corners of the workspace
```
