## Symptom

Given a goal it cannot reach, the vehicle drives towards it and stops at a wall.

## Cause

The search reached its limit and returned the nearest pose. A goal walled in was reported as not found after 4000 expansions, with an empty path.

## Fix

Return found = false and an empty path, and let the mission decide.

```cpp
if (!r.found) report_unreachable();
```
