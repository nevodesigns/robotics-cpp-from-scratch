## Symptom

A policy with a good value is put in charge, and some runs still end badly, which is taken as a fault.

## Cause

A value is an expected return, an average over the outcomes of slips and chances. The east policy's value was -5.852, and 20000 runs of it averaged -5.836, yet 47.7 percent of them ended in a pit. The average is right and the bad runs are part of it.

## Fix

Report the share of runs that end badly beside the value, and if a bad end must be rare, make it a constraint (39-09), not a part of the average.

```cpp
if (ended == 'P') ++pits;   // count it, beside the mean
```

On a robot, the share of bad ends is often what a safety case is about, and an average return hides it.
