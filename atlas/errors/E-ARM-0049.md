## Symptom

The arm holds a pose for a while and then a joint cuts out or slows, with nothing wrong in the code. It happens after the same minutes in the same pose, every day.

## Cause

Holding is a current and a current is heat. Holding 37.7 N m at the stretched shoulder brought the winding to its limit in 293 s; 2.1 N m folded never did.

Torque is current, and current heats the winding as its square times the resistance. Holding a stretched pose draws a large current all the time, and the winding warms until the drive's thermal limit cuts it.

## Fix

Work out the time to the limit for the poses the task holds, and fold, rest or use the brake in them.

```cpp
const double t = rc::arm::time_to_limit(motor, torque_to_hold);   // 1e18 if it never gets there
```
