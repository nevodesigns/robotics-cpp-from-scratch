## Symptom

The SCARA moves slowly and shakes at the far edge of its work area, and straight under its base it barely moves at all.

## Cause

The task was laid out where the arm is near a singularity. The planar Jacobian's determinant is l1 l2 sin q2: 0.0506 at a right angle (with l1 and l2 of 0.225 m) and 0.0000 with the arm straight or folded. Near those poses the tool cannot move along the arm, and joint speeds blow up for a small motion of the tool.

## Fix

Lay the work out where q2 stays well away from 0 and pi, and check the determinant over the whole area before choosing where to mount the arm.

```cpp
const double det = rc::arm::scara_det(arm, q2);   // 0.0506 at q2 = 1.57, 0 straight
if (std::fabs(det) < 0.2 * arm.l1 * arm.l2) warn("near the edge of reach or folded");
```
