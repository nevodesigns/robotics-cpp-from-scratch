## Symptom

The delay is allowed for and the error returns whenever the robot speeds up or turns.

## Cause

The fix was moved on by the velocity, which holds only if the velocity is constant over the delay. When the robot braked to a stop during it, the fix was 0.25 m beyond the robot; the history put it 0.000 m out.

## Fix

Compare with the state at the stamp, from a history, instead of extrapolating the measurement.

```cpp
history.at(stamp, then);
```
