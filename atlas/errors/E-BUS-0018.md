## Symptom

A node starts and drives to a goal that was set an hour ago and cancelled in a way nobody remembered.

## Cause

What is remembered stays remembered. At 100 s, remembered messages from 0 s and 98 s were both heard with no age limit, and only the one from 98 s with a limit of 5 s, with the skipped one counted. Keep 3 gave a late subscriber 3 4 5 then 6, in order.

## Fix

Give the subscription an age limit where the data goes stale.

```cpp
bus.subscribe("goal", handler, now, 5.0);
```
