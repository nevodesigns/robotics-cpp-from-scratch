## Symptom

Raising the gains does not make the robot more robust against a big push.

## Cause

A push of 25 N m is beyond what the feet can answer (17.7 N m): the body rolled over (1), however stiff the law; a push of 10 N m was answered in 0.12 s.

## Fix

Work out the largest push the feet can answer and, beyond it, use steps (35-07) rather than stiffer gains.

```text
the limit is the weight times the half width
```
