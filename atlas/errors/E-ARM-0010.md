## Symptom

The inverse kinematics returns the angles the pose was made from, and some of its other solutions do not reach the pose.

## Cause

The test compared the solver's answer with the angles it was made from, and an arm with a spherical wrist has up to eight solutions for one pose. The others were never checked.

## Fix

Test every solution through the forward kinematics, position and rotation, for many poses. Checked that way, all of them (8.0 on average) reached the pose to 0.0000000000 m.

```cpp
for (const auto& s : rc::arm::ik_spherical(table, target, base, tool))
  assert(pose_distance(rc::arm::forward(table, s, base, tool), target) < 1e-9);
```
