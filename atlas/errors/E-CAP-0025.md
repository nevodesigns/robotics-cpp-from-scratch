match: across twenty robots a candidate with no safety layer reaches all of them without the gate and none with it
match: a good candidate passes the gate, goes through the pipeline of phase 20, and is the rover on the robot
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-09-a-gate-before-the-robot

## Symptom

Somebody simplifies the control loop and removes the limiter, the tests that ran were all of the robot driving, and the release goes to the fleet.

## Cause

With the limiter and supervisor out the gate failed S2, S4 and S5 (hazards G1, G2, G4, G8) and S1 passed; the robot's disk and installed version were untouched. Twenty robots offered it: 20 ran it with no gate, 0 with the gate. The good candidate passed 5 of 5 and was confirmed on 1.5.0.

## Fix

Make the safety suite a condition of shipping, with no way round.

```cpp
if (!run_gate(candidate).passed) return;
```
