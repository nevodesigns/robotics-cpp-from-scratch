## Symptom

A rover in a long corridor jumps by metres after each correction, or the correction is right one moment and wrong the next.

## Cause

Every position along a corridor fits the scan equally well. A 30 m corridor tied 41 poses with the best, where a room with a block against one wall tied 1. A search that keeps the first or last of the tied poses moves the rover along the corridor for no reason. Taking the one nearest the guess kept it at 15.00 against a truth of 15.00, and a guess drifted by 0.250 m in a room that did determine the pose came back to 0.000 m.

## Fix

Break ties toward the guess, and report the tied count.

```cpp
if (scores[i] == best && d < nearest) { nearest = d; result.best = poses[i]; }
```
