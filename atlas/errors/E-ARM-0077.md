## Symptom

The part stays in the fingers while the arm is still, and slips the moment it lifts or turns quickly. The drops come at the start of a fast lift, never in a slow one.

## Cause

The squeeze was worked out for the weight at rest. The hand's acceleration adds to gravity: a kilogram at rest took 9.81 N at a friction of 0.5, and lifted at 5 m/s2 it took 14.81 N.

To accelerate the part upward the pads must push it with its weight plus its mass times the acceleration. A squeeze set for the weight at rest leaves nothing for the acceleration, and the part slides as soon as the arm moves briskly.

## Fix

Work out the force with g plus the largest acceleration of the moves the part goes through, and a safety factor on top.

```cpp
const double f = rc::arm::required_force(hand, mass, accel, 1.5);   // accel of the fastest move
```

A turn adds a centripetal acceleration as well, and a sideways one tests the friction in a direction the pads may grip less.

Squeezing harder is not free: a soft part is crushed, so the acceleration of the move is sometimes the thing to lower.
