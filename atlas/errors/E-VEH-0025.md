## Symptom

A parameter is fitted and the gap stays large in one case, however it is chosen.

## Cause

The behaviour depended on context, which one number cannot follow. A backend with a turn 30 percent short after it had moved could not be fitted below a worst gap of 0.013 m, against 0.0000 m for one that did not.

## Fix

Measure the same quantity in several contexts before fitting, and look for the one that changes.

```cpp
if (context_dependent(turn_tests(b, scenario, robot))) investigate();
```
