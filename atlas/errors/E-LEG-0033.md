## Symptom

The estimated tilt drifts steadily until the robot is leaning against a floor it thinks is tilted.

## Cause

The gyro's bias was added up: 0.5 degrees per second gave 23.1 degrees of error after 60 s.

## Fix

Pull the estimate gently toward the accelerometer (and the feet) so that the drift cannot build up.

```cpp
attitude_update(f, gx, gy, acc, fr, fp, feet_valid, dt);
```
