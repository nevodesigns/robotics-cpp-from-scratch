## Symptom

After a payload is added the robot works for a while and then the knees overheat in a trot that was fine before.

## Cause

The payload was added without checking the knees. The heaviest body whose knees settle at or under their limit in a trot at 0.30 m was 17.5 kg; a robot built for 12 kg has that much room at that height and much less crouched.

## Fix

Check the body plus payload against the knees' heat limit for the gait and height that will be used.

```cpp
const double heaviest = rc::legged::max_body_mass(leg, knee, 0.30, 2, 0.5, 0.3);   // 17.5 kg in a trot
```
