## Symptom

The vehicle backs up, drives forward into the same wall, backs up again, until the battery is flat.

## Cause

No limit on the attempts or on a behaviour: a wall that never moved was given up on after 3 attempts and 31.2 s only because the attempts are counted, and forgotten only after 1 m of real progress.

## Fix

Count attempts, forget them only after real progress, end every behaviour by a time limit, and give up in an orderly way.

```cpp
if (b == kGiveUp) return DriveCmd();
```
