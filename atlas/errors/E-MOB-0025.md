## Symptom

The base stops well empty and overshoots or tips when loaded and moving at the same speed.

## Cause

The speed was limited without the load; with it the base could brake at 5.19 m/s2 without tipping and to stop in 0.5 m at a margin of 0.8 it was held to 2.04 m/s.

## Fix

Work out the speed limit from the tipping deceleration of the present pose and load.

```cpp
tip_speed_limit(tip_brake, margin, stop_dist);
```
