## Symptom

A fast robot does not give a fast cycle.

## Cause

The hand's time is part of the cycle: with a vacuum cup the delta's cycle was 0.81 s, and a pinching hand's longer closing and opening add much more.

## Fix

Put the hand's time in the task and in the cycle.

```cpp
task.dwell_s = 0.2;
```
