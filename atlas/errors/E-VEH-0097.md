## Symptom

A skid steer vehicle takes every bend wide, more so on a floor that grips. It is a skid steer vehicle with wide tyres on a grippy floor.

## Cause

The wheel speeds were worked out with chi = 1 and no slip, but the body of a skid steer vehicle turns as if on a track chi times wider. The worst error was 0.115 m, against 0.042 m with the real values.

A skid steer vehicle turns by scrubbing its wheels sideways, and it turns less than the wheel speed difference says: as if its track were chi times wider. Wheel speeds worked out with the geometric track ask for too little difference.

## Fix

Work out the wheel speeds with chi times the track, and the slip, both calibrated on the vehicle (23-02, 23-08).

```cpp
const rc::drive::SkidCommand c = rc::drive::skid_wheel_speeds(geometry, chi, slip, v, w);
```
