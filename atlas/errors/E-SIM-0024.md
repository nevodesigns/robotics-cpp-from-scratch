## Symptom

The motor was replaced, the model was not, and every simulated test still passes while the real robot behaves differently.

## Cause

A simulation is trusted only while it stays within limits that a test can check. With limits of 0.02, 0.05 and 1 sample, a model fitted to the bench gave 0.0048, 0.0141 and 0, exceeding none, and a model with the wrong motor and no sensor delay gave 0.1243, 0.3657 and 6, exceeding rmse, max and lag.

## Fix

Keep a recorded bench trace and test the model against it.

```cpp
const auto bad = exceeded(compare(sim, bench, 20), limits);
```
