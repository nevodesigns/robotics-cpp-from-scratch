## Symptom

A step on the joystick, from rest to a brisk walk, makes the robot lurch, drag a foot or trip on the first stride. Small changes of command are fine; it is the jumps that hurt.

## Cause

The command was handed to the gait as it came. The gaits of phase 35 place each foot from the commanded velocity, and the body's position is integrated from it, so a jump in velocity tells the body to be somewhere it is not and the feet cannot follow. A stick moves in a few milliseconds; a 12 kg robot does not.

## Fix

Ease the command in use toward the asked one by at most an acceleration times the time step. A jump from rest to 0.8 m/s with a limit of 1.0 m/s2 then takes 0.80 s, in both directions.

```cpp
rc::legged::Cmd now;                          // what the gait is using
for (;;) {
  now = rc::legged::limit_command(stick, now, lim, dt);   // at most lim.ax * dt of change a tick
  gait.step(now, dt);
}
```

The cost is response time: 0.8 s to reach 0.8 m/s. Raise the acceleration limit only as far as the swing can follow.
