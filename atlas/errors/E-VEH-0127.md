## Symptom

The vehicle goes out with a check failed, because a person pressed continue. The override button was pressed so often that nobody remembers why it exists.

## Cause

Every failure could be overridden, so a failure was a nuisance to click past. Some checks guard against what cannot be undone.

A check that can always be overridden becomes a dialogue to click through. Some failures (no emergency stop, no scanner) must never be accepted, and the only way to make sure is to make them impossible to override.

## Fix

Decide which checks are blocks and which are warnings. Refuse overrides of blocks (6 of the 10 checks here), and require and keep a reason for overriding a warning.

```cpp
const rc::drive::Decision d = rc::drive::decide(results, overrides);
```
