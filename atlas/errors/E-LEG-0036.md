title: A weight not shared among the feet that are down

## Symptom

The balance control asks a foot that is in the air for force, or the other feet carry less than the weight.

## Cause

Forces were worked out for four feet when three were down: with 29.4 N each for four, three carry the weight and the lifted foot must have none.

## Fix

Give the force distribution the list of feet that are on the ground (34-07).

```cpp
foot_forces(feet, on_ground, weight, mx, my, f);
```
