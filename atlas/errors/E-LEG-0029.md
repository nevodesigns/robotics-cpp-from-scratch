title: A trot with a stance that is too long for the body's offset
match: the swing feet landed after the body had fallen
match: the stance was lengthened and the trot failed
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Lengthening the stance (a slower trot, for safety) makes the trot fall over.

## Cause

A longer stance gives the body more time to fall before the next feet land: 0.25 s was too long for an offset of 30 mm (0).

## Fix

Shorten the stance, or keep the offset small, or stand taller.

```cpp
p.period = shorter;
```
