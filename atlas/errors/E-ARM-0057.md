title: A margin put in the wrong place
match: I fattened every obstacle to get a margin and now nothing fits
match: the margin applies to the arm and not to the obstacles
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 30-02-how-far-it-is-safe-to-step

## Symptom

Obstacles were made bigger to keep the arm away, and now the arm cannot reach what it must touch.

## Cause

The margin was put in the obstacles. A clearance of 3 cm is free with no margin and not with 5 cm: the margin is the check's.

## Fix

Keep a margin in the check, and set it to zero (or small) for the final approach to a thing the arm is meant to touch.

```cpp
free_pose(check, q, margin);
```
