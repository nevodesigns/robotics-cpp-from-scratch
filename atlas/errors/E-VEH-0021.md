## Symptom

A car driven straight has a path in the log that curves. It is a car with a steering angle sensor.

## Cause

The steering sensor's zero was never measured. It read one degree with the wheels straight, and over 20 m the odometry believed the car had turned 61 degrees.

A steering sensor reads an angle relative to its own zero, which is not exactly the straight-ahead position. A degree of zero offset is a degree of steady turn in the odometry.

## Fix

Measure the zero offset with the car on a straight line and subtract it.

```cpp
const double delta = steer_measured - zero_offset;
```
