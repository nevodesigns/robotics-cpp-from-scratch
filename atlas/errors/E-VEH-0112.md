match: the mission never ends because one point is blocked
match: a list of waypoints is reached in order, standing at the one that asks to
match: a leg that cannot be reached times out, and the policy skips it or ends the mission
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

One blocked waypoint holds up the whole mission, or the vehicle goes on to the next as if nothing was wrong.

## Cause

No timeout and no decision. With a timeout of 10 s the abort policy ended the mission after 10.0 s; the skip policy went on and kept the leg as timed out.

## Fix

Give every leg a timeout, choose skip or abort for the job, and keep the result of each leg.

```cpp
WaypointMission m(legs, kSkipOnTimeout);
```
