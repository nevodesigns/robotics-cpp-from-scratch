## Symptom

The heading from the gyro walks away, and a robot that goes in a straight line believes it is curving.

## Cause

The bias adds up: the gyro alone ended 68.0 degrees out in two minutes.

## Fix

Estimate the bias, from the wheels going straight or from standing still, and subtract it.

```cpp
const double rate = gyro - bias.bias();
```
