## Symptom

A status bar is hidden and a blank band stays where it was, or the plot does not grow into the room.

## Cause

A widget that is hidden is removed from the layout calculation only if it is in a layout. A child placed by hand with `move` and `resize` leaves its space empty. Measured: with the layout, hiding a 20 pixel status bar took the plot from **250** to **270**, and showing it took it back to **250**.

## Fix

Put every part of the panel in the layout and hide the widget, not its contents:

```cpp
layout->addWidget(status_);
status_->hide();     // the plot grows into its place
```
