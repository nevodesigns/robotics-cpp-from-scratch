match: the vehicle goes straight on at a bend because it braked too late

platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A vehicle that brakes hard for a bend runs wide at the entrance.

## Cause

It braked in the bend. Near the sliding speed almost all the grip is spent on turning: at 99 percent of it only 20 percent of the straight line's braking is left.

## Fix

Brake before the bend, on the straight, and enter at a speed that leaves some grip.

```cpp
if (b.share > 0.8) request_slower_entry();
```
