## Symptom

The arm is accurate empty and sags when it holds something.

## Cause

The model has the arm and not the payload: 2 kg left an error of 0.0646 rad.

## Fix

Put the payload in the model (mass, centre, inertia), from a weighing or the payload data.

```cpp
with_payload(model, mass, where);
```
