## Symptom

The calibration is repeatable and consistently off by about a percent.

## Cause

The gyro's bias added to the heading of a spin one way: 0.31 percent in the track, against 0.000 percent for a spin each way.

## Fix

Calibrate with runs in both directions and take the sizes.

```cpp
runs.push_back(spin_left); runs.push_back(spin_right);
```
