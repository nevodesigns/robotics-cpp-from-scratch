## Symptom

Two people meet, stop and walk on, and afterwards one track has the other's velocity.

## Cause

When two targets are close and change what they are doing, the detections are nearer the wrong predictions. In 20 runs, people passing a metre apart swapped 0 times, and two standing side by side 5.

## Fix

Do not trust the identities of tracks through close encounters, or keep more than one hypothesis until they part.

```cpp
// mark the ids of two tracks that came within a metre as uncertain until they separate
```
