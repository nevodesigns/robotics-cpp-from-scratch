## Symptom

After an update the vehicle turns too little or too much, and every path is followed wide. The bundle was built on another vehicle of the same model.

## Cause

The bundle carried another vehicle's calibration. A track of 0.40 m taken for 0.25 m makes the vehicle turn 1.60 times too little.

A calibration is a measurement of one vehicle: its own effective track, its own zero offsets. Copied to another, it makes the second vehicle wrong by their difference.

## Fix

Bind a calibration to a vehicle id, refuse a bundle that carries another's (kWrongCalibration), and let a bundle carry none.

```cpp
if (!b.calibration_for.empty() && b.calibration_for != v.id) refuse(rc::drive::kWrongCalibration);
```
