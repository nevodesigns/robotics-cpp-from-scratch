## Symptom

The arm goes to where the camera saw the part and misses by a large, steady amount.

## Cause

A point from the camera was used as the arm's target without putting it into the base's frame. A pixel 60 to the right of the centre at a depth of 0.5 m, with a focal length of 600 pixels, is 0.050 m to the side in the camera's frame, which says nothing about where it is in the base's.

## Fix

Put every point seen by the camera through the measured base from camera transform before it becomes a target.

```cpp
double p_base[3];
rc::arm::camera_to_base(base_from_camera, p_cam, p_base);
```
