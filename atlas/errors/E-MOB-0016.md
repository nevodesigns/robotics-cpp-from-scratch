## Symptom

The base accelerates well empty and is slow or stalls when loaded.

## Cause

The wheels' force over the mass falls as the mass rises; the grip caps it at 5.9 m/s2 at best. The traction limit for 0.5 m/s2 was 130.7 kg here.

## Fix

Work out the acceleration for the total mass, and limit commands to it.

```cpp
base_accel_limit(drive, total_mass);
```
