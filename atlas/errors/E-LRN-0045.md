## Symptom

A policy gradient makes no progress at all, its estimates are all zero or undefined.

## Cause

The noise in the actions is how the policy tries actions other than its own, and the score is built from the difference between the action taken and the policy's mean. With no noise that difference is zero (and the formula divides by the noise's spread squared), so there is nothing to learn from.

## Fix

Keep the noise above zero while learning, and reduce it as the policy improves.

```cpp
const double sigma = 0.3;   // never 0 while learning
```

The noise is exploration (39-04) in a continuous action.
