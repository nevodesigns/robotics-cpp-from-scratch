title: Poses for the sweep from a source that is too slow
match: deskewing makes the cloud worse
match: the poses at the start and the end of the sweep are the same pose
match: a vehicle driving at the wall makes it look slanted, and deskewing straightens that too
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-07-a-scan-taken-while-moving

## Symptom

Deskewing changes nothing, or makes the cloud worse. The poses came from GNSS or a slow localiser.

## Cause

The two poses came from a source too slow to give the poses at the start and end of the sweep. A pose at 1 Hz is the same pose for both ends, so the interpolation does nothing; it needs poses within the sweep's 0.1 s.

Deskewing interpolates the vehicle's pose across the sweep. If the two poses are the same, because the source updates more slowly than the sweep, the interpolation does nothing.

## Fix

Take the poses from the wheel odometry or an inertial sensor, at the scan's rate or faster.

```cpp
rc::perceive::deskew(scan, odometry.at(sweep_start), odometry.at(sweep_end));
```
