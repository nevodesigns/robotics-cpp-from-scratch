## Symptom

A thin obstacle appears in the scan only now and then at a distance, and the sensor's range is far greater.

## Cause

At one degree between beams, the points are 0.175 m apart at 10 m, and a post of 0.3 m gets two beams only up to 17.2 m.

## Fix

Plan the stopping distance from the range at which the narrowest thing that matters is seen twice.

```cpp
const double seen_to = max_range_for_width(0.3, angle_inc, 2);
```
