match: a field worked out with no latency lets the vehicle through a thing
match: points behind, beside or past the end of the field are not in it

## Symptom

A thing enters the protective field and the vehicle stops, but not before reaching it. The field was sized on paper from the braking distance at the speed.

## Cause

The field was worked out without the latency (0.3 s here) between the thing entering it and the brakes acting. The vehicle stopped 0.25 m past where the thing was.

Between something entering the field and the brakes acting there is the scanner's cycle and the controller's: the latency. The vehicle travels that far at full speed before braking starts.

## Fix

Make the field the distance covered in the latency, plus the braking distance, plus a margin, at the speed the vehicle has now. With the latency it stopped 0.20 m short.

```cpp
const double len = rc::drive::field_length(speed, cfg);   // v t + v^2 / 2a + margin
```
