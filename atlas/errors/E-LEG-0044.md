## Symptom

The push the robot can answer is much smaller than the leg's reach suggests.

## Cause

The swing time costs exponentially: the largest push was 0.56 m/s for a swing of 0.15 s and 0.17 m/s for 0.35 s.

## Fix

Make the swing as quick as the servos allow (34-05) and work out the push that can be answered from the swing time.

```cpp
max_push_speed(reach, margin, t_land, h);
```
