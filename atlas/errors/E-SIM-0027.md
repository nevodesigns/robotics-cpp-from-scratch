title: A bench loop that assumes the link is perfect
match: over a clean link the loop settles, and over a lossy one it still does, with more ticks spent on a measurement that was not new
match: on a link where frames overtake each other, taking each measurement as it arrives sends the controller back in time, and the tick check does not
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-09-the-bench-link
---

## Symptom

The loop works on the bench on a short cable and misbehaves on a long one, and nobody knows how often the controller is acting on old data.

## Cause

A link loses, delays and reorders. Over loss of 0, 5, 10, 20 and 40 percent each way the controller held the last measurement on 1, 19, 34, 64 and 115 of 300 ticks, and the loop still settled (0.0001 to 0.0007). Counting the held ticks and the refused frames says how bad the link was, and the age of the information acted on, 2.17 ticks against 2.42, says what it cost.

## Fix

Count what was held and what was refused, and look at the counts.

```cpp
controller.end_tick();   // held if nothing new came
```
