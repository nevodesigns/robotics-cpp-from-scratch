## Symptom

The car runs wide at the tightest bend of a path that looks fine on the screen.

## Cause

The path asked for a circle smaller than L / tan(max steer), here 0.742 m. The steering was held at its limit and the car drove a bigger circle.

## Fix

Give the planner the smallest turning radius, and refuse a curvature beyond it.

```cpp
const double min_r = min_turning_radius(car);
```
