## Symptom

The rover drives straight at a closed door and pushes at it until someone stops it, with the right map and the right plan for an hour ago.

## Cause

A plan is a statement about the map when it was made. With a door that closed at step 15, a rover that never replanned bumped 284 times in 300 steps and did not arrive, and one that replanned when its next step was a wall reached the goal in 83 steps with 6 plans and no bumps. Replanning every step is the other extreme: 33 plans for 33 moves in a building that did not change, with the same 33 steps.

## Fix

Replan when the next step is blocked on the map.

```cpp
if (map.classify(next.x, next.y) == Cell::occupied) need_plan = true;
```
