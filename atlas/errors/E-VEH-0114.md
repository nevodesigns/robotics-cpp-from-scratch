## Symptom

A car-like vehicle drives round and round a waypoint without ever being within the tolerance.

## Cause

The tolerance was 0.10 m, finer than the car can hit from where it was with a smallest radius of 0.742 m: still circling after 40 s; with 0.70 m it was there in 1.8 s.

## Fix

Set the tolerance of a waypoint to at least what the vehicle can do (for a car, about its smallest radius), or give it a heading to arrive with.

```cpp
w.tolerance = 0.7;
```
