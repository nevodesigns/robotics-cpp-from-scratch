## Symptom

With a load in the hand the base tips when it stops, at a deceleration that was safe empty. It happens on the first job with a real part, after every test with an empty hand passed.

## Cause

The payload was not counted. With 5 kg in the hand at full reach, the braking limit fell to 5.19 m/s2, below the 5.89 m/s2 the wheels grip on a dry floor: the base tips before it skids.

A payload at the tool is a mass at the end of the longest lever on the robot. It moves the centre of mass forward more than its own share of the mass, and the tipping limit falls below the grip of the floor: the wheels would hold, and the base goes over instead.

## Fix

Include the payload in the mass model at the tool, and cap the braking at the tipping limit as well as the grip.

```cpp
rc::mobile::MassModel masses;
masses.payload = 5.0;   // kg at the tool
rc::mobile::com_world(m, base, q, masses, com);
```

From 1.0 m/s the loaded base needed 0.096 m to stop at that limit.

A heavier base or a lower mount raises the limit; slowing down only uses less of it.
