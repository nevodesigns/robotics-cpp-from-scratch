## Symptom

One blocked waypoint holds up the whole mission, or the vehicle goes on to the next as if nothing had gone wrong. A parked car or a locked gate is enough to do it.

## Cause

The leg had no timeout and no policy. A waypoint behind a parked car or in a flooded corner is never reached, and a mission with no rule for that either waits for ever or quietly drops the leg.

A waypoint mission is a list of things that may each be impossible on the day. If the code has no notion of a leg that took too long, the only outcomes are waiting for ever or a silent skip, and neither is a decision anybody made.

## Fix

Give every leg a timeout, choose skip or abort for the job, and keep the result of each leg. With a timeout of 10 s the abort policy ended the mission after 10.0 s; the skip policy went on and kept the leg marked as timed out.

```cpp
rc::drive::WaypointMission m(legs, rc::drive::kSkipOnTimeout);   // or kAbortOnTimeout for a job where order matters
```
