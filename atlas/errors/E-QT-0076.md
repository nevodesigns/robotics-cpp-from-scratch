## Symptom

An item follows its parent until a handler sets it once, and then it stops following. Resizing the window leaves it behind. There is no error.

## Cause

A value assigned in JavaScript, or written through `QQmlProperty`, replaces the binding. The property has a value and nothing it follows. Measured: with `width: outer.width / 2`, outer 400 gave **200**; after `width = 10` it gave **10** with outer at 400, and after `Qt.binding` it was **200** and then **250** at outer 500.

## Fix

Assign a binding and not a value:

```qml
width = Qt.binding(function() { return outer.width / 2 })
```
