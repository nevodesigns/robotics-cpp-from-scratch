## Symptom

After the payload is added to the model the compensation is better but wrong by different amounts in different poses.

## Cause

The mass was added to the last link and its centre of mass left where it was. The weight is then in the wrong place, and its torque varies with the pose the wrong way. Put at the tool, where the estimate from six poses assumed it (2.00 kg for a 2 kg payload), the error fell to 0.00010 rad.

## Fix

Move the link's centre of mass to the weighted mean of the two, and its inertia by the parallel axis rule.

```cpp
const double com = (m_link * com_link + m_pay * where) / (m_link + m_pay);   // per axis, which with_payload does
```
