## Symptom

A calibration finishes with a residual well above the noise and is accepted anyway; the feet are still off afterwards.

## Cause

The model, not only the zeros, was wrong. With the thigh 10 mm longer than the geometry says, no offsets explain it: with noise-free measurements the fit still left 3.6 mm. Noise of 2 mm in each axis leaves 3.4 mm on its own, so on a real jig an error of this size adds to the noise instead of standing above it, and it is seen only if the noise of the jig is known.

## Fix

Know the noise of the jig, and refuse a calibration whose residual is larger than that noise accounts for. Then measure or fit the link lengths.

```cpp
const double noise_3d = 0.002 * std::sqrt(3.0);   // 3.5 mm for 2 mm in each axis
if (rms > 1.2 * noise_3d) reject("the model is wrong: check the link lengths");
```
