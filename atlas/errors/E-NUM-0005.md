## Symptom

A rate limited value never settles. It steps past the target, comes back, and
repeats, which on real hardware is a visibly vibrating actuator.

## Cause

Always moving by the maximum step, even when less than a full step of distance
remains. The value jumps past the target and then jumps back.

## Fix

Compare the remaining change against the maximum step first. When it is smaller,
take the whole remaining change and land exactly on the target. Only clamp to the
maximum step when the remaining distance is genuinely larger.
