## Symptom

The part fits between the fingers and the hand cannot hold it: it slides out as the arm lifts.

## Cause

The hand was chosen by its stroke alone. A stroke of 0.085 m says what fits; the squeeze and the friction say what is held. Each pad resists a pull of at most mu times the squeeze, so two pads give 2 mu F, and the mass held is 2 mu F over a safety factor times (g plus the acceleration). With the full 235 N, a friction of 0.5 and a safety factor of 1.5, the hand held at most 16.0 kg at rest.

## Fix

Check the width against the stroke and the force needed against the squeeze, at the acceleration of the move.

```cpp
if (!rc::arm::fits(hand, width) || rc::arm::required_force(hand, mass, accel, 1.5) > hand.f_max) refuse();
```
