## Symptom

A robot that does 1.7 m/s on the flat does little more than one on a hill.

## Cause

The top speed is where the force equals the resistance, and the slope adds to it. On a slope of 10 degrees it was 0.99 m/s against 1.67 on the level.

## Fix

Compute the top speed from the terrain under the vehicle, and plan with it.

```cpp
const double v = top_speed(drive_train, cell, heading);
```
