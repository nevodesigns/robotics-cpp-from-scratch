## Symptom

At speed the robot's body drifts back over its legs during each stance and it slows down by itself, or it pitches forward at every step.

## Cause

The foot was put down under the hip. A foot on the ground moves back relative to the body at the body's speed, so a foot that lands under the hip ends the stance behind it. Raibert's rule puts it ahead by half the distance the body goes in the stance: at 0.4 m/s with a stance of 0.25 s, 0.050 m ahead, leaving 0.050 m behind.

## Fix

Put the foot ahead by half the stance distance, plus a term on the speed error that brakes a body going too fast.

```cpp
double td[2];
rc::legged::touchdown_xy(hip, v, v_cmd, 0.25, 0.1, td);   // 0.050 m ahead at 0.4 m/s, 20 mm more for 0.2 m/s too fast
```
