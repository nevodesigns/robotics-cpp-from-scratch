title: A timeout chosen for comfort and not for a distance
match: the timeout that fits a distance is what is left after the braking, and none fits when the braking alone is too long
match: a link that goes out stops the robot after the timeout and the braking, and a longer timeout is a longer way
match: when the link comes back the robot does not go on by itself, and it goes on when a person presses reset after a second of good link
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-06-a-robot-you-drive-from-a-chair

## Symptom

The stop path has a timeout of a second because that felt safe, and the robot goes a metre and a half after the link drops.

## Cause

At 1 m/s the robot went 0.398, 0.598 and 1.298 m after the link went for timeouts of 0.1, 0.3 and 1.0 s, and 1.463, 1.863 and 3.263 m at 2 m/s. A timeout of 0.667 s was the most for 1 m at 1 m/s and at 2 m/s none fitted. With no reset the robot stayed still when the link returned; with a reset after 1 s of good link it went on.

## Fix

Compute the timeout from the distance.

```cpp
config.timeout = max_timeout(distance, speed, decel);
```
