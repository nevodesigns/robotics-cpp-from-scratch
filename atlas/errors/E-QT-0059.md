## Symptom

Two small changes, one at each end of the chart, cost almost as much as a full repaint, although only two columns changed.

## Cause

The paint event gives the rectangle that bounds the dirty region. Two rectangles far apart have a large bounding rectangle. Measured: updates of columns 10 and 200 were **1** paint of x = 10 to 200, over **191** columns.

## Fix

Group changes that are near each other, or use `event->region()` to visit each rectangle:

```cpp
for (const QRect& r : event->region()) { ... }
```
