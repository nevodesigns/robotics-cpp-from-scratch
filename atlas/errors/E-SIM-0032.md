id: E-SIM-0032
title: Steps that are not steps, and a settling time that is not a time
match: a step of no size is left out, so are changes outside the trace, and each step stops at the next change
match: a run that never stays within the band has no settling time, and the verdict says that and not a number
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-11-pass-or-fail
---

## Symptom

A run with a target that did not really change reports a perfect step, or a loop that rings for ever passes the settling criterion because its settling time is -1.

## Cause

A change of target to where the position already is moves nothing and is left out, as is a change beyond the end of the trace: four changes gave 2 steps measured, at 0 and 4 s. A step that never stays within the band has a settling time of -1, and a verdict that compared -1 with a limit would pass it. An oscillation about the target gave settling -1.0000 and a verdict of settling, overshoot and final error.

## Fix

Treat a negative settling time as a failure.

```cpp
if (s.settling_time < 0.0 || s.settling_time > c.max_settling) failures.push_back(where + "settling");
```
