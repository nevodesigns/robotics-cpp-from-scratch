## Symptom

Under admittance control the tool buzzes or bounces when it touches a hard surface and is steady on a soft one.

## Cause

The measurement delay and the inner loop's lag are inside the loop, and a stiff surface has a high gain: a damper of 100 N s/m was 0 (1 steady) on the stiff surface and 1 on the soft one; 1500 gave 1.

## Fix

Raise the virtual damper to about the surface's stiffness over the inner loop's bandwidth, or use an impedance controller for stiff surfaces.

```cpp
a.dv = 1500.0;
```
