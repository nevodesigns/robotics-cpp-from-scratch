## Symptom

The cell is laid out to the reach on the data sheet, and in places the arm can get to, it cannot do the job.

## Cause

Reach is the outside of the shape. At table height the farthest the tool could get in any orientation was 0.86 m, and with the tool pointing down 0.74 m. The dexterous poses (smallest singular value at least 0.1) were 62 percent of the reachable volume.

## Fix

Design round the dexterous region and the task's own orientation, not the reach.

```cpp
rc::arm::sample_reach(table, base, tool, n, seed, 0.1, grid);   // count only poses with sigma_min >= 0.1
```
