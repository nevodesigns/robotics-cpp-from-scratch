title: A speed limit not set from the legs
match: the speed limit was higher than the legs could step
match: full stick made the robot trip
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 36-01-what-the-operator-asks-for

## Symptom

At full stick the robot trips or drags its feet.

## Cause

The limit was set above what the legs allow: 1.60 m/s from a step of 0.20 m over a stance of 0.125 s; 2.0 m/s needs more than the leg's reach.

## Fix

Work out the fastest speed from the longest step and the shortest stance and set the limit below it.

```cpp
max_speed(step_max, duty, period_min);
```
