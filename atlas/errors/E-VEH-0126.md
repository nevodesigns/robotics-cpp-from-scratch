## Symptom

A stretch of the log is missing and the report says the run was fine.

## Cause

Nothing looked at the time between rows. A jump of 20 s is a finding of its own, and the other tests, run across it, say nothing about what happened inside it.

## Fix

Report every gap longer than a limit (1.0 s by default), and treat what happened in it as unknown.

```cpp
if (rows[i].t - rows[i - 1].t > cfg.max_gap) report_gap(rows[i - 1].t, rows[i].t);
```
