## Symptom

Asked to draw a line longer than its reach, the arm stretches out, slows, and stops short. It looks like an inverse kinematics failure, and better solvers do not fix it.

## Cause

The arm alone was asked to do it. It ended 1.18 m short of a 1.5 m line, at full stretch, with its manipulability at 0.0000: a singularity.

The line was longer than the arm. As the arm stretches toward the end of its reach it nears a singularity, its manipulability falls to zero, and the damped solver slows to a stop short of the goal rather than ask for impossible joint rates.

## Fix

Let the base move as part of the motion. Arm and base together reached the end to 0.000 m, the base driving 1.43 m, and the arm's manipulability never fell below 0.0316.

```cpp
const rc::mobile::WbRun r = rc::mobile::run_line(m, base, q, goal, 0.15, 1.0, true, 0.01);   // use_base true
```

Moving the base costs time and floor; the run of 33-04 shows the base drove 1.43 m to give the arm 1.5 m of line.
