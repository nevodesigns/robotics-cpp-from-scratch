## Symptom

When the tool points straight along the forearm, joints 4 and 6 swing against each other with no motion of the tool. Joints 4 and 6 spin opposite ways while the tool stands still.

## Cause

Joint 5 is at zero, the wrist's singularity: joints 4 and 6 then turn about the same axis and only their sum is fixed.

When joint 5 is straight, the axes of 4 and 6 line up. Turning 4 one way and 6 the other leaves the tool unchanged, so any split of their sum is a solution, and the solver drifts among them.

## Fix

Plan the orientation so that joint 5 stays away from zero, or take the other wrist solution of the closed form, and keep the smallest singular value above a floor along the path.

```cpp
if (std::fabs(q[4]) < 0.1) choose_other_wrist_or_replan();
```
