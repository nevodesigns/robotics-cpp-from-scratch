id: E-SIM-0008
title: A model used beyond the range it was fitted in
match: a model fitted on one step predicts another well inside the motor's range, and badly once the real motor is clamped
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-03-measuring-the-motor
---

## Symptom

The simulation of a hard push on the motor is nothing like the real one, though gentle pushes matched perfectly.

## Cause

A fit describes the linear range. A model fitted on a step of 1 predicted steps of 2, 4, 6 and 12 to within 0.0014, 0.0028, 0.0042 and 0.0083, and a step of 20, which the motor clamps to 12, to within 14.4390: the model said 40 and the motor did 24.

## Fix

Measure the limit and give it to the model separately.

```cpp
SimMotor motor(f.gain, f.tau, measured_limit);
```
