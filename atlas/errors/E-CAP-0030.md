title: A refused release treated as a failure of the robot
match: the release is shipped through the gate and the pipeline, the robot runs the day, and when the gate refuses, the robot's day is the day it had before
match: the report of a day is a fixed five lines, which is what is handed to somebody who was not there
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-10-the-whole-robot-for-a-day
---

## Symptom

The gate refuses a release and the team treats the robot as broken, or the robot runs on without anybody saying which release it is running.

## Cause

The good candidate was shipped and confirmed on 1.5.0; the unsafe one was refused and the robot stayed on 1.4.2, and its day on 1.4.2 had 3 delivered, 2 stopped and 0 bumps, the same as the day on 1.5.0 with the same policy.

## Fix

Put the version and the gate in the first line of every report.

```cpp
out << "Rover " << version;
```
