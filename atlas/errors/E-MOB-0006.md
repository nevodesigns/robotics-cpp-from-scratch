## Symptom

The grasp works when the base stops at the planned place and not when it stops a little short or long.

## Cause

Only the ideal place was checked; the arm reaches a ring with a tolerance of 36.0 cm each way.

## Fix

Work out the tolerance from the ring and make the base's stopping accuracy meet it with room to spare.

```cpp
park_tolerance(ring);
```
