## Symptom

The exploration rate is raised and the learner still does not find the better solution; it only behaves worse while learning.

## Cause

Finding a reward at the end of a long sequence by random moves needs that whole sequence to come up by chance, and the chance shrinks with every step of it. With half the moves random the learner reached the large goal 27 times, too seldom to learn it was better, and its greedy policy still returned 1.

## Fix

Make exploration systematic (optimistic values, visit-count bonuses) rather than more random, and shorten the way where the task allows (a shaped reward, 39-05).

```cpp
const double q0 = 20.0;   // above anything the task can return
```

More randomness is paid for in every episode; systematic exploration is paid for once per move.
