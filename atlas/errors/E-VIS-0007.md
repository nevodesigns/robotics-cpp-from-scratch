id: E-VIS-0007
title: A point behind the camera that appears in front of it
match: a point at or behind the camera does not appear, instead of appearing mirrored
match: expected this to be true: !?visible
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 15-10-where-a-pixel-points
---

## Symptom

A projected map or a set of waypoints overlaid on a camera image has a few points in places that make no sense, mirrored
across the middle of the picture, jumping across it as the robot turns. They are the points the robot has already passed, or
that are beside it, and they appear as if they were ahead.

## Cause

Projection divides by the depth, `u = fx * X / Z + cx`. For a point behind the camera the depth is negative, and the
division gives a perfectly good pixel: the mirror image of the point the same distance in front. Nothing in the arithmetic says
it is wrong. At exactly zero the division is by nothing, and the result is infinity or not a number, which then goes into a
drawing call.

## Fix

A point at or behind the camera does not appear. Decide that before dividing, and say so:

```cpp
visible = Z > 0.0;
if (!visible) return p;
```

and make the caller look at `visible`. A pixel for a point that is not visible is not a coordinate, and the function gives
the default one and a flag so that it cannot be mistaken for one. The test checks a depth of zero, a negative depth, and that
the same point two metres in front is visible.
