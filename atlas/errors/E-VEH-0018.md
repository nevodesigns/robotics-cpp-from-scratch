## Symptom

Stronger motors are fitted and the robot accelerates as before.

## Cause

On gravel the grip is the limit and the motors are not: the motors could climb 25.6 degrees where the gravel holds 22.3.

## Fix

Find which limit binds on the floors in use before spending on either.

```cpp
const Achievable a = achievable_accel(drive_train, cell, heading, speed);  // a.binding
```
