id: E-NAV-0041
title: Appearances and vanishings counted as one thing
match: a scan of the map as it is agrees with it everywhere, and counts nothing as new or gone
match: a door that has closed is a wall that appeared, and a door that has opened is a wall that vanished
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 16-15-the-door-that-closed
---

## Symptom

The robot notices that a door closed and never notices that one opened, or the reverse, and plans through the second kind of change.

## Cause

A wall that is not on the map ends a beam in free space, and a wall that is on the map but gone lets a beam pass through it. They are different counts: a closed door gave 82 agree, 9 appeared and 0 vanished, an opened one 80 agree, 0 appeared and 11 vanished, and a scan of the map as it is gave 82, 0 and 0.

## Fix

Count each separately.

```cpp
if (crossed) ++s.vanished; else if (all_free_around_end) ++s.appeared; else if (occupied_near) ++s.agree;
```
