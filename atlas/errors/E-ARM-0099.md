match: the follower carried on after the leader was unplugged
match: when the link is lost a follower with a watchdog holds, and one without goes on to the last message

## Symptom

The link is lost and the follower keeps moving, to where the leader used to be.

## Cause

There was no watchdog. With no new message the follower keeps going toward the last one, which was where the leader was a moment ago, not where it is: it went 0.398 rad on.

## Fix

Hold the follower when no message has come within a timeout, and say so. With a watchdog it moved 0.000 rad after the timeout.

```cpp
if (rc::arm::link_alive(now, last_rx, 0.2)) q = rc::arm::follower_step(q, target, 0.05, vmax, dt);
else hold_and_report();
```
