## Symptom

The robot does something its current state should not allow: it starts to walk while still rising, tries to stand with hot knees, or rises with the link to the operator gone. Each one-off fix adds a condition somewhere else and the next combination breaks.

## Cause

Mode changes were made wherever they were asked for, with no single place that knew what each mode may and must not do. The lesson's machine has seven states (sit, rising, stand, walk, lowering, fallen, fault) and checks every transition: a sit becomes a rise only if the operator wants more than a sit and the battery is enough and the knees are under 0.8 of their limit and the link is alive. A day of sit, stand, walk, stand and sit went through 6 changes in order, rising in 0.9 s, and a robot with knees at 0.85 refused to rise.

## Fix

Route every request through one state machine, called every tick, and let a refusal be a state that does nothing.

```cpp
rc::legged::Dog dog;
rc::legged::DogIn in;
in.want = rc::legged::kWantWalk;
in.heat = knee_heat_share;     // over its limit: 1.0 is the limit
in.link_ok = link_alive;
rc::legged::dog_step(dog, in, dt);   // dog.s is the only place the mode lives
```
