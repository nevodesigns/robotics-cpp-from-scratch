## Symptom

The ground plane tilts towards a cluster of obstacles, and some of the ground is classed as an obstacle.

## Cause

Every point voted equally. With 450 points on boxes among 2000 on the ground the tilt was 6.57 degrees against 5.00.

## Fix

Find the plane that most points agree on, and do not let the others vote.

```cpp
const RansacResult r = fit_plane_ransac(points, 100, 0.03, 30.0, seed);
```
