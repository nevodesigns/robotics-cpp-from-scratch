## Symptom

The belief becomes sharp and wrong after a long drive, because nothing in it ever grows less sure.

## Cause

Wheels slip, so each move spreads the belief: all of it at place 5, moved one place with slip, was 0.10 at 5, 0.80 at 6 and 0.10 at 7. Sensing sharpens it, and the net effect is a race: entropy went from 3.91 bits at the first door reading to 1.17 at step 14, with rises at steps 2 and 8 where a move widened it more than a wall reading narrowed it.

## Fix

Predict every step, with the real slip, and correct after.

```cpp
b.predict(1, 0.8, 0.1, 0.1);
b.correct(world, reading, 0.95, 0.05);
```
