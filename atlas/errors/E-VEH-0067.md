## Symptom

Localisation works when the robot starts where it was told, and never recovers when it does not.

## Cause

The cloud was started near a wrong pose and had no particles near the truth. A particle filter can only keep particles that exist.

## Fix

When the pose is not known, start over the whole map and let the evidence thin the cloud. Started over the whole room, 3000 particles found the robot in 5 steps.

```cpp
rc::locate::MonteCarlo mc(map, 3000, seed);
mc.init_uniform(x0, x1, y0, y1);
```
