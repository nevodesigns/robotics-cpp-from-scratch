## Symptom

The part fits between the fingers and the hand cannot hold it.

## Cause

Stroke was checked and force was not: a hand with a squeeze of up to 235 N holds at most 16.0 kg at a safety factor of 1.5.

## Fix

Check the width against the stroke and the weight against the force, at the acceleration of the move.

```cpp
if (!fits(hand, w) || required_force(hand, m, a, 1.5) > hand.f_max) refuse();
```
