## Symptom

The smoothed path rounds a corner by cutting through the obstacle on its inside. The corner is on the inside of a tight bend.

## Cause

The smoother knew only the points, not the grid. With no grid, 5 points ended inside the inflated obstacle.

Smoothing a corner moves its points toward the inside, which is where the obstacle is. With no check on the grid, nothing stops a point from entering it.

## Fix

Give the smoother the grid and refuse any move of a point into a cell that is inscribed or worse. With the check, 0 points were inside.

```cpp
if (rc::drive::cell_blocked(grid, nx, ny)) continue;   // keep the point where it was
```
