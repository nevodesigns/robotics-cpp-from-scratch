id: E-ARM-0110
title: A pose sent without checking the leg stroke
match: the hexapod's leg ran out of stroke
match: one leg hit its end stop on a tilt
match: the leg lengths at home are all equal for a symmetric hexapod, and a raise lengthens them
match: a raise and a tilt need a range of leg lengths
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 32-03-six-legs-and-a-table
---

## Symptom

One leg reaches the end of its travel as the platform tilts.

## Cause

The legs' lengths for the pose were never compared with their limits; a raise of 50 mm and a tilt of 10 degrees needed 0.366 to 0.410 m.

## Fix

Compute the six lengths for every pose on the path and check each against its stroke.

```cpp
stewart_lengths(hexapod, pose, len);
```
