## Symptom

A straight move that includes the vertical axis takes much longer than the horizontal axes would suggest.

## Cause

The path speed is limited by each axis's share of the direction, and different axes can set the speed and the acceleration: 0.900 s in a line against 0.825 s with each axis on its own.

## Fix

Work out the line's time from every axis's share, and keep vertical parts of a move short.

```cpp
straight_line_time(machine, d);
```
