## Symptom

The arm is sent to a set of angles that reach the pose and are outside its limits, or that need a swing right round from where it is.

## Cause

The first of the eight was taken. With limits of plus and minus 120 degrees only 1 were allowed.

## Fix

Filter the solutions by the limits, then pick the nearest to the present angles.

```cpp
within_limits_only(table, ik_spherical(table, target, base, tool));
```
