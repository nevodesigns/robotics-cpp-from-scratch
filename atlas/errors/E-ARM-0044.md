## Symptom

At the first moment of contact the force spikes far above what the arm settles to.

## Cause

A stiff controller meets the surface with its momentum and its stiffness: a peak of 68.6 N at 2000 N/m against 27.6 N at 200.

## Fix

Approach slowly, soften the impedance for the approach, and stiffen after contact.

```cpp
press(model, q0, target, tool, kz, dz, surface, seconds, dt);
```
