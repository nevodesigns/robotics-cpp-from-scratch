## Symptom

The robot gets up or walks when it should not: hot motors, a low battery, a lost link.

## Cause

Nothing checked before the change: with the knees at 0.85 of their limit and a low battery the machine refused to rise (1, 1).

## Fix

Check the battery, the heat and the link before every change that uses the legs.

```cpp
in.battery_ok && in.heat < 0.8 && in.link_ok
```
