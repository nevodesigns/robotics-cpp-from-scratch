## Symptom

The controller's predicted path turns tighter than the vehicle can, so anything that relies on the prediction (a stopping check, an obstacle beyond the next second) is wrong, though the tracking looks fine.

## Cause

The rollouts used a unicycle for a car. Tracking was hardly different (0.018 m against 0.047 m), because the plan is remade from the real pose every step, but 2 s of controls ended 0.67 m from where the car ended.

## Fix

Plug in the vehicle's own model, with its curvature limit, in the rollouts.

```cpp
MotionModel m; m.bicycle = true;
```
