## Symptom

At a low speed the joint moves in jerks, and every start and every change of direction is followed late.

## Cause

The controller supplied the friction through its error and integral, which have to wind up before the joint breaks free: starting a ramp of 0.03 rad/s the load was up to 0.91 mrad behind and took 0.32 s to come within 0.1 mrad, against 0.38 mrad and 0.06 s with the friction fed forward. (Once the ramp was under way the integral had found the friction and neither was off: 0.00000 rad.)

## Fix

Add the friction torque for the reference velocity (not the measured one) as a feedforward.

```cpp
c.friction_ff = true;
```
