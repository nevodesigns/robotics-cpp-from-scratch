## Symptom

The cloud is distorted at some speeds and fine at others, and a change of sensor mode changes it.

## Cause

The skew is speed times sweep time: 0.50 m at 5 m/s for 0.1 s, and a point 20 m away moves 2.00 m for a turn of 1 rad/s.

## Fix

Know the sweep time of the sensor, and work out the skew before deciding that deskewing is not needed.

```cpp
const double skew = skew_distance(max_speed, sweep_seconds);
```
