platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-02-a-rover-in-a-building
---

## Symptom

The rover drives perfectly in the empty building and nobody knows what it does with a person in it.

## Cause

With nobody in it the rover went through the door in 12.3 s, 9.6 m, 7 plans, no bumps, a top speed of 0.80 m/s and a closest approach of 0.18 m to a wall.

## Fix

Put something in the building in every test.

```cpp
run_delivery(building, {person}, start, goal, config, 120.0);
```
