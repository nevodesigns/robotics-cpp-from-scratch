## Symptom

A simulated robot driving a circle ends further from the centre than it began, and its loop does not close. Halving the time step halves the gap and never removes it.

## Cause

The step of lesson 00-05 moves the whole step along the heading the robot had at the start. A robot on a curve turns during the step, so it should have moved along a
heading that is part way round. Every step goes a little too far outward, and the errors add up in the same direction.

Measured on a quarter circle of radius 1 m, the miss in millimetres:

| steps | 10 | 20 | 40 | 80 | 160 | 320 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00-05 step | 111.110 | 55.541 | 27.769 | 13.884 | 6.942 | 3.471 |

The radius from the centre was bigger than 1 at every count, and 1111 steps were needed to be within a millimetre.

## Fix

Use a smaller step, which is slow, or a better step (the next two entries).
