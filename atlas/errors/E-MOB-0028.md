## Symptom

The job is released because the arm can lift the part, and it fails on the floor.

## Cause

The arm's rating is one of seven requirements: a job of 2 kg passed all of them, and one of 8 kg failed at 3 while the stopping and the parking were fine.

## Fix

Run every requirement against the job and release only if all pass.

```cpp
check_job(robot, job).released;
```
