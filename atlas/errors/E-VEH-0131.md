## Symptom

The vehicle lurches between speeds as a marginal sensor drops out and comes back.

## Cause

The mode followed the requirement. It changed 60 times raw in 120 s; with a dwell of 10 s and one step at a time the manager changed it 1 time.

## Fix

Go down at once, go up only after a dwell in which the requirement stayed better, and one step at a time.

```cpp
ModeManager manager(policy); manager.update(now, required);
```
