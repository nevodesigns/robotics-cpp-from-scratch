## Symptom

The base is sent to a place that is inside an obstacle, or is told to park though there is nowhere to.

## Cause

No place was free and the choice returned one anyway: 0 of 252 places were free in the test and the answer has to be none.

## Fix

Return found = false when nothing is clear and in reach, and let the caller relax the margin or give up.

```cpp
if (!choice.found) ask_for_help();
```
