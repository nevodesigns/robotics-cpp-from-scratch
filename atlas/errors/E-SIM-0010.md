## Symptom

A simulated encoder gives readings that are not on its resolution, or adding a delay to a test changes its noise and an unrelated test starts failing.

## Cause

A real sensor adds noise and then rounds, and the simulated one must too. A bias of 0.3 read 2.3000 with no spread, noise of 0.05 read a spread of 0.0502, a range held readings to -5 and 5, and a resolution of 0.1 gave only multiples of it with an rms error of 0.02885 (step over root twelve is 0.02887). With the random numbers drawn on every reading, a delayed sensor read exactly what the plain one had read two samples before.

## Fix

Apply the effects in one fixed order and draw the random numbers every time.

```cpp
const double n = gaussian();
const double lost = uniform();
```
