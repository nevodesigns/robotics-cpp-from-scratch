## Symptom

A delta picks fast and then cannot turn a part over or insert it at an angle. A part has to go in at an angle, or be turned over between pick and place.

## Cause

The delta's parallelograms keep the platform parallel to the base whatever the arms do. That gives three movements, x, y and z, and no tilt; it is what makes the delta light and simple, and it is a hard limit.

Three parallelograms make the platform translate only: whatever the three arms do, the platform stays parallel to the base. That constraint is what lets three light arms, driven from fixed motors, move fast; it also means no orientation can be commanded.

## Fix

Choose a delta for pick and place from above. For tilting work use a six axis arm, or a delta with a wrist on the platform, and count the wrist's mass and speed.

```cpp
rc::arm::TaskSpec task;
task.needs_tilt = true;
const rc::arm::Verdict v = rc::arm::check_family(robots, delta_family, task);   // v.tilt_ok false
```

A wrist on the platform adds mass where the delta is fastest, and costs some of its speed.
