## Symptom

The arm slows to a stop at each vertex of the path, or jerks as it passes it.

## Cause

Each vertex is a corner in joint space: the sharpest was 2.25 rad raw and 0.60 rad after cutting the corners.

## Fix

Cut the corners where there is room, and let the time parametrisation slow for the corners that are left.

```cpp
path = cut_corners(problem, path, 0.25, 2, problem.margin);
```
