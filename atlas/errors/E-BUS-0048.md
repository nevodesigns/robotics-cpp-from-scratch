id: E-BUS-0048
title: A count believed, and a replay out of step
match: a count that claims more messages than the file could hold is refused before anything is allocated for them
match: a player puts messages back at the pace they were made, none early and none twice, counted from the first
match: at twice the rate the same messages come in half the time, and at half the rate in twice
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-16-record-and-replay
---

## Symptom

Opening a small corrupt file uses all the memory, or a replay publishes everything at once at the start, or at the wrong pace.

## Cause

A count is input. A header claiming 4000000000 messages in 27 bytes was refused as too_many because each message takes at least 12 bytes. A player counts time from the first message and publishes each when it comes due: at rates of 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 the last message came at 2.00, 1.00, 0.50 and 0.25 s, none early and none twice.

## Fix

Bound the count by what the bytes could hold, and publish by due time.

```cpp
if (n > r.remaining() / 12) return BagStatus::too_many;
```
