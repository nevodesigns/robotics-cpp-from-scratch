## Symptom

A traversability map that is right on one floor is wrong on another.

## Cause

A slope of 24 degrees cost 198 on tarmac and 254 on gravel, because the grip differs.

## Fix

Build the limits from the surface at each place, or one map for each surface in use.

```cpp
const VehicleLimits lim = vehicle_limits(drive, surface_at(x, y), track, cg);
```
