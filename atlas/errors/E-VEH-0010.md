## Symptom

A tracked robot that the model says climbs a slope at a few percent slip slips and stalls on the real ground. The soil is loose sand or soft mud.

## Cause

The soil values were taken from a book. Thrust against slip depends on the soil's cohesion, friction angle and shear modulus: for the lesson's loose sand, half the largest thrust came at a slip of 10.0 percent, and a different soil gives a different curve.

A track's thrust comes from shearing the soil under it, and how much thrust it gets at a given slip depends on the soil's cohesion, friction angle and shear modulus. Values from a book are for a book's soil.

## Fix

Measure thrust against slip on the ground in use (a drawbar pull test) and fit the three numbers to it.

```cpp
rc::vehicle::Soil soil;   // fitted from a drawbar pull test, not copied
const double s = rc::vehicle::slip_for_thrust(soil, track, needed);
```
