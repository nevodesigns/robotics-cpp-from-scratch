## Symptom

Each time something blocks the path, the vehicle stops and waits for the planner. The planner takes about as long as the vehicle takes to reach the block.

## Cause

The block was only noticed 0.6 m ahead, and a plan takes 2 s at 1 m/s. The vehicle waited 2.1 s.

If a block is noticed only a short way ahead, the vehicle reaches it before the new plan arrives and must stop. Looking further ahead gives the planner its time.

## Fix

Check at least the speed times the planning time of the path ahead, and keep a short safety stop for the rest. With a look-ahead of 3 m the wait was 0.3 s.

```cpp
rc::drive::ReplanConfig cfg;
cfg.check_ahead = speed * planning_time + margin;
```
