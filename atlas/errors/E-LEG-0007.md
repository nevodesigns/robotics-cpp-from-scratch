## Symptom

The body's roll and pitch estimated from the feet jump at every step, or the robot thinks it is tilted whenever a leg is in the air.

## Cause

The swinging foot was included in the plane fitted through the feet. It is not on the ground, and putting it in pulls the plane up at its corner.

## Fix

Fit the plane through the stance feet only, and require three that are not in a line.

```cpp
const bool on_ground[4] = {in_stance(0), in_stance(1), in_stance(2), in_stance(3)};
const rc::legged::BodyPose p = rc::legged::body_pose_from_feet(feet, on_ground);
```
