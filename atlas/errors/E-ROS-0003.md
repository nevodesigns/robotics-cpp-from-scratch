id: E-ROS-0003
title: A node's timer armed at its first poll
match: a timer on the node fires once per period of the world's clock, and the component's own status goes out on it
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-17-the-seam-where-ros-plugs-in
---

## Symptom

A status message or a control tick is late by up to a whole period at start up, and only then.

## Cause

A timer made at time 0 and first looked at 0.6 s arms itself then and has lost its first 0.6 s. Armed when it is created, a 1.0 s timer fired 3 times in 3.5 s of the clock and the status read handled 1, handled 2, handled 3.

## Fix

Poll a new timer once as it is made.

```cpp
timers_.back().timer.poll(world_.clock);
```
