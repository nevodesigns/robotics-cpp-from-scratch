## Symptom

A sum or mean over fractional values comes back with the fractions missing. Two
temperatures of 20.5 and 21.5 average to 20 rather than 21.

## Cause

std::accumulate takes the type of its accumulator from the initial value. Passing
0 asks for an integer accumulator, so every addition converts to int and discards
the fraction before the next one. The result is not rounded once at the end; it
is truncated at every step.

The compiler says nothing, because integer addition is a perfectly reasonable
thing to have asked for.

## Fix

Give it a starting value of the type you want to accumulate in:

```cpp
std::accumulate(readings.begin(), readings.end(), 0.0, add_celsius);
```

One character. The same rule applies to any fold with an explicit initial value,
and it is worth reading every accumulate in a codebase for this once.

This is the same family as E-NUM-0001: the arithmetic is doing exactly what was
written, and what was written is not what was meant.
