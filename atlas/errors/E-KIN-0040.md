title: Joint limits applied once at the end, or not at all
match: with a limit on the elbow only one of the two solutions is found, and with limits that allow neither it stops where the limits stop it

## Symptom

The solver finds angles that put the tool on the pose and the arm cannot take them, or a joint is driven past its stop by a path that went through an angle outside its range.

## Cause

A limit applied only at the end lets the solver wander outside it and come back to something that is not the solution it found. The angle has to be held at each step so that the next step starts from a pose the arm can be in. Measured: with the elbow limited to bend one way the pose was reached from **5** of 5 starts with elbow **2.55128**, and the other way **-2.55128**; with a limit of 0.05 rad the angle held at every step and the solver did not converge.

## Fix

Clamp after every step:

```cpp
r.q[c] += step.dq[c];
if (options.use_limits) r.q[c] = std::max(options.lo[c], std::min(options.hi[c], r.q[c]));
```
