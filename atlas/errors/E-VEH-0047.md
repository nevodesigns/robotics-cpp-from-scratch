## Symptom

The cloud is distorted at some speeds and fine at others, and a change of sensor mode changes it. The sensor was set to a slower rotation for more points.

## Cause

Nobody checked the sweep time. The skew is the speed times the sweep time: 0.50 m at 5 m/s for 0.1 s, and a point 20 m away moves 2.00 m for a turn of 1 rad/s.

The skew is the distance the vehicle moves during a sweep. A slower sweep, or a faster vehicle, makes it larger, and far points move more in a turn because of the lever.

## Fix

Know the sensor's sweep time, and work out the skew before deciding that deskewing is not needed.

```cpp
const double skew = rc::perceive::skew_distance(max_speed, sweep_seconds);
```
