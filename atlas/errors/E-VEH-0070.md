## Symptom

A route with fixes every 100 m has a robot that is several metres out before each one.

## Cause

The error of dead reckoning does not grow in proportion to the distance. It was 0.22 m after 10 m and 7.07 m after 100: 31.6 times for ten times the distance.

## Fix

Work out the bound for the odometry noise and choose the spacing of the fixes from it.

```cpp
const double d = distance_to_bound(limit);  // how far before a fix is needed
```
