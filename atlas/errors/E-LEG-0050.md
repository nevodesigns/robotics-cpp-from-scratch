## Symptom

Falls break servos or shells although they are not from a great height.

## Cause

The legs were stiff when the body landed: 1118 N over 0.02 m, against 224 N over 0.10 m with soft legs (5 times less).

## Fix

Cut the joint stiffness, keeping some damping, when a fall cannot be stopped.

```cpp
if (act == kFallSoft) set_stiffness(0.1);
```
