## Symptom

A category that was switched on for a test is silent, or one that should be quiet is not, after a rule was added to the end of a list of rules.

## Cause

Rules are tried in order and the last that matches decides. A general rule after a specific one overrides it. Measured: `*.debug=false` then `station.link.debug=true` gave **1** and **0**; the other way round gave **0** and **0**.

## Fix

Write the general rules first and the specific ones after:

```
*.debug=false
station.link.debug=true
```
