title: A battery check made only at the start
match: a round trip costs twice the distance, and the mission starts only if the battery covers it and the reserve, and turns back only when what is left would not get it home
match: with enough battery the whole mission is done, and with too little it is refused before the robot moves
match: a robot that is using more than it expects turns back in time if it checks on the way, and one that checks only at the start is stranded with the road ahead of it
match: over seventy two worlds the robot is never beyond the road and always ends in a state somebody can name, and a margin on the rate turns every stranding into a refusal
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-07-a-delivery-not-a-route

## Symptom

The robot delivers the parcel and stops on the road on the way back with a flat battery.

## Cause

At 0.1 Wh/m the round trip of 100 m needed 25 Wh with the reserve and the robot refused to start on 24.9. With 26 Wh and 0.15 Wh/m used, the robot that checked on the way got home with 0.79 Wh and the other was stranded 26.7 m out. Over 72 worlds, planning with the expected rate stranded 5 and delivered 30, 1.5 times it stranded 0 and delivered 15, and no check stranded 10.

## Fix

Check on the way, with a margin, and choose the margin on purpose.

```cpp
must_turn_back(battery, position, expected * margin, reserve);
```
