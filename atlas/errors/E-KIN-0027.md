## Symptom

An arm is told to go to a point that is well inside its reach and says it cannot, or the planner promises a pose that no angles give.

## Cause

With a heading, what the first two links have to reach is the wrist and not the tool, and the wrist is a link length away from where the tool is, in the direction opposite the heading. Measured: (2.9, 0) with links 1, 1, 1 was reachable pointing along x and `TooFar` pointing back, though the total reach is 3.

## Fix

Test the wrist:

```cpp
const double wx = target.x - l3 * std::cos(target.phi);
const double wy = target.y - l3 * std::sin(target.phi);
rc::kin::solve(wx, wy, l1, l2);
```
