## Symptom

The interface updates from a function called by the work itself, and widgets are touched by two threads: the program crashes now and then, or draws wrongly.

## Cause

A callback called by the worker runs on the worker. A `QFutureWatcher` lives on the thread that made it, and its signals are delivered there. Measured: the result and every progress value arrived on the thread that asked, in order, ending at **20**; a second start while one ran was refused.

## Fix

Take the result from a watcher and not from a callback, and refuse a second job:

```cpp
if (running_) return false;
connect(&watcher_, &QFutureWatcher<int>::finished, this, &PlanRunner::on_finished);
```
