title: Reliable chosen for every topic
match: over a link that loses a fifth of what it carries, sending once loses a fifth of the messages and retransmitting loses none

## Symptom

A wireless link to the robot is saturated, scans arrive late and stale, and everything slows down when the signal gets weak.

## Cause

Reliability is paid for in traffic. With 20 percent loss each way, 500 messages sent once gave 398 arrivals at 1.00 packets per message, and acknowledged and resent gave 500 in order at 1.79 packets per message. Spent on a stream where the next message replaces the last, the extra traffic makes the loss worse for everything sharing the link.

## Fix

Use reliable for commands and parameters, and best effort for streams that replace themselves.

```cpp
ReliableSender commands(10, 120);   // a gripper command
// scans: send once
```
