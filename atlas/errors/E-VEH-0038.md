## Symptom

A path that is clear on the map scrapes a wall or a post.

## Cause

The map had the obstacles and not the vehicle. Inflation by the inscribed radius gave a cost of 253 inside 0.25 m of a post.

## Fix

Inflate by the vehicle's inscribed radius and plan the centre through cells below 253.

```cpp
inflate(master, robot_radius, inflation_radius, scale);
```
