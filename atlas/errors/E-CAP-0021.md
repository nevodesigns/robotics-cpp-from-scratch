title: A pause that restarts or counts as waiting
match: a stop by the supervisor holds the mission where it is and the mission goes on from there, and one that never ends leaves it held for ever
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

After a lost link the robot drives back to the start of its road, or turns back as soon as the link returns, as if the pause were a blockage.

## Cause

A link lost from 30 s to 60 s held the mission for 31.0 s: it finished at 435 s against 404 s, with the battery 0.01 Wh the same. With the link lost for ever it was held at 14.0 m for 570 of 600 s.

## Fix

Keep the state, the position and the timers, and count the pause apart from waiting.

```cpp
held_time_ += dt;
```
