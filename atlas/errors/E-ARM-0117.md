## Symptom

The robot with the largest reach is bought and does the job slowly, or takes too much floor.

## Cause

Volume left out the shape of the work and everything else: 1.731 m3 for the arm against 0.084 m3 for the SCARA, which does flat work faster.

## Fix

Choose from the task (its shape, payload, cycle time and accuracy), with the volume as a check and not the criterion.

```text
the capstone of this phase chooses from the task
```
