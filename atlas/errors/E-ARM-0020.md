## Symptom

A seven joint arm sits against a joint limit and its extra joint is never used. The arm reports a joint limit while the tool is in an easy place.

## Cause

Nothing used the freedom. The seventh joint gives a family of poses for each tool pose; without a secondary goal the solver picks one and stays there.

For each tool pose a seven joint arm has a line of joint poses, and an inverse kinematics with nothing else to do picks one and stays. If that one is near a limit, the arm stays near the limit.

## Fix

Use the null space to steer the joints toward the middle of their limits (or away from a singularity), at a rate the joints can take. With it the margin rose from 0.086 to 0.156 over 6 s with the tool held; without it, it stayed at 0.086.

```cpp
const rc::arm::SelfMotion s = rc::arm::self_motion_run(table, q, base, tool, k, kp, 6.0, dt, lambda);
```
