## Symptom

The estimate of the position is used as if it were exact, and nothing says when it should not be.

## Cause

Only the pose was kept. The covariance, carried by the odometry's Jacobians, gave a radius of 7.07 m after 100 m, and a simulation of 400 runs gave 5.10 m.

## Fix

Carry the covariance with the pose and expose the radius to everything that uses the pose.

```cpp
const double radius = dr.radius95();
```
