## Symptom

The robot starts to recover, then goes soft part way through the fall, with the worst of both.

## Cause

The decision was made again every tick as the time to the ground fell; the quick fall had 0.22 s and was a soft case from the start (2).

## Fix

Decide once, early, from the time to the ground, and hold the decision.

```cpp
fall_action(theta, omega, h, t_need, warn, warn_rate, fallen);
```
