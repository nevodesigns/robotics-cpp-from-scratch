## Symptom

The map is clearly out of date, the detector never fires, and the robot plans through a wall until the map slowly turns into one.

## Cause

After one scan of the closed door the doorway cells have one hit against two misses, no longer free but unknown, and a beam that ends in unknown cells is not an appearance. The count falls before the run reaches three. Against the map as it was, the closed door was noticed at scan 3; against the map being repaired, never, while the repaired map's doorway cell became a wall at scan 11.

## Fix

Keep a copy of the map as it was and compare with that until the change is decided.

```cpp
detector.update(compare_scan(reference, beams, 6.0, pose));
```
