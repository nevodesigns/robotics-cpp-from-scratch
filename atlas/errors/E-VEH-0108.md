id: E-VEH-0108
title: A risk that is only blocked or free
match: the planner crosses a risky patch that it could go round
match: the planner treats risky ground like safe ground
match: a patch of risk that is not blocked is crossed unless risk has a weight
match: blocked cells are never crossed, and a goal that cannot be reached is not found
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-09-planning-over-rough-ground
---

## Symptom

The path goes over loose or rocky ground that is not blocked, when firm ground was a short way round.

## Cause

Only blocked cells were avoided. The patch of cost 200 was crossed at a worst cell cost of 200; with a weight on risk, 0.

## Fix

Add a weight on the cell's cost over 255 to the price of a step, and keep blocked cells as a hard no.

```cpp
w.risk = 5.0;
```
