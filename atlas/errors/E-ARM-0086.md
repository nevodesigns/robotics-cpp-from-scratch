## Symptom

One noisy reading of the motor current stops the arm.

## Cause

Contact was declared on one tick over the threshold. Noise reaches the threshold now and then; a filter and a count of ticks in a row are what make the signal believable.

## Fix

Filter the residual and require it to stay over the threshold for several ticks.

```cpp
rc::arm::ContactDetector det;
det.alpha = 0.2;   // the share of the new residual
det.need = 5;      // ticks in a row
```

Every tick of the count is delay, which 31-04 measures in the force the wall reaches; choose the count with that in view.
