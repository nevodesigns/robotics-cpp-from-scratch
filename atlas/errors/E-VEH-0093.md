## Symptom

The vehicle takes the inside of a corner of the path and touches what the planner went round.

## Cause

The lookahead cuts corners. At a corner of 90 degrees the closest the car came to the corner point was 0.38 m on the raw path and 0.41 m on the smoothed one.

## Fix

Leave clearance in the plan for the cut, or inflate the grid by the cut of the follower, and check the traced path against the grid.

```cpp
inflate(grid, robot_radius + follower_cut, ...);
```
