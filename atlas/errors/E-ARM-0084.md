title: A degree of tilt and a long reach
match: the error grows with the distance from the camera
match: the pick is right near the base and wrong at the far side
match: a small tilt in the rotation is a larger error at the end of a reach

## Symptom

Picks are right near one part of the cell and more and more wrong farther from it.

## Cause

The calibration's rotation was a little out, and a rotation error is multiplied by the distance: 1 degree put a part 10.5 mm out at 0.6 m. A millimetre of noise in a point is a millimetre; a degree in the rotation is ten.

## Fix

Calibrate with points over the whole working volume, and check the error at the far corners, not only in the middle where the calibration points were.

```cpp
for (const Check& c : corner_checks) {   // points measured at the far corners
  double seen[3];
  rc::arm::camera_to_base(f.t, c.cam, seen);
  if (std::hypot(seen[0] - c.base[0], seen[1] - c.base[1], seen[2] - c.base[2]) > tol) recalibrate();
}
```
