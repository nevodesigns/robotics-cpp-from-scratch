match: the mission restarts from the beginning after a power cycle
match: restoring saved progress goes on from the leg, where starting again drives the first legs twice

## Symptom

After a restart in the middle of the job, the vehicle drives the first waypoints again. A brown-out or a watchdog reset half way through is the usual cause.

## Cause

Nothing was saved. The mission's progress lived only in memory, so a reboot started it from the top. After a restart at the third of four legs the vehicle drove 19.4 m with the saved progress and 38.4 m without.

Progress held only in memory is lost with power. A vehicle that restarts knows its list of legs and nothing about which are done, so it begins again.

## Fix

Save the leg number and the results as text after every leg, restore them at start, and refuse text that does not fit the list of legs.

```cpp
store.write(mission.save());
if (!mission.restore(store.read())) start_from_the_top_and_say_so();
```

A restored leg starts its timer afresh, which is what you want: the time spent rebooting is not the leg's.

Saving after every leg costs one small write; the restore must check that the saved text matches the mission, or a stale save restores the wrong progress.
