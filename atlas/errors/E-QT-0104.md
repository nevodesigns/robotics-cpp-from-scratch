id: E-QT-0104
title: A long job that cannot be stopped, or a runner that is gone before its job
match: cancel stops a plan of a second long before the end, and says it was cancelled and not finished
match: a runner that is destroyed while its plan is running waits for it, and nothing is delivered after
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 12-04-the-plan-that-froze-the-window
---

## Symptom

The Stop button does nothing for ten seconds, or the program crashes on exit, in a thread that was still running when the window closed.

## Cause

A cancel is a request, and work that does not check it does not stop. A watcher that is destroyed while its future runs is a future that delivers into nothing. Measured: a plan of a second cancelled after 50 ms was over after **55** ms with **1** cancellation and **0** finished; a runner destroyed 30 ms into a plan delivered **0** results afterwards.

## Fix

Check at every step, and cancel and wait when the owner goes:

```cpp
if (promise.isCanceled()) return;
// ...
~PlanRunner() { watcher_.cancel(); watcher_.waitForFinished(); }
```
