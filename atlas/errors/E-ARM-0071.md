id: E-ARM-0071
title: An object trusted for ever where it was last seen
match: the arm hit a person who had stepped out of the camera's view
match: the planner assumes the box is where it was last seen
match: an object not seen for a while is treated as bigger by how far it could have gone, up to a cap
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 30-07-a-world-that-changes
---

## Symptom

The arm collides with something that was last seen a while ago and has moved since. The object was seen once at the start and not since.

## Cause

The object was taken to be where it was last seen, for ever. The longer since it was seen, the farther it may have gone.

An object's place is known only at the moment it was seen. Since then it may have moved by up to its speed times the time elapsed. Taking it as fixed is a guess that gets worse every second.

## Fix

Give every object the time it was last seen and a speed it might move at, and grow it by their product, up to a cap. An object seen 2.0 s ago at 0.5 m/s grew by 0.50 m, and a pose that was 0.31 m clear of a slower one was 0.11 m clear of it.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Scene now_scene = rc::arm::scene_at(scene, now, cap);
```
