## Symptom

A planner using the geometry of the wheels turns the robot less than it asks.

## Cause

Fixed wheels scrub, and the turn is smaller by the ICR coefficient. A skid steer of 1.4 and a differential drive with an efficiency of 1 over 1.4 gave the same path.

## Fix

Use the ICR coefficient, or the equivalent turn efficiency, measured on the floor in use.

```cpp
DiffSlip model(r, track, slip, 1.0 / chi);
```
