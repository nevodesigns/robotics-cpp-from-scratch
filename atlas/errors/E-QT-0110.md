## Symptom

A window that shows warnings and worse is full of information messages. Or a log pane is complete for a minute and then silently loses its oldest lines, and nobody knows that anything was lost.

## Cause

`QtInfoMsg` is 4, above `QtFatalMsg` (3), so `type >= QtWarningMsg` includes it. And a bounded log has to say what it dropped. Measured: at least a warning was **2** by severity and **3** by the numbers; **5000** messages into a ring of 1000 kept **1000** and counted **4000** dropped.

## Fix

Map the type to an order of your own, and count what is dropped:

```cpp
case QtInfoMsg: return 1;
// ...
if (entries_.size() > capacity_) { entries_.pop_front(); ++dropped_; }
```
