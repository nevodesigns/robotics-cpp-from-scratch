id: E-VEH-0132
title: Dead reckoning with no limit
match: the vehicle drove for a kilometre with no GNSS and got lost
match: the position uncertainty grew and nothing slowed the vehicle
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 27-07-when-something-stops-working
---

## Symptom

After the GNSS is lost the vehicle goes on at full speed until it is lost.

## Cause

Nothing tied the speed to the growing sigma. It reaches 1.5 m after 468 m of dead reckoning.

## Fix

Make the sigma part of the requirement (reduced, creep and stop at 0.3, 0.8 and 1.5 m) so the vehicle slows before it is lost.

```cpp
h.sigma_m = current_sigma; required_mode(h, policy);
```
