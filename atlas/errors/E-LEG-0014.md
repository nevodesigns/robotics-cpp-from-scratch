## Symptom

At a faster gait the steps sag and lag: the foot does not reach the height it was told to and drags.

## Cause

The step asked more of the servos than they have. Over a swing of 0.25 s the joints needed up to 4.7 rad/s; over 0.125 s, 9.4 rad/s, more than the 8 rad/s a typical hobby servo gives. The rate is inversely proportional to the swing time.

## Fix

Check the joint rates of a swing against the servos before using it, and lengthen the swing or lower the step until it fits.

```cpp
double peak = 0.0;
if (!rc::legged::swing_peak_joint_rate(leg, p0, p1, 0.05, t_sw, -1, &peak) || peak > 8.0) lengthen_swing();
```

Doubling the swing time halves the speed and quarters the acceleration.
