## Symptom

Smooth goals give a joint that moves in tiny jumps, and the tool in jumps of about a millimetre. The tool's motion is jerky in a smooth plan.

## Cause

The servo has 4096 steps a turn: 0.00153 rad each, 1.07 mm at 0.7 m. A goal finer than that is rounded to the nearest step.

A servo that counts 4096 steps a turn can only be at those steps. A goal between two is rounded to one, and a slowly changing goal moves in steps, each a millimetre at the end of a long arm.

## Fix

Plan with the step in mind: give every joint a tolerance of at least a step, and do not expect smoother motion than the encoder allows.

```cpp
const double sent = rc::arm::quantise(angle, 4096);
```
