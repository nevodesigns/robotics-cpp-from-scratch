id: E-SIM-0025
title: A command or a measurement taken as the present because it arrived last
match: the emulator acts on the newest command and holds it, and one that was overtaken on the way is ignored
match: the controller keeps the newest measurement by the emulator's tick, and counts those that were overtaken or repeated
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-09-the-bench-link
---

## Symptom

The plant jerks backwards for a step on a good link, or a controller acts on a position older than the one it already had, and the log shows nothing wrong.

## Cause

Frames overtake each other and a late frame is not the present. Commands 2 then 1 arriving in that order: the plant used command 2, position 5.520, and 1 was ignored. Measurements 5, 7, 6, 7 gave 2 kept and 2 refused. With delays of 1 to 4 ticks 69 measurements arrived older than one already seen, and the information acted on was 2.17 ticks old with the check and 2.42 without.

## Fix

Number every frame and refuse one that is not newer than what is held.

```cpp
if (have_ && m.tick <= newest_tick_) { ++stale_; return false; }
```
