title: A base's rate taken as free to use
match: the base jerked back and forth while the arm stayed still
match: the base moved a great deal for a small hand motion
match: the rates give the hand its velocity, and a dearer base moves less
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 33-04-the-base-and-the-arm-as-one

## Symptom

The base wobbles to and fro to give the hand small motions the arm could have made.

## Cause

No price was put on the base's rates: with a cost of 1 the base drove 1.43 m and at a cost 30 times higher 1.20 m.

## Fix

Weight the base's rates in the least cost solution so that the arm does the small motions.

```cpp
wb_rates(j, xi, w_base, lambda);
```
