id: E-ROS-0011
title: A cascade restarted, and a restart retried for ever
match: a sensor that cannot be restarted is restarted the allowed number of times, then given up on, and the graph is taken down in reverse
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-20-a-graph-that-looks-after-itself
---

## Symptom

One loose cable makes the whole graph restart over and over, the log is full of stalls from every node, and the real fault is the first line of it.

## Cause

Silence spreads downstream. The viewer, fed from the stalled sensor, was reported starved 1 time and restarted 0 (and starved at every stall in the failing case), so the restart went to the cause. A restart that fails is noticed one deadline later: 5.17 stalled, restart_failed, 5.43 stalled, restart_failed, 5.69 stalled, gave_up, 0.69 s after the stall, with the allowed 2 restarts, and the graph taken down in 8 transitions.

## Fix

Tell each node what it listens to, limit the restarts, and give up.

```cpp
supervisor.watch("viewer", viewer_life, 0.25, false, {"sensor"});
```
