## Symptom

When the operator lets go or the link drops, the vehicle goes on at the last speed. The link was lost at the edge of radio range.

## Cause

The last request was held for ever. A teleoperated vehicle must assume that silence means stop.

A vehicle that holds the last request treats silence as consent to carry on. On a remote link, silence is the most likely failure, and it should mean stop.

## Fix

Treat a request older than a timeout as zero, ramp down at the braking limit, and count the timeout in the stopping distance: the vehicle went 1.99 m after the commands stopped with a timeout of 0.5 s, and 4.99 m with 2.0 s.

```cpp
if (t - last_request > timeout) target = 0.0;
```
