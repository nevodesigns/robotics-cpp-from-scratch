## Symptom

The robot stops in the middle of a mission and nobody can tell why, or it keeps trying for ever.

## Cause

The mission stopped with no reason, or did not stop at all. Each way it can fail should end in a named fault: 1 no part after 3 tries, 2 the part fell (seen at once from the gap while driving), 3 a step took too long, 4 the battery is not enough for the mission with a margin of 1.2 (4.5 Wh against 4.0 needed was refused at the start).

## Fix

Give every failure a fault code, every step a time limit, and check the battery before starting.

```cpp
if (m.s == rc::mobile::kMFault) report(m.fault);   // 1 no part, 2 fell, 3 took too long, 4 the battery
```
