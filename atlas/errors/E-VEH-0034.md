## Symptom

Objects are found well at the range of the test and fall apart or merge at others.

## Cause

No fixed radius suits all ranges. In the scene a radius of 0.02 m found 0 objects, 0.2 m found 4, 0.6 m found 3 and 5 m found 1.

## Fix

Make the radius a function of the range at which the points were seen.

```cpp
cluster_points(points, base, gain, min_points);
```
