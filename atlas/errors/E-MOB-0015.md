## Symptom

The base turns well with the arm forward and rolls over with the arm out to the side.

## Cause

The base is narrower than it is long; the arm to one side cut the limit on turning towards it to 4.22 m/s2 from 7.92 m/s2.

## Fix

Check the side limits, and tuck the arm (or slow the turn) before turning.

```cpp
tip_limits(base, com, hw, ht).left_turn;
```
