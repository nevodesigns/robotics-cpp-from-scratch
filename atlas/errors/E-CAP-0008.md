title: A speed limit that is a target and not a wall
match: a limit on the speed of the tip costs a little at 0.8 m/s and a lot at 0.25, and the limit is held with a margin that the ramp makes
match: somebody stands at the pick point, and the arm holds short of them where an arm that does not look carries on to 8 cm and for 66 ticks is in contact
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-03-an-arm-that-picks-and-places
---

## Symptom

The arm is certified at 0.25 m/s and a measurement shows 0.30, though the code has the limit.

## Cause

A limit of 0.25 m/s gave a first cycle of 7.63 s (against 4.10 with none) and a tip speed of 0.30; 0.8 m/s gave 4.28 s and exactly 0.80. With somebody 8 cm from the pick point the monitored arm came no closer than 0.150 m with 0 ticks in contact, the unmonitored one 0.077 m with 66.

## Fix

Measure the fastest speed reached, and put the margin into the limit.

```cpp
config.tip_limit = certified_limit / 1.25;
```
