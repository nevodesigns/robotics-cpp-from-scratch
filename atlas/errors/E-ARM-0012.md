## Symptom

The arm is sent to angles that reach the pose and are outside its limits, or that need a swing right round from where it is.

## Cause

The first of the eight solutions was taken. With limits of plus and minus 120 degrees only 1 of them was allowed for the lesson's pose.

## Fix

Filter the solutions by the limits, then choose the one nearest the present angles.

```cpp
const auto ok = rc::arm::within_limits_only(table, rc::arm::ik_spherical(table, target, base, tool));
const auto& best = nearest(ok, q_now);
```
