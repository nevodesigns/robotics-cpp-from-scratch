## Symptom

A robot that does 1.7 m/s on the flat does little more than 1 on a hill.

## Cause

The top speed ignored the slope. It is where the drive force equals the resistance, and a slope adds to the resistance: 0.99 m/s on 10 degrees, against 1.67 on the level.

## Fix

Work out the top speed from the terrain under the vehicle, and plan with it.

```cpp
const double v = rc::vehicle::top_speed(drive_train, cell, heading);
```
