## Symptom

The job check's report is a page of figures and a red result, and the reader cannot see what to change.

## Cause

All requirements were listed with equal weight. The first in the order that fails is the one to deal with: a shelf ringed by obstacles failed at 1, a base stopping to 15 cm at 2, 8 kg at 3, a camera of 12 mm at 4, a route of 30000 m at 5, a fast route with a load at 6, a person 0.45 m from the route at 7.

## Fix

Report the first failure by number and keep the figures of the others.

```cpp
switch (v.first_fail) {
  case 3: report("payload: budget " + std::to_string(v.budget) + " kg"); break;
  default: break;
}
```
