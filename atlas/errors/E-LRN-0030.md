## Symptom

The computed policy is provably optimal and still does something the owner of the robot would never accept.

## Cause

It is optimal for the task as written: one number, the expected discounted return. Anything not in that number (how often it falls, how close it goes to a person, wear) is worth nothing to it, and it will trade any amount of it for a little return. The policy for gamma 0.95 fell into a pit on more than half of its runs because the average said so.

## Fix

Write what must not happen as a constraint the policy has to satisfy, and check it separately from the return.

```cpp
if (stats.goal_share < required_share) reject(policy);
```

Optimal means best for the numbers given; the numbers are the specification, and 39-05 shows how easily they say the wrong thing.
