## Symptom

Given the same speed on both tracks, the robot curves away on uneven ground. It is on uneven or mixed ground.

## Cause

The heading was held by symmetry. The slips differ from side to side: slips of 10 and 20 percent turned a straight command by 44.1 degrees in 10 s.

Equal track speeds give a straight line only if both tracks slip equally. On uneven ground they do not, and the vehicle turns toward the side that slips more, by an amount that grows with time.

## Fix

Hold the heading with feedback from a gyro or a compass, and treat the track command as a request.

```cpp
const double correction = heading_pid.update(target_heading, measured_heading);
```
