## Symptom

The arm goes through the whole pick and place and nothing was moved. The cell runs at full rate and the output tray stays empty.

## Cause

Each step ran because the one before had been commanded, not because it had worked. A pick and place is a sequence of steps each of which can fail, and a script that does not look carries on after the failure.

A sequence of commands assumes each one worked. The world disagrees often: a part is missing, the grasp slips, a move stops short. Without a check, the machine's idea of where it is in the task and the world's state come apart, and everything after is wasted.

## Fix

Write it as a state machine that, after every step that can fail, checks what was sensed before going on. A run that went to plan took 36 ticks of 0.1 s; one that closed on nothing and was given a fresh try took 48 ticks with 1 retry.

```cpp
if (rc::arm::holding(in.width, m.obj_width, m.tol)) rc::arm::enter(m, rc::arm::kPickLift);
else retry_or_fault(m);
```
