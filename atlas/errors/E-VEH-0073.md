## Symptom

After the robot is lifted and put down, its heading is off by a fixed amount for the rest of the run.

## Cause

It was taken for still because its wheels had stopped. While it was carried the gyro read real turns, which were taken for the bias. The wheels alone said still for 176 of 200 samples of the carry.

## Fix

Also require the accelerometer to be quiet over a window. With it, 0 samples of the carry were taken as still.

```cpp
rc::locate::StillnessDetector still(window, wheel_limit, accel_limit);
const bool s = still.update(left, right, accel_magnitude);
```
