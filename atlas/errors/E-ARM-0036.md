## Symptom

After a large move the joint overshoots by a lot and takes a long time to settle.

## Cause

The torque sat at its limit and the integral term went on adding the error: 186 percent overshoot, against 19.0 with the integral held.

## Fix

Hold (or back-calculate) the integral while the output is at its limit.

```cpp
if (sat == tau_cmd) integ += ki * error * dt;
```
