## Symptom

A good localisation is thrown off now and then by random particles it did not need. A person stood in front of the scanner.

## Cause

A monitor with no memory reacts to every bad scan, such as one blocked by a person.

One scan that fits poorly is evidence of a passing obstacle as often as of a lost robot. A monitor that reacts to every poor scan scatters particles away from a good estimate.

## Fix

Compare a fast average with a slow one, and cap the share put in at once. With two averages it put in 0.1 percent on average while the robot was where it should be, and the error was 0.006 m.

```cpp
return std::max(0.0, std::min(s_.max_share, 1.0 - fast_ / slow_));
```
