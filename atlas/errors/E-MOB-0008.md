## Symptom

The parking spot the planner picks seems arbitrary: sometimes far away, sometimes at the edge of reach, and nobody can say why. Small changes to the room change the choice a lot, and nobody can say whether a choice is good.

## Cause

The weights of the score were never chosen; they were whatever was typed first. They are the decision: margin, travel and clearance pull different ways. With travel weighted most the base stopped at the near edge after 1.24 m; with no weight on travel it went 2.56 m to the best place.

A score of several terms is a decision about what matters, and its weights are that decision. Left at whatever was typed first, they choose for reasons nobody holds: the planner may drive twice as far for a little margin, or hug the edge of the ring to save a metre.

## Fix

Choose the weights for the job and write down why.

```cpp
rc::mobile::ParkParams p;
p.w_margin = 1.0;   // room to stop short or long
p.w_travel = 0.3;   // per metre
p.w_clear = 0.5;    // up to a cap
```

Two or three tasks with known good places make a test of the weights.
