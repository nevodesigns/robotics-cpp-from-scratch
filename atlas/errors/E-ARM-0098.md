## Symptom

One wrong reading, or a jump in the leader, throws the follower across its range.

## Cause

The follower went to the target in a single tick. A lag with no speed limit passes every jump on as fast as the motors allow.

## Fix

Follow as a first order lag and clamp the velocity, whatever the target does. A jump of 1.0 rad was then followed at no more than 2.0 rad/s, in 0.48 s.

```cpp
q = rc::arm::follower_step(q, target, 0.05, 2.0, dt);
```

Rate and delay add to the error: with a time constant of 50 ms the rms error was 0.059 rad at 100 Hz, 0.081 at 20 Hz and 0.168 at 100 Hz with 100 ms of delay.
