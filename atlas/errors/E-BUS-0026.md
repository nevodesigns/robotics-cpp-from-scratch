id: E-BUS-0026
title: A late answer that changes what the caller was told
match: an answer that arrives after the call has timed out is not wanted, and does not bring the call back to life
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-09-a-call-across-the-bus
---

## Symptom

A call reported as failed turns out to have succeeded, and the caller's recovery action runs on top of a result that arrived afterwards.

## Cause

The answer to a call that already timed out may still arrive. It is counted as unwanted and the call stays timed out. An answer after the timeout, one for another client and one for a call never made gave 3 unwanted and left the call timed out with no answer.

## Fix

Accept an answer only for a call that is still pending and belongs to this client.

```cpp
if (response.client != client_ || found == calls_.end() || found->second.status != CallStatus::pending) { ++unwanted_; return; }
```
