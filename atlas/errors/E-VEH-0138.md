## Symptom

A vehicle was stuck for a while in the middle of the day and the report says it went well.

## Cause

Nothing read the log. The doctor read the day's log and found 5 findings, one of them the snag; the stop for the person was not a finding.

## Fix

Read every day's log for the faults of 27-05 and put the findings in the report.

```cpp
r.findings = analyse_log(rows, DoctorConfig());
```
