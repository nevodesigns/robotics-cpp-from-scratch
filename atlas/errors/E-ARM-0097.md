## Symptom

The follower arm copies the leader's motions faithfully, a constant angle away from it. It is the same offset on every motion, joint by joint.

## Cause

The two arms' zeros were taken as equal. Each joint's follower angle is its reading less the leader's zero plus the follower's zero (times a scale), and both zeros come from putting the two arms in the same pose.

The leader and the follower read their joints with their own encoders, each with its own zero. The same physical pose gives different readings, and a follower that copies readings copies the difference.

## Fix

Calibrate the zero of each arm in a known pose and store it with the arm.

```cpp
rc::arm::JointMap map;
map.leader_zero = leader_reading_at_pose;
map.follower_zero = follower_reading_at_pose;
const double q = rc::arm::map_joint(map, leader_q);
```
