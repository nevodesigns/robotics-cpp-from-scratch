## Symptom

The heading is good for the first minutes and drifts thereafter, more as the day warms.

## Cause

The bias wanders. Read once, the heading ended 9.9 degrees out after 300 s, against 4.6 with a stop every 60 s and 0.8 every 10.

## Fix

Read the bias at every stop, and decide how often the robot must stop from how fast it wanders.

```cpp
bias.update(gyro_z, still.update(left, right, accel), dt);
```
