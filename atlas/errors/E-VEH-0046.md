## Symptom

A map made from a robot that turns is smeared, and walls are doubled or bent.

## Cause

Every beam was placed from the pose at the start of the sweep. A wall straight ahead was 0.119 m rms from straight with the vehicle turning at 1 rad/s, and 0.0000 m after deskewing.

## Fix

Put each beam in the world from the pose it was fired from, with the pose interpolated through the sweep.

```cpp
const auto cloud = deskew(scan, start_pose, end_pose);
```
