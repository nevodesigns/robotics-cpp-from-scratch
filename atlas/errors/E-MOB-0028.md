## Symptom

A fetch-and-carry job is released because the arm can lift the part, and fails on the floor: the base cannot park, the grasp misses, or the battery runs out.

## Cause

Only the arm's data sheet was checked. The job check has seven requirements: parking, stopping accuracy (the ring's 35 cm tolerance against three times a 3 cm stopping sigma), payload (4.3 kg), grasp (1.000 with the camera), battery (13.1 km of range against 1.2 times the route), travel speed, and people.

## Fix

Write the job and the robot down as numbers and check all seven.

```cpp
const rc::mobile::JobVerdict v = rc::mobile::check_job(robot, job);
if (!v.released) explain(v.first_fail);   // 1 parking ... 7 people
```

A job of 2 kg over 6 m passed all seven. That is a screen; the trial on the real robot is the release.
