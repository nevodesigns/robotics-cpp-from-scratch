match: the arm does the move once and overheats after twenty
match: the cycle time is set by the motor temperature
match: repeating a move with a wait lets the motor cool, and a move that cannot be repeated at once can be repeated with a wait
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A move works in every test and the arm overheats a few minutes into a production run.

## Cause

The rms heat over the cycle is what warms the winding: 118 K steady with no wait, 27 K with a wait of 4 s, against a limit of 75.

## Fix

Work out the steady rise for the cycle, and add the wait, or slow the move, until it is under the limit.

```cpp
duty_rise(model, move, motor, joint, wait);
```
