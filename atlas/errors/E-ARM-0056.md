## Symptom

The planner is slow, and most of its time is spent on motions that are nowhere near anything.

## Cause

Fixed small steps look at every part of a motion alike: 201 poses for an edge that conservative advancement crossed in 2.

## Fix

Let the step grow with the clearance (conservative advancement).

```cpp
du = (d - margin) / full_sweep;
```
