## Symptom

A robot turning on the spot has a GNSS track that circles, and a speed that is not zero. The antenna is on a mast at the back of the robot.

## Cause

The antenna's position was used as the robot's. The antenna sits on a lever arm from the reference point, and turning swings it round: a lever of 0.5 m made the antenna move at 0.50 m/s for a turn of 1 rad/s, and wander 1.00 m.

GNSS gives the antenna's position. The antenna is some distance from the robot's reference point, and as the robot turns it swings round it. Taken as the robot's position, that swing looks like motion.

## Fix

Subtract the lever, turned by the heading, before using the position.

```cpp
double rx = 0.0, ry = 0.0;
rc::locate::antenna_to_reference(ax, ay, heading, lever, rx, ry);
```
