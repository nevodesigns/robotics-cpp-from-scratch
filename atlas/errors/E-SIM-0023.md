## Symptom

A model that was tuned until it matched the bench to the last wiggle behaves worse on the next run, or a time constant 17 percent out passed review because the plots overlapped.

## Cause

A measurement has noise and a model cannot beat it: two real runs gave a floor of 0.0102 at noise 0.01, and a perfect model scored 0.0105. The error against a motor of 0.12 s was 0.0810, 0.0501, 0.0272, 0.0098, 0.0374, 0.0930 and 0.1822 for models of 0.05 to 0.30, smallest at the true value. A sensor delay is a pure shift in an open loop (shift 5, error 0.0000) and not in a closed one (shift 4, error 0.1396).

## Fix

Compare with numbers and stop at the floor.

```cpp
const double floor = noise_floor(bench_run_1, bench_run_2);
```
