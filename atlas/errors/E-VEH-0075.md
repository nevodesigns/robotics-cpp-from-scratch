## Symptom

The heading drifts fast after a moment that coincides with the gyro warming or the robot working hard. It is after a period of heavy work.

## Cause

The bias stepped and nothing looked. A step of 0.01 rad/s with no stop to read it runs on at 0.6 degrees a second.

A filter whose variance does not grow between measurements becomes sure of its estimate and ignores new evidence. A step in the bias is then taken as noise for a long time.

## Fix

Keep reading the bias at the stops, and let its variance grow between them so that a step is believed when it is seen. With stops the step was found 19 s later.

```cpp
p_ += q_ * dt;   // the bias's variance grows while moving
```
