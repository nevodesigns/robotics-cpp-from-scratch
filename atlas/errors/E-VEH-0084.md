## Symptom

The planner is slow, and slower when the headings are finer.

## Cause

Each cell holds a node for every heading. With 12 headings the search took 1146 expansions and with 48 2675.

## Fix

Use the fewest headings that let the car reach the goal heading within the tolerance, and cap the expansions.

```cpp
cfg.theta_bins = 24; cfg.max_expansions = 30000;
```
