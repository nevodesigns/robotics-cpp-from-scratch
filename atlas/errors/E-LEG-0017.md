## Symptom

A robot that walked well for an hour overheats when a payload is added.

## Cause

The knees' heat has a limit: they could trot with 17.5 kg at 0.30 m, and the robot weighed 12 kg.

## Fix

Work out the heaviest body the knees allow for the gait and height, and keep the payload within it.

```cpp
max_body_mass(g, motor, h, 2, duty, tau_swing);
```
