## Symptom

After a long run the tool seems to be stretched or sheared: lengths of vectors change when the pose is applied.

## Cause

A rotation composed a million times with a small turn drifted 0.000000000126 from orthonormal.

## Fix

Re-orthonormalise a rotation that is integrated (or keep a quaternion and normalise it), and test its rotation error.

```cpp
acc = orthonormalised(acc);
```
