## Symptom

A sensor name arrives as `Âµs` where `µs` was sent. Or a name written with a euro sign comes back with a question mark. Or a log has a replacement character in it and nothing reported an error.

## Cause

A byte string is not text until the encoding is said. The same bytes read as Latin-1 and as UTF-8 are different letters. Latin-1 cannot hold a euro sign at all, and writes a question mark. A decoder that meets a byte that is not valid UTF-8 puts in U+FFFD and carries on, so the damage is silent unless the decoder is asked.

Measured: the bytes C2 B5 73 were **3** letters as Latin-1 (U+C2, U+B5, s) and **2** as UTF-8. The euro sign as Latin-1 was the byte **63**. The bytes 61 62 FF 63 gave 4 letters with U+FFFD third, and `clean` was false. A euro sign cut after two of its three bytes, as a whole frame, was not clean; through a decoder that keeps its state, the same two chunks made U+20AC.

## Fix

Decode with a named encoding and ask whether it went wrong:

```cpp
QStringDecoder decoder(QStringDecoder::Utf8, QStringConverter::Flag::Stateless);
d.text = decoder(bytes);
d.clean = !decoder.hasError();
```

Stateless for a whole frame, a decoder that keeps its state for a stream whose chunks may split a character.
