title: A delta asked to work in the whole box
match: the delta could not reach the corners of the cell
match: the working area was a dome and not a box
match: the platform is put back where it was asked, from the angles the inverse kinematics gave
match: the three arms are symmetric: a point on the axis gives equal angles, and the reach is a dome
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 32-02-three-arms-and-one-plate
---

## Symptom

Points in the corners of the cell that looked reachable are refused.

## Cause

The reach is a dome: only 386 of 486 points of a box were reachable, and at -0.40 m the platform went 0.310 m out.

## Fix

Lay the work out in the widest part of the dome and check every point with the inverse kinematics.

```cpp
delta_ik(robot, x, y, z, th);
```
