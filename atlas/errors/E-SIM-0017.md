## Symptom

A monitor cries wolf every run, or it sleeps through a slow drift until the position is already far wrong.

## Cause

The line trades false alarms against late detection. At a threshold of 0.004, twice the noise, 200 of 200 healthy runs raised an alarm with persist 1 and 16 with persist 3; from 0.01 none did. A drift of 0.05 a second was found after 0.46, 1.06, 2.05 and 4.04 s at thresholds of 0.02, 0.05, 0.1 and 0.2.

## Fix

Set it above the noise and as low as the false alarm count allows, from the simulation.

```cpp
ResidualMonitor monitor(0.02, 3);
```
