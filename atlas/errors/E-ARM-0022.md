## Symptom

The estimated reach is smaller than what the arm can do, and bigger each time the sample is.

## Cause

A point near the edge of the reach is reached by few combinations of angles and the sample was not many to a cell: 1.081 m3 from 2 thousand poses and 2.958 m3 from 200 thousand in cells of 0.10 m, and in cells of 0.05 m 200 thousand was still rising (2.033 to 2.554).

## Fix

Raise the sample until the volume stops growing, make it many to a cell, and say how many were used.

```cpp
sample_reach(table, base, tool, 200000, seed, 0.0, grid);
```
