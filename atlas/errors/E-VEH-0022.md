## Symptom

Odometry calibrated in the workshop turns wrongly outdoors.

## Cause

The track width was taken as constant. The effective track depends on the floor: for a turn efficiency of 0.78 on a track of 0.16 m it is 0.2051 m.

## Fix

Calibrate on the floor in use, and keep one value for each floor.

```cpp
const double track = rc::vehicle::calibrate_effective_track(runs);
```
