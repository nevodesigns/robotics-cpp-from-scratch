## Symptom

The arm slows by a fixed share when a person is detected, however near they are. The arm goes at its reduced speed with a person close enough to touch it.

## Cause

The speed allowed depends on the separation: the greatest speed whose protective distance fits it. That was 1.23 m/s at 1.0 m and 2.49 m/s at 2.0 m, and zero inside the distance for standing still.

The protective distance grows with the robot's speed. At a given separation there is a highest speed whose distance fits; a fixed share of the normal speed fits only at one separation and is too fast closer in.

## Fix

Compute the limit from the separation every cycle and clamp the command to it.

```cpp
const double v_cmd = std::fmin(v_nominal, rc::arm::max_speed(ssm, separation));
```
