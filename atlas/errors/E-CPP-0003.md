## Symptom

A wall of errors appears inside a standard library or Qt header. Nothing in that
file is yours and none of it looks related to what you changed.

## Cause

Including a header copies its text into your file before compilation. If your own
code left a statement unfinished, a missing semicolon or an unclosed brace, the
compiler carries that confusion into whatever it reads next, which is often a
header.

## Fix

Read the very first error, not the last, and look at the include chain the
compiler prints. Check the end of the last thing you wrote before the include, or
the last brace you closed. Cascading errors inside untouched headers almost
always trace back to one unfinished line of your own.
