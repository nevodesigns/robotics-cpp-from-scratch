## Symptom

Near the end of a long step the knee runs to full stretch and the joint speeds jump.

## Cause

At full stretch (0.42 m) the knee is straight: the straightness is 0.000 and the joint rates for a foot velocity grow without bound.

## Fix

Keep the foot well inside the leg's length, and clip the places asked of the inverse kinematics.

```cpp
straightness(q[2]) > 0.2
```
