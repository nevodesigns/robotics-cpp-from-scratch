id: E-QT-0101
title: A health that is announced at every look
match: the monitor says it was late and lost no earlier than the limits, once each, and alive again as soon as it is told
match: a robot that keeps sending is never late, and one that stops is, with the changes in order
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 12-02-the-silence-that-was-a-failure
---

## Symptom

The log says `robot lost` a hundred times a second, or an alarm sounds again at every poll, or a dialog is opened over and over.

## Cause

A watchdog that is looked at on a timer answers the same thing at every look, and a handler that acts on the answer acts every time. Measured: a link that stayed lost through many looks gave **2** signals in all, one for late and one for lost; a robot that kept sending gave **none**.

## Fix

Say it only when it changes:

```cpp
if (now == health_) return;
health_ = now;
emit health_changed(static_cast<int>(now));
```
