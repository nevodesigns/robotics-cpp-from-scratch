## Symptom

The first arm's angle is right and the other two put the platform somewhere else.

## Cause

The arms are 120 degrees apart: the target has to be turned into each arm's frame before the same solution is used.

## Fix

Rotate (x, y) by minus 120 degrees times k and solve each arm in its frame.

```cpp
xk = x * cs[k] + y * sn[k]; yk = -x * sn[k] + y * cs[k];
```
