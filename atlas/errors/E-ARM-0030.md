## Symptom

The simulated arm accelerates the wrong way for a torque, or blows up.

## Cause

A mass matrix must be symmetric (here to 0.000000000000) and positive in every direction (60 of 60 random vectors): a link whose inertia has been given an impossible value, or a frame that was left out, makes it neither.

## Fix

Check symmetry and positivity of the mass matrix for a few poses whenever the link parameters change.

```cpp
x^T M x > 0;
```
