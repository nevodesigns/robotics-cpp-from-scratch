## Symptom

The fitted time constant is much too long, the controller tuned on it is sluggish, and the fit's own error bar says it is accurate.

## Cause

The mean of the logarithm of a noisy number is not the logarithm of its mean, so noise pushes the straight line fit one way. With noise of 0.05, 0.30 and 0.50, the quick fit gave 0.0992, 0.1483 and 0.2221 s for a time constant of 0.1, with small spreads, and reading a 63 percent crossing leaned the other way, 0.0994, 0.0876 and 0.0794. A search for the smallest rms error gave 0.0989 at every level.

## Fix

Use the quick fit to start and refine against the data.

```cpp
const Fit f = refine_first_order(times, speeds, command);
```
