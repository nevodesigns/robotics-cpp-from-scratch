## Symptom

A robot held in a low pose for a demonstration goes limp after half a minute.

## Cause

A crouch held for a long time passes the thermal limit. The 40 kg robot of the test, on four feet at a crouch of 0.20 m, reached its limit in 25 s. A trot crouched at 0.20 m settled at 44.3 K, close to the limit of 60 K.

## Fix

Work out the time to the limit for any pose that will be held, and stand straighter when holding.

```cpp
const double t = rc::arm::time_to_limit(knee, std::fabs(tau[2]));   // 25 s for the 40 kg robot crouched
```
