## Symptom

The base consistently drives farther than needed, or parks with no room.

## Cause

The travel and margin weights were left at defaults: travel weighted most gave 1.24 m, none gave 2.56 m.

## Fix

Choose the weights for the task, write them down, and test the choice they give.

```cpp
p.w_travel = 0.3;
```
