## Symptom

The rover is corrected to a pose that is exactly the opposite way round, and every later move is mirrored.

## Cause

A rectangular room looks the same from the far end, rotated by a half turn. The true pose and its mirror through the middle both scored 1.000 in the empty room. With a block against one wall the mirror scored 0.869 against 1.000.

## Fix

Use the tied count and the guess: do not accept a correction that is far from the guess unless the map really separates the two.

```cpp
if (m.tied == 1 && m.score > 0.9) correct_odometry(m.best);
```
