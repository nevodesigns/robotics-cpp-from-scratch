## Symptom

A small car at full lock scuffs its front tyres and turns less than the model says.

## Cause

The inner and the outer wheel were given the same angle. At the smallest circle they should be 5.8 degrees apart.

## Fix

Steer each wheel at right angles to the line to the centre of the circle.

```cpp
ackermann_angles(car, radius, inner, outer);
```
