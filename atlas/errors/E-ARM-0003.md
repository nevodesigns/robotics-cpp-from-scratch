## Symptom

After a long run the tool seems stretched or sheared: vectors change length when the pose is applied. It shows only after a long run that integrated the rotation step by step.

## Cause

A rotation composed again and again with small turns drifts away from orthonormal through rounding. A million small turns put it 0.000000000126 away; more steps or single precision put it much farther.

Every multiplication of rotations adds rounding error, and nothing in the product keeps the columns unit length and at right angles. Over many steps the matrix drifts from a rotation into a general matrix, which scales and shears what it is applied to.

## Fix

Re-orthonormalise a rotation that is integrated (or keep a quaternion and normalise it), and test its rotation error.

```cpp
acc = rc::arm::orthonormalised(acc);
assert(rc::arm::rotation_error(acc) < 1e-9);
```
