## Symptom

One bad reading of the current stops the arm.

## Cause

Contact was declared on one tick over the threshold; noise reaches it. A count of ticks in a row, and a filter, are what make the signal believable.

## Fix

Require the filtered residual to stay over the threshold for several ticks.

```cpp
det.need = 5;
```
