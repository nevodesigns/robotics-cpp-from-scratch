## Symptom

A robot with a broken leg or a dead servo lies on the floor trying to get up, again and again, until its motors are hot and its battery is flat. Nothing tells the operator, because every try looks like normal behaviour.

## Cause

Getting up was retried with no limit and no time limit. A rise that cannot succeed will not succeed on the tenth try either, and each try costs heat and wear. The lesson's machine counts tries after a fall and stops at 3: a fourth request ends in fault 5, out of tries. Every move also has a time limit, so a rise that never finishes is fault 4.

## Fix

Count the tries, time every move, and make a fault something only a person can clear. After a reset the machine starts in sit, not in its old state.

```cpp
rc::legged::Dog dog;
dog.max_tries = 3;
dog.move_timeout = 4.0;     // s for a rise or a lowering
// fault codes: 1 link, 2 hot, 4 a move took too long, 5 out of tries
if (dog.s == rc::legged::kDFault) report(dog.fault);
```

Three is a choice. It should be few enough that a robot that cannot rise is found before it has cooked its knees.
