## Symptom

A car-like vehicle is told to spin in place to get unstuck, and cannot. The ladder was copied from a skid steer robot.

## Cause

One ladder for every vehicle. A car cannot spin; a vehicle that can neither reverse nor spin can only wait and give up.

A car cannot spin on the spot; asked to, it either does nothing or drives in a circle. The rungs of a recovery ladder have to be things the vehicle can do.

## Fix

Leave out the rungs the vehicle cannot do. A car with can_spin false backs up twice instead.

```cpp
rc::drive::UnstickConfig cfg;
cfg.can_spin = false;
```
