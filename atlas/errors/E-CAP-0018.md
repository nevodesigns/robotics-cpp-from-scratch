title: A speed too high for any timeout
match: a timeout chosen from a distance of one metre at one metre a second stops it inside it, and at two a second no timeout can
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-06-a-robot-you-drive-from-a-chair
---

## Symptom

The robot is allowed to go 2 m/s with a stop path that is meant to keep it within a metre, and it cannot.

## Cause

The timeout of 0.667 s for 1 m at 1 m/s gave 0.958 m. At 2 m/s even 0.1 s gave 1.463 m and max_timeout was 0, since the braking alone is 1.333 m.

## Fix

Lower the speed until a timeout fits.

```cpp
max_timeout(1.0, speed, decel) > 0.0
```
