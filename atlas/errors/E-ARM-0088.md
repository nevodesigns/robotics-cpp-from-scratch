## Symptom

The peg presses on the lip of the hole and the force rises until the cell faults, though the peg is within a millimetre or two. The insertion works in the lab with a perfect fixture and jams on the line.

## Cause

The hand was stiff sideways and the offset larger than the clearance (100 micrometres here). A peg 2 mm off needed 0.8 N from a hand of 300 N/m sideways, 5.6 N from one of 2000 N/m, and 279 N from a stiff one of 100000 N/m, more than the 50 N the hand could give: it jammed. Over offsets of 0 to 4 mm the soft hand got the peg in 13 times of 17 and the stiff one 2.

With a clearance of 100 micrometres, the peg must be within 50 of the axis to drop in. The chamfer guides a peg that is off by more, but only if the hand lets the peg move sideways as the chamfer pushes it. A stiff hand resists, and the push becomes a large sideways force on the lip.

## Fix

Make the hand soft sideways and stiff along the hole, and give the part a chamfer.

```cpp
if (rc::arm::insert(hole, offset, hand_k, 50.0) != rc::arm::kInserted) back_off_and_retry();
```

A soft hand is a compliance in the wrist or a softly tuned controller; either costs some accuracy in free motion.
