id: E-CAP-0002
title: A camera delay that nobody measured
match: a delayed camera makes a fast robot wander, and one delayed enough loses the line, which the supervisor turns into a stop
match: the camera sees the line to the left or the right by as much as the robot is off it, and sees nothing when it is dead or the line is out of view
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-01-a-robot-that-follows-a-line
---

## Symptom

The robot works on the bench and swings out and loses the line when the camera is on the real bus.

## Cause

At 0.8 m/s and kp 5 the rms error was 0.0454, 0.0459, 0.0488 and 0.0669 for delays of 0, 120, 200 and 300 ms, and at 400 ms the line was lost and the robot stopped at x 1.69 m with the cause sensors.

## Fix

Measure the delay, and let a lost line be a stop.

```cpp
in.sensors = line_sensor_state(lost, debounce);
```
