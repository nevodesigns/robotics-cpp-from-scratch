title: A ditch narrower than the distance between scans
match: the vehicle misses narrow ditches at speed
match: the hole fell between two scans
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-06-holes-and-drops
---

## Symptom

At low speed the vehicle stops for every ditch, and at high speed it drives into the narrow ones.

## Cause

The tipped sensor sees the ground along one line, and at 5 m/s with a scan every 0.1 s that line moves 0.5 m between scans. A hole of 0.3 m was seen by 0 scans at one phase and 1 at another.

## Fix

Limit the speed so that the distance between scans is less than the narrowest hole that matters, or add a second line.

```cpp
const double widest_missed = max_undetected_width(speed, scan_rate);
```
