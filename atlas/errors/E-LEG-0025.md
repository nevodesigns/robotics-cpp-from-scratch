## Symptom

The robot lifts a leg and sags or tips toward the empty corner, then catches itself on the swinging foot. Its crawl is jerky and sometimes falls on a slight slope.

## Cause

The leg was lifted with the body centred. Taking a corner away from a rectangle leaves a triangle whose long edge is the diagonal through the middle, and a centred centre of mass sits exactly on that diagonal: margin 0.000 m. A push, a bump or the swing of the leg itself then tips it.

## Fix

Shift the body over the middle of the three feet that will stay down before lifting the fourth. Over the centroid of the triangle the margin was 0.078 m, for a shift of up to 8.1 cm.

```cpp
double shift = 0.0;
const double m = rc::legged::crawl_margin_shifted(feet, lifted, &shift);   // 0.078 m after a shift of up to 0.081 m
body.move_to_centroid_of_support(lifted);
leg.lift(lifted);
```

The cost is time: every step of a crawl is a shift, a lift, a place and a shift back.
