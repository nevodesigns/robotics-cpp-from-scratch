## Symptom

A command with both forward and sideways parts stumbles the robot, though each part is within its limit.

## Cause

A box was used for the limit: a command of 1.0 forward and 0.4 sideways is outside the ellipse and was scaled to 0.71 and 0.28.

## Fix

Limit the two together on an ellipse, scaling back and keeping the direction.

```cpp
limit_command(ask, now, limits, dt);
```
