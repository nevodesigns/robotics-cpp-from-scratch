## Symptom

A car-like vehicle is told to spin in place when it is stuck, and cannot.

## Cause

One ladder for every vehicle. A car cannot spin; a vehicle that cannot reverse or spin gives up after waiting.

## Fix

Leave out the rungs the vehicle cannot do (can_reverse, can_spin); a car backs up twice.

```cpp
cfg.can_spin = false;
```
