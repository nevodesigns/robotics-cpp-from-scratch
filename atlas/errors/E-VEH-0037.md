title: A costmap that never forgets
match: the planner will not go through a doorway that a person has left
match: ghost obstacles stay in the costmap
match: an obstacle that has gone stays in a layer that only marks, and is forgotten by one that clears
match: layers are joined cell by cell, the bottom row is part of the grid, and a wall or a beam that runs along y is drawn where it is
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-04-costmap-layers

## Symptom

Obstacles that have gone stay in the map, and the planner finds closed routes that are open. A person walked through and the cells they passed stay blocked.

## Cause

The obstacle layer only marked. Nothing ever cleared a cell, so after an obstacle went 7 cells of ghost stayed.

A costmap that only marks cells where beams end adds every obstacle it ever saw. When an obstacle moves, nothing removes it. A beam that passes through a cell is evidence that the cell is free.

## Fix

Clear the cells each beam passes through as well as marking the one it ends in. Clearing along the beams left 0 ghost cells.

```cpp
rc::perceive::mark_and_clear(layer, sensor_x, sensor_y, hits, true);
```
