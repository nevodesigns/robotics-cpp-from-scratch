## Symptom

A traversability map that is right on one floor is wrong on another. The map was built on the test track.

## Cause

It was made for one surface. A slope of 24 degrees cost 198 on tarmac and 254 on gravel, because the grip differs.

A slope's cost depends on how much of the grip it uses, and the grip depends on the surface. The same slope is easy on tarmac and nearly impossible on gravel.

## Fix

Build the limits from the surface at each place, or make one map for each surface in use.

```cpp
const rc::perceive::VehicleLimits lim = rc::perceive::vehicle_limits(drive, surface_at(x, y), track, cg_height);
```
