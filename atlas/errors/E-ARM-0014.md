## Symptom

The solver passes its tests and fails from the poses the arm is actually in. It works in the test suite and fails from the poses the arm is in during the job.

## Cause

The tests started near the answer, within 0.6 rad, and it converged from 37 of 40. A local solver's success depends on the start: from all zeros it reached 31 of 40.

A local solver walks downhill from its start, and the error has many valleys. From near the answer it finds it; from far it may find a local minimum or run out of steps. The tests' starts were not the arm's.

## Fix

Test from the starts the arm will have, near and far, and count the failures.

const rc::arm::IkResult r = rc::arm::ik_dls(table, target, base, tool, current_q, cfg);
