## Symptom

One wrong reading or a jump in the leader throws the follower across its range.

## Cause

The follower went to the target in a single tick. A jump of 1.0 rad was followed at no more than 2.0 rad/s and took 0.48 s.

## Fix

Clamp the follower's velocity (and limit its acceleration) whatever the target does.

```cpp
q = follower_step(q, target, tau, vmax, dt);
```
