## Symptom

A learner with optimistic values tries, early in learning, moves that damage the robot or its surroundings.

## Cause

Optimism makes every untried move look best until it has been tried once. On the grid that found the large goal (1970 times, return 4) by trying every move in every cell; on a robot, every move includes the ones that fall, collide or overload.

## Fix

Combine optimism with a shield that refuses moves known to be dangerous (39-09), and do the systematic trying in simulation.

```cpp
if (shield_forbids(s, m)) m = safe_move(s);
```

The shield takes away the dangerous trying; the learner still has every safe move to explore.
