title: A speed limit not set from the legs
match: the speed limit was higher than the legs could step
match: full stick made the robot trip
match: the legs allow a top speed, and a speed asks for a step that must be within the leg's reach
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 36-01-what-the-operator-asks-for

## Symptom

Asked for full speed, the robot's steps grow until a foot reaches the end of its travel, and then it stumbles or falls. It happens at the top of the stick and never in testing at a walk.

## Cause

The top speed of the command limit was chosen without looking at the legs. The step is the speed times the stance time, and the stance cannot be shorter than the quickest swing the servos allow, so the fastest the legs can carry the body is the longest step over the shortest stance: 0.20 m over 0.125 s is 1.60 m/s. A speed of 0.5 m/s needed a step of 0.062 m, well within reach; 2.0 m/s needed more than the leg has, however the stick was pushed.

## Fix

Work the top speed out from the legs and set the command limit below it.

```cpp
const double v_legs = rc::legged::max_speed(0.20, duty, 0.25);   // 1.60 m/s for these legs
rc::legged::CmdLimits lim;
lim.vx_max = 0.9 * v_legs;                                     // keep a margin
const rc::legged::Stride s = rc::legged::gait_for_speed(speed, stance, 0.20);
if (!s.ok) slow_down();
```

A heavier load or a slower servo lowers this figure, so it is a number to recompute whenever either changes.
