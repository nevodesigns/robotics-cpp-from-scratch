title: No floor on the speed in a turn
match: the vehicle stops in a sharp turn and never moves on
match: the speed cap in a turn went to zero
match: a limited controller never drops below its floor speed
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-06-a-skid-steer-controller-that-knows-slip

## Symptom

At a sharp turn the vehicle slows to nothing and sits there.

## Cause

The cap on the speed has no floor, so a tight enough curve gives a limit of nearly zero and the vehicle never gets through.

## Fix

Keep a floor speed in a turn (0.15 m/s here), and let the planner avoid curves the surface cannot hold.

```cpp
v = std::max(cfg.min_speed, std::min(v, vmax));
```
