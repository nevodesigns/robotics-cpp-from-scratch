## Symptom

A tracked robot that the model says climbs a slope at a slip of a few percent slips and stalls on the real ground.

## Cause

The soil values were typical ones. Half the largest thrust came at a slip of 10.0 percent for the loose sand of the lesson, and a different soil gives a different curve.

## Fix

Measure the thrust against the slip on the ground in use, and fit the three numbers to it.

```cpp
Soil soil;  // fitted from a drawbar pull test, not copied
```
