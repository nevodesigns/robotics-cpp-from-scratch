## Symptom

Stronger motors are fitted and the robot accelerates as before. The robot was upgraded after slipping on gravel.

## Cause

The wrong limit was upgraded. On gravel the grip binds, not the motors: the motors could climb 25.6 degrees where the gravel holds 22.3.

A vehicle's acceleration is limited by the smaller of what the motors give and what the tyres grip. Upgrading the one that is not the limit changes nothing.

## Fix

Find which limit binds on the floors in use before spending on either.

```cpp
const rc::vehicle::Achievable a = rc::vehicle::achievable_accel(drive_train, cell, heading, speed);
// a.binding says motors or grip
```
