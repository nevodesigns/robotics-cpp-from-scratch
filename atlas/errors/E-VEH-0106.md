## Symptom

The path goes straight up a slope that the vehicle could cross along the side.

## Cause

The cost of the cell was a single number. A metre up 14 degrees cost 25.2 J and across it 1.47 J.

## Fix

Price the step, with the slope along the heading of the step.

```cpp
energy_per_metre(cell, atan2(dy, dx), mass);
```
