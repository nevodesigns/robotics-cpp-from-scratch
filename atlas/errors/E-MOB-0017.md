## Symptom

The robot is sold as carrying 5 kg because the arm is rated for it, and with 5 kg it tips over in an emergency stop.

## Cause

The arm's rating was taken as the robot's. The base must also carry, stop and accelerate with the load. Of the three limits here, 5.0 kg for the arm, 4.3 kg for tipping in an emergency stop from 1.5 m/s in 0.20 m (5.62 m/s2), and 130.7 kg for traction, the least was tipping.

## Fix

Report the least of the three and say which one binds.

```cpp
const rc::mobile::Budget b = rc::mobile::payload_budget(m, base, q, masses, model, limits, 5.0, drive, req);
// b.best 4.3 kg, b.binding 1 (tipping)
```

More payload comes from a wider base, a lower arm, a longer stop or a slower approach, not from a stronger arm.
