title: A member variable overwritten by the call that ran inside
match: a handler that lets the loop run is entered again by the readings that arrive while it works, from inside itself

## Symptom

A handler stores the reading it is working on in a member, does something that lets the loop run, and uses the member afterwards. Sometimes it is working on a different reading from the one it started with, and the result is a number that belongs to no reading in the log.

## Cause

A member is shared by every call, and a call that is entered while another is running writes over it. The outer call comes back to find somebody else's value. Nothing is wrong with either call on its own.

Measured, with the same three readings: reading 1 found the member holding **3** when it came back, **1** reading corrupted, and the others were fine. Which readings are hit depends on the timing, which is why it appears under load and not on the bench.

## Fix

Either do not let anything run in the middle (the guard, next entry) or keep the value in a local variable, which each call has its own copy of.
