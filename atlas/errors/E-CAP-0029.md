title: A day that is judged by deliveries
match: the same day with the safety layer taken out delivers more and is the worse day by every other measure, and the gate would have said so
match: a day's jobs are counted as delivered when they reach the goal and as stopped when the supervisor ended them
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-10-the-whole-robot-for-a-day
---

## Symptom

The robot with the safety layer removed delivers more parcels and is preferred, until it hits somebody.

## Cause

With the limiter and supervisor out the day delivered 5 of 5 jobs with 0 stops, 0 resets and 288 bumps, against 3 delivered, 2 stopped, 2 resets and 0 bumps with them. The two jobs it did not deliver were the link lost and the sensor dead, where stopping was the right answer.

## Fix

Report the stops and the contacts beside the deliveries.

```cpp
out << stopped << " stopped";
```
