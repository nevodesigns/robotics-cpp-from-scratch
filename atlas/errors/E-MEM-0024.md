## Symptom

Under load two parts of the program write to the same message, and the readings in it are a mixture of both. It happens only when many messages are in flight at once, and never in a quiet test.

## Cause

A pool has a size. When all of its messages are out, the right answer is "none" and the caller must cope. A pool that wraps round and lends the oldest again lends a message that someone is still using.

Measured: after 64 lends, a 65th gave `nullptr`, and after one release it gave back exactly that slot and the next ask was refused again.

## Fix

Return `nullptr` when the stack of free slots is empty, and make the caller decide what to do about it:

```cpp
if (free_count_ == 0) return nullptr;
```

Whether to drop the reading, wait or raise a fault is a decision for the caller. It is not for the pool to hide.
