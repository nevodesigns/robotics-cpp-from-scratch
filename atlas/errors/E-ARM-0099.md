## Symptom

The link is lost and the follower goes on moving, to where the leader was.

## Cause

With no watchdog the follower went 0.398 rad on toward the last message; with one it moved 0.000 rad after the timeout.

## Fix

Hold the follower when no message has come within a timeout, and say so.

```cpp
if (!link_alive(now, last_rx, 0.2)) hold();
```
