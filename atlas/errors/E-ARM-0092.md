## Symptom

The arm slows by a fixed share when a person is detected, however near they are.

## Cause

The speed allowed depends on the separation: 1.23 m/s at 1.0 m and 2.49 m/s at 2.0 m, and zero inside the distance for standing still.

## Fix

Compute the limit from the separation every cycle and clamp the command to it.

```cpp
v_cmd = fmin(v_nominal, max_speed(p, separation));
```
