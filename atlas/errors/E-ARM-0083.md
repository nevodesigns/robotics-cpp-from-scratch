## Symptom

The calibration gives a different transform each time, or none.

## Cause

With 5 points and 2 mm of noise the error was 3.5 mm; with 40 it was 1.1 mm; points in a plane gave no fit at all.

## Fix

Use many points spread through the volume the arm works in, and check the residual.

```cpp
if (!fit.ok || fit.rms > limit) recalibrate();
```
