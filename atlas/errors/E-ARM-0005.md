title: A tool offset left out
match: the arm picks everything 15 cm off
match: the tool point is the flange, not the gripper tip
match: the tool offset moves the tool along the flange's own axis
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 28-02-the-tool-in-three-dimensions

## Symptom

Every pick and place is off by the length of the gripper, along the way the tool points.

## Cause

The tool was taken to be the flange. A gripper puts the point that matters some way out along the flange's axis; a tool offset of 0.15 m moved the tool 0.150 m along it, and that is the error when it is left out.

## Fix

Put the tool offset in the model, measured on the real tool, and use it in the forward kinematics, the Jacobian and the inverse kinematics alike.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Pose tip = rc::arm::forward(table, q, base, rc::arm::trans(0.0, 0.0, tip_length));
```
