id: E-BUS-0014
title: A window of one or a timeout shorter than the round trip
match: a sender puts no more than its window on the wire, and sends more as acknowledgements free room
match: a packet with no acknowledgement is sent again after the timeout and not before, and counted
match: on a link with delay, a window of one is slow, and a wider one delivers the same messages in a fraction of the time
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-05-reliable-or-fast
---

## Symptom

A reliable topic carries a fraction of what the link could, or the link is full of packets that were never lost.

## Cause

A window of one sends one message per round trip: 100 messages at a one way delay of 50 ticks took 10000 ticks at window 1, 5000 at 2, 2000 at 5, 1000 at 10 and 500 at 20. A timeout shorter than the round trip resends packets whose acknowledgements are still on their way.

## Fix

Set the window to fill the link and the timeout above the round trip.

```cpp
ReliableSender sender(10, 120);
```
