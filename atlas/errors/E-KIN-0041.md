## Symptom

The arm will not go to a pose, the operator is told it failed, and tries the same pose again, and again, with different results on different days.

## Cause

Three different reasons look the same from outside: no angles exist, angles exist outside a joint limit, or the angles are legal and the motors cannot hold them. Each has a remedy the operator can use, and the remedies are not interchangeable. Measured: an out of reach pose, one outside the limits, one at **1.07297** of the shoulder limit and one at **0.889031** were four different verdicts.

## Fix

Keep the reasons apart in the answer:

```cpp
enum class Verdict { kOk, kUnreachable, kOutsideLimits, kTooHeavy };
```
