## Symptom

Torques worked out for a force at the tool are wrong, or the torque of a joint is not zero where it should be.

## Cause

The Jacobian was used the wrong way (not transposed) or a wrench in the tool frame was applied in the base frame. With the right use the powers agreed to 0.000000000000000 W and a vertical force asked 0.000 N m of a vertical first joint.

## Fix

Use the transpose, in the frame the wrench is in, and test with power (torque times rate equals wrench times velocity).

```cpp
joint_torques(j, wrench);
```
