## Symptom

In a cluttered place the ground plane jumps between scans, though the ground does not.

## Cause

Few draws are clean when little of the cloud is ground. 5 are enough at 85 percent and about a hundred times that at 30.

## Fix

Choose the number of draws from the share of ground you expect and the confidence you want.

```cpp
const int draws = ransac_samples(expected_ground_share, 0.99);
```
