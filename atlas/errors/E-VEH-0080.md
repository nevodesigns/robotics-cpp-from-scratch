## Symptom

A good localisation is thrown off now and then by random particles that were not needed.

## Cause

A monitor with no memory reacts to every bad scan. With two averages it put in 0.1 percent on average while the robot was where it should be, and the error was 0.006 m.

## Fix

Compare a fast average with a slow one, and cap the share put in at one time.

```cpp
return std::max(0.0, std::min(s_.max_share, 1.0 - fast_ / slow_));
```
