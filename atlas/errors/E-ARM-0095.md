## Symptom

The arm is made faster and the cell is barely quicker.

## Cause

At half speed the cycle was 5.70 s against 3.68 s: the hand's 0.8 s and the contact approaches (1.07 s) do not depend on the override.

## Fix

Find which pieces of the cycle are the largest, and shorten those first.

print each piece of the cycle
