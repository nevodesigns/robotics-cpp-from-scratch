title: A contact sense taken as the gait's timetable
match: the robot kept stepping into the air over a hole
match: the gait assumed the foot was down when the timetable said so
match: touchdown and lift-off are believed after a run of readings, and a reading on the wrong side resets the run
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 34-07-feeling-the-ground
---

## Symptom

The robot's gait is driven by the contact sense alone, and on rough ground it loses its rhythm; or it is driven by the timetable alone and ignores bumps and holes.

## Cause

The contact sense and the gait's timetable answer different questions: whether the foot is down, and whether it should be. Each alone is not enough. Where they disagree the robot has learned something: down early is a bump, late is a hole, down and moving is a slip.

## Fix

Compare the two and act on the difference: stop a swing that touched early, keep reaching when it is late (36-04), slow down when a stance foot moves.

```cpp
const bool should = rc::legged::in_stance(g, leg, t);
const bool is = rc::legged::contact_update(s, force, since_lift, p);
if (is && !should) stop_swing(leg);   // a bump
if (!is && should) reach_down(leg);   // perhaps a hole
```
