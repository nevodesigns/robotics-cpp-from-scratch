title: A speed limit that forgets the delay
match: the operator drives into things they could see on the screen
match: the teleoperated vehicle cannot stop in the distance the operator sees
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The operator sees the wall in time and the vehicle hits it anyway.

## Cause

The speed limit used the braking distance only. The picture is old, the person reacts and the command travels: with 2.0 s of delay in all the allowed speed for a 6 m sight line is 2.32 m/s, not 4.00.

## Fix

Add the video delay, the reaction and the command delay to the latency in the stopping distance, and solve for the speed.

```cpp
operator_speed_limit(sight, video_delay, reaction, command_delay, decel);
```
