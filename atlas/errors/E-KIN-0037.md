## Symptom

The tool of an arm leaves the path it was supposed to follow in the middle of a move, and is back on it at the end.

## Cause

Each joint planned alone finishes when it finishes. The ones with less to do are done early and the tool is carried by the rest. Measured: joints of distance 2 and 0.5 took **2.5** s and **1.0** s alone; with a common time both took **2.5** s, the second at a peak of **0.208712**, and both reached their targets at that instant.

## Fix

Give every joint the time of the slowest, at its own acceleration:

```cpp
t.v_peak = (a * slowest - std::sqrt(a * a * slowest * slowest - 4.0 * a * d)) / 2.0;
```
