## Symptom

The vehicle climbs the first part of a long slope and then stops or slows as the motors overheat.

## Cause

The current for 20 degrees makes more heat than the motor can lose: it reached its limit in 62 s, which at 0.5 m/s is 31 m. A slope of 14 degrees settled at 71 K and could be held.

## Fix

Work out the time to the limit for the slopes of the route, and plan a rest, a slower climb or another way for a climb that is longer than it allows.

```cpp
time_to_limit(motor_current(slope_force(m, angle, crr), r, n, eff, kt), thermal);
```
