## Symptom

What is learned from the demonstrations waits before it starts and after it ends. The learned policy hesitates at the start of each task.

## Cause

Idle ends were left on the episodes. A person starts the recording and then reaches; stops and then ends it. The idle ends teach doing nothing. Trimming 1 s of idling from each end left 2.8 s, a little under the 3 s reach because its slow start and end fall below the threshold.

A person starts and stops the recording by hand, so each episode has stillness at both ends. A policy learns what it sees: in the lesson, 2 s of every episode said wait, against 2.8 s of the task.

## Fix

Trim the stretches in which the leader was not moving, with a little padding kept, and use a lower threshold or more padding if the ends of the motion are cut.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Episode e = rc::arm::trim_idle(steady, 0.02, 3);   // empty if it never moved
```
