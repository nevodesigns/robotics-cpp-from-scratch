## Symptom

A path from a stored roadmap collides with something that was added to the cell after the roadmap was built.

## Cause

The roadmap says what was true when it was built: 34 percent of its edges were no longer free after a box was added, and queries returned paths with 12 bad edges.

## Fix

Check the edges a query uses against the scene as it is now, or rebuild, whenever the scene may have changed.

```cpp
edge_free_fraction(problem_now, roadmap);
```
