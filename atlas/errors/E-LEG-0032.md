title: An abrupt switch of the table
match: the leg jerked when the gait changed
match: a foot jumped at the moment of the change
match: the offsets move toward the target by the shortest way round and no faster than the rate
match: a change of gait keeps feet down either way, but an abrupt one jumps the legs' phases, and the speed switch has a gap
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Switching from walk to trot, the robot jerks: a foot that was on the ground is suddenly told it is mid swing. Counting feet on the ground shows nothing wrong.

## Cause

The gait table was swapped in one tick. Both the abrupt and the smooth change kept 2 or more feet down, so a check on the feet misses it. What hurts is the jump of a leg's phase: the abrupt change moved one by 0.250 of a cycle in a single tick, which moves the commanded foot a quarter of the way round its path.

## Fix

Move the offsets toward the new table at a bounded rate. At 0.2 cycles a second the phase moved 0.0002 a tick and the change took 1.25 s.

```cpp
for (;;) {
  rc::legged::step_offsets_toward(offsets, new_table, 0.2, dt);
  gait.set_offsets(offsets);
}
```

The duty and the period should be eased the same way when they change.
