## Symptom

At a low speed the joint moves in jerks, and every start and change of direction is followed late. It shows at the start of slow moves and at every reversal.

## Cause

The controller supplied the friction through its error and integral, which have to wind up before the joint breaks free. Starting a ramp of 0.03 rad/s, the load was up to 0.91 mrad behind and took 0.32 s to come within 0.1 mrad.

While stuck, the joint's velocity is zero, so a controller that compensates friction from the measured velocity adds nothing. The error and the integral must build to the breakaway torque first, and then the joint jumps. The reference velocity says which way friction will act before the joint moves.

## Fix

Feed forward the friction torque for the reference velocity, not the measured one (which is zero while stuck). With it the error was 0.38 mrad, settled in 0.06 s.

```cpp
rc::arm::JointController c;
c.friction_ff = true;   // friction_torque(believed, v_ref) added
```

Once the ramp was under way, the integral had found the friction and neither was off (0.00000 rad): the feedforward matters at starts and reversals.
