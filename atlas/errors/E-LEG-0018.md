## Symptom

On a soft or uneven floor the contact state flickers on and off.

## Cause

One threshold was used for both: a force hovering around 8 N changed the state 10 times in 0.4 s, and 0 times with 8 N on and 4 N off.

## Fix

Use a lower threshold to go off than to go on, and a run of readings.

```cpp
p.f_on = 8.0; p.f_off = 4.0;
```
