## Symptom

A guarded learned policy succeeds on the job, and the logs show the fall-back controller acting on a large share of the steps.

## Cause

Either the network was not taught the states the job reaches and the guard was covering for it, or the guard was set so tight it would not let a good network drive. The plain clone of 38-05 handed over on 12 percent of the steps, over the job's 5; the DAgger policy, which needed little help, handed over on 10 percent behind a guard with a threshold of 1 spread, and failed at requirement 3.

A guard is a safety net. A policy that lives in it is not the policy that was meant to ship.

## Fix

Look at where the guard acts: if the states are ones the job reaches, teach the network there (DAgger, 38-06); if the network is good there, loosen the threshold, measured as 38-07 did.

```cpp
if (v.first_fail == 3) look_at_where_the_guard_acted();
```

If the fall-back is doing the job well on its own, consider shipping the fall-back.
