## Symptom

Every turn is short by the same fraction, on a particular floor.

## Cause

The body turns less than the wheel difference says. At an efficiency of 0.8 a commanded quarter turn was 72.0 degrees.

## Fix

Include a turn efficiency in the model, measure it on the floor, and ask for the turn the floor will give.

```cpp
const double w = eff * r * (wr - wl) / b;
```
