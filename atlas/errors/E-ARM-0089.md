## Symptom

Insertion works for small errors and the peg lands beside the chamfer for larger.

## Cause

Softness does not widen the chamfer: beyond 3 mm the peg misses, however the hand gives.

## Fix

Keep the position error (31-03) within the chamfer's reach, and search if it is not.

```cpp
if (fabs(e) > clearance / 2 + chamfer_w) search();
```
