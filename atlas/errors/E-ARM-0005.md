## Symptom

Every pick and place is off by the length of the gripper, along the way the tool points.

## Cause

The tool was the flange. A tool offset of 0.15 m moved the tool 0.150 m along the flange's own axis.

## Fix

Put the tool offset in the model, and measure it.

```cpp
forward(t, q, base, trans(0, 0, tip_length));
```
