id: E-ARM-0071
title: An object trusted for ever where it was last seen
match: the arm hit a person who had stepped out of the camera's view
match: the planner assumes the box is where it was last seen
match: an object not seen for a while is treated as bigger by how far it could have gone, up to a cap
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 30-07-a-world-that-changes
---

## Symptom

The arm collides with something that was last seen a while ago and has since moved.

## Cause

The object was taken to be where it was last seen. Made bigger by speed times age (up to a cap), an object seen 2.0 s ago at 0.5 m/s grew 0.50 m and a pose 0.31 m clear of a slower one was 0.11 m clear.

## Fix

Give every object a time last seen and a speed it might move at, and grow it by their product, up to a cap.

```cpp
scene_at(scene, now, cap);
```
