## Symptom

Things line up while the robot is steady and smear while it turns.

## Cause

One sensor's clock is behind the other. A lag of 40 ms at 1 rad/s is 2.3 degrees; the shift that matches a gyro and the wheels was found as 0.12 s.

## Fix

Estimate the lag from two signals of the same motion and correct the stamps.

```cpp
const int lag = estimate_lag(wheel_rate, gyro_rate, max_lag);
```
