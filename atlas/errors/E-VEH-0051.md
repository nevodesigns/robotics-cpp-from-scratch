## Symptom

The camera places the foot of a slope or a kerb at the wrong distance and the planner mistakes a gentle rise for a wall.

## Cause

The ground plane is a plane, level. Over a rise, the ray meets the floor sooner than the level plane says.

## Fix

Take the plane from the lidar of lesson 24-02 where it is available, instead of assuming it is level.

```cpp
// plane from fit_plane_ransac, not z = -height
```
