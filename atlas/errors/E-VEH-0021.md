## Symptom

A car driven in a straight line has a path in the log that curves.

## Cause

The sensor read one degree with the wheels straight. Over 20 m the odometry believed the car had turned 61 degrees.

## Fix

Measure the zero offset with the car on a straight line and subtract it.

```cpp
const double delta = steer_measured - zero_offset;
```
