title: Speeding up the part that is not the bottleneck
match: the speed override went up and the throughput hardly changed
match: a faster arm made little difference
match: the override for a target cycle is found, and a target faster than the rated speed is refused
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 31-07-how-many-parts-an-hour

## Symptom

The arm is made faster and the cell is barely quicker; or it is slowed by half and makes far more than half its parts.

## Cause

Much of the cycle does not depend on the robot's speed. At half speed the cycle took 5.70 s and at a quarter 10.13 s, less than double and quadruple, because the hand's 0.8 s and the contact approaches (1.07 s, held to the contact speed) do not scale.

## Fix

Find which pieces of the cycle are the largest and shorten those first. Ask for the override a target needs, and accept a refusal: a target of 5.16 s needed 0.576; one of 2.95 s gave -1.

```cpp
const double k = rc::arm::scale_for_cycle(cell, target);
if (k < 0.0) change_the_cell();   // a faster hand, shorter moves
```
