title: A loop that cannot start or cannot stop
match: a map that starts unknown cannot be planned through if unknown is a wall, and can if it is merely dear
match: when every way through is shut, the rover stops with no route and does not loop
match: in a building that does not change, the rover reaches the goal in the fewest moves the building allows
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 16-16-a-rover-that-notices
---

## Symptom

The rover sits at the start for ever, or it drives round and round a sealed building until its battery dies.

## Cause

With unknown cells forbidden, a map that knows nothing has no route, found after 0 steps. With them allowed at a cost, the rover reached the goal in 33 moves, the fewest the building allows, with 1 plan. In a building with every way shut the planner found no route after 54 steps and 7 plans, and the loop stopped with that as its answer.

## Fix

Let unknown be dear, not forbidden, and stop on a failed plan.

```cpp
options.unknown = UnknownIs::expensive;
if (!plan.found) { result.no_route = true; return result; }
```
