match: teleoperation with no protection from the vehicle's own sensor
match: an obstacle the vehicle senses stops it short whatever the operator asks, and without the cap it goes through
match: a vehicle that has heard nothing stays still

## Symptom

The operator holds full speed at a wall that the vehicle's sensor sees, and the vehicle drives into it.

## Cause

Nothing between the request and the wheels. The governed vehicle stopped 0.50 m short of an obstacle; the ungoverned one went 58.7 m past where it was.

## Fix

Cap the speed at what stops within the sensed distance less a margin, whatever the request.

```cpp
target = std::min(target, stop_speed(obstacle - margin, latency, decel));
```
