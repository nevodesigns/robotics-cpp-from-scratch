## Symptom

The robot sees the closed door, replans, and sees it as free again a moment later, and tries it again.

## Cause

An occupancy grid is an average, built to ride out noise, and a cell that has been free for many scans takes several scans of a wall to turn. The rover that let the map average a contradiction reached the goal in 87 steps with 4 bumps; the one that believed it at once reached it in 83 steps with none.

## Fix

Force a cell the map decided on when the sensor says the opposite, if the sensor is clean, or use a detector with persistence (lesson 16-15) when it is noisy.

```cpp
if (trust && contradicts) force_cell(map, x, y, wall);
```
