## Symptom

The localisation works when the robot starts where it was told it is, and never recovers when it does not.

## Cause

A cloud started near the wrong pose has no particles near the truth. Started over the whole room, 3000 particles found the robot in 5 steps.

## Fix

Start over the whole map when the pose is not known, and let the evidence thin the cloud.

```cpp
mc.init_uniform(x0, x1, y0, y1);
```
