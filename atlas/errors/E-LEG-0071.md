## Symptom

A loop is stable on the bench at one bus speed and not at another, or after a software update.

## Cause

The delay was never measured and the margin never worked out: the stiff gains stood 19 ms and the gentle 43 ms.

## Fix

Measure the delay of every loop on the real robot, work out the largest the gains can stand, and keep the difference as a margin.

```cpp
max_stable_delay(j, target, secs, dt, limit);
```
