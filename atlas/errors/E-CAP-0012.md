## Symptom

A balancing robot that falls drives across the room, still trying, at full speed.

## Cause

Started 25 degrees over, the supervisor cut the motors at 0.195 s with the cause tilt: the axle went 0.54 m, up to 0.69 m/s, and 0.45 m while it fell. Without it, 3.17 m, 1.51 m/s and 3.07 m while it fell. A dead IMU at 2 s gave a stop at 2.010 s with the cause sensors, and without the supervisor no stop and no cause.

## Fix

Cut the motors when the lean cannot be recovered.

```cpp
in.tilt = monitor.update(theta_hat);
```
