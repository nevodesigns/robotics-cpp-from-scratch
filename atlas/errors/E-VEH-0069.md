## Symptom

In a plain room the robot believes it is in the opposite corner, facing the other way.

## Cause

The map looks the same from both places. In 2 of 8 runs of an empty rectangle the cloud ended on the mirror image, and 0 with a partition and a post.

## Fix

Look at the spread and the modes, and use something that breaks the symmetry before acting on the pose.

```cpp
if (mc.spread() > 0.4) keep_exploring();
```
