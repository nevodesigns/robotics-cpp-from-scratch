id: E-CAP-0009
title: A stop distance that nobody measured at the speed it has
match: a person sensor that dies is a stop of the arm with its cause, within a centimetre or two of the tip
match: an open e-stop loop stops the arm in a distance that grows with the speed it had
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-03-an-arm-that-picks-and-places
---

## Symptom

The guard is placed for the distance the arm stops in at the slow speed, and the arm runs faster.

## Cause

A person sensor dead from 3 s stopped the arm with the cause sensors, the tip 0.011 m further on. An e-stop opened at 2 s stopped it 0.044 m further at the 0.8 m/s limit and 0.065 m with the limit at the arm's full 1.14 m/s.

## Fix

Measure the stopping distance at the highest speed the arm can reach.

```cpp
const ArmRunResult r = run_cell(cell, plan, human, config, 30.0);
```
