## Symptom

A healthy node is reported dead and alive again several times a minute, and whatever depends on it stops and starts.

## Cause

A silence takes a lease to mean gone, so a death is noticed after about one lease: 1.6, 2.6, 3.6 and 5.6 s at leases of 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 and 5.5. A lease of 1.5 s with heartbeats every second declares a node gone after a single lost one, and with 10 percent loss gave 172 false gone events in 2000 s against 17, 3 and 0 at the longer leases.

## Fix

Set the lease to a few heartbeat periods and test it against the link's real loss.

```cpp
Discovery table(3.5);
```
