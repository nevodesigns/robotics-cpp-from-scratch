id: E-SIM-0031
title: Overshoot measured in the wrong direction or in the wrong units
match: a smooth approach to the target has no overshoot, a rise time of tau times ln 9, and settles in about four time constants
match: a step down is measured as a step up is, and the same shape mirrored gives the same numbers
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-11-pass-or-fail
---

## Symptom

A loop that visibly overshoots on the way down is reported as having none, or a small step is judged leniently because the overshoot was an absolute number.

## Cause

Past the target means the side the step was going toward, and the overshoot is a fraction of the step's size. A lag of 0.2 s gave overshoot 0.0000, rise 0.4400 s (tau ln 9 is 0.4394) and settling 0.7900 s (tau ln 50 is 0.7824). An underdamped step up and its mirror down gave the same 0.0759, 0.1800 and 0.5600.

## Fix

Multiply the distance past by the sign of the step.

```cpp
const double past = (p - m.to) * sign;
```
