## Symptom

On a turn the vehicle clips a thing that is on the arc it is driving but not straight in front of it.

## Cause

The field was a rectangle in front. A thing on the arc of a turn of radius 2 m at 40 degrees round was outside the rectangle and inside the swept field.

## Fix

Sweep the field along the arc of the turn, with the curvature taken from the steering or the measured turn rate.

```cpp
const bool hit = rc::drive::in_swept_field(x, y, length, half_width, curvature);
```
