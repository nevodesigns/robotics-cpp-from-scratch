## Symptom

The hand follows a line until the arm is at full stretch and stops short of the end.

## Cause

Only the arm moved: it ended 1.18 m short with its manipulability at 0.0000; with the base too, 0.000 m.

## Fix

Give the base a share of the hand's velocity from the start, so the arm stays in the middle of its range.

```cpp
run_line(m, base, q, goal, speed, w_base, true, dt);
```
