## Symptom

The solver returns the angles that were used to make the pose, and some of its other solutions do not reach it.

## Cause

The test compared the solver with the angles it was started from. Checked by forward kinematics, all 8.0 solutions on average reach the pose to 0.0000000000 m.

## Fix

Test every solution through forward kinematics, for many poses, position and rotation.

```cpp
forward(t, solution, base, tool) == target;
```
