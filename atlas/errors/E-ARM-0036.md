title: An integral that winds up at the torque limit
match: the arm overshoots badly after a big move
match: the joint overshoots when the torque saturates
match: a step into the limit overshoots far more if the integral keeps running
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

After a large move the joint overshoots a long way and takes a long time to settle. The large moves overshoot; the small ones are fine.

## Cause

The torque sat at its limit and the integral kept adding the error the whole time. When the joint arrived the integral was huge and had to unwind: a step of 1.5 rad into a limit of 20 N m overshot 186 percent.

While the torque is at its limit, the integral keeps adding the error, which is large during a long move. When the joint reaches the target the integral holds a torque that keeps pushing, and only an error of the other sign can unwind it.

## Fix

Hold (or back-calculate) the integral while the output is at its limit. With it held the overshoot was 19.0 percent.

```cpp
if (!anti_windup || sat == tau_cmd) integ += g.ki_vel * ev * dt;   // only when not saturated
```
