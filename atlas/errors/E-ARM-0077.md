## Symptom

The part stays in the fingers at rest and slips as the arm lifts or turns.

## Cause

The squeeze was worked out for the weight alone (9.81 N for a kilogram) and the lift needed 14.81 N.

## Fix

Add the acceleration of the hand to gravity when working out the force.

```cpp
required_force(hand, mass, accel, safety);
```
