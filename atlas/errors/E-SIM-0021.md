id: E-SIM-0021
title: One generator shared by the whole simulation
match: adding a part to a simulation changes nothing for the others with a stream each, and changes everything for them when they share one
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-07-one-seed-many-streams
---

## Symptom

Adding a logger, or a camera, or a debug sensor changes the behaviour of the rest and a bug found without it cannot be found with it.

## Cause

With one shared generator each part's numbers depend on how many draws were made before its own. Three sensors read noise for 1000 steps and a camera was added to the middle of the list: the imu's noise changed in 0 of 1000 values with a stream each, and in 1000 of 1000 with one shared stream.

## Fix

Give every part a stream by name.

```cpp
streams.get("imu").gaussian();
```
