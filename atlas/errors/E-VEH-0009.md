## Symptom

The car clips the outside wall in a turn of a corridor wider than the car.

## Cause

The corridor was sized to the car's width. In a turn the outer front corner sweeps a circle larger than the path of the centre: 0.904 m at the smallest radius, and this lane was only 0.062 m wider than the car.

## Fix

Size a turn by the swept radius of the outer front wheel, not by the body's width.

```cpp
const double outer_edge = rc::vehicle::swept_radius_outer(car, radius);
```
