## Symptom

The contact is detected and the force on the part is still too large. The detection works in the test and the parts are still marked.

## Cause

Detection takes time, and the arm keeps moving while it does. A joint driven at 0.2 rad/s into a stiff wall was found to have touched 15 ms after reaching it, with the wall pushing back 1.31 N m. At 0.6 rad/s detection took 10 ms, and by the time the arm stopped the wall's torque had reached 5.20 N m.

In the time detection takes, the arm keeps moving into the surface, and the force grows with the stiffness times that extra distance. A faster approach covers more distance in the same delay and hits harder, so detection alone cannot keep the force low.

## Fix

Approach slowly where contact is expected, with the speed chosen from the force the part can bear and the time detection and stopping take.

```cpp
const double v = expect_contact ? v_approach_slow : v_free;
```
