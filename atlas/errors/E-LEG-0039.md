## Symptom

The robot stands firmly and tips as soon as it speeds up or brakes, though its centre of mass is inside the feet.

## Cause

A body that accelerates presses behind its centre: 83 mm for 3 m/s2 at 0.27 m. The point that must be inside the feet is the zero moment point.

## Fix

Check the zero moment point, not the centre of mass, against the support polygon.

```cpp
zmp_from_com(com, h, accel, z); support_margin(feet, on_ground, z);
```
