## Symptom

Wheel odometry closes every loop and the robot is nowhere near where it believes.

## Cause

The ideal parameters have no slip and no loss of turn, and the robot has both. The calibrated parameters put it within 5 cm of the truth.

## Fix

Close a loop on the real floor and see how far off it ends; do not judge odometry by its own closure.

```cpp
WheelOdometry odo(track / efficiency, 1.0 - slip);
```
