## Symptom

The simulated robot turns about a quarter short after a drive and the real one does not.

## Cause

The centre of mass was over the axle, so the caster touched the floor only when the body rocked. With the weight behind the axle the turns were 0.99, 1.01 and 1.02.

## Fix

Put the weight between the axle and the caster, and test the simulated robot with a turn after a drive.

```cpp
com_back 0.0144 source typical
```
