## Symptom

A gentle, slow stand-up overheats the knees where a quick one does not.

## Cause

The heat of a rise is proportional to its time at torque: 0.202 of the thermal life in 6 s against 0.034 in 1 s.

## Fix

Rise promptly (within what the acceleration allows) and check the heat before trying.

```cpp
rise_plan(...).life_used < 1.0;
```
