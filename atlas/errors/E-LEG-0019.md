## Symptom

Every lift-off is followed by a short false touchdown and the gait gets confused.

## Cause

The leg's own acceleration gave a force spike just after the lift was commanded: the state changed 24 times in six steps without a blank and 12 with it.

## Fix

Ignore touchdown for a blank period after the foot is told to lift.

```cpp
p.blank = 0.06;
```
