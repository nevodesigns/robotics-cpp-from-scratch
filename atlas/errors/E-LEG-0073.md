## Symptom

A job check prints a long list of figures and a red result, and nobody can tell quickly what to change.

## Cause

Every requirement was reported with equal weight. What matters first is the first requirement in the order that fails, because changing it may change the others. The check reports that one: 2.0 m/s failed at 1, a slope of 40 degrees at 2, a push of 0.9 m/s at 3, 30 kg at 4, a route of 5000 m at 5, a person 0.3 m away at 6.

## Fix

Report the first failure by number and name, and keep the figures of the rest so it can be seen how far each is from passing.

```cpp
const rc::legged::JobVerdict v = rc::legged::check_job(robot, job);
// 0 none, 1 speed, 2 terrain, 3 push, 4 knees, 5 battery, 6 people
if (v.first_fail == 4) report("knees: rise " + std::to_string(v.rise) + " K");
```
