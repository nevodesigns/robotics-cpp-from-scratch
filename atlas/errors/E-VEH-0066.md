## Symptom

The scan match drifts when someone walks near the robot.

## Cause

Points with no true partner were paired with the nearest wall. Without a limit the pull was 0.042 m; with 0.3 m it was 0.000 m.

## Fix

Limit the pairing distance, and shrink it as the match converges.

```cpp
icp(source, target, guess, rounds, 0.3, tol);
```
