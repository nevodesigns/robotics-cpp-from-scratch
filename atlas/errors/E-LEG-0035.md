## Symptom

The tilt estimate from the feet is meaningless in a trot.

## Cause

Two feet are down: no plane; in the trot the filter had to fall back on the gyro and the accelerometer (1.02 degrees) where in a walk the feet took it to 0.40.

## Fix

Use the feet only when three or more are down, and say so to the filter.

```cpp
feet_valid = feet_down >= 3;
```
