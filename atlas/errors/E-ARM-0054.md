## Symptom

The arm is reported colliding with itself in every pose.

## Cause

Links that share a joint always touch at it. Only capsules more than one apart are asked (3 of the 6 pairs for four capsules); with the flange brought down beside the base the distance was -0.120 m.

## Fix

Leave out the pairs that share a joint (an allowed collision matrix), and rely on the joint limits for the arm folded on itself.

```cpp
for (std::size_t j = i + 2; j < caps.size(); ++j)
```
