## Symptom

In a plain room the robot believes it is in the opposite corner, facing the other way. The room is a plain rectangle.

## Cause

The map looks the same from two places. In an empty rectangle, 2 of 8 runs ended on the mirror image.

In a symmetric room, the scans from a pose and from its mirror image are the same. The evidence cannot tell them apart, and the cloud may settle on either.

## Fix

Look at the spread and the modes before acting on the pose, and use something that breaks the symmetry. With a partition and a post, 0 of 8 ended on the mirror.

```cpp
if (mc.spread() > 0.4) keep_exploring();
```
