## Symptom

The arm takes a roundabout route between nearby poses, and spends time and torque on it.

## Cause

The raw path was 4.83 times the straight line; after 150 shortcuts it was 1.85 times.

## Fix

Run a shortcut pass (and keep the margin) after every plan.

```cpp
path = shortcut(problem, path, 150, seed, problem.margin);
```
