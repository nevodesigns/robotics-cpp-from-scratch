## Symptom

The arm clears everything and the part it is holding does not. The arm is clear and the part in its hand hits the fixture.

## Cause

The planner used the arm alone. A part in the hand makes the last link longer and fatter: a part of 0.25 m took a pose from 0.090 m clear of an obstacle to -0.090 m.

A held part becomes part of the arm for planning: it extends the last link and makes it wider. A planner that does not know about it plans for a shorter, thinner arm.

## Fix

When a part is picked up, plan with the longer tool and the fatter last link, and bump the scene's version so old plans are rechecked.

```cpp
const rc::arm::HeldArm h = rc::arm::held_arm(radii, tool_length, 0.25, part_radius);
const auto caps = rc::arm::arm_capsules(table, q, h.radii, h.tool_length);
```
