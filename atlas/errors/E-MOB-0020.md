## Symptom

Grasps planned from the map and the base's position succeed about one time in three.

## Cause

With a hand sigma of 25.6 mm and a tolerance of 10 mm, the chance of a good grasp is 0.30; 20000 simulated grasps gave the same. For 99 percent the sigma would have to be 3.9 mm, which localisation of the base does not give.

## Fix

Measure the part from the hand. A camera on the arm leaves out every error of the base: with a camera good to 2 mm at working range the sigma was 2.0 mm and the chance 1.000.

```cpp
const double p_map = rc::mobile::grasp_probability(rc::mobile::hand_sigma(s, 0.9), 0.010);   // 0.30
const double p_cam = rc::mobile::grasp_probability(rc::mobile::sigma_after_servo(0.002, 0.0001), 0.010);   // 1.000
```
