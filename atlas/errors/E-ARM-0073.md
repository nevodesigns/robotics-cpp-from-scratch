## Symptom

A plan that was free when it was made is followed into something that moved into its way.

## Cause

Nothing looked along the plan again after it was made. A thing that moves can cross the path at any time.

## Fix

Predict the moving things and check the rest of the trajectory against them at every tick. Looking along it found an obstacle 1.78 s away for one 1 m off and coming at 0.5 m/s.

```cpp
const double ttc = rc::arm::time_to_collision(table, radii, tool, traj, traj_time, track, now, r, margin, 1.0, 0.05);   // -1 none within the horizon
```
