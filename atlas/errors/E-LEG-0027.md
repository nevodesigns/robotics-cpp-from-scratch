## Symptom

The trot holds for a moment and then the body topples sideways faster and faster.

## Cause

The fall accelerates: x(t) is x0 cosh(t / T) with T = 0.166 s for a body 0.27 m high; the first 10 mm look harmless and a stance of 0.25 s is at the edge of the 0.41 s the body has.

## Fix

Use the pendulum's time, not a constant rate, when working out how long a stance can be.

```cpp
time_to_move(x0, v0, L, h);
```
