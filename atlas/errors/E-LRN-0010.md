## Symptom

Training the same network on the same data twice gives two different results, or a change that should not matter changes the outcome, and nobody can tell a real improvement from luck.

## Cause

The random parts of training (the starting weights and the order of the samples) came from an unseeded or shared source, so every run differs. Or the samples were never shuffled, and the network was pulled toward each episode in turn as it met them in a row.

Without a fixed seed, a difference between two runs cannot be put down to the change that was made.

## Fix

Draw the starting weights and every epoch's shuffle from one stream made from a seed, and judge a change over a few seeds.

```cpp
rc::learn::Rng r = rc::learn::make_rng(seed);
for (int e = 0; e < epochs; ++e) rc::learn::train_epoch(net, scale, train, lr, r);
```

Results that hold for one seed only are not results; report the spread over several.
