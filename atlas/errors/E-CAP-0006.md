title: A map that is out of date, and a stop with no cause

match: a lost link stops the rover and keeps it stopped when the link returns, and a rover with no supervisor never notices
match: a sensor that goes dead is a stop with the supervisor, a stop without a fault with only the limiter, and a rover driving blind with neither
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-02-a-rover-in-a-building

## Symptom

The rover waits for ever at a doorway that has been clear for minutes, or stops with no word of why.

## Cause

A rover that never forgot waited 2296 ticks and did not arrive in 120 s; one that forgot after 4 s waited 301 and arrived at 27.2 s. A lost link stopped the rover for 2300 ticks with the cause link, and with the sensor dead it stopped after 2.4 m with the cause sensors, where the limiter alone stopped with no cause.

## Fix

Forget after a while, and keep the cause.

```cpp
if (t - no_path_since >= cfg.forget_after) map = prior_map(building);
```
