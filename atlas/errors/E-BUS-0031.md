## Symptom

A topic that exists and cannot be echoed or recorded from the command line, or a name with a hyphen or space that the middleware refuses in one place and accepts in another.

## Cause

A topic name has rules: letters, digits, underscores and slashes, no empty part, no trailing slash, no part starting with a digit, a tilde only at the start. The test accepted all 9 good names and rejected all 16 bad ones, among them scan/, a//b, 1scan, a b, a-b, ~x and a/~/b.

## Fix

Check the name when the topic is created.

```cpp
if (!valid_topic_name(name)) return false;
```
