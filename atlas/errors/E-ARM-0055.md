## Symptom

The planner says a motion is clear and the arm hits something thin between the poses it looked at. The obstacle that is hit is thin: a rod, a cable, a corner.

## Cause

The motion was checked at a few poses only. Between two poses the arm sweeps through space that was never asked about: fixed steps of 0.5 rad missed a sphere of 5 mm on a move of 2.0 rad, and steps of 0.01 rad found it, at 70 poses.

Checking poses along a motion says nothing about the space swept between them. A link moving through a step of 0.5 rad sweeps a large arc, and a thin obstacle can sit entirely inside a gap between checked poses.

## Fix

Use conservative advancement, whose step is bounded by the clearance so nothing can be skipped, or a fixed step small enough for the thinnest thing in the cell.

```cpp
const rc::arm::EdgeResult e = rc::arm::edge_conservative(check, a, b, bounds, margin);
```
