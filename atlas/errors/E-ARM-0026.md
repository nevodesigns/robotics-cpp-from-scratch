## Symptom

The calibration reports an error of almost nothing and the arm is not more accurate elsewhere.

## Cause

Too few measurements for the corrections: 4 poses gave 0.00000000 mm on their own and 0.83 mm on others, and 60 poses gave 0.101 mm.

## Fix

Measure many more poses than corrections, and report the error on poses kept back.

```cpp
rms_error(calibrated_table, held_out_poses);
```
