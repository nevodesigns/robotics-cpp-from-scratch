## Symptom

A longer vehicle is more stable at speed and turns badly on the spot, with torn ground and a hot motor.

## Cause

The front wheels slide at atan(L / B): 50.2 degrees for 0.30 by 0.25, and more as the chassis gets longer.

## Fix

Choose the shape for the work: short and wide to pivot, long and narrow to go straight.

```cpp
const double angle = front_slip_angle(wheelbase, track);
```
