## Symptom

The camera places the foot of a slope or a kerb at the wrong distance, and the planner mistakes a gentle rise for a wall. It is at the foot of a slope or a ramp.

## Cause

The ground was taken as a level plane. Over a rise, the ray meets the floor sooner than the level plane says.

The camera finds distance from a ray meeting a level plane. On a rise, the ground is higher than the plane, so the ray meets the real ground sooner, and the distance read is too far.

## Fix

Take the plane from the lidar of 24-02 where one is available, instead of assuming it is level.

```cpp
const rc::perceive::RansacResult g = rc::perceive::fit_plane_ransac(points, draws, 0.03, 30.0, seed);
// intersect the pixel's ray with g's plane, not with z = -height
```
