## Symptom

The arm reaches towards a place that is far from the object by about the base's own pose.

## Cause

A target in the world frame was given to the arm's inverse kinematics, which wants the arm's own base frame.

## Fix

Put the target through the inverse of the arm base's pose in the world.

```cpp
world_to_arm(m, base, world_point, arm_point);
```
