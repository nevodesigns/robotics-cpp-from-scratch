id: E-LEG-0043
title: A step aimed at the capture point of the push
match: the recovery step landed short because the body kept falling during the swing
match: the step was aimed where the body was going at the start
match: the capture point is where the body would come to rest, and grows exponentially if nothing is done
match: the foot must land beyond the capture point of the landing time, and the push that can be answered falls with the swing time
match: a foot landed at the target leaves the capture point inside it, and one landed short leaves it outside
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 35-07-taking-a-step-when-pushed
---

## Symptom

The robot steps to where the capture point was when it was pushed, and still falls, landing short.

## Cause

The capture point runs away exponentially while the foot swings: `xi0 * exp(t / T)`, 2.7 times for every time constant. With a swing of 0.25 s the foot had to land 24.5 cm out (the capture point at landing plus a margin of 2 cm), against 5.0 cm at the moment of the push.

## Fix

Aim at the capture point of the landing time, plus a margin, and check it is within reach. Landed at the target, the capture point was left 2.0 cm inside the foot, so the body stops over it.

```cpp
const rc::legged::StepPlan p = rc::legged::step_target(xi0, 0.25, 0.27, 0.25, 0.02);
if (p.ok) swing_to(p.target);   // 0.245 m out, within a reach of 0.25
```
