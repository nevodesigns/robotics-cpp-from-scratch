id: E-SIM-0001
title: Leftover time thrown away
match: real time that builds up is turned into whole steps, the remainder is kept for next time, and no time is lost
match: a frame shorter than a step does no work, and a negative or zero elapsed time adds nothing
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-01-a-simulator-is-a-loop-with-a-step
---

## Symptom

The simulation runs a little slow, by the same fraction on every frame, and a robot that takes 10 s in the sim takes 10.6 s of simulated time.

## Cause

A frame that does not fill a whole step still has time in it. A frame of 16 ms against a step of 10 ms is 1 step and 6 ms owed, and the next is 2 steps. Over 3.048 s of real time in frames of 3 and 16 ms the stepper took 304 steps and 0.8 of a step was still owed: no time lost, none invented. A negative or zero elapsed time adds nothing.

## Fix

Keep the remainder.

```cpp
accumulated_ -= steps * dt_;
```
