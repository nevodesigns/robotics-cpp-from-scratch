## Symptom

A planner that has put the arm into a collision cannot tell which way to move it out, and searches blindly. The planner's repair step moves the arm deeper in as often as out.

## Cause

The check returned only yes or no. A boolean says nothing about how far in or out the arm is, and a planner, a smoother and a monitor all need that. A signed distance does: with the floor at 0.4 m the arm was -0.160 m through it.

A yes or no tells a planner that a pose is bad, not which way is better. The signed distance has a gradient: moving to increase it moves the arm out of collision, and its value says how far to go.

## Fix

Return the signed distance, and the capsule and obstacle it is for, so the caller knows how deep and where.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Clearance c = rc::arm::scene_distance(caps, scene, true);
if (c.d < 0.0) report(c.capsule, c.other, -c.d);   // other -1 the floor, -2 another link
```
