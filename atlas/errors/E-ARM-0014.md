## Symptom

The solver passes its tests and fails from the poses the arm is actually in.

## Cause

The tests started within 0.6 rad of the answer (37 of 40). From all zeros it reached 31 of 40.

## Fix

Test from the starts the arm will have, near and far, and count the failures.

```cpp
ik_dls(table, target, base, tool, current_q, cfg);
```
