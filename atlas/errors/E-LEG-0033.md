## Symptom

The robot's idea of which way is level drifts steadily: after a minute of walking it leans to keep what it thinks is upright.

## Cause

The gyro was integrated alone. Integrating the rate adds up the bias too: at 0.5 degrees per second, a cheap gyro's bias, the angle was 23.1 degrees wrong after 60 s.

## Fix

Pull the integrated angle gently toward something that does not drift: the accelerometer's angle (k_acc 0.5 per second) took the error to 1.02 degrees.

```cpp
rc::legged::AttitudeFilter f;     // k_acc 0.5, gate 0.15
rc::legged::attitude_update(f, gx, gy, acc, feet_roll, feet_pitch, feet_valid, dt);
```

The pull leaves a steady offset of about the bias over the gain, here about a degree. Estimating the bias (25-07) removes it.
