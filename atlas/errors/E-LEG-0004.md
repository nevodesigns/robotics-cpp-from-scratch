## Symptom

On one side of the robot the lean torque is the wrong way round and the legs fight each other.

## Cause

The side's sign in the hip offset was left out of the Jacobian; it is in the lean column.

## Fix

Use the same geometry, with the side, in the kinematics and the Jacobian, and check it against differences.

```cpp
leg_jacobian(g, q0, q1, q2, j);
```
