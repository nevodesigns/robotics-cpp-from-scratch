## Symptom

The robot falls and lands rigid, and a servo is stripped or the shell cracks. Falls that should have been survivable break it.

## Cause

The legs stayed stiff as it landed. The energy of the drop, mass times g times height, is taken up over the stroke of the legs, and force is energy over stroke. A 12 kg robot dropping 0.19 m was hit with 1118 N over 0.02 m on stiff legs and 224 N over 0.10 m on soft ones: five times less.

## Fix

When a fall cannot be stopped, cut the joints' stiffness and keep their damping, so the legs give as the body lands.

```cpp
const double stiff = rc::legged::impact_force(12.0, 0.19, 0.02);   // 1118 N
const double soft = rc::legged::impact_force(12.0, 0.19, 0.10);    // 224 N
if (act == rc::legged::kFallSoft) joints.set_gains(kp_low, kd_kept);
```

After the fall the robot should not get straight up: the state machine of 36-02 checks first.
