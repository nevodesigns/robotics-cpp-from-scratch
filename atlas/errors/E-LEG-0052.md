## Symptom

A sudden push of the stick makes the robot lurch or stumble.

## Cause

The command was used as it came: a jump to 0.8 m/s should take 0.80 s at 1.0 m/s2.

## Fix

Ease the command in use toward the one asked for at a bounded acceleration.

```cpp
now.vx += clamp(t.vx - now.vx, -ax * dt, ax * dt);
```
