## Symptom

The arm presses on a surface with a force that depends on where the target was put, and breaks things when the target is a little too low. The force is right on one job and too high on the next, with the same settings.

## Cause

Under impedance control the force is the stiffness times how far the target is past the surface. With the target 2 cm in, 200, 500 and 1000 N/m gave 4.02, 9.75 and 19.00 N.

An impedance controller is a spring between the target and the tool. Against a surface the force is the spring's stiffness times how far the target is beyond the surface. Moving the target or the surface by a centimetre changes the force by the stiffness times a centimetre.

## Fix

Choose the stiffness for the force wanted and put the target the right depth past the surface, with the stiffness low along the direction of contact.

```cpp
const double k[6] = {2000, 2000, 500, 10, 10, 10};   // soft in z, the direction of contact
```
