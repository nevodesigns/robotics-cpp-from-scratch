## Symptom

A push response that works in tests with instant steps fails on the robot, whose servos take a quarter of a second to swing a leg.

## Cause

The swing time was left out of the design. Because the capture point grows exponentially while the foot is in the air, each tenth of a second of swing costs a large share of the push that can be answered. With a reach of 25 cm, the largest push answered was 0.56 m/s for a swing of 0.15 s, 0.31 m/s for 0.25 s and 0.17 m/s for 0.35 s.

## Fix

Design with the real swing time of the servos, and treat a faster swing as worth more than a longer leg.

```cpp
for (double t_sw : {0.15, 0.25, 0.35})
  log(t_sw, rc::legged::max_push_speed(0.25, 0.02, t_sw, 0.27));   // 0.56, 0.31, 0.17 m/s
```
