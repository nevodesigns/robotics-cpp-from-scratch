## Symptom

A learner by trial makes no progress for a long time, and then suddenly some, or never any.

## Cause

The only reward was at the end, so nothing guided the learner until a random run happened to get there. After 20 episodes the plain reward's policy had not found the goal (200 steps); with a hint shaped from a potential it took the shortest way, 4 steps.

## Fix

Add a hint shaped from a potential that measures progress, which speeds learning without changing what is best.

```cpp
rc::learn::q_learning_shaped(task, 20, 0.2, 0.99, 0.1, rc::learn::kPotential, seed);
```

A potential that is a poor measure of progress slows learning; it still cannot change the best behaviour.
