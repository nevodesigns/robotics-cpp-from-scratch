title: A distance read from a far row
match: the camera places a kerb 30 m away and it is 12 m
match: one pixel row is metres of ground near the horizon
match: a pixel looks at a point of the ground, and the point falls back on the same pixel
match: the horizon is above the centre of the image by the focal length times the tangent of the pitch, and nothing above it is ground
match: one row spans more ground the further away it looks, and nearly all of it at the horizon
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-08-the-camera-and-the-ground

## Symptom

Far obstacles from the camera are placed with errors of a metre or more and jump from frame to frame.

## Cause

Near the horizon one row spans a great deal of floor: 0.0090 m at the centre of the image, 0.139 m at row 140 and 0.82 m at row 120.

## Fix

Do not use the ground plane for distance beyond the range where a row is a small part of what matters; use another sensor, or accept the error and say so.

```cpp
const double resolution = ground_step(cam, height, pitch, v);
```
