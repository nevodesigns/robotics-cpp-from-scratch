## Symptom

When the receiver drops to a plain fix, the estimated position jitters and wanders though the robot is still. It is under trees or near buildings where the fix drops to a single solution.

## Cause

Every fix was given the noise of the best fix level. A plain single fix is good to metres, an RTK fix to centimetres, and treating them alike lets the poor fixes drag the estimate. On the single fixes the rms error was 2.81 m.

A receiver's error depends on its fix level: centimetres with RTK, metres with a single solution. A filter that gives every fix the same small noise trusts the poor fixes as much as the good ones.

## Fix

Give each fix the noise of its level, and ignore a fix whose level is none. Weighed by level the error was 0.67 m.

```cpp
const double sigma = rc::locate::fix_sigma(fix.fix);
```
