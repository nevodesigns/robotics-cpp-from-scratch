## Symptom

The rover heads for a frontier close beside it on the map, runs into a wall and spends a long time going round, when another opening was nearer.

## Cause

The straight line ignores walls. From (5,5) with a wall in between the nearest frontier cell as the crow flies was (19,5), 34 steps to drive, and the one with the fewest steps was (19,15), 24 steps.

## Fix

Search outwards over free cells and take the first frontier reached.

```cpp
if (wanted[index(p)]) { goal = p; return Explore::found; }
```
