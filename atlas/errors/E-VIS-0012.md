## Symptom

Speckle all over the background, thicker where the picture is noisier, with the marks found perfectly.

## Cause

Noise puts about half the pixels a little below their neighbourhood's mean. With noise of 6 levels and no offset, 0.269 of the background was marked. An offset of 10 marked 0.030, 20 marked 0.001 and found 0.987 of the marks, and 30 began to lose them: 0.755 found.

## Fix

Choose an offset above the noise and below the marks' contrast.

```cpp
const auto marks = adaptive_threshold(frame, 16, 20);
```
