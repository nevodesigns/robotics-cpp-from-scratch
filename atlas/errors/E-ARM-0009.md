## Symptom

Joint torques worked out for a force at the tool are wrong, or a joint's torque is not zero where it plainly should be. Force control pushes the wrong way, or a joint is loaded that should not be.

## Cause

The Jacobian was used the wrong way round (not transposed), or a wrench given in the tool's frame was applied as if in the base's. With the transpose in the right frame, the powers agreed to 0.000000000000000 W, and a vertical force asked 0.000 N m of a vertical first joint.

Joint torques for a wrench are the Jacobian transposed times the wrench, both in one frame. Using the Jacobian itself, or mixing a wrench in the tool frame with a Jacobian in the base frame, gives torques that do not balance the force. Power gives a test: torque times joint rate must equal wrench times velocity.

## Fix

Use the transpose, in the frame the wrench is in, and test with power: torque times rate equals wrench times velocity.

```cpp
const std::vector<double> tau = rc::arm::joint_torques(j, wrench);
```
