## Symptom

The heading from the gyro walks away, and a robot driving straight believes it is curving.

## Cause

The gyro was integrated with no bias removed. A bias is a constant error in rate, so it adds up in angle: the gyro alone ended 68.0 degrees out in two minutes.

## Fix

Estimate the bias, from the wheels going straight or from standing still, and subtract it before integrating.

```cpp
rc::locate::GyroBiasFilter bias;
const double rate = bias.update(gyro, wheel_rate, dt);   // gyro less the bias
```
