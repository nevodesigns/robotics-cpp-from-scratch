## Symptom

Things on the ground are placed too near or too far, more so with range, and the error changes when the vehicle brakes. The camera was mounted to a drawing.

## Cause

The camera's pitch was assumed. A pitch 1 degree wrong put row 140 37 percent out in range; a height 10 percent wrong put it 10 percent out. Braking pitches the vehicle and the camera with it.

A ray's meeting with the ground depends on the camera's height and pitch. A pitch error rotates every ray, and the error in range grows with the range because rays near the horizon are sensitive. Braking pitches the vehicle and adds to it.

## Fix

Calibrate the height and pitch with the vehicle at rest on flat ground, and add the vehicle's own measured pitch.

```cpp
double x = 0.0, y = 0.0;
rc::perceive::pixel_to_ground(cam, measured_height, calibrated_pitch + vehicle_pitch, u, v, x, y);
```
