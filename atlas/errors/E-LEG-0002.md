## Symptom

A leg bends the knee the wrong way, or two legs of the same robot bend opposite ways.

## Cause

Each place has two answers; the sign of the knee picks one, and 680 of 756 places were reachable with either.

## Fix

Use one knee sign for the whole robot, matching the way its knees are built.

```cpp
leg_ik(g, x, y, z, -1, q);
```
