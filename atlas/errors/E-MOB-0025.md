match: the base was fast enough when empty and could not stop when loaded
match: the speed limit was set without the payload
match: the speed to stop in a distance by braking at a fraction of the tipping deceleration
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Loaded, the base goes at its usual speed and cannot stop in time without tipping.

## Cause

The base's speed limit did not know about the load. The base may go only as fast as it can stop in the room it has, braking at a fraction of the tipping deceleration: with the load's 5.19 m/s2 and a margin of 0.8, stopping in 0.5 m allows at most 2.04 m/s.

## Fix

Derive the base's speed limit from the current tipping deceleration.

```cpp
const double v = rc::mobile::tip_speed_limit(5.19, 0.8, 0.5);   // 2.04 m/s
```
