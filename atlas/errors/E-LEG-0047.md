## Symptom

The robot is told it can climb steps of 20 cm because its legs are long enough, and at walking pace it catches its toe on a 5 cm step.

## Cause

The step height came from the geometry, which says 0.229 m. The swing has to lift the foot over the step and onto it within the swing time, and the servos have a rate. With a swing of 0.20 s and servos that give 8 rad/s, the highest step was 0.033 m.

## Fix

Find the step height from the joint rates and the swing time, and slow down to climb higher: a longer swing allows more.

```cpp
const double s = rc::legged::max_step_servo(leg, 0.10, 0.27, 0.20, 0.01, 8.0);   // 0.033 m at this pace
```

A robot that must climb stairs has to change its pace to do it.
