## Symptom

A policy learned from demonstrations does well from the starts the expert used and badly from others, though the expert itself would have managed. It shows the first time the robot starts somewhere the demonstrator never did.

## Cause

Demonstrations record where the expert went, and a good expert goes to the same few places: the centre of the lane, the middle of its reach. The states a learner most needs to be taught, those away from where the expert keeps things, are the ones with the fewest samples. Here 4000 samples from starts up to 0.6 m off centre never went further than 0.71 m from it.

A learner can only interpolate what it was shown; outside that, its output is whatever its form happens to give.

## Fix

Record from starts that cover the states the policy will meet, add noise to the executed action so the episodes wander off the expert's line, and later (38-06) ask the expert about the states the learner reaches.

```cpp
const auto d = rc::learn::demonstrations(task, 40, 100, 0.6, 0.4, 0.1, 0.3, seed);   // wide starts, noisy execution, a style for each episode
```

Noise in the executed action with the expert's own action as the label gives samples off the line with correct labels, which is the cheapest way to widen the data.
