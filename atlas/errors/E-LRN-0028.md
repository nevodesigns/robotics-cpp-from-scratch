## Symptom

A policy made from computed values is right near the goal and wrong, or arbitrary, far from it, and it changes when the computation runs longer.

## Cause

The sweeps were stopped before the values settled. Each sweep carries the effect of a reward one step further; in cells the rewards have not reached yet, all moves look alike and the greedy choice is arbitrary. On this grid value iteration took 36 sweeps to settle.

## Fix

Sweep until the largest change is below a tolerance, then take the greedy policy, and check that the policy's own value equals the optimal value.

```cpp
const auto V = rc::learn::value_iteration(g, gamma, 1e-10, &sweeps);
const auto p = rc::learn::greedy_policy(g, V, gamma);
```

A policy whose own value differs from the optimal value was built from values that had not settled.
