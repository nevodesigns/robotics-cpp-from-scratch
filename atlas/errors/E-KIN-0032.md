## Symptom

A tool that is on its orientation spins a full turn to correct a tiny error when its yaw passes plus or minus 180 degrees, or a controller reports an orientation error of 360 degrees.

## Cause

Euler angles repeat every turn and are not a space in which subtraction measures a distance. Measured: a yaw of -1 degree against 359 degrees was **-360** degrees as a difference and a rotation error of **9.4e-16** rad.

## Fix

Take the turn from one orientation to the other:

```cpp
rotation_vector(rc::math::multiply(target.r, rc::math::transposed(current.r)));
```
