## Symptom

The ground plane tilts toward a group of obstacles, and some ground is classed as obstacle. The scene has many obstacles on the ground.

## Cause

Every point voted equally in a least squares fit. With 450 points on boxes among 2000 on the ground, the fitted tilt was 6.57 degrees against the true 5.00.

A least squares plane is the plane closest to all the points, and points on obstacles are far from the ground. They pull the plane toward them, and it tilts. Ground points near the obstacles then sit above or below the tilted plane and are classed wrongly.

## Fix

Find the plane that most points agree on (RANSAC), and do not let the others vote.

```cpp
const rc::perceive::RansacResult r = rc::perceive::fit_plane_ransac(points, 100, 0.03, 30.0, seed);
```
