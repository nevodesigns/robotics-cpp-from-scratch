id: E-BUS-0036
title: A rule about two settings checked one at a time
match: a rule about the settings together is checked on how they would end up, so a pair that is fine together can be changed together
match: a valid change takes effect and is recorded with what it replaced
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-12-settings-that-change-while-running
---

## Symptom

A pair of limits cannot be moved up together because the first step breaks the rule, or a rule is satisfied step by step and broken at the end.

## Cause

A rule such as min_speed below max_speed is about the end state. Moving 0.1 and 1.0 to 2.0 and 3.0 was refused one at a time and accepted together, and 5.0 with 4.0 together was refused. A change that takes effect is recorded with what it replaced.

## Fix

Give the validator the whole proposed state.

```cpp
const std::string why = validator_(proposed);
```
