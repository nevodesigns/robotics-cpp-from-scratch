title: Treating the joints as independent when they are not
match: the arm follows a fast move badly though each joint is tuned
match: the tracking error grows with the speed of the move
match: with an exact model computed torque follows a fast move far more closely than a PD controller with gravity
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 29-05-using-the-model-to-move

## Symptom

Each joint is tuned and the arm lags a fast move, more at higher speeds.

## Cause

The coupling between joints (a full mass matrix and the velocity products) is a disturbance to a single joint loop: the joint PD with gravity had an rms error of 0.04293 rad on a fast move and computed torque 0.000056948.

## Fix

Compute the torque from the model for the wanted acceleration and add feedback on the errors.

```cpp
computed_torque(model, q, qd, q_ref, qd_ref, qdd_ref, kp, kv);
```
