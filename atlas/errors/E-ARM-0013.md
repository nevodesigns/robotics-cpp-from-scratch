## Symptom

When the target takes the arm near a straight elbow, the joints are commanded to move enormously and the arm jerks.

## Cause

The step was the pseudo-inverse, whose gain grows without limit near a singularity: 20.61 rad for an error of 1 cm, against 0.021 rad with damping.

## Fix

Damp the solve (lambda squared on the diagonal), limit the joint step, and say so when the result is inaccurate in the hard directions.

```cpp
a[r][r] += lambda * lambda;
```
