## Symptom

At one pose the platform gives way under a load it carries easily everywhere else. It was found by a test with a load in one direction; in the others the platform was stiff.

## Cause

The pose is near a singularity of the legs' Jacobian. There, a direction exists in which the platform moves while the legs hardly change, which is also a direction in which the legs cannot resist a force. The condition measure was 0.120 at home, 0.038 with the platform turned 80 degrees about the vertical, and 0.000 at 90: for this layout, a quarter turn is singular.

Near a singular pose the six legs' directions no longer span every motion of the platform. A small change of pose along the missing direction changes no leg's length, so no leg's force resists it. The condition measure says how close a pose is to that.

## Fix

Scan the working volume for poses where the condition measure is low, and keep the path well away from them.

```cpp
const double c = rc::arm::stewart_condition(hexapod, pose);
if (c < 0.05) refuse(pose);   // a threshold chosen for the load and the layout
```

The scan is of one geometry and one kind of motion; the full set of singular poses is a research subject.
