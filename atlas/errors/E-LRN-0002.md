## Symptom

The learned policy scores well on its test set and noticeably worse on new runs. The test error and the real error disagree by a steady factor.

## Cause

The test set was a random share of the samples, so each test sample had near copies of itself, from its own episode a moment before and after, in the training set. A learner that remembers does well on that test without having learned the task. A nearest neighbour, which only remembers, had an error of 0.00118 split by sample and 0.00317 split by episode, 2.7 times as much.

The same leak happens with any unit that ties samples together: a day, a robot, a person, a place.

## Fix

Split by the independent unit, whole episodes here, and check that no episode is on both sides.

```cpp
rc::learn::split_by_episode(data, 5, train, test);
assert(rc::learn::shared_episodes(train, test) == 0);
```

With more than one robot or operator, split by those too: the policy will be used on new ones.
