## Symptom

The battery runs down sooner than planned when the robot carries loads all day.

## Cause

The range was worked out for the empty base: 15509 m, against 13838 m with 10 kg aboard.

## Fix

Work out the energy per metre for the loaded base, and plan the shift with the loads it will have.

```cpp
range_m(drive, mass_with_load, speed);
```
