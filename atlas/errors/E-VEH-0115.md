title: A speed limit that forgets the delay
match: the operator drives into things they could see on the screen
match: the teleoperated vehicle cannot stop in the distance the operator sees
match: the speed a person can be allowed falls with the delay, and it does stop within the sight line
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The operator sees the wall in time and the vehicle hits it anyway. The operator reacted as soon as the wall appeared on screen.

## Cause

The speed limit used the braking distance alone. The picture is already old when it arrives, the person takes time to react, and the command takes time to travel back; all that while the vehicle keeps going. With 2.0 s of delay in all, the speed allowed for a 6 m sight line is 2.32 m/s, not the 4.00 of braking alone.

What the operator sees is old by the video delay; their hand takes a reaction time; the command takes its own delay to reach the vehicle. The vehicle covers all of that at full speed before braking begins, and only then needs its braking distance.

## Fix

Put the video delay, the reaction and the command delay in the latency of the stopping distance, and solve for the speed.

```cpp
const double v = rc::drive::operator_speed_limit(sight, video_delay, reaction, command_delay, decel);
```
