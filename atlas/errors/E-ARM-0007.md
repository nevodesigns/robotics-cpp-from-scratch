## Symptom

A tool velocity command makes the arm move in a plausible but wrong direction, in some poses more than others.

## Cause

The axis of each joint was taken from the frame before its row for a table that is in the modified convention (which puts it after): the Panda's Jacobian was 1.29 out.

## Fix

Take the axis from the frame the convention says, and keep a finite difference Jacobian as a test.

```cpp
jacobian(table, q, base, tool, table.convention);
```
