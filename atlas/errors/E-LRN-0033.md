## Symptom

A learning controller is run on the robot from an empty table, and the robot falls, crashes or drops things many times before it improves.

## Cause

Learning by trial learns from failures, and on a robot every failure is a real one. On the grid the learner fell into a pit 759 times in 2000 episodes while it learned.

## Fix

Learn in simulation first, carry the result to the robot with a margin (39-08), and on the robot keep a shield that refuses moves known to be dangerous (39-09).

```cpp
const auto Q = rc::learn::q_learning(simulated_task, 2000, 0.1, 0.95, 0.1, 0.0, seed, &st);   // in simulation
```

Count the failures during learning and report them: they are the price of the policy.
