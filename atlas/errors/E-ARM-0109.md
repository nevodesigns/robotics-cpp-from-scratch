## Symptom

The pose of the Stewart platform worked out from its leg lengths is far from the real one, or the solver never settles.

## Cause

The forward kinematics of a platform has no formula; it is solved by Newton's iteration, and the general problem has many solutions. From a guess near the answer it converged, finding the pose again to 0.000000000 in 4 steps; from a poor guess it may find another of the solutions, which fits the lengths and is not the platform's pose.

## Fix

Start from the last pose known (a controller always has one), and refuse a result far from it or one where the solver returned false.

```cpp
rc::arm::StewartPose pose = last_pose;   // the guess
int iters = 0;
if (!rc::arm::stewart_pose(hexapod, lengths, pose, &iters)) fault("no pose");
```

The other way, lengths from a pose, needs no search: at home all six legs were 0.343 m.
