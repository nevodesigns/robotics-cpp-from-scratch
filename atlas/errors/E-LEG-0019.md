match: a spike at lift-off counted as a step
match: a stepping foot gives one touchdown and one lift-off a step, found within a few readings
match: the blank keeps the lift-off spike out, and two thresholds keep a grazing foot from flapping
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 34-07-feeling-the-ground

## Symptom

Just after a foot is told to lift, the robot thinks it has touched down again, and the gait stalls or double steps.

## Cause

The leg's own acceleration when it lifts gives a spike in the estimated force that is not the ground: 12 N for 30 ms. Read as contact, it made 24 changes in six steps where there should be 12.

## Fix

Ignore touchdown for a short blank after the lift is commanded (60 ms here).

```cpp
rc::legged::ContactParams p;
p.blank = 0.06;   // s after a lift during which touchdown is not believed
```
