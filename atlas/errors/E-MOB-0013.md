title: A limit worked out for the arm tucked
match: the robot tipped when it braked with the arm out
match: the tilt limit was set with the arm folded
match: with no arm mass and no load the centre of mass is the base's, and masses pull it by their moments
match: reaching and carrying take the centre of mass towards the edge and cut the braking limit
match: a centre of mass straight over a base that is away from the origin is over the middle of its wheels
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The base brakes hard with the arm out and tips forward, though it was tested at that braking with the arm tucked.

## Cause

The braking limit was worked out for the arm tucked. The arm's links move the centre of mass: with the arm reaching 0.65 m forward it went from -0.006 m to 0.069 m ahead of the base's centre. The braking limit fell from 11.01 m/s2 tucked to 8.88 reaching.

## Fix

Work out the tipping limits for the pose and load the robot is actually in, every time either changes.

```cpp
double com[3];
rc::mobile::com_world(m, base, q, masses, com);
const rc::mobile::TipLimits t = rc::mobile::tip_limits(base, com, 0.25, 0.22);
```
