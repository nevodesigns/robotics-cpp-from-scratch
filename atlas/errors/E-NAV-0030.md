## Symptom

The rover is fast and slides wide on corners, or tips, or the wheels slip so the odometry is wrong afterwards.

## Cause

Sideways acceleration is speed squared times curvature. At a steady 2 m/s on a radius 1 m corner it was 4.00 m/s^2, against 1.00 that the wheels grip. With limits from the square root of a over k the same corner gave 1.00, and the limits were 0.707, 1.000 and 1.414 m/s for radii of 0.5, 1 and 2.

## Fix

Limit the speed by the curvature.

```cpp
limits[i] = std::min(max_speed, std::sqrt(max_lateral / k));
```
