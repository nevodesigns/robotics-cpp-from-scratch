title: A choice that cannot say none
match: the tool picked a robot that could not lift the part
match: the selection always returned something
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The selection always returns a robot, even for a job none can do.

## Cause

The choice took the best of a list without checking that the best passes: a job of 500 kg has to give -1.

## Fix

Return a refusal when nothing passes everything, and say which requirement stopped each one.

```cpp
if (best < 0) tell_the_user_why(verdicts);
```
