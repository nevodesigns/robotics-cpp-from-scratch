id: E-BUS-0037
title: A timer that does not follow the simulator's clock
match: a timer fires once for every period of the clock's time that goes by, and not at all while the clock stands still
match: firings per second of the clock do not depend on how fast the clock runs, firings per second of the wall do
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-13-whose-clock-is-it
---

## Symptom

A node keeps commanding a robot that the simulator paused, or a controller tuned at 100 Hz behaves differently when the simulation is sped up.

## Cause

A timer on the wall clock knows nothing of the simulator. On the simulator's clock, a period of 0.5 s fired 20 times in 10 s and 0 times in 1000 polls of a stopped clock, and fired 2.00 times per clock second at speeds of 0.5, 1, 2 and 10 (1.00, 2.00, 4.00 and 20.00 per wall second).

## Fix

Ask the clock the node was given.

```cpp
if (timer.poll(clock) > 0) run_controller();
```
