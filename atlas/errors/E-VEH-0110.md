## Symptom

Each time something blocks the path the vehicle stops, and waits for the planner.

## Cause

The blocked point was only noticed 0.6 m ahead, and a plan takes 2 s at 1 m/s: the vehicle waited 2.1 s, against 0.3 s with a look-ahead of 3 m.

## Fix

Check at least the speed times the planning time of the path ahead, and keep a short safety stop for the rest.

```cpp
cfg.check_ahead = speed * planning_time + margin;
```
