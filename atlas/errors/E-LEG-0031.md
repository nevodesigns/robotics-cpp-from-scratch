## Symptom

During a gait change one leg's phase goes the long way round and the foot makes a large, wrong move.

## Cause

The difference 0.95 to 0.05 was taken as 0.9 backward rather than 0.1 forward; phases lie on a circle.

## Fix

Wrap the difference to the range minus a half to a half before limiting it.

```cpp
d -= std::floor(d + 0.5);
```
