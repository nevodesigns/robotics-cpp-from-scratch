## Symptom

A robot that worked fine goes limp all at once when the motors reach their limit.

## Cause

Full torque was kept until the limit; derating from 0.8 gave 0.50 of the torque at 0.9 and 0.25 at 0.95, so the robot slows before it fails.

## Fix

Derate the torque as the temperature approaches its limit, and refuse to rise when hot (36-02).

```cpp
scale = (heat_off - heat) / (heat_off - heat_warn);
```
