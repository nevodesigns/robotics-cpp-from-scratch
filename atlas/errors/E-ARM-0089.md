## Symptom

Insertion works for small errors and the peg lands beside the chamfer for larger ones.

## Cause

Softness does not widen the chamfer. The chamfer reaches 3 mm out from the hole; a peg further off lands on the flat and the hand's give cannot bring it back.

## Fix

Keep the position error (31-03) within the chamfer's reach, and search (a spiral or a tilt) when it is not.

```cpp
if (std::fabs(e) > hole.clearance / 2 + hole.chamfer_w) search();
```
