## Symptom

A policy with a tiny error per decision does badly over a whole run, and the longer the run, the worse.

## Cause

An error per decision of 0.000014 measures single answers in the expert's states. In use, each small error moves the policy a little off the expert's path, into states where its errors are larger, which move it further: the errors compound with the length of the run.

## Fix

Judge a policy on whole runs from a spread of starts, and report the share that succeed, not the error per sample.

```cpp
const double share = rc::learn::success_rate(task, &policy, seen, 0.6, 0.8, 100, seed, 300);
```

The error per sample is still useful for comparing two networks on the same data; it is not a test of driving.
