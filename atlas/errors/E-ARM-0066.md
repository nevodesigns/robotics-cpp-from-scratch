## Symptom

After smoothing, the path passes much closer to obstacles than the plan did.

## Cause

The shortcut was checked with no margin: the clearance along the path was 0.0001 m against 0.0202 m with the planner's margin kept.

## Fix

Use the planning margin in every pass that moves the path.

```cpp
shortcut(problem, path, n, seed, problem.margin);
```
