## Symptom

At rest the arm creeps back and forth by a small amount, with a period of a second or so.

## Cause

The load's encoder is inside the position loop, which has an integral, and the gear has a dead zone of 0.0100 rad: the load moved 0.0013 rad over a second; with the motor's encoder 0.000000 rad, but 0.0034 rad off.

## Fix

Close the fast loop on the motor, the slow one on the load with a deadband, or approach every target from the same side.

```cpp
c.feedback_load = false;
```
