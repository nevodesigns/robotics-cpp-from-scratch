## Symptom

The vehicle cannot follow the arcs of a short path and overshoots the turns.

## Cause

The path was planned with a radius of 0.4 m for a car whose smallest is 0.742 m: a curvature of 2.50 per metre against a limit of 1.35.

## Fix

Take the radius from the vehicle model (wheelbase over the tangent of the largest steering angle), with a margin.

```cpp
const double r = wheelbase / std::tan(max_steer);
```
