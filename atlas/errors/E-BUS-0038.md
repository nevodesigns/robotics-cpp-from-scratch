## Symptom

After the simulator skips ahead, the node sends hundreds of identical commands in the same instant and the robot lurches.

## Cause

A jump of 100 s over a period of 0.5 s leaves 200 periods due. A timer that makes up each one fired 200 times at once; one allowed to catch up once fired 1 and counted 199 skipped, and carried on from the next period.

## Fix

Limit the catch up and count what is skipped.

```cpp
SimTimer control(0.01, 1);
```
