## Symptom

Localisation usually works and sometimes settles in the wrong place. It happens in a minority of runs, with a different seed.

## Cause

Too few particles. With 50 over the room the robot was lost in 10 of 10 runs; with 3000, in 0.

Particles must cover the places the robot might be. With few of them over a large space, the chance that none is near the truth at the start is high, and the cloud settles on the best wrong one.

## Fix

Use enough particles to have some near the truth at the start, and more for a bigger map.

```cpp
rc::locate::MonteCarlo mc(map, 3000, seed);
```
