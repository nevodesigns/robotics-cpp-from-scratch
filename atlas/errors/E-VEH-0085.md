## Symptom

The vehicle arrives near the goal but not lined up with it, and misses a dock or a gate by a few centimetres or degrees. It is the last metre that is wrong, at a dock, a charger or a gate.

## Cause

The search keeps one pose to a coarse cell and stops within a tolerance (0.3 m and 20 degrees in 26-01), so the path ends near the goal pose, 0.073 m from it here, not on it.

Hybrid A* stops when it is within a tolerance of the goal, because hitting a pose exactly on a grid of headings is unlikely. A Dubins path joins two poses exactly with arcs of a given radius and a straight, so it can finish the job.

## Fix

Finish with a Dubins path from the last pose to the goal pose. It ends exactly: within 0.000000000 m over 200 pairs of poses.

```cpp
const rc::drive::Dubins tail = rc::drive::dubins_shortest(last, goal, turn_radius);
```
