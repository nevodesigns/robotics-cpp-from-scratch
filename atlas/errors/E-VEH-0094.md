## Symptom

The controller's predicted path turns tighter than the vehicle can, so anything relying on the prediction is wrong, though the tracking looks fine. The predicted trajectory is drawn on the screen and is tighter than the car's real turns.

## Cause

The rollouts used a unicycle for a car. Tracking was hardly worse (0.018 m against 0.047 m), because the plan is remade from the real pose every step, but 2 s of the same controls ended 0.67 m from where the car really went.

A sampling controller rolls out its candidate controls through a model. A unicycle can turn on the spot, so its rollouts make turns the car cannot. Because the controller replans every step from the real pose, tracking is fine; the predictions are not.

## Fix

Use the vehicle's own model in the rollouts, with its curvature limit.

```cpp
rc::drive::MotionModel m;
m.bicycle = true;
m.wheelbase = 0.33;
m.max_steer = 0.4189;
```
