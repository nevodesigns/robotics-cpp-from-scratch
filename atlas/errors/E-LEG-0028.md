title: A body left off the line of its feet
match: the trot drifted to one side and fell
match: a few centimetres of body offset made the trot fail
match: a body falls away from the line of its feet sooner the further off it starts, and later the taller it is
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 35-02-falling-and-catching-in-a-trot

## Symptom

The trot works on a good day and falls on another, with nothing obvious changed. It falls more after a turn or with a payload slightly off centre.

## Cause

The body was not kept over the line between the two feet that are down. The time a trot has before it must catch itself falls quickly with the offset: 0.41 s from 10 mm, 0.22 s from 30 mm, and 0.15 s from 30 mm if the body is already moving away at 0.10 m/s. A centimetre or two decides it.

## Fix

Hold the body over the line of the stance feet as a control target, and treat an offset of a couple of centimetres as a reason to shorten the stance.

```cpp
const double t = rc::legged::time_to_move(offset, rate, 0.06, h);
if (t < stance + 0.1) gait.shorten_stance();
```
