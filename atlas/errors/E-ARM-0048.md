## Symptom

A servo arm reaches its goals unloaded and sags under load, more at the shoulder.

## Cause

The servo's loop is a spring: a load of 2 N m on 15 N m/rad drooped 0.133 rad; with the goal shifted by the load over the stiffness it was 0.0002 rad off.

## Fix

Shift each goal by the model's torque of gravity (and payload) over the servo's stiffness.

```cpp
joint.set_goal(goal_for_load(spec, wanted, load));
```
