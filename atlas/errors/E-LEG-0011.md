## Symptom

At speed the body gets ahead of its feet and the robot keeps speeding up or falls forward.

## Cause

The feet were put down under the hip: the body goes 0.050 m ahead of the hip in half a stance at 0.4 m/s, and with no speed term it was 20 mm too far for 0.2 m/s over.

## Fix

Use Raibert's rule: half the stance distance, and a gain times the speed error.

```cpp
touchdown_xy(hip, v, v_cmd, t_stance, k, out);
```
