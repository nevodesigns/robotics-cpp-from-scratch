## Symptom

Rounds of collecting on the learner's path make no improvement: the learner after each round behaves as it did before.

## Cause

The samples collected on the learner's runs were labelled with the learner's own action, so the new learner was trained to repeat its mistakes. With the expert's labels, two rounds took the success from 87 to 98 percent, against the expert's 98, and the error on the learner's own states from 0.01082 to 0.00009.

## Fix

Move the car with the learner's action, and record the expert's action as the label.

```cpp
x.a = rc::learn::lane_expert(task, e, h);        // the label
rc::learn::lane_step(task, e, h, rc::learn::learner_act(policy, e, h));   // the motion
```

Measure the success after every round; when it stops rising, more rounds are not the answer.
