## Symptom

A controller trained by a policy gradient has a gain that jumps past the good range, or goes unstable during training.

## Cause

Each step moves the gain by the learning rate times a noisy estimate. A large rate turns one unlucky batch into a large step, possibly past where the controller is stable. At a rate of 0.002 the gain climbed from 0 to 0.898, near the best 0.916.

## Fix

Choose the learning rate by trying a few over a factor of ten, use a baseline to quieten the estimates, and limit the size of a single step.

```cpp
k += std::max(-0.05, std::min(0.05, lr * g));   // a cap on one step
```

On a robot, a gain that goes unstable during training is a trial that fails hard; cap the step.
