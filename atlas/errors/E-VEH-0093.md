## Symptom

The vehicle takes the inside of a corner and touches what the planner went round.

## Cause

A follower with a lookahead cuts corners. At a corner of 90 degrees the car came within 0.38 m of the corner point on the raw path and 0.41 m on the smoothed one.

## Fix

Leave clearance in the plan for the cut, or inflate the grid by the follower's cut as well as the vehicle's radius, and check the traced path against the grid.

```cpp
rc::perceive::inflate(grid, robot_radius + follower_cut, inflation_radius, scale);
```
