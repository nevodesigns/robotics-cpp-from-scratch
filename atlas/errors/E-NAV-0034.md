## Symptom

The rover announces that the map is complete while part of the building is unseen, or it drives to a speck in the middle of a room and back.

## Cause

A single cell missed inside a seen room is a frontier of 4 cells, and a minimum size of 5 ignores it where 1 goes to it. With nothing left of that size the answer is finished; with some left and no route over free cells it is unreachable. A sealed room with a sliver seen through a window, and a start inside a wall, both gave unreachable, and everything seen gave finished.

## Fix

Keep the three results apart.

```cpp
enum class Explore { found, finished, unreachable };
```
