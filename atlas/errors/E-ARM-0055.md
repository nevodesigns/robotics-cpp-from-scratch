## Symptom

The planner says a motion is clear and the arm hits something thin between the poses it looked at.

## Cause

Fixed steps of 0.5 rad missed a sphere of 5 mm on a move of 2.0 rad; steps of 0.01 rad found it, at 70 poses.

## Fix

Use conservative advancement, whose steps are bounded by the clearance, or a step small enough for the thinnest thing there is.

```cpp
edge_conservative(check, a, b, bounds, margin);
```
