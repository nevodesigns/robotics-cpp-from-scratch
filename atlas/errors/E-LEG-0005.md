## Symptom

A robot that only stands gets warm, and its battery runs down with it not moving.

## Cause

At 0.20 m the knee gives 5.43 N m just to stand, against 1.88 N m at 0.40 m.

## Fix

Stand as high as the control allows, and work out the heat of the stance (34-06).

```cpp
stand_torques(j, ground, tau);
```
