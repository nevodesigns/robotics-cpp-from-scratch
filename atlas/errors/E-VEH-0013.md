id: E-VEH-0013
title: A braking limit taken from the straight line
match: the vehicle slides when it brakes in a bend at the braking limit of the straight
match: the braking available falls when the vehicle is turning
match: on a straight line the budget is the whole grip, less the rolling resistance
match: a turn takes a share of the grip and the rest is what is left to speed up and to brake with
match: past the edge the tyres let go, and the speed at which that happens is the one the limit gives
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 23-05-the-traction-circle

## Symptom

The vehicle brakes as planned on a straight and slides when the same braking is asked in a bend.

## Cause

The turn took 69 percent of the grip at 1.5 m/s round 0.742 m, and the braking available fell from 4.81 to 3.60 m/s2.

## Fix

Take the braking limit from the budget at the speed and curvature of the moment, not from the straight line.

```cpp
const TractionBudget b = traction_budget(cell, heading, speed, curvature);
```
