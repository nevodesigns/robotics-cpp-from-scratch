## Symptom

The scan match drifts when someone walks near the robot. Someone walked past the robot during the scan.

## Cause

The pairing distance had no limit, so points on the person, with no true partner, were paired with the nearest wall and pulled the match: 0.042 m.

ICP pairs every point with something. A point on a person has no partner in the other scan, so it is paired with the nearest wall, and that false pair pulls the match toward it.

## Fix

Limit the pairing distance, and shrink it as the match converges. With 0.3 m the pull was 0.000 m.

```cpp
const rc::locate::IcpResult r = rc::locate::icp(source, target, guess, rounds, 0.3, tol);
```
