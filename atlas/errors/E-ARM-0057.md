## Symptom

Obstacles were made bigger to keep the arm away, and now the arm cannot reach what it has to touch.

## Cause

The margin was put in the obstacles, so it applies everywhere, including at the part being picked. A pose with 3 cm of clearance is free with no margin and not with 5.

## Fix

Keep the margin in the check, not the scene, and set it to zero or small for the final approach to a thing the arm is meant to touch.

```cpp
const bool ok = rc::arm::free_pose(check, q, approaching ? 0.0 : 0.05);
```
