## Symptom

The robot stops at every dashed section, or drives on blind for a second across a bend.

## Cause

A gap of 0.2 m was crossed; one of 0.6 m stopped the robot at debounces of 3, 10 and 25 frames and was crossed at 50. A camera dead at 10 s stopped it at 10.04 s, 0.022 m further at 0.4 m/s and 0.122 m at 0.8 m/s, and the black box held three offsets and then three frames with none.

## Fix

Choose the debounce from the longest gap and the tightest curve.

```cpp
config.lost_debounce = frames_for(gap_metres, speed);
```
