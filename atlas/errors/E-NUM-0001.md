id: E-NUM-0001
title: A conversion that always reports zero
match: division .* zero
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 00-03-numbers-that-lie
---

## Symptom

A sensor conversion reports 0.0 for every input except the maximum. Nothing warns
and nothing crashes.

## Cause

Whole number division discards the remainder. In (raw / 4095) * 3.3, the division
happens first and gives zero for every raw below 4095, and zero multiplied by
anything is still zero.

## Fix

Multiply before dividing, or make one operand fractional: (raw * 3.3) / 4095.0.
The rule worth carrying is to divide last, or divide in doubles.
