## Symptom

The vehicle reaches a bend at full speed and slides or overshoots. The profile has a cap at each bend and nothing before it.

## Cause

The speed was capped at the bend and not before it. Going into a bend of radius 1 m, the capped speeds asked for a deceleration of 20.0 m/s2, where the limit is 1.5.

A cap at a point says how fast the vehicle may be there. It cannot get there at that speed if it was going much faster just before, because braking takes distance. A backward pass makes each point's speed one from which the next can be reached.

## Fix

Add the backward pass: no point faster than one from which the vehicle could still brake to the next. With it the deceleration was 1.50 m/s2.

```cpp
for (std::size_t i = n - 1; i-- > 0;)
  v[i] = std::min(v[i], std::sqrt(v[i + 1] * v[i + 1] + 2.0 * a_dec * ds[i]));
```
