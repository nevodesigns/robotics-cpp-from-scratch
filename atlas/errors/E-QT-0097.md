## Symptom

After a drop the station makes two connections at once, or its retry timer fires twice as often as it should, and the count of attempts in the log goes up by two for each drop.

## Cause

A link that was up and is closed by the other end reports an error and a disconnection. A handler on both schedules a retry from each. Measured: with a guard on the state, the drop scheduled **1** wait, of **20** ms; a failed attempt reported only the error.

## Fix

Schedule only if a retry is not already waiting:

```cpp
if (!running_ || state_ == kWaiting || state_ == kStopped) return;
set_state(kWaiting);
```
