## Symptom

The part stays in the fingers while the arm is still, and slips the moment it lifts or turns quickly.

## Cause

The squeeze was worked out for the weight at rest. The hand's acceleration adds to gravity: a kilogram at rest took 9.81 N at a friction of 0.5, and lifted at 5 m/s2 it took 14.81 N.

## Fix

Work out the force with g plus the largest acceleration of the moves the part goes through, and a safety factor on top.

```cpp
const double f = rc::arm::required_force(hand, mass, accel, 1.5);   // accel of the fastest move
```

A turn adds a centripetal acceleration as well, and a sideways one tests the friction in a direction the pads may grip less.
