title: A reconnect loop with no wait, or a wait that never grows
match: a connection that is refused is retried after waits that grow, and a success starts them again from the base
match: the wait doubles for each failure up to a cap, and a large number of failures does not overflow
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 12-01-the-link-that-dropped
---

## Symptom

A station fills its log with connection attempts while the robot is off, uses a core, and when the robot comes back it is met by thousands of attempts at once. Or its wait is a minute from the first failure, and a robot that rebooted is not found for a minute.

## Cause

A retry at a fixed short wait is a busy loop with a socket in it, and a fixed long wait is slow to recover. The wait should start short, double, stop at a cap, and start short again after a success. Measured with a base of 20 ms: the waits were **20**, **40**, **80**; a success took the count of failures back to **0**; with a base of 100 and a cap of 5000 the waits reached **5000** and stayed.

## Fix

Double the wait for each failure up to a cap, and reset on success:

```cpp
for (int i = 0; i < attempt && delay < cap_ms; ++i) delay *= 2;
// and in on_connected:  attempts_ = 0;
```
