## Symptom

A policy fails a job's success requirement, is retrained many times with more data and bigger networks, and never passes.

## Cause

The job asked for starts some of which no controller can recover from: within 0.95 m and 1.2 rad the DAgger policy succeeded on 73 percent and failed at requirement 2, while still beating the line. The expert, run from the same starts, says what is possible.

## Fix

Run the expert, or the best hand-written controller, from the job's starts first; a job that it fails is a job to change, not a policy to retrain.

```cpp
const double ceiling = rc::learn::success_rate(task, nullptr, seen, job.offset0, job.heading0, 100, 77, 300);
```

Write the ceiling into the job, so the requirement is a share of what is possible.
