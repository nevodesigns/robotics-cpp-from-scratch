## Symptom

When the tool points straight up or down, the roll and yaw of the pose swing wildly with no movement. It shows only when the tool points straight up or down.

## Cause

Euler angles have a hole. At a pitch of a quarter turn roll and yaw turn about the same axis, and only their sum is fixed: a rotation a hair away differed by 57 degrees in roll.

Roll, pitch and yaw describe a rotation as three turns about fixed axes. At a pitch of a quarter turn, the first and third axes line up, and only their sum is defined; an arbitrarily small change in the rotation can swing each of them a long way.

## Fix

Do not filter, limit or interpolate the three angles. Work with the rotation (or a quaternion), and take the angles only for display.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Rpy shown = rc::arm::rpy_from_rotation(r);   // for a person to read, nothing else
```
