## Symptom

The estimated position jumps several metres for a fix or two and comes back.

## Cause

A fix thrown by a reflection was used. The worst error over five runs was 6.04 m with no gate and 2.29 m with one.

## Fix

Test each fix against the prediction with a chi-square gate and refuse the ones outside it.

```cpp
if (d2 > cfg_.gate) return false;
```
