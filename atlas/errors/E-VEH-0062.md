## Symptom

The estimated position jumps several metres for a fix or two and comes back.

## Cause

A fix thrown by a reflection off a building was used. Multipath gives fixes that look normal and are metres out. Over five runs the worst error was 6.04 m with no gate.

## Fix

Test each fix against the prediction with a chi-square gate and refuse those outside it. With the gate the worst was 2.29 m.

```cpp
if (d2 > cfg_.gate) return false;   // refused
```
