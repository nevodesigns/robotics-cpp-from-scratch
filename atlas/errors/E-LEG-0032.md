## Symptom

At the moment of a gait change a foot jumps or a leg lags and drags.

## Cause

The offsets were switched at once: a leg's phase jumped 0.250 of a cycle in one tick, where moving it at 0.2 cycles per second gave 0.0002 a tick.

## Fix

Move the offsets toward the new gait's at a bounded rate.

```cpp
step_offsets_toward(offsets, target, rate, dt);
```
