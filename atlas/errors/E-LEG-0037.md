## Symptom

A large demand for a level body makes some feet unload completely and the body tips anyway.

## Cause

The most that could be given was 17.7 N m (the weight times the half width); more means a foot would have to pull.

## Fix

Limit the moment to what the feet can give, keeping its direction, and treat the limit as the edge of the centre of pressure's range.

```cpp
foot_forces(feet, on_ground, W, mx, my, f) == false
```
