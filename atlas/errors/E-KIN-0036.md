## Symptom

Short moves overshoot, or take longer than they should, or the cruise time comes out negative and a move never ends.

## Cause

The cruise time is the distance left after the two ramps divided by the speed. For a short distance that is less than nothing: the ramps would pass each other. The move is a triangle with a lower peak. Measured: for 0.25 the cruise would have been negative; the peak was **0.707107** and the total **0.707107** s; at 0.5 the triangle just reached the limit.

## Fix

Test whether the ramps fit, and use the triangle if not:

```cpp
if (2.0 * ramp_distance >= d) { t.v_peak = std::sqrt(amax * d); t.t_cruise = 0.0; }
```
