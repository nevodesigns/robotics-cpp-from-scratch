## Symptom

The robot with the largest reach volume is bought, and it does a flat table job slowly while taking a lot of floor. The purchase was justified by the biggest number on the comparison sheet.

## Cause

Volume was used as the criterion. The six axis arm's 1.731 m3 is twenty times the SCARA's 0.084 m3, but the SCARA works in a thin slab (a 0.15 m slide stroke) because its job is a flat table, and most of the arm's ball is of no use there.

Volume counts every point the tool can reach, including the many that the job never uses. A robot that works in a thin slab over a table can have a small volume and be the best at that job, and one with a large ball of reach can be slower and take more room.

## Fix

Choose from the task: its area, payload, tilt, accuracy and cycle time. Use the volume, and the share of the robot's room it uses (0.33 for the SCARA, 0.98 for the arm), as a check, not as the answer.

```cpp
const int f = rc::arm::choose_family(robots, families, n, task, verdicts);
```
