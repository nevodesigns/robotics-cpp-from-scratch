match: a 12 kg robot can rise, a slow rise uses more of the knee's thermal life than a fast one, and the heaviest bodies are limited by torque and by heat
match: a shallower crouch is easier, and the heaviest body that can rise falls as the crouch deepens
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A robot carrying a heavy payload falls, and then cannot get up again: it can walk with the load and cannot rise with it.

## Cause

Walking and rising have different limits. In the walk the knees hold a share of the weight with the legs fairly straight; in the rise they lift all of it from a crouch, where the lever is longest. The lesson's quadruped job check refused 30 kg of load at requirement 4 for exactly this: the knees in a trot with the load, and whether the loaded robot can still get up from a crouch.

## Fix

Check the rise with the payload on as part of deciding whether to carry it, and treat "cannot get up again" as a reason to refuse the load.

```cpp
rc::legged::Job loaded = job;
loaded.payload = 30.0;
const rc::legged::JobVerdict v = rc::legged::check_job(robot, loaded);
if (!v.getup_ok || !v.knees_ok) refuse_load();   // first_fail 4 for 30 kg
```

A robot that can fall must be able to recover with what it carries, or it must be able to drop it.
