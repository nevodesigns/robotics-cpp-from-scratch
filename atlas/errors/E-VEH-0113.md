match: the vehicle repeats the first legs after it restarts
match: the mission restarts from the beginning after a power cycle
match: restoring saved progress goes on from the leg, where starting again drives the first legs twice
match: progress that does not fit the list is refused
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 27-01-a-list-of-places-to-go
---

## Symptom

After a restart in the middle of the job the vehicle drives the first waypoints again.

## Cause

Nothing was saved. After a restart at the third of four legs the vehicle drove 19.4 m with the saved progress and 38.4 m without.

## Fix

Save the leg and the results as text after every leg, restore them at start, and refuse text that does not fit the list.

```cpp
store.write(mission.save()); mission.restore(store.read());
```
