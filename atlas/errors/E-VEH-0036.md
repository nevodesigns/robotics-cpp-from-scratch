match: the vehicle will not drive between two posts it could pass
match: two posts are one wall to the clustering
match: two posts close together are one object to a radius that is too big for the near range, and two to one that grows with it

## Symptom

The planner finds no way between two obstacles that are further apart than the vehicle is wide.

## Cause

A radius made big enough for the far wall joined the posts at 2 m into 1 object; the grown radius kept 2.

## Fix

Keep the radius no bigger than the spacing of the points at the range they were seen.

```cpp
cluster_points(points, 0.05, 1.5 * angle_inc, 1);
```
