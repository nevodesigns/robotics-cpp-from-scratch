title: A monitor blind to the fault it was not built for
match: a sensor that stops changing is noticed only when it was expected to change
match: a healthy run raises no alarm, and each fault is caught after a delay that depends on what it is
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-06-breaking-it-on-purpose
---

## Symptom

A frozen sensor is not noticed for seconds, or a lone spike goes through, and the monitor is working exactly as it was written.

## Cause

Each monitor sees one kind of fault. With a threshold of 0.02 over 3 readings, an offset of 0.3 was caught after 0.02 s, a drift after 0.46 s, a spike one reading in 25 never, and a stuck sensor at the target after 2.85 s. The stuck monitor caught it when the robot was asked to move: an unchanging reading with no motion expected gave no alarm in 20 readings and with motion expected one at the 5th.

## Fix

Use a residual monitor and a stuck monitor together, and a filter for spikes.

```cpp
StuckMonitor stuck(25, 1e-9);
```
