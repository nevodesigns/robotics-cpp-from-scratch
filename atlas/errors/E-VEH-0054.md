## Symptom

Two people meet, stop and walk on, and afterwards the track of one has the velocity of the other.

## Cause

When two targets are close and change what they are doing, the detections are nearer the wrong predictions. In 20 runs people passing a metre apart swapped 0 times and two standing side by side 5.

## Fix

Do not trust the identity of tracks through close encounters, or keep more than one hypothesis.

```cpp
// treat the ids of two tracks that have been close as unknown
```
