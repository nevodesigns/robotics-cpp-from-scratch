## Symptom

The arm replans round something that goes away a moment later, and the move takes much longer.

## Cause

A plan round the obstacle took 4.20 s where waiting took 2.15 s (against 1.50 s with nothing in the way).

## Fix

Wait a moment before planning round, if the thing may be leaving, and plan only if it stays.

```cpp
cfg.replan = false; // wait first
```
