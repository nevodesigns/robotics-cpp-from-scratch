## Symptom

A SCARA is bought for a job and turns out unable to present the tool at an angle, which one step of the job needs. It is found when the first part with an angled face arrives.

## Cause

The family was chosen for its speed without asking what it cannot do. A SCARA has four values: q1 and q2 place the tool in the plane, the slide sets its height and the roll its heading. There is no axis to tilt it. The same layout is why it is fast: its first two axes are vertical, so a 2 kg payload put 0.0 N m on them, where a six axis arm holding it 0.4 m out carries 7.85 N m on its shoulder.

The SCARA's speed and stiffness come from the same choice that removes the tilt: two vertical axes that gravity does not load, a slide and a roll. Adding a tilt means adding a wrist, which puts the weight back on the joints the design kept free of it.

## Fix

Write the task's need for a tilt down before choosing. Use a SCARA for flat work from above and a six axis arm (phases 28 to 31) where the tool must tilt.

```cpp
rc::arm::TaskSpec task;
task.needs_tilt = true;   // rules out the SCARA, the delta and the gantry in check_family
```

Writing the task as numbers first (32-06) would have ruled it out before purchase.
