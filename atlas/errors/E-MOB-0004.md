## Symptom

The planner parks the base where the target is within the arm's length of the base's centre, and the arm still cannot reach it.

## Cause

Reach was measured from the base's centre. The arm's axis is at the mount, some way forward, and with the tool pointing down the arm reaches a ring around that axis, not a disc: at a target 0.10 m below the mount, from 0.00 m to 0.72 m from the axis.

## Fix

Find the arm's axis from the base pose and the mount, and test the target's distance from it against the ring.

```cpp
const rc::mobile::Annulus ring = rc::mobile::reach_annulus(m, -0.10, 0.01);   // 0.00 to 0.72 m
const bool ok = rc::mobile::annulus_can_reach(m, ring, base, target);
```
