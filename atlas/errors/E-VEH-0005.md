## Symptom

The motors strain or stall in a pivot, though they drive the same robot forward with ease.

## Cause

A pivot was asked of a floor that cannot pay for it. Turning on the spot drags the wheels sideways, which needs mu m g sqrt(L squared + B squared) / (2 B) from each side: 34.5 N on gravel, against the 28.0 N available.

## Fix

Work out the force a pivot needs before planning one, and plan a wider turn where the floor cannot pay for it.

```cpp
if (!rc::vehicle::can_pivot(thrust, mass, mu, wheelbase, track)) use_an_arc();
```
