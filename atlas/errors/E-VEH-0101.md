title: A stop line met at speed
match: the vehicle stops after the stop line, not at it
match: the vehicle overshoots the point where it should stop
match: the speeding up is within the limit, from rest
match: a stop line is reached at zero speed, with the slowing begun in time
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-07-how-fast-the-path-can-be-driven
---

## Symptom

The vehicle reaches the point where it should stop at speed and stops past it.

## Cause

Nothing planned the slowing: with a cap of zero at the line, the slowing must begin 1.30 m before it, at the braking limit.

## Fix

Put a cap of zero at the stop point and run the backward pass.

```cpp
caps[stop_index] = 0.0;
```
