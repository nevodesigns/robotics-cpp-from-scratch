## Symptom

The budget of the grasp says the hand will be within 2 cm, and it is often out by more, sideways.

## Cause

A heading error was counted as if it were a position error. It is multiplied by the lever from the base's centre to the hand: one degree at 0.9 m is 16 mm. With the base known to 20 mm along an axis and to a degree in heading, the mount to 0.2 degree and the arm repeatable to 0.1 mm, the hand's sigma came to 25.6 mm.

## Fix

Add the errors in quadrature, with the angular ones multiplied by the lever first.

```cpp
rc::mobile::Sigmas s;   // pos 0.020, heading 0.01745, mount 0.0035, arm 0.0001
const double sigma = rc::mobile::hand_sigma(s, 0.9);   // 0.0256 m
```
