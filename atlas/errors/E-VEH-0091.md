## Symptom

On a straight the vehicle weaves from side to side, or on a curve it cuts inside the path. The lookahead was tuned at one speed and the vehicle now goes at another.

## Cause

The pure pursuit lookahead was too short for the speed, or too long for the curve. At 0.1 m the vehicle crossed the path 9 times on a straight, against 2 at 1.5 m; at 2.0 m it strayed 0.149 m from a curve, against 0.015 m at 0.6 m.

Pure pursuit steers toward a point a lookahead ahead on the path. A short lookahead aims at a point close by and corrects hard, so at speed it weaves; a long one aims far ahead and cuts the inside of curves.

## Fix

Make the lookahead a time ahead at the speed, within a range that suits the tightest curve on the path.

```cpp
rc::drive::FollowConfig cfg;
cfg.lookahead = std::max(0.6, 1.0 * speed);
```
