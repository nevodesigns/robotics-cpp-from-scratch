## Symptom

The hand's path bends when the base turns, though the arm's joints are doing the right thing.

## Cause

A turn of the base about its centre sweeps the hand round it; the second column of the whole-body Jacobian is that sweep, and it was left out.

## Fix

Put the base's rates in the Jacobian with the hand's position relative to the base's centre.

```cpp
wb_jacobian(m, base, q);
```
