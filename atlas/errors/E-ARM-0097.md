## Symptom

The follower copies the leader's motions and is a constant angle away from it.

## Cause

The two arms' zeros were taken as equal. Each joint's reading less the leader's zero plus the follower's zero is what maps them, and both zeros come from putting the two in the same pose.

## Fix

Calibrate the zero of each arm in a known pose, and store it with the arm.

```cpp
map_joint(map, leader_reading);
```
