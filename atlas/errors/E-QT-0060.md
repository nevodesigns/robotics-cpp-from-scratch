## Symptom

A test of a chart passes on the machine that made the expected image and fails on the others, and the two pictures look identical.

## Cause

Antialiasing is not specified to the last bit, and a tenth of a pixel of difference moves a few coverage values. Measured on Qt 6.2: **150** pixels differed exactly, by at most **16** of 255, and none by more than 64.

## Fix

Give each pixel a tolerance, and allow a count of strays:

```cpp
if (delta > tolerance) ++d.differing;
```
