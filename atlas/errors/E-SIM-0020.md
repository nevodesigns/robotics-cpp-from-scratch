title: Streams whose numbers depend on the order of asking
match: a stream gives numbers that are spread evenly from 0 up to but not including 1, and normal ones with mean 0 and spread 1
match: two parts of a simulation with the same master seed get streams that are not correlated, and the same part gets the same numbers on every run
match: asking for a stream a second time gives the same stream, carrying on from where it was, and the order the streams are first asked for in changes nothing
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-07-one-seed-many-streams

## Symptom

A refactoring that only reorders when parts are created changes the simulation's output, or a part's numbers differ between two runs of the same seed.

## Cause

Each part's stream must come from its name and nothing else. Streams asked for in opposite orders gave the motor 0.912040 then 0.609638 and the imu 0.190753 in both. Two streams from one seed had a correlation of -0.0040, and a stream asked for again carried on and did not restart. 100000 uniform numbers had mean 0.4992 and variance 0.0830, and normals mean 0.0001 and spread 0.9999.

## Fix

Make the stream the first time its name is asked for, and give the same one after.

```cpp
if (found == streams_.end()) found = streams_.insert({name, Stream(derive_seed(master_, name))}).first;
```
