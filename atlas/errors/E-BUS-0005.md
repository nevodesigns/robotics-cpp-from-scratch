id: E-BUS-0005
title: A deeper queue chosen to cure a slow subscriber
match: a deeper queue absorbs a burst, and does nothing for a subscriber that is slower all the time
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-02-a-queue-for-each-listener
---

## Symptom

Messages are still lost after the queue was made a hundred times deeper, and what arrives is seconds old.

## Cause

Depth is a buffer, and a buffer rides out a burst: 8 messages lost 7, 4, 0 and 0 at depths 1, 4, 8 and 10. It does not cure a rate. A publisher at 100 Hz for 10 s against a subscriber taking 10 Hz lost 900, 891 and 801 of 1000 at depths 1, 10 and 100.

## Fix

Size the depth to the burst, and fix the rate mismatch where it is.

```cpp
bus.subscribe("scan", burst_length, Overflow::drop_oldest);
```
