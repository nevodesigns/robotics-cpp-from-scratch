## Symptom

Repeating the calibration gives a different track each time. The calibration was one spin each way.

## Cause

One spin was trusted. The floor varies from spin to spin (the turn efficiency by 3 percent here), and direction does not cancel that, only averaging does. One spin each way had a spread of 0.0034 m; four each way, 0.0019 m.

The floor varies from place to place and spin to spin. One spin each way averages two samples of that variation; more spins average more, and the spread shows how good the estimate is.

## Fix

Use several spins each way and report the spread with the estimate.

```cpp
const rc::vehicle::Spread s = rc::vehicle::spread_of(estimates);
```
