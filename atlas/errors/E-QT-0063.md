## Symptom

The terminal chart of a sensor is a calm flat line and the window chart of the same sensor is a storm, or the other way round. Both are the data of one robot.

## Cause

Each view worked out its own range, with its own padding and its own idea of a steady signal. Measured with one model: 5 plus or minus 1e-6 was **1** of 13 rows and **1** of 130 pixel rows, and without the minimum span **11** of 13 and **119** of 130.

## Fix

Work out the range once, in the model, and give it to both:

```cpp
Range range() const { return value_range(decimate(samples_, t0_, t1_, 1), min_span_); }
```
