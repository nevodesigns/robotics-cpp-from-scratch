id: E-ARM-0096
title: A throughput that ignores the person
match: the cell is slower when someone is at the station
match: the cycle time was quoted with no one near the robot
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 31-07-how-many-parts-an-hour
---

## Symptom

The cell is slower on the line than in the test.

## Cause

A person 1.0 m away capped the tool at 1.23 m/s and the cycle became 3.77 s; at 2.0 m it was 3.68 s.

## Fix

Work out the throughput for the distances people really stand at, or lay the cell out so they stand farther.

```cpp
cycle_with_person(params, ssm, separation);
```
