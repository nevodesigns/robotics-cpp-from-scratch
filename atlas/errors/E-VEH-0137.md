## Symptom

The vehicle drives into what is in the road and presses on against what holds it.

## Cause

No checklist, field, modes or ladder: of the same day it completed 2 of 4 legs, with 1 contact, took 975 s and ran out of charge 150 m from the dock.

## Fix

Do not run a vehicle without the layers of phase 19 and 27: each one is a test passed by a day like this.

```cpp
run_day(cfg, true);
```
