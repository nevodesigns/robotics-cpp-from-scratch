## Symptom

The beam turns slightly and positions are out by an amount that grows with the distance from the centre.

## Cause

The two ends were driven apart by 0.2 mm on 1.0 m: 60 micrometres of error at a lever of 0.3 m.

## Fix

Measure both ends and correct the difference (or drive with one encoder as master and the other following).

```cpp
rack_error(skew, span, lever);
```
