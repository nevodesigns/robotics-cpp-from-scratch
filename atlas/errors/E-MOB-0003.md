## Symptom

Grasps near the base's centre succeed and those far from it miss.

## Cause

A heading error is multiplied by the distance to the hand: 1 degree at 0.9 m is 15.7 mm, where a position error of 5 mm stays 5 mm.

## Fix

Know the heading to a fraction of a degree before reaching, or correct with the camera at the end (33-07).

```cpp
hand_error_from_base(base, hand, dpos, dheading);
```
