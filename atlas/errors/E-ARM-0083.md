## Symptom

The hand to eye calibration gives a different transform each time it is run, or none at all.

## Cause

It was fitted to too few points, or to points that all lie in a plane. With 2 mm of noise on what the camera saw, 5 points gave errors of 3.5 mm and 0.64 degrees, and 40 points 1.1 mm and 0.20 degrees; points in a plane do not fix the rotation, and the fit fails.

## Fix

Use many points spread through the volume the arm works in, check that the fit succeeded, and check its residual against a limit.

```cpp
const rc::arm::FitResult f = rc::arm::fit_rigid(camera_points, base_points);
if (!f.ok || f.rms > 0.003) recalibrate();
```
