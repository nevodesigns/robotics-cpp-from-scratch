## Symptom

The tilt from the feet is noisy by a degree or so even on flat ground and with good joint angles.

## Cause

The feet are close together. Tilt is a height difference over a distance, and the feet are only about 0.4 m apart. With 5 mm of noise in each coordinate of each foot, the roll was uncertain by 0.92 degrees with four feet down and 1.29 degrees with three.

## Fix

Use the feet's tilt as a slow, driftless correction to the IMU (35-04), not as the attitude itself.

```cpp
rc::legged::attitude_update(f, gx, gy, acc, p.roll, p.pitch, feet_down >= 3, dt);
```

A wider stance measures tilt better; a fourth foot averages some noise out.
