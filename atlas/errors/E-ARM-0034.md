title: Gains for one pose used for all
match: the arm is smooth in some poses and shaky in others
match: the controller was tuned with the arm folded
match: the inertia the shoulder sees changes several fold over the poses, and a gear makes it change less
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 29-03-a-gain-that-knows-the-pose

## Symptom

The joint moves well in the pose it was tuned in and rings, or is sluggish, in others.

## Cause

The inertia a joint sees changes with the pose. The shoulder's went from 0.691 to 2.091 kg m2 over the poses, a ratio of 3.0, and the gains were for one of them.

## Fix

Work the gains out from the inertia of the present pose, or tune for the worst case if the loop must stay simple. A gear of 100:1 with a rotor of 1e-4 kg m2 brought the ratio down to 1.8, because the rotor's inertia does not change.

```cpp
const double J = rc::arm::effective_inertia(model, q, 1, rotor, gear);
const rc::arm::CascadeGains g = rc::arm::scheduled_gains(J, wp, wv);
```
