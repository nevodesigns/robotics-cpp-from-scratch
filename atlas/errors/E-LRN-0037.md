## Symptom

A learned behaviour collects a high reward and never completes the task: it oscillates, circles or repeats a small motion.

## Cause

The reward paid for getting closer, and nothing charged for getting further away, so a step away and a step back earn a bonus every two steps for ever. Over 200 steps the learner collected 101 in bonus and never reached the goal. The learner is not broken; the reward specified the oscillation.

## Fix

Pay hints as the change in a potential (gamma times the potential after less the potential before), so that their discounted sum along any run depends only on where it starts and ends.

```cpp
r += gamma * potential(next) - potential(s);   // not: if (closer) r += 1
```

Any reward paid for a state rather than for a change in it can be farmed by staying near that state.
