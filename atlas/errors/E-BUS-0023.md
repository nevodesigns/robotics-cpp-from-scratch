title: A deadline as short as the period
match: a deadline equal to the period cries wolf at the slightest jitter, and one a little longer than the worst gap never does
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-08-too-late-to-be-true

## Symptom

A healthy sensor is reported late over and over, and the team learns to ignore the deadline warnings, including the real ones.

## Cause

Messages do not arrive exactly on time, and the worst gap is the period plus the jitter on both ends. With a message every 0.10 s and up to 0.03 s of jitter, 2000 messages gave 992, 441, 124 and 0 misses at deadlines of 0.10, 0.12, 0.14 and 0.17 s.

## Fix

Set the deadline to the period plus the worst measured jitter and a margin.

```cpp
DeadlineMonitor lidar(0.17);
```
