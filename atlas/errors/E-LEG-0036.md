## Symptom

The body tilts toward a lifted leg, or the controller commands a force on a foot that is in the air, which does nothing.

## Cause

The weight was shared among four feet whether or not they were down, or divided by four when only three carried it. The forces must add to the weight (12 kg, 117.7 N) over the feet that are on the ground. With four down and no moment wanted, each carried 29.4 N; with three down, the three must carry all of it.

## Fix

Solve for the forces of the feet that are down only: three conditions (the weight and two moments), least norm when four are down, exact when three are.

```cpp
bool on_ground[4] = {true, true, false, true};
double f[4];
rc::legged::foot_forces(feet, on_ground, 117.7, mx, my, f);   // f[2] stays zero
```

With fewer than three feet down there is no solution, and the function says so.
