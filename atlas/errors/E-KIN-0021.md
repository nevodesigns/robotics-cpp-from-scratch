## Symptom

A move between two points the arm can reach fails in the middle. The planner reported both ends as fine, and the
arm stops, or is sent a stream of `not a number`, part of the way along. It happens only on moves that pass near
the base, and it is intermittent as a result of where the points happen to be.

## Cause

The workspace of a two link arm is not convex. It is a ring: the arm cannot reach beyond the sum of the links, and
it cannot reach inside the difference of them, the hole round the base that the shorter link cannot reach into. Two
points in the ring can have a straight line between them that passes through the hole.

Measured, 0.5 m and 0.4 m links, a hole of radius 0.1 m, a line from (0.30, 0.05) to (-0.30, 0.05): both ends are
reachable, and the line enters the hole at point 72 of 200, where `|x|` falls below 0.0866.

## Fix

Check every point. `cartesian_path` solves at each step and, where the solver says unreachable, stops: it reports
`complete = false` and where, and returns the poses before it and nothing after. The points beyond may be reachable
again, and the arm cannot get from one side to the other, so carrying on would hide the gap.

Then decide what the failure means. A joint move between the same two ends does exist, and it goes round: it
strayed from the straight line by more than 0.05 m. The planner can offer that, or the line can be replaced by a
path that goes round the hole on purpose.
