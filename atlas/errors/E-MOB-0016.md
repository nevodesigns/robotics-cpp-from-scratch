## Symptom

Loaded, the base accelerates slowly or its wheels spin; empty it was fine. On a slippery floor the wheels spin before the motors reach their torque.

## Cause

The wheels' force was worked out for the empty base. Acceleration is force over total mass, until the grip gives out: with 80 N of drive and 40 kg it is 2 m/s2. For a wanted acceleration of 0.5 m/s2 the heaviest load the wheels allowed was 130.7 kg, so here the wheels were not the limit, but they have to be checked.

Acceleration is the drive force over the total mass, and the drive force is limited twice: by the motors and by what the floor's friction gives the wheels. A heavier load lowers the acceleration for the same force, and on a slippery floor the grip is the limit, not the motors.

## Fix

Work out the acceleration with the load on, and the grip of the floor.

```cpp
const double a = rc::mobile::base_accel_limit(drive, base_mass + payload);
```

A floor with too little friction gives no budget at all.

Both limits belong in the payload budget; here traction was far from binding, but that is a result of this floor.
