title: A task asked of a position that cannot support it
match: the robot is told to dock within 10 cm using a position that is good to a metre
match: needs_fix
match: the distance a robot may go before it might be 1 m out, or 5 m, is worked out before it sets off
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 25-06-how-lost-can-it-be

## Symptom

A task that wants centimetres is given a position good to metres. The task was a docking or a precise pick at the end of a long drive.

## Cause

Nothing compared the bound with what the task needed. The robot could go 27 m before it might be 1 m out, and 80 m before 5 m.

Every task has a tolerance, and every position has an uncertainty. The task can only succeed if the uncertainty is inside the tolerance, and nothing checked that.

## Fix

Check the bound against the task's tolerance and get a fix before it is crossed, or refuse.

```cpp
if (dr.needs_fix(task_tolerance)) get_a_fix();
```
