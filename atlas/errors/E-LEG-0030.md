## Symptom

Walking at a speed near the changeover, the robot keeps switching between walk and trot, stumbling at every change.

## Cause

The gait was chosen with one threshold. A speed that hovers around it crosses it at almost every reading, and every crossing is a change of gait. With the speed wandering between 0.3 and 0.7 m/s around 0.5, one switch speed changed the gait 175 times in 400 readings.

## Fix

Use two speeds: change up above one and back down only below a lower one. With 0.6 up and 0.4 down the same readings changed it 0 times.

```cpp
gait = rc::legged::gait_for_speed(v, gait, 0.6, 0.4);   // up to the trot above 0.6, back to the walk below 0.4
```

The gap is hysteresis, as for a thermostat. Its width is set by how much the speed estimate wanders.
