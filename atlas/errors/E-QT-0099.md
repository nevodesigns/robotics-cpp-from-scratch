## Symptom

The numbers on the screen stop changing and nothing else does: the link indicator is green, no error is logged, and the robot is not there. It stays that way until someone notices.

## Cause

A TCP connection is closed by a message, and a robot that loses power or hangs sends none. The socket stays connected until something is sent and not acknowledged, which can be hours. Measured: **300** ms of silence from a server that was up left the socket connected with **0** bytes.

## Fix

Judge by the age of the last message, with a watchdog:

```cpp
if (age >= lost_) return Health::kLost;
```
