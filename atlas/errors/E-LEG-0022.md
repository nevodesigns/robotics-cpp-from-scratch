## Symptom

A calibration gives nonsense offsets, or refuses, though the measurements are good.

## Cause

The poses could not tell the joints apart. With the leg straight in every pose, a little more knee and a little less hip move the foot the same way, and the equations are singular.

## Fix

Use poses with the leg bent, leaned and swung by different amounts. A single bent pose in fact fixes three offsets, but a dozen let the noise average.

```cpp
if (!rc::legged::calibrate_offsets(leg, q, p, n, 30, delta, &rms)) choose_more_varied_poses();
```
