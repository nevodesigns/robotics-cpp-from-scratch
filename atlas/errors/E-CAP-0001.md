## Symptom

The line follower works in the demo at walking speed and wanders at double it, or a higher gain is tried and the robot is worse.

## Cause

At 0.4 m/s the rms distance from the line was 0.0983, 0.0324, 0.0152 and 0.0182 for kp of 1.5, 3, 5 and 8. At 0.8 m/s with kp 5 it was 0.0454, 3.0 times that at 0.4 m/s.

## Fix

Tabulate the error against the gain and the speed.

```cpp
const LineResult r = follow_line(track, config, fault, seconds);
```
