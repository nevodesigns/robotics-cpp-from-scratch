match: the protective field is too small at high speed
match: a device with a few field sets uses the smallest that was designed for the speed, and refuses a speed it has no set for
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

Above the largest field set, the vehicle is protected by a field too short for its speed. The top speed was raised in software without anyone looking at the scanner's sets.

## Cause

The safety device has sets for 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 m/s and the vehicle was at 2.1: no set was long enough. Choosing a set below the speed is choosing too short a field.

A safety scanner has a fixed number of fields, each designed for a speed. Above the highest, every field is too short, and picking the nearest leaves the vehicle unprotected beyond it.

## Fix

Choose the smallest set designed for at least the speed, and slow the vehicle when there is none. At 1.1 m/s the set for 1.5 is 1.46 times longer than needed: the price of the safe choice.

```cpp
if (rc::drive::select_field_set(speed, design) < 0) speed = design.back();
```
