## Symptom

Positions are out by an amount that grows with the distance from the beam's centre, and the beam turns slightly. The error is zero in the middle of the beam and grows toward its ends.

## Cause

A wide beam driven from both ends had its two ends out of step, so it racked. Ends 0.2 mm apart on a span of 1.0 m put 60 micrometres of error at a lever of 0.3 m: the angle times the lever.

A beam driven at both ends turns if one end lags the other. The turn is the difference over the span, and a point some way from the centre moves by that angle times its distance, as any angle error does.

## Fix

Measure both ends and correct the difference, or drive one end as master and the other as follower, and check the ends after homing and after any stall.

```cpp
const double e = rc::arm::rack_error(0.0002, 1.0, 0.3);   // 60 um
```
