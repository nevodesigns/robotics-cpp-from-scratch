## Symptom

The emergency stop is pressed while the robot is standing and it collapses onto its belly, hard. People learn not to press it.

## Cause

Off was taken to be safe. For a wheeled robot it is; for a legged one standing up it is not, because with no torque the legs fold and the body drops, with the landing force of 35-09 (1118 N on stiff legs). The safety layer turns off while standing into soft: low stiffness with the damping kept, so the body is let down, and only goes to off when it is down. The stop with the robot standing gave mode 2 (soft), and sitting 3 (off).

## Fix

Make the stop two stages for a legged robot, and tell the people who may press it that the robot will go down.

```cpp
rc::legged::SafetyIn in;
in.estop = pressed;
in.standing = !sitting;
const rc::legged::SafetyOut s = rc::legged::safety_decide(in, lim);   // kSafeSoft while standing, kSafeOff when down
```

This is a model of the logic. A real stop function runs on separate hardware with its own path to the motors (19-02).
