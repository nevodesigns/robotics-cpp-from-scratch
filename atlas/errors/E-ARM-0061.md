## Symptom

Some queries on a roadmap find no path, though one exists. The queries that fail are those between two parts of the cell separated by a gap.

## Cause

The roadmap had too few nodes to join the free space into one piece: it had 6 pieces at 40 nodes and 5 at 300. It answered 7 of the queries with a free end both times in this roomy scene, and would answer fewer in a tighter one.

A roadmap joins nodes that can see each other. Where the free space narrows, few random nodes land in the gap, and the graph splits into pieces that the query cannot cross. Counting the pieces says whether that has happened.

## Fix

Count the pieces, and add nodes until they join; for narrow gaps sample near the obstacles, or grow a tree from the query's ends.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Roadmap rm = rc::arm::build_roadmap(problem, 300, 6, seed);
if (rc::arm::components(rm) > 1) add_nodes_near_obstacles();
```
