id: E-LEG-0065
title: A controller that does not know the bound is coming
match: the balance controller wasted the wide window and had nothing in the narrow one
match: the recovery was slow in a trot and fast in a walk
match: when the bound on the ZMP changes with the support, the predictive controller recovers from larger pushes than a clipped fixed gain law that cannot see it coming
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 36-05-looking-ahead-with-the-body
---

## Symptom

The balance recovery is slow in some phases of the gait and fine in others.

## Cause

A fixed gain law looks only at the bound now: for a push of 0.6 m/s the body took 1.00 s to settle against 0.60 s when the bound ahead was known.

## Fix

Give the controller the schedule of the ZMP bound for the steps ahead.

```cpp
double bound[15]; for (k) bound[k] = bound_at(i + k);
```
