## Symptom

At one pose the platform moves under a load that it carries easily elsewhere.

## Cause

The leg Jacobian is nearly singular there (condition 0.038 at 80 degrees of yaw and 0.000 at 90, against 0.120 at home): the legs cannot resist a force in some direction.

## Fix

Scan the working volume for poses where the condition measure is low and keep the path away from them.

```cpp
stewart_condition(hexapod, pose);
```
