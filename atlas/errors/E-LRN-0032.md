## Symptom

A Q-learned policy is good along the route it learned on and wrong in cells off it, which the robot reaches after a slip or a push.

## Cause

A cell's values are only as good as the number of times it was visited. After 2000 episodes the learned policy agreed with the optimum in 17 of 17 cells; the ones it got wrong last are the ones the learner rarely went to, where a few noisy outcomes decide.

## Fix

Count visits per cell and treat the policy in seldom-visited cells as untrusted (a guard, as in 38-07), or start some episodes from those cells.

```cpp
if (visits[s] < min_visits) use_fallback(s);
```

Starting episodes from a spread of cells, not only the start, is the cheapest way to fill them.
