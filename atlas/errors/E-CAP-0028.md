## Symptom

The report of the day says that jobs were delivered and nothing was hit, and the hazards nobody has tested are nowhere in it.

## Cause

The day on 1.5.0 delivered 3 of 5 jobs, was stopped in 2 (link and sensors), had 0 bumps and 2 operator resets, kept a verified black box of 10 events, and its last line read Open: hazards with no scenario: G3, G5, G6, G7.

## Fix

End the report with what has no test.

```cpp
out << "Open: hazards with no scenario: ";
```
