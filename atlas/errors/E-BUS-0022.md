title: A deadline reported at every check
match: a silence longer than the deadline is a miss, reported once, and a message makes the monitor ready to report the next
match: a publisher that dies is noticed one deadline after its last message, and not before
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-08-too-late-to-be-true
---

## Symptom

One stalled sensor fills the log with thousands of identical lines, and the real error scrolls off the screen.

## Cause

A silence is one event however long it lasts. The monitor reports it once and is armed again only by the next message. Two silences gave 2 misses, a publisher that died after its last message at 1.0 s was noticed at 1.26 s with a deadline of 0.25 s, and nothing was reported before the first message.

## Fix

Remember that a silence has been reported.

```cpp
if (!started_ || reported_) return 0;
```
