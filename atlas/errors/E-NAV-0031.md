## Symptom

The rover brakes hard at the last moment before each corner, or the speed command jumps and the motor controller faults.

## Cause

The limits give a speed for each point and nothing for getting between them. With points 5 cm apart a drop from 2 to 1 m/s is nearly 20 m/s^2: 19.81 for both gain and loss. A forward and a backward pass limited them to 1.00 and 1.50. The time cost of a stricter limit was 12.45 s, 12.90, 13.60 and 14.49 for corner speeds of 2.00, 1.41, 1.00 and 0.71 m/s.

## Fix

Build a profile from the limits and the wheels' acceleration and braking.

```cpp
const auto speeds = speed_profile(path, limits, 1.0, 1.5, current_speed);
```
