## Symptom

A controller that works on one robot fails on another of the same design.

## Cause

Robots differ in inertia, delay and lag: over 100 random joints the gains settled on 46 percent (stiff) and 97 percent (gentle).

## Fix

Test against a spread of parameters (draw many, count the successes) and choose gains with a margin.

```cpp
success_rate(nominal, spread, max_delay, max_lag, n, seed, target, secs, dt);
```
