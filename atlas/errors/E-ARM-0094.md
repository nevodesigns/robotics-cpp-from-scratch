## Symptom

The cell makes fewer parts an hour than its specification said. The specification was written from the robot's data sheet.

## Cause

The throughput was worked out from the moves at the rated speed and left out the rest of the cycle. The lesson's cycle of six moves and four waits took 3.68 s at the rated speed, 977 an hour, and that included the hand's 0.8 s and the contact approaches' 1.07 s; a figure from the moves alone is much shorter.

A cycle is the moves and the waits between them. The waits (closing and opening the hand, settling) and the slow moves onto contact do not shrink with the robot's speed, and caps on speed from people or payload stretch the rest.

## Fix

Add every piece of the cycle with the limits that apply to it, then measure it on the cell.

```cpp
const double t = rc::arm::cycle_time(cell, 1.0);   // 3.68 s
```
