title: A watchdog whose answer is not connected to what moves the robot
match: a robot that goes quiet is lost and the speed is zero, and when it comes back the station does not move it until the key is pressed again
match: expected after_return
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 12-12-a-station-you-can-ship

## Symptom

The indicator says the robot is lost and the station goes on sending speeds to it. Or, when the robot comes back, the station sets off by itself.

## Cause

A monitor that only shows a state protects nothing. Its answer has to reach the interlock, and the interlock has to require a deliberate press to start again. Measured: with the robot silent the station said **lost** and the speed was **0**; with the robot back and the key still held the speed was **0**; after a release and a press it was **1**.

## Fix

Connect the health to the interlock, and treat only alive and heard as alive:

```cpp
interlock_.set_link_alive(heard_ && health == static_cast<int>(rc::qt::Health::kAlive));
```
