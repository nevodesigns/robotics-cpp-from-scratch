id: E-CAP-0011
title: A delay or a filter that works just inside its edge
match: a delay between the filter and the wheels is harmless up to 50 ms and fatal from 55, because the body falls with a time constant of 0.12 s
match: a filter that is too quick passes the accelerometer's noise to the wheels, and one that is too slow lets the gyro's bias build up until it falls
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 21-04-a-robot-that-balances
---

## Symptom

The robot balances for weeks and falls over the day the IMU driver is updated, or the day it is slightly warmer.

## Cause

A delay of 0 to 50 ms stood (rms lean 0.0092 to 0.0164) and 55, 60 and 70 ms fell at 2.79, 1.55 and 1.04 s. With a gyro bias of 0.05 rad/s, a time constant of 0.05 s fell at 1.15 s, 0.2 and 0.5 s stood and drifted -4.87 and -9.38 m, and 2, 5 and 1000 s fell at 7.77, 4.71 and 3.92 s.

## Fix

Find the edges of the delay and the filter and stay well inside them.

```cpp
config.delay_frames = 4;
config.filter_tau = 0.5;
```
