title: A tolerance wide enough to miss a wrong pixel
match: one wrong pixel is found at any tolerance below 255, with the rectangle that holds it
match: expected d.differing to equal 1
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 10-12-the-pixel-that-differed

## Symptom

A chart test never fails again after it is loosened, even when the chart is clearly wrong.

## Cause

A tolerance is a bound on one channel of one pixel. A wrong pixel is found while the tolerance is below the size of its error. Measured: a pure red pixel on white differed by **255**, was found at a tolerance of **254**, and not at **255**.

## Fix

Keep the tolerance small and add a count, and report where:

```cpp
d.bounds = d.bounds.isNull() ? QRect(x, y, 1, 1) : d.bounds.united(QRect(x, y, 1, 1));
```
