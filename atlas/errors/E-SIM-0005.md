title: A plant that does not clamp, tested only on where it ended
match: a motor that follows its command with a lag reaches the speed gain times command, and does it in about one time constant for each 63 percent
match: a command beyond the limit does exactly what the limit does, in either direction
match: a controller written against the interface drives either plant, and the plant with no clamp behaves differently where it matters
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

The gains tuned in simulation make the real motor slam, trip a current limit or saturate, and the simulation showed a smooth move to the same end point.

## Cause

A real motor cannot act on a command beyond its limit. A controller driving two plants to position 1 ended at 1.0000 on both, and the plant with no clamp reached a peak speed of 22.71 against 14.70, past what the motor can do. A command of 500 gave exactly the position a command of 12 gave, in both directions, on the simulated motor.

## Fix

Clamp the command in the plant, and test peak speed and not only the end.

```cpp
const double u = command > limit_ ? limit_ : (command < -limit_ ? -limit_ : command);
```
