## Symptom

A recorded session is a binary blob, or a text file that a typo has quietly cut short, and the test either cannot be reviewed or compares against half a conversation.

## Cause

A transcript as text, an arrow and hexadecimal bytes to a line, reads back identically: the three messages of the example round tripped, and a 3 request session was 6 lines. A hand edited file may have comments, blank lines and lower case, and a bad line (an unknown arrow, half a byte, a non hexadecimal digit, or two bytes with no space) is refused with its number and leaves the transcript empty.

## Fix

Keep it as text and refuse a bad line.

```cpp
error = "line " + std::to_string(line_number) + ": bytes are two hexadecimal digits each";
```
