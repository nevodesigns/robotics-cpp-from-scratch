## Symptom

After a wheel spins on loose ground the heading is wrong for the rest of the run.

## Cause

The wheels' turn was used without a check. A slip that halved the turn for 2 s left the wheels alone 3.1 degrees out; a gate on the difference refused it.

## Fix

Gate the wheel/gyro difference against the estimate and refuse what is too far from it.

```cpp
if (innovation * innovation <= gate * gate * s) update(); else ++refused;
```
