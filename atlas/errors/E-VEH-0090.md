## Symptom

The smoothed path rounds a corner by cutting through an obstacle on the inside of it.

## Cause

The smoother knew only the points. With no grid, 5 points ended inside the inflated obstacle.

## Fix

Refuse any move into a cell that is inscribed or worse (0 points inside with the check).

```cpp
if (cell_blocked(grid, nx, ny)) continue;
```
