## Symptom

The fitted offsets change wildly from run to run, or are large.

## Cause

With the leg straight in the poses the knee's offset cannot be told from the hip's; a fit says false, and a poor set of bent poses leaves the noise in 1.95, -3.16 and 4.19 degrees.

## Fix

Use poses with the leg bent, leaned and swung by different amounts, and a dozen of them.

```cpp
spread the poses; check the fit returns true
```
