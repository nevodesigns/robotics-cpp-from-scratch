title: A limit taken from the motors alone
match: the vehicle slides on a slope its motors can climb
match: the slope limit does not include the grip or the balance
match: what the vehicle can climb is the smallest of its motors, its tyres and its balance
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-05-can-it-drive-there
---

## Symptom

The map says a slope is passable and the vehicle slips on it, or leans over.

## Cause

The motors could climb 25.6 degrees and the gravel held 22.3: the limit is the smallest of the motors, the grip and the balance.

## Fix

Take the least of the three, and take the grip from the surface under the vehicle.

```cpp
v.max_slope = std::min(motors, std::min(grip, tip));
```
