## Symptom

The design says the robot can recover from 22 degrees and in the field it falls at 17.

## Cause

With a motor limit of 4 m/s^2 the formula gave 22.2 degrees and the steepest recovered lean was 16.5; at 2 m/s^2 8.9 against 11.5, and at 8 m/s^2 21.7 against 39.2, where the axle's speed limit bit.

## Fix

Measure the recoverable lean in simulation and test it on the robot.

```cpp
boundary(a_max)
```
