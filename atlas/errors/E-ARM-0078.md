## Symptom

A grip that held on the bench drops parts on the line.

## Cause

The friction used was for a clean dry pad; at half the friction the payload falls from 16.0 kg to 8.0 kg.

## Fix

Use a lower friction than the sheet's and a safety factor, and test with the real surface.

```cpp
hand.mu = 0.5 * sheet_mu;
```
