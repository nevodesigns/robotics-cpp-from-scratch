title: A turn in place asked for on a floor that cannot pay for it
match: the robot can drive forward but cannot turn on the spot on gravel
match: the scrubbing torque of a pivot is larger than the motors give
match: a pivot needs a force from each side that depends on the floor, and the motors may not have it
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 23-02-a-skid-steer-and-its-centre-of-rotation
---

## Symptom

The motors strain or stall in a pivot, though they drive the same robot forward without trouble.

## Cause

A pivot needs mu m g sqrt(L2 + B2) / (2 B) from each side: 34.5 N on gravel, against 28.0 N available.

## Fix

Compute the force a pivot needs before planning one, and plan a wider turn where the floor cannot pay for it.

```cpp
if (!can_pivot(thrust, mass, mu, wheelbase, track)) use_an_arc();
```
