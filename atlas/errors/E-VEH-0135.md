## Symptom

A release is confirmed by its own health check and turns out to drive the wrong way, or to have lost its calibration.

## Cause

Nothing measured what it does on the vehicle. A wheel wired backwards failed 1 check of the self test and a lost calibration failed 1.

## Fix

Run a short drive on the new software and confirm only if the measured numbers pass; a number that is not a number fails.

```cpp
if (post_update_selftest(measured, limits).empty()) confirm(boot);
```
