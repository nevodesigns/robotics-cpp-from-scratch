## Symptom

The simulated robot turns about a quarter short after a drive, and the real one does not. It is the simulator's model of a three wheeled robot.

## Cause

The model's centre of mass was over the axle, so the caster touched the floor only when the body rocked, which it did after driving.

A robot with two driven wheels and a caster rests on three points only if its weight is between the axle and the caster. With the weight over the axle it balances on two, and the caster touches only when the body tips, which driving and stopping make it do.

## Fix

Put the weight between the axle and the caster, and test the simulated robot with a turn after a drive. With the weight behind the axle the turns were 0.99, 1.01 and 1.02.

```cpp
com_back 0.0144 source typical
```

The line above is from the robot's model file; the value is typical and the file says so.
