id: E-ARM-0091
title: A protective distance with the person taken as still
match: the arm stopped too close to the person
match: the safety distance was only the robot's braking distance
match: the protective distance is the sum of its terms, and grows with the robot's speed
match: a robot that brakes at the protective distance stops before the person, and one that leaves out a term does not
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 31-06-staying-far-enough-from-a-person
---

## Symptom

The arm stops, as designed, and the person is touched or nearly.

## Cause

The distance counted only the robot's own stopping distance. The person walks at 1.6 m/s during the reaction and the braking: a robot at 1.0 m/s needed 0.85 m, and one at rest 0.33 m.

## Fix

Add the person's travel over the reaction and stopping time, the robot's travel before braking and the uncertainties.

```cpp
protective_distance(p, v_r);
```
