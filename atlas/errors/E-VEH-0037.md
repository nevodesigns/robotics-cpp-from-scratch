title: A costmap that never forgets
match: the planner will not go through a doorway that a person has left
match: ghost obstacles stay in the costmap
match: an obstacle that has gone stays in a layer that only marks, and is forgotten by one that clears
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-04-costmap-layers
---

## Symptom

Obstacles that have gone stay in the map, and the planner finds routes closed that are open.

## Cause

The obstacle layer only marked. After an obstacle went, 7 cells of ghost stayed in a layer that only marks and 0 in one that clears along the beams.

## Fix

Clear the cells a beam passes through, as well as marking the one it ends in.

```cpp
mark_and_clear(layer, sensor_x, sensor_y, hits, true);
```
