## Symptom

The vehicle climbs the first part of a long slope and then stops or slows as the motors overheat. The slope is longer than any on the test track.

## Cause

The current for 20 degrees makes more heat than the motor can lose. It reached its limit in 62 s, which at 0.5 m/s is 31 m of slope. A slope of 14 degrees settled at a rise of 71 K and could be held.

Climbing needs a current that stays high for as long as the climb lasts, and the motor warms with the current squared. If the steady temperature for that current is above the limit, the motor reaches the limit after a time; a short climb ends first, a long one does not.

## Fix

Work out the time to the limit for the slopes of the route, and plan a rest, a slower climb or another way where the climb is longer than it allows.

```cpp
const double force = rc::drive::slope_force(mass, angle, crr);
const double t = rc::drive::time_to_limit(current_for(force), thermal);
```
