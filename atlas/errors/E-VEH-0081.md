## Symptom

The robot drives confidently from a wrong position until it meets a wall.

## Cause

Nothing told the rest of the robot that the localisation had failed. The monitor called it lost after 1 steps.

## Fix

Turn the likelihood and the spread into a verdict and stop or slow the robot when it is lost.

```cpp
if (verdict(monitor.fast(), monitor.slow(), mc.spread()) == Health::lost) stop();
```
