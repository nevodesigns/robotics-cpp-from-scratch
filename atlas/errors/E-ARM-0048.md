## Symptom

A servo arm reaches its goals unloaded and sags under load, most at the shoulder.

## Cause

The servo's own loop is a spring. A load of 2 N m on a stiffness of 15 N m/rad drooped 0.133 rad.

## Fix

Shift each goal by the model's gravity and payload torque over the servo's stiffness. The droop was then 0.0002 rad.

```cpp
joint.set_goal(rc::arm::goal_for_load(spec, wanted, load));
```
