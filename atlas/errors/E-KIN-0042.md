## Symptom

The arm holds a part in some poses and trips its overload in others, with the same part, and the limit on the datasheet is for one pose only.

## Cause

The torque to hold a payload depends on how far out it is, and each motor has its own limit, so the heaviest payload is a property of the pose and of the weakest motor in it. Measured: for straight out it was **0.801648** kg, with the shoulder at **80** of 80 N m and the elbow at 37.8 of 40; a little below was ok and a little above was too heavy.

## Fix

Work out the torque of every joint for the pose, and compare each with its own limit:

```cpp
ratio = std::fmax(ratio, torque[i] / arm.torque_limit[i]);
```
