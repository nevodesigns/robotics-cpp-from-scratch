## Symptom

The arm goes to where the camera saw the part in the camera's frame, and misses by a large and steady amount.

## Cause

A point from the camera was used as a target without the transform; a pixel 60 to the right at 0.5 m is 0.050 m in the camera and means nothing in the base.

## Fix

Put every point seen by the camera into the base frame with the measured transform before it is a target.

```cpp
camera_to_base(base_from_camera, p_cam, p_base);
```
