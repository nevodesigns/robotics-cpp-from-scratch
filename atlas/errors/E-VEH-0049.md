## Symptom

Far obstacles seen by the camera are placed a metre or more out, and jump from frame to frame. The object is near the horizon in the image.

## Cause

Distance was read from a row near the horizon, where one row spans a great deal of floor: 0.0090 m at the centre of the image, 0.139 m at row 140 and 0.82 m at row 120.

The ground under a pixel row is where that row's ray meets the floor. Near the horizon the rays are nearly level, so a small change in row is a large change in distance, and the row's error is a large distance error.

## Fix

Use the ground plane for distance only where a row is a small part of what matters; beyond that use another sensor, or accept the error and say so.

```cpp
const double resolution = rc::perceive::ground_step(cam, height, pitch, v);
```
