title: A beam that returned nothing, used as a range

## Symptom

A ring of phantom obstacles sits at the maximum range of the sensor, or a gap in a scan is filled with a wall.

## Cause

A beam that returned nothing was kept as a range. With a tenth of the beams lost the scan gave 325 points, and the lost beams must give none.

## Fix

Keep only beams whose range is a number between the limits, and look at the valid fraction.

```cpp
if (!(r == r) || r < scan.min_range || r > scan.max_range) continue;
```
