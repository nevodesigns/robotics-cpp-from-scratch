## Symptom

Any fault stops the vehicle, or no fault changes what it does.

## Cause

One response for every loss. The scanner lost needs a stop (mode 3) and a lost GNSS only a reduced mode (1); two losses together cost a creep (2).

## Fix

Give each loss its own mode, count the losses, and let the position's sigma move the mode on as well.

```cpp
const Mode m = required_mode(health, policy);
```
