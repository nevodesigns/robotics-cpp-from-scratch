## Symptom

The limits look right in a table and the robot gets too close to a person who walks at it.

## Cause

The limits have a reaction time and a braking limit behind them; in the test the unsupervised robot met the person at 3.1 m/s and the supervised one stopped 0.14 m away (short of its margin by 28 mm).

## Fix

Test the supervisor against a moving person in simulation and then on the real robot, measuring the reaction time and the stop.

```cpp
a simulation of the person walking at the robot
```
