title: The four elementary moves of a row multiplied in the wrong order
match: a row of the standard convention is Rz Tz Tx Rx, and its rotation and position are the closed form of that product
match: expected f.r
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 13-12-the-table-that-describes-an-arm

## Symptom

The tool is in the right place for some angles and wrong for others, or the orientation is turned, and every number in the table was checked.

## Cause

A rotation and a translation do not commute: a distance along x and then a turn is not a turn and then a distance along x. The row is one fixed product, and a different order is a different arm. Measured: `Rz Tz Tx Rx` with a quarter turn of twist gave the rotation `[[c, 0, s], [s, 0, -c], [0, 1, 0]]` and the position (0.4 c, 0.4 s, 0.5).

## Fix

Write the product in the order of the convention:

```cpp
compose(compose(compose(rot_z(theta), trans_z(d)), trans_x(row.a)), rot_x(row.alpha));
```
