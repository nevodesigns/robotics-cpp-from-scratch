## Symptom

A small foot motion near full extension asks for large joint speeds, or none can be found.

## Cause

The inverse of the Jacobian grows as the leg straightens: 3.4 rad/s at 0.30 m and 15.5 rad/s at 0.40 m for 0.5 m/s.

## Fix

Keep the leg short of full stretch and refuse foot velocities where the determinant is small.

```cpp
if (!joint_rates(j, v, r)) hold();
```
