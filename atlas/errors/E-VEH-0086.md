## Symptom

The vehicle cannot follow the arcs of a short path and overshoots the turns. The radius was a round number, or the one from a smaller robot.

## Cause

The path was planned with a radius smaller than the car's: 0.4 m, a curvature of 2.50 per metre, for a car whose smallest radius is 0.742 m (a curvature limit of 1.35).

Every arc of a Dubins path has the radius it was planned with. A car that cannot turn that tightly runs wide on every arc.

## Fix

Take the radius from the vehicle model, the wheelbase over the tangent of the largest steering angle, with a margin.

```cpp
const double r = 1.1 * wheelbase / std::tan(max_steer);   // 10 percent margin
```
