## Symptom

Commanded in terms of the fingertip, the tool moves as if the point being controlled were the flange. The fingertip moves in arcs when a straight line was asked for.

## Cause

The Jacobian was worked out at the flange. Moving the point out along the tool changes the linear part of the Jacobian (a turn of the wrist now sweeps the tip) and leaves the angular part alone; with a tool of 0.15 m that difference is large.

The linear velocity of a point depends on where the point is: a turn of the wrist moves the flange little and the fingertip a lot. A Jacobian at the flange gives the flange's velocity, and commanding it moves the fingertip on a path that includes the swing.

## Fix

Work the Jacobian out at the point being controlled, with the tool offset.

```cpp
const rc::arm::Jacobian j = rc::arm::jacobian(table, q, base, rc::arm::trans(0.0, 0.0, tip_length), table.convention);
```
