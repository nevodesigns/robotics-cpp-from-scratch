## Symptom

In a corridor the match is steady across it and the motion along it is whatever the guess was.

## Cause

Nothing in the scan constrains the move along a straight wall. It left 0.10 m of error along the corridor with a small rms; a partition fixed that to 0.000 m.

## Fix

Check that the scan constrains all three directions (the eigenvalues of the normal equations) and fall back on the odometry along any it does not.

```cpp
if (!constrained_along_x) move.x = odometry.x;
```
