## Symptom

A regression test passes on one machine and fails on another, or fails sometimes, and the failures are not repeatable.

## Cause

The step size is part of the answer. A spring simulated for 5 s ended at 0.906213 with a fixed step at steady 60, steady 30 and jittery frames alike, and at 0.917488 and -0.809887 when stepped by the frame time at 60 and 30. The same jittery frames run twice gave 0.90621265316080712 both times.

## Fix

Step by a fixed dt and let the frame decide how many.

```cpp
const int n = stepper.advance(elapsed);
for (int i = 0; i < n; ++i) world.step(stepper.dt());
```
