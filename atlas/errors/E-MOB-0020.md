## Symptom

The arm reaches to where the object is on the map and misses most of the time.

## Cause

A tolerance of 10 mm against a sigma of 25.6 mm gives a chance of 0.30; 20000 simulated grasps gave 0.30.

## Fix

Work out the chance for the tolerance and the budget before building the cell, and add a measurement at the hand if it is low.

```cpp
grasp_probability(sigma, tolerance);
```
