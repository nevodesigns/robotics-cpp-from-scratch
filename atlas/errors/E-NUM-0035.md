## Symptom

A very small reading, a time step or a gain, is written to a file and read back as 0. The program then divides by it, or concludes the sensor was dead.

## Cause

`std::to_string(double)` always writes six places after the decimal point, in fixed notation. Anything smaller than half a millionth is written as zeros.

Measured: 3.5e-9 through `std::to_string` gave **"0.000000"**, and through `shortest_text` gave "3.5e-09", which reads back exactly.

## Fix

Use a format that adapts to the size of the number (significant digits, as in the digit search of `shortest_text`), and not one with a fixed number of places.
