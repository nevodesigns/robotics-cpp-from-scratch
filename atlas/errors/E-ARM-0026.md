## Symptom

The calibration reports an error of almost nothing and the arm is no more accurate elsewhere. The calibration report shows almost no error.

## Cause

It was judged on its own poses, with too few of them for the corrections. 4 poses gave 0.00000000 mm on themselves and 0.83 mm on others; 60 poses gave 0.101 mm on others.

With more corrections than independent measurements, the fit can match the measured poses exactly by using its freedom to fit their noise. Measured on the same poses, the fit looks perfect; on others it is no better than before.

## Fix

Measure many more poses than there are corrections, and report the error on poses kept back from the fit.

```cpp
const rc::arm::ArmTable fitted = rc::arm::perturbed(nominal_table, c.correction);
const double held = rc::arm::rms_error(fitted, held_out_poses);   // not c.rms_after
```
