## Symptom

Some queries find no path although one exists.

## Cause

Too few nodes to join the free space: the graph had 6 pieces at 40 nodes and 5 at 300 (it answered 7 and 7 of the queries with a free end in this roomy scene, and would answer fewer in a tighter one).

## Fix

Add nodes until the pieces join, count them (components), and for narrow gaps sample near the obstacles or grow a tree.

```cpp
components(roadmap);
```
