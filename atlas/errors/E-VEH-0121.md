## Symptom

The battery is flat long before the range worked out from the motors.

## Cause

The energy counted only the work at the wheels. The computer and sensors draw power all the time the vehicle is out: 79 percent of the 10.1 J a metre of tarmac cost at 1 m/s.

## Fix

Add the idle power times the time the route takes, and remember that a slower vehicle runs its computer for longer per metre.

```cpp
const double joules = work / efficiency + idle_w * (length / speed);
```
