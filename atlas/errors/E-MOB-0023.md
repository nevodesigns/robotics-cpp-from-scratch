## Symptom

The mission stops and nobody can say which step failed.

## Cause

No fault code was kept; here 1 is no part (3 tries), 2 a part dropped, 3 a step that took too long, 4 a battery that would not last.

## Fix

Give every way of stopping a code and log it with the state.

```cpp
m.fault = 2; enter(m, kMFault);
```
