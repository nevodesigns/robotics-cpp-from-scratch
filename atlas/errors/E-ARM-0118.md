## Symptom

The robot is installed and a part of the work is out of its reach.

## Cause

The area was not put to the robot's own reach test: a SCARA over the middle of the work reached 0.48 of it, and the delta 0.33 of a wide table.

## Fix

Write the task down and check every point of its area, from the place the robot will be mounted.

```cpp
check_family(robots, family, task).area_ok;
```
