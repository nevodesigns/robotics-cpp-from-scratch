title: A cost that does not depend on the heading
match: the planner sends the vehicle straight up a slope
match: the path climbs a hill when it could go round the side
match: a metre costs more over sand than tarmac, and more up a slope than across it
match: on flat tarmac with no weights the path is as long as the straight line, to the cell
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-09-planning-over-rough-ground

## Symptom

The path goes straight up a slope that the vehicle could have crossed along its side.

## Cause

The cell's cost was one number, the same whatever way the vehicle crossed it. Climbing costs work: a metre up 14 degrees cost 25.2 J and a metre across it 1.47 J.

## Fix

Price the step, with the slope along the step's heading.

```cpp
const double j = rc::drive::energy_per_metre(cell, std::atan2(dy, dx), mass);
```
