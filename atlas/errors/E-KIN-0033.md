## Symptom

A tool never quite converges in orientation: the error sticks at a few thousandths of a radian, or the controller shows zero orientation error while the tool is visibly off.

## Cause

(trace - 1) / 2 is a number near 1, and its arc cosine is the angle only above the precision of the number: for small angles it rounds to 1 and the arc cosine is 0. Measured: for a turn of 1e-10 about z, the arc cosine gave **0** and the antisymmetric part gave **1e-10**.

## Fix

Below a small angle read the vector from the antisymmetric part:

```cpp
if (angle < 1e-7) return Vec3{0.5 * vee.x, 0.5 * vee.y, 0.5 * vee.z};
```
