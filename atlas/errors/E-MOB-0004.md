## Symptom

The base stops at a place that looks right and the arm cannot reach the object.

## Cause

The distance was taken from the base's centre; the arm's axis is the mount's distance away, and the ring is 0.00 to 0.72 m from the arm's axis.

## Fix

Use the arm's axis (the base's place moved by the mount) for every reach test.

```cpp
arm_base_in_world(m, base);
```
