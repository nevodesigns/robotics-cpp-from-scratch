## Symptom

When the robot starts to fall it either does nothing useful or does something too late, because nothing told it how long it had.

## Cause

The time to the ground was never worked out. A tilting body is a pendulum, and the time depends on the tilt and its rate: a body 0.27 m up, tilted 23 degrees and turning at 3.0 rad/s, reached the ground in 0.22 s; one tilted 17 degrees at 0.2 rad/s took 0.38 s.

## Fix

Work out the time to the ground from the tilt and rate, and compare it with what a recovery needs.

```cpp
const double t = rc::legged::time_to_ground(theta, omega, 0.27, 5.0);   // 0.22 s or 0.38 s
```

The time means nothing while the balance control is holding the body; it is for when it is not.
