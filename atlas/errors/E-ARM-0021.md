## Symptom

Over several seconds of null space motion the tool drifts away from where it was.

## Cause

The null space holds to first order only. With no correction the tool drifted 0.00076 m; with a correction on its error, 0.00037 m.

## Fix

Correct the tool's error every step as well as projecting the secondary motion.

```cpp
back = dls_step(j, kp * error * dt, lambda);
```
