## Symptom

At each corner of the planned path the controller saturates its steering and the vehicle overshoots or crawls.

## Cause

The path had a corner: a sharpest curvature of 7.1 per metre with points 0.2 m apart, where the car's limit is 1.35.

## Fix

Resample and smooth, then check the curvature that is left (3.64 here, 1.55 with a heavy smoother) against the car's limit; if it is over, plan with arcs (26-01).

```cpp
const auto s = smooth_path(resample(plan, 0.2), &grid, cfg).path;
```
