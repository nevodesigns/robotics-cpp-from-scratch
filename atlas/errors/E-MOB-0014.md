title: A load that was not counted
match: the base tipped when it was carrying the box
match: the payload was left out of the stability check
match: reaching and carrying take the centre of mass towards the edge and cut the braking limit
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 33-05-tipping-with-the-arm-out

## Symptom

The base is stable empty and tips when carrying something with the arm out.

## Cause

The payload moves the centre of mass: 5 kg in the hand cut the braking limit from 8.88 to 5.19 m/s2, below the 5.89 m/s2 the wheels would grip.

## Fix

Put the payload's mass (known or estimated) into the model and re-check.

```cpp
masses.payload = payload_kg;
```
