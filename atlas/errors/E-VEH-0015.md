## Symptom

A vehicle that brakes hard for a bend runs wide at the entrance.

## Cause

At 99 percent of the sliding speed only 20 percent of the braking of the straight line is left.

## Fix

Do the braking before the bend, on the straight, and enter it at a speed that leaves some grip.

```cpp
if (b.share > 0.8) request_slower_entry();
```
