## Symptom

The arm replans round something that goes away a moment later, and the move takes much longer. The arm took a long detour round a person who stepped through and out.

## Cause

It replanned at once. A plan round the obstacle took 4.20 s, where waiting for it to pass took 2.15 s (against 1.50 s with nothing in the way).

Replanning at once treats every obstacle as permanent. Many obstacles in a shared cell are passing: a person walking by, a cart. Waiting a moment lets them go, and the original path is often still the best.

## Fix

Wait a moment before planning round, if the thing may be leaving, and plan only if it stays.

```cpp
rc::arm::ReplanConfig cfg;
cfg.replan = false;   // wait where it stands and look again next tick
```

A thing that stays (a box put down) needs the replan; a time limit on the wait decides which it is.
