title: A contact sense taken as the gait's timetable
match: the robot kept stepping into the air over a hole
match: the gait assumed the foot was down when the timetable said so
match: touchdown and lift-off are believed after a run of readings, and a reading on the wrong side resets the run
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 34-07-feeling-the-ground
---

## Symptom

The robot steps on as if its foot were on the ground when it is not (a hole), or does not notice a bump.

## Cause

The gait's timetable was used as the contact state; the sensed state and the expected one differ whenever the ground is not what was expected.

## Fix

Use the sensed contact, and treat a difference from the timetable as an event to act on.

```cpp
contact_update(state, force, since_lift, p) != in_stance(gait, leg, t)
```
