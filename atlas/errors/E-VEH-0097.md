title: A skid steer controller that uses the plain track
match: the skid steer vehicle turns wider than the controller asks
match: the vehicle takes bends too wide on a skid steer drive
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 26-06-a-skid-steer-controller-that-knows-slip
---

## Symptom

Every bend is taken wide, and more so on a floor that grips.

## Cause

The wheel speeds were worked out with chi = 1 and no slip, but the body turns on a track chi times wider: the worst error was 0.115 m, against 0.042 m with the real values.

## Fix

Work out the wheel speeds with chi times the track, and the slip, calibrated on the vehicle (lessons 23-02, 23-08).

```cpp
skid_wheel_speeds(g, chi, slip, v, w);
```
