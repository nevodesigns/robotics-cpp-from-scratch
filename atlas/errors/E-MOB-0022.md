## Symptom

The robot carries on with a mission after a step has gone wrong: it drives off with nothing in the hand, or places nothing at the table. The log shows every step done, in order, and the part is still on the shelf.

## Cause

The mission was a script with no check after each step. After closing, the gap between the fingers says whether the grasp worked: the part's width, within a tolerance, if it did, and near zero if it did not.

A script commands each step and moves on; it treats commanded as done. Every step that touches the world can fail, and the only way to know is to look at what the sensors say after it.

## Fix

Check after each step, and decide the next step from the result. A mission that went to plan took 97 ticks; one that missed once and tried again took 113.

```cpp
rc::mobile::Mission m;
const rc::mobile::MissionCmd c = rc::mobile::mission_step(m, in);   // verifies the gap before lifting
```

The check after closing costs a tick; a mission that carries on after a miss costs a whole trip.
