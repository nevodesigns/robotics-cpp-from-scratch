## Symptom

The task needs the tool tilted or the part turned over and the robot cannot.

## Cause

The parallelograms keep the platform parallel to the base: three movements and no tilt.

## Fix

Use a six axis arm, or a delta with a wrist, for tilting work.

```text
choose the family from the task
```
