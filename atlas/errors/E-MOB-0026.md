## Symptom

Near a person, the base and the arm each keep within the safe speed, and together they approach the person faster than that. Each of the two speed limits passes its own test.

## Cause

The person's speed budget was given to the base and to the hand separately. What reaches the person is the base's speed plus the hand's. With a person 1.0 m away allowing 1.23 m/s in all, a base at 1.0 and a hand at 0.5 add to 1.5.

What reaches a person is the hand, and the hand moves with the base plus its own motion relative to the base. Two budgets that are each within the person's limit can add to more than it. The limit belongs to the sum.

## Fix

Hold the base to its own limit first, then scale base and hand by one factor so their sum is within the person's total: here to 0.82 and 0.41.

```cpp
const rc::mobile::SpeedLimits s = rc::mobile::supervise(ssm, 1.0, 1.0, 0.5, 5.19, 0.8, 0.5);
// s.base 0.82, s.hand 0.41, s.status 1 (scaled)
```

Inside the distance for standing still, 0.2 m here, both stop.

Scaling both by one factor keeps the direction of the hand's motion, so the task is slower but the same.
