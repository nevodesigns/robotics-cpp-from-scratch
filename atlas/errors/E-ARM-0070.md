## Symptom

The arm follows a plan made some time ago, through a place that has changed since. The plan was made before a person moved a box, and nothing replanned.

## Cause

Nothing recorded which scene the plan was made for. A scene changes as things are moved, added, picked up and put down.

A plan is a promise about a scene. When the scene changes, the plan may still be free or may not; the only way to know is to recheck it, and the only way to know to recheck is to know that the scene changed.

## Fix

Give the scene a version that every change bumps, record it with the plan, and recheck the path or replan when it differs. Four changes took the version from 0 to 4 and the plan was stale; the recheck found the path free when the object moved 0.5 m away and not when it moved onto the path.

```cpp
if (rc::arm::plan_is_stale(plan_version, scene)) recheck_or_replan();
```
