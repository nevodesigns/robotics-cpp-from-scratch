## Symptom

A move over a short distance takes a long time, the arm stopping and starting along the way.

## Cause

Every vertex of the path is a stop. A wandering path of 17 points took 14.33 s; its straight shortcut took 1.45 s.

## Fix

Shortcut the path (30-05) before timing it, and cut the corners that are left.

```cpp
path = rc::arm::shortcut(problem, path, 150, seed, problem.margin);
const auto traj = rc::arm::plan_trajectory(path, limits);
```
