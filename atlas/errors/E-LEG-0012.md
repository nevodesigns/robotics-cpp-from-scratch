## Symptom

Each step lands with a thump and the body jolts at every footfall.

## Cause

The swing went straight up, across and down, and the speed and acceleration jump at the corners. The foot meets the ground still moving: a path that goes straight up and down at the same height landed at 0.80 m/s.

## Fix

Use a curve with zero speed and acceleration at both ends: the minimum jerk curve along the ground and a smooth bump in height. The foot then landed at 0.000 m/s.

```cpp
double p[3];
rc::legged::swing_position(p0, p1, 0.05, tau, p);   // tau from 0 to 1 over the swing
```
