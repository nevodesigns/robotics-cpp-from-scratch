## Symptom

The hand misses its targets by a few millimetres in a direction that depends on where it is reaching, though the base and the arm are each well calibrated.

## Cause

The arm's mount on the base was measured carefully in position and roughly in angle. A turn in the mount is multiplied by the distance out to the hand, like the base's heading: the grasp budget of 33-07 carries a mounting turn of 0.2 degree, which at 0.9 m is a few millimetres at the hand, and a mount turned by a whole degree would cost 15.7 mm there.

## Fix

Measure the mount's angles as well as its offset, and put them in the mount transform used by every conversion.

```cpp
rc::mobile::MobileManipulator m;
m.mount = rc::arm::compose(rc::arm::trans(0.15, 0.0, 0.40), rc::arm::rot_z(mount_yaw));   // the angle measured, not assumed
const rc::arm::Pose arm_base = rc::mobile::arm_base_in_world(m, base);
```
