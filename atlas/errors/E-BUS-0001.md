id: E-BUS-0001
title: A message published to nobody
match: a message reaches every subscriber of its topic, in the order they subscribed, and nobody else
match: a topic with nobody listening drops the message, counts it, and is not an error
match: unsubscribing stops delivery, and says whether there was anything to stop
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-01-topics-by-hand
---

## Symptom

A value is published and nothing happens, and nothing reports it, because the subscriber used a different spelling or subscribed a moment too late.

## Cause

A publisher cannot know whether anyone is listening, so a message to an empty topic is dropped. Two such messages gave a dropped count of 2, and a subscriber that arrived afterwards heard 1 of 3: messages are not kept for those who come later unless something is built to keep them.

## Fix

Count what is dropped and look at the count.

```cpp
if (bus.dropped() > 0) report_unheard_topics();
```
