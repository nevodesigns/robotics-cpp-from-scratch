## Symptom

The robot is said to be accurate to a centimetre a metre and is a metre out at the end of a long run.

## Cause

A wheel base 5 percent out cost nothing driving straight. A left wheel 1 percent big gave 1.66, 6.55 and 14.49 m at 10, 20 and 30 m. Fixes every 10, 5 and 2 s gave 5.04, 2.86 and 1.42 m at 30 m; fixes that died at 20 s gave 13.75 m.

## Fix

State the error with the distance, and watch the position source.

```cpp
const double error = position_error(believed, truth);
```
