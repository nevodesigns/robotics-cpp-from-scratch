title: A fixed radius for a far wall
match: a far wall disappears from the obstacle list
match: points of a far thing are further apart than the clustering radius
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A wall that is in the scan is not among the objects, and appears when the vehicle gets close.

## Cause

At 11 m the points are 0.19 m apart. A fixed radius of 0.15 m found 0 objects in the wall; a radius that grows with range found 1.

## Fix

Let the radius grow with the beam spacing at the range of the points.

```cpp
const double limit = base + gain * std::max(ri, rj);
```
