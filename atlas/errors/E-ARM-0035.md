## Symptom

A joint buzzes or oscillates when the arm is in a pose where it is light.

## Cause

The loop's bandwidth is Kv over J: with gains for the heaviest pose it was three times too high at the lightest, past what the delay allows (overshoot 13909338 percent against 0.0).

## Fix

Scale Kv with the inertia, or tune for the lightest pose and accept a slow response at the heaviest.

```cpp
g.kp_vel = inertia * wv;
```
