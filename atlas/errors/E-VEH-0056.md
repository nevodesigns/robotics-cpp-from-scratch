## Symptom

The bias estimate wanders by as much as the bias, whenever the robot turns.

## Cause

The wheels are wrong by a share of their turn rate, and the difference was weighed the same at every rate. With the wheels 12 percent out the filter that did that ended 3.7 degrees out on average, and the one that weighed by the turn rate 1.7.

## Fix

Make the variance of the measurement include (share times turn rate) squared.

```cpp
const double r = sigma * sigma + share * share * wheel_rate * wheel_rate;
```
