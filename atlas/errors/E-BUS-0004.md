id: E-BUS-0004
title: A slow subscriber that held the others up
match: a subscriber takes messages oldest first, one at a time, and finds none when it is empty
match: every subscriber has its own queue, so a slow one loses messages and the fast one beside it does not
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-02-a-queue-for-each-listener
---

## Symptom

One slow part of the robot makes every other part late, because they all wait on the same delivery.

## Cause

A subscriber called directly runs on the publisher's thread, and one queue shared among subscribers is held up by the slowest. With a queue each, ten messages gave the fast subscriber 10 taken and 0 lost, and the slow one 8 lost with 2 waiting.

## Fix

Give each subscriber its own queue and let it take when ready.

```cpp
const auto h = bus.subscribe("scan", 2, Overflow::drop_oldest);
```
