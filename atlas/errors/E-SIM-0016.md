## Symptom

A robot's handling of a frozen or drifting sensor has never been run, and on the day it is needed it does the wrong thing.

## Cause

Faults can be switched on in a simulation at a chosen time and do one thing each. A stuck sensor reading 2.0 and asked for 3.0 and 9.0 repeated 2.0; an offset of 0.5 turned 2.0 into 2.5; a drift of 0.1 a second turned 2.0 into 2.2 after two seconds; and a spike of 4 on every 25th reading gave 4 spikes in 100 readings.

## Fix

Put the faults in the scenario and run them every day.

```cpp
fault.set(FaultKind::drift, 2.0, 0.05);
```
