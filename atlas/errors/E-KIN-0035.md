## Symptom

An arm that is sent to a new pose jerks, the drive faults on over-current, or the gearbox is noisy and wears.

## Cause

A joint that is told to be at a position at once is asked for a step in speed, which is an acceleration of infinity, and a motor gives what it can and trips. A move needs ramps. Measured: a distance of 2 under limits of 1 and 2 took **2.5** s, with ramps of **0.5** s and a cruise of **1.5** s, and the positions at the times along it were those of the trapezoid.

## Fix

Plan the ramps from the limits:

```cpp
const double ramp_distance = vmax * vmax / (2.0 * amax);
t.t_cruise = (d - 2.0 * ramp_distance) / vmax;
```
