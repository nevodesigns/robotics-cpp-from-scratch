## Symptom

The task needs the tool tilted and the arm has no axis to do it.

## Cause

The four values (q1, q2, slide, roll) give a place and a heading only; the weight carried by the first two joints is 0.0 N m because the arm has been built to stay flat.

## Fix

Use a six axis arm (phases 28 to 31) where a tilt is needed.
