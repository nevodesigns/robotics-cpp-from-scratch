## Symptom

After the robot is lifted and put down elsewhere, or after a long outage, its position never comes right.

## Cause

The gate had no way back. Every fix was far from the prediction and refused, until the filter's uncertainty had grown enough to admit them: 68 fixes.

## Fix

After a number of refusals in a row, start again from the next fix. A gate that gave up after 5 refusals found the robot 5 fixes after it was put down.

```cpp
if (++refused_in_a_row_ >= cfg_.max_refused) start(fix);
```
