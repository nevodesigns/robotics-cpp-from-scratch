## Symptom

The robot drives to the table, puts nothing down and reports success.

## Cause

Each step ran because the one before had been commanded; with the grasp checked, a miss was retried (113 ticks against 97).

## Fix

Check what was sensed after each step that can fail before going on.

```cpp
if (holding(gap, obj, tol)) enter(m, kMLift);
```
