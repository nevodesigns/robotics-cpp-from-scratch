## Symptom

The tilt estimate wobbles at the step rate although the accelerometer is gated.

## Cause

A sideways acceleration changes the size of what is felt only a little: the shake of 2.5 m/s2 was accepted 100 percent of the time and the accelerometer alone was 10.2 degrees wrong.

## Fix

Pull with a small gain so that the shake averages out, and do not rely on the gate for sideways acceleration.

```cpp
f.k_acc = 0.5;
```
