## Symptom

A simulated arm let fall swings back higher than it began, or comes to rest sooner than it should with no friction.

## Cause

A first order step: the energy was out by 0.03723 of itself after a second at a step of a millisecond, against 0.000000000496 with a fourth order step.

## Fix

Use a higher order integrator (or a smaller step) and test the energy of a free fall.

```cpp
step_dynamics(model, q, qd, tau, dt, true);
```
