## Symptom

The planner says a path is clear and the arm touches something on the way.

## Cause

The collision model is smaller than the arm. The links are drawn as capsules, a segment and a radius, and the radii here are 4 to 8 cm; a motor housing, a cable loom or a wide gripper sticks out past a radius guessed from a drawing of the bare link.

## Fix

Take the radii from the arm's model or a measurement of the real arm with what is fitted to it, and add a margin. A model a little too big costs a little reach; one too small costs a collision.

```cpp
const std::vector<rc::arm::Capsule> caps = rc::arm::arm_capsules(table, q, radii, tool_length);   // radii measured, tool included
```
