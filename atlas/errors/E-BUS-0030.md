id: E-BUS-0030
title: A second goal with no rule
match: a second goal while one runs is turned away or takes over, and the server's rule decides which
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-10-goals-that-take-time
---

## Symptom

Two commands drive the arm at once, or a command is silently ignored and the operator does not know.

## Cause

One arm does one thing. When a goal arrives while another runs, the server must choose. Under reject_new the second got -1 and the first kept executing; under preempt_old the first was asked to cancel and was canceling, not yet stopped, while the second was accepted at once.

## Fix

Decide the rule, and let the new goal wait for the old one's confirmation if the hardware needs it.

```cpp
ActionServer server(WhenBusy::reject_new);
```
