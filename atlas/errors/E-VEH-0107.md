## Symptom

The vehicle crosses sand or mud by the shortest way, slowly, and at a high cost to its battery. The planner was told only the start and the goal.

## Cause

The planner minimised distance only: 16.0 m and 90 J, where a way of 31.2 m cost 66 J.

The shortest path is the shortest; it is not the cheapest. Over sand or mud each metre costs more energy, and a longer way round on firm ground can cost less in total.

## Fix

Add a weight on energy per metre to the price of a step, chosen for the mission.

```cpp
rc::drive::OffroadWeights w;
w.energy = 1.0;
```
