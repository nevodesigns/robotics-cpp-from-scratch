## Symptom

At a sharp turn the vehicle slows to nothing and sits there. It happens at the tightest corner of the path.

## Cause

The speed cap had no floor, so a tight enough curve gave a limit of nearly zero and the vehicle never got through.

The traction cap is sqrt(mu g / kappa), which goes to zero as the curvature grows. Without a floor, a sharp enough corner asks for a speed so low that the vehicle cannot overcome friction to move.

## Fix

Keep a floor speed in a turn (0.15 m/s here), and let the planner avoid curves the surface cannot hold.

```cpp
v = std::max(cfg.min_speed, std::min(v, vmax));
```
