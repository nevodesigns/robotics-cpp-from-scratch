match: the vehicle will not drive between two posts it could pass

## Symptom

The planner finds no way between two obstacles that are farther apart than the vehicle is wide. The radius was raised to find the far wall.

## Cause

A fixed radius made large enough for the far wall joined two posts at 2 m into 1 object.

A large radius joins points that belong to different objects when the gap between them is smaller than the radius. Two posts close together become one, and the gap between them disappears from the map.

## Fix

Keep the radius no bigger than the point spacing at the range the points were seen. The grown radius kept the posts as 2.

```cpp
const auto objects = rc::perceive::cluster_points(points, 0.05, 1.5 * angle_inc, 1);
```
