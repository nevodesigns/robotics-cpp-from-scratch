## Symptom

A cell that was checked on paper lets the arm get closer to a person than it should.

## Cause

A reaction time left out or too small shortens the distance; with none at all the robot came to rest -0.09 m away against 0.17 m.

## Fix

Measure the whole chain (sensor, processing, brake) on the real robot and keep the uncertainty terms.

```cpp
p.t_r = measured_reaction_time;
```
