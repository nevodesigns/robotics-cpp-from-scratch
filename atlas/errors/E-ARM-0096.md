id: E-ARM-0096
title: A throughput that ignores the person
match: the cell is slower when someone is at the station
match: the cycle time was quoted with no one near the robot
match: a person near the robot caps its speed and lengthens the cycle, and one too close stops it
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 31-07-how-many-parts-an-hour
---

## Symptom

The cell is slower on the line than in the acceptance test.

## Cause

The throughput ignored the people near it. The speed a person's distance allows caps the tool: a person 1.0 m away cut it to 1.23 m/s and the cycle to 3.77 s; at 2.0 m the cycle stayed 3.68 s; closer than the distance for standing still the robot stops.

## Fix

Work the throughput out for the distances people really stand at, or lay the cell out so they stand farther away.

```cpp
const double t = rc::arm::cycle_with_person(cell, ssm, separation);   // -1 if the robot must stop
```
