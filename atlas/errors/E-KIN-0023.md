## Symptom

The robot is given a task at a point that passed the reachability check, and does it badly: the tool crawls, the joints race, and a small
move of the target asks for a large move of the elbow. Moving the workpiece twenty centimetres fixes it.

## Cause

Reachable is only the ring between |l1 - l2| and l1 + l2. Near both edges the arm is almost straight or almost folded, which is the
singularity of lesson 13-04, and a point there can be reached and not worked at.

Measured on a 0.5 and 0.4 metre arm: reachable **2.5129 m^2**, which is the ring's 2.5133 to a fraction of a percent. Demanding a manipulability of half the best
possible, which means an elbow between 30 and 150 degrees, keeps **2.1763 m^2**, inside radii 0.252 and 0.870 m, and it matches the prediction from
the geometry, 2.1766. Demanding 0.9 of the best keeps 1.0963 m^2, 43.6 percent.

## Fix

Ask for the usable area and not the reachable one when placing work.

```cpp
const WorkspaceMap m = map_workspace(l1, l2, limits, 0.5, 0.005);
// m.dexterous, not m.reachable, is the area to put the task in
```
