## Symptom

An arm that works in most poses misbehaves in a few: it cannot move one way, or moves that way at an enormous joint speed. The odd behaviour comes at the same few poses.

## Cause

Nobody measured how near a singularity the pose was. The smallest singular value of the Jacobian says it: 0.172 in a generic pose, falling smoothly to zero as joint 5 straightened (0.1337, 0.0313, 0.00317).

A singular value of the Jacobian is how much tool motion a unit of joint motion makes in one direction. As the smallest goes to zero, motion in its direction needs ever larger joint speeds, and at zero the arm cannot move that way at all.

## Fix

Compute the smallest singular value for every pose that is planned, and keep it above a floor.

```cpp
const double s = rc::arm::smallest_singular(rc::arm::jacobian(table, q, base, tool, table.convention));
```
