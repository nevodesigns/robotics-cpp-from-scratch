## Symptom

After the lever arm is corrected, the position still wanders when the robot turns. The heading came from wheel odometry or a magnetometer.

## Cause

The correction uses the heading, and the heading was wrong. The residual is the lever times the heading error: 5 degrees wrong left 0.044 m.

The lever arm correction subtracts the lever turned by the heading. An error in the heading turns the lever the wrong way, and the error left is the lever's length times the angle.

## Fix

Shorten the lever (mount the antenna over the reference point), and improve the heading, before relying on the correction.

```cpp
// residual = |lever| * heading_error: 0.5 m * 0.087 rad = 0.044 m
```
