title: A zero pose that is not allowed
match: the arm will not go to its zero pose
match: the home pose is outside the joint limits
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A command to go to all zeros is refused or the arm drives into a stop.

## Cause

The table's zero pose is not within the joint limits: for the Panda, joint 4 may not be zero (within_limits said 0).

## Fix

Check every pose, the first and the home pose included, with within_limits, and pick a home pose inside the limits.

```cpp
within_limits(table, q);
```
