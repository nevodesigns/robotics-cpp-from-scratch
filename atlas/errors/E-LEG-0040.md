## Symptom

The correction for an acceleration makes the tilt worse.

## Cause

The zero moment point is behind the centre of mass for a forward acceleration (minus h over g times a); 83 mm behind here.

## Fix

Use minus h over g times a, and check it against the centre of pressure of the feet.

```cpp
out[0] = com[0] - h / 9.81 * a[0];
```
