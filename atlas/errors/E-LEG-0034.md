## Symptom

The tilt estimate wobbles at the step frequency, though the accelerometer is gated so that only quiet readings are trusted.

## Cause

The gate tests the size of the acceleration, and a sideways acceleration barely changes the size. Gravity with 2.5 m/s2 at right angles has a magnitude of 10.1 m/s2, three percent above g, well inside a gate of 15 percent: the gate accepted 100 percent of the readings in the walk. It rejects jolts along the vertical, not the sideways shake that tilts the answer. The accelerometer alone was 10.2 degrees wrong.

## Fix

Do not rely on the gate to remove shake. Make the pull toward the accelerometer slow enough to average the shake out, and add a source with no shake when there is one: the feet.

```cpp
rc::legged::AttitudeFilter f;
f.k_acc = 0.5;     // a corner at 0.08 Hz, far below a 4 Hz step
f.k_feet = 1.0;    // the plane of the feet, when three or more are down
```

A tight gate is no better: it passes so little that the gyro's drift is left uncorrected.
