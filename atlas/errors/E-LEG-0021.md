## Symptom

The robot leans, one leg stands shorter, and feet are out by a centimetre from where the model puts them.

## Cause

The zero of each joint is where the horn was pressed on: offsets of a few degrees put the foot 14.6 mm from where it was commanded.

## Fix

Measure the foot in a number of poses and fit the offsets.

```cpp
calibrate_offsets(g, commanded, measured, n, 20, offsets, &rms);
```
