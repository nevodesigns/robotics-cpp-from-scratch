title: A payload added to the mass and not to the centre

match: the payload's centre of mass is where the flange is
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

After a payload is added to the model the compensation is better but wrong by a different amount in different poses.

## Cause

The mass was added to the last link and its centre of mass left where it was, so the weight is in the wrong place; the estimate from six poses was 2.00 kg for a payload of 2 kg because it assumed the payload at the tool, and the error fell to 0.00010 rad when it was put there.

## Fix

Move the centre of mass to the weighted mean of the two, and the inertia by the parallel axis rule.

```cpp
com = (m_link * com_link + m_pay * where) / (m_link + m_pay);
```
