title: A cycle worked out with the wrong hand
match: the delta was slow because of the gripper
match: the cycle estimate left out the time to close the hand
match: four different jobs go to four different robots, and a job nothing can do is refused
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A fast robot gives a slow cycle.

## Cause

The cycle was worked out from the robot's moves alone, and the hand's time was left out. Closing, opening and settling are part of every cycle, and a vacuum cup takes a fifth of a pinching hand's time. With a cup the delta's cycle was 0.81 s.

## Fix

Put the hand's measured time in the task, and work the cycle out with it.

```cpp
rc::arm::TaskSpec task;
task.dwell_s = measured_hand_time;   // the default 0.8 s is a pinching hand
```
