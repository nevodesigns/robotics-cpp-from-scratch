title: A slope limit from friction alone
match: the robot ran out of leg reach on a slope it should have gripped

platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

On a slope that the feet could grip the robot fails: legs at full stretch, or it tips.

## Cause

Three limits apply: grip 31.0 degrees, tipping 35.1, and for a level body reach 27.8: number 2 gave first.

## Fix

Take the least of the three, and tilt the body to the slope's angle to remove the reach limit.

```cpp
fmin(max_slope_friction(mu), fmin(max_slope_tip(hw, h), max_slope_level(hw, up, down)));
```
