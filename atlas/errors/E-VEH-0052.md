## Symptom

The estimated velocity of a person jumps about by a metre a second though they walk steadily.

## Cause

Two positions 5 cm in error over 0.1 s are 0.7 m/s apart in velocity: the difference gave an error of 1.073 m/s against 0.122 for the filter.

## Fix

Estimate position and velocity together with a filter that uses all the past.

```cpp
kf_predict(f, dt, accel_var); kf_update(f, zx, zy, meas_var);
```
