## Symptom

A hand-written learner trains badly and every setting is tried before anyone suspects the code.

## Cause

The gradient was trusted because the code ran. Nothing in training notices a wrong gradient: each step moves the weights a little, and the error moves a little. The central difference, two forward passes per weight, gives the derivative independently; over 33 weights it agreed with the backpropagated gradient to better than one part in a million.

## Fix

Compare every weight's gradient with a central difference at a few random inputs whenever the gradient code changes, and require a small relative difference.

```cpp
RC_CHECK(rc::learn::numeric_check(net, x, target, 1e-5) < 1e-6);
```

Choose h near 1e-5 in double precision: much smaller and rounding dominates, much larger and the curvature does.
