title: A step missed by a smooth slope test
match: the vehicle drives into a kerb that the slope map calls flat
match: a step is a roughness, not a slope
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A kerb or a step is in no cell's slope but stops the vehicle.

## Cause

A plane over 3 by 3 cells smooths a step away: a step of 5 cm read as 14.0 degrees and a roughness of 0.0144 m, both inside the limits. The rise between neighbours, 26.6 degrees, was not, and made 2 cells a row lethal.

## Fix

Compare neighbouring cells as well as fitting a plane, and use the steepest rise.

```cpp
const double share = std::max(std::max(slope, max_gradient) / max_slope, roughness / max_roughness);
```
