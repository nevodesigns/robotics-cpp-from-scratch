## Symptom

A move of a short distance takes a long time, with the arm stopping and starting along the way.

## Cause

Every vertex is a stop: a wandering path of 17 points took 14.33 s and its straight shortcut 1.45 s.

## Fix

Shortcut the path (30-05) before timing it, and blend the corners that are left.

```cpp
path = shortcut(problem, path, 150, seed, margin);
```
