## Symptom

The robot is lost for good after a single bad reading, and it finds itself again only by luck, or announces a place with high confidence that is nowhere near.

## Cause

A probability of zero for a wrong reading multiplies the true place by zero the first time the sensor is wrong. After 25 steps and one wrong reading, a belief told zero kept 0.042 of the true place (1/24) and took 5 steps to get back above a half; told 0.01 or 0.1 it kept 1.000. Over 200 trips the right place was found 0.625 of the time at 0.00, 0.710 at 0.05 and 0.715 at 0.10.

## Fix

Give the sensor a small probability of being wrong, near its real error rate.

```cpp
b.correct(world, reading, 0.95, 0.05);
```
