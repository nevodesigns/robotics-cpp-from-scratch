## Symptom

A robot with a damaged leg keeps trying to get up and heats its motors until they fail.

## Cause

Nothing counted the tries: with a limit of 3 it ended in fault 5.

## Fix

Limit the tries at getting up, and fault when they run out.

```cpp
if (d.tries >= d.max_tries) fault(5);
```
