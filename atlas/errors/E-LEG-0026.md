## Symptom

The body leans toward the leg being lifted and the robot tips that way.

## Cause

The shift has to be toward the middle of the three feet that stay down: forward helped with a hind foot lifted (0.031 m) and not with a front one.

## Fix

Take the direction of the shift from the feet that remain, for each leg.

```cpp
centroid(triangle, 3, target);
```
