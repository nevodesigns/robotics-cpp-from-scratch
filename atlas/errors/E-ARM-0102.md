## Symptom

A recorded motion jumps across a place where frames were lost, and what is learned from it jumps too.

## Cause

20 frames in a row were lost: the longest gap was 0.70 s, and interpolation drew a straight line across it.

## Fix

Check the longest gap of every episode and refuse those over a limit, at the time of recording.

```cpp
if (!check_episode(e, 0.02, 0.2, 2.0, 0.7).ok) discard();
```
