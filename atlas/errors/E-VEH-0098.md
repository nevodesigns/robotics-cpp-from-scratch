## Symptom

On a slippery surface the vehicle goes straight on out of a bend.

## Cause

The sideways acceleration reached what the surface grips: 51 steps of letting go at 1.2 m/s on ice, against 0 with the speed capped.

## Fix

Cap the speed in the turn at a margin of the traction limit (lesson 23-05), and take the time it costs (5.4 s against 4.6 s).

```cpp
v = std::min(v, margin * max_speed_for_curvature(cell, heading, k));
```
