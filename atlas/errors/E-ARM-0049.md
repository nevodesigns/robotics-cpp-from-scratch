title: A pose that heats the motor to its limit in minutes
match: the arm stops after holding a pose for a few minutes
match: the shoulder motor overheats when the arm is held out
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 29-08-how-much-the-motors-can-give
---

## Symptom

The arm holds a pose for a while and then a joint cuts out or slows, with nothing wrong with the code.

## Cause

Holding is a current and a current is heat: 37.7 N m at the stretched shoulder reached the limit in 293 s, and 2.1 N m folded never did.

## Fix

Work out the time to the limit for the poses the task holds, and fold, rest or brake in them.

```cpp
time_to_limit(motor, torque_to_hold);
```
