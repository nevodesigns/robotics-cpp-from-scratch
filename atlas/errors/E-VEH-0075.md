## Symptom

The heading drifts quickly after a time that coincides with the gyro warming or the robot working hard.

## Cause

A step in the bias of 0.01 rad/s, with no stop to read it, runs on at 0.6 degrees a second. With stops it was found 19 s later.

## Fix

Keep reading the bias at the stops, and let its variance grow between them so that a step is believed.

```cpp
p_ += q_ * dt;
```
