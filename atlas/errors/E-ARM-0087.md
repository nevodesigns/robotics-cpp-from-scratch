## Symptom

The contact is detected and the force is still too large.

## Cause

Detection takes time; a joint at 0.6 rad/s let the wall's torque reach 5.20 N m by the time it stopped, against 1.31 N m at 0.2 rad/s.

## Fix

Approach slowly where contact is expected, and choose the speed from the force the part can bear and the time detection takes.

```cpp
vdes = v_approach_slow;
```
