id: E-VEH-0072
title: A task asked of a position that cannot support it
match: the robot is told to dock within 10 cm using a position that is good to a metre
match: needs_fix
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 25-06-how-lost-can-it-be
---

## Symptom

A task that wants centimetres is given a position that is good to metres.

## Cause

The robot could go 27 m before it might be 1 m out and 80 m before it might be 5 m out, and nothing compared that with what the task needed.

## Fix

Check the bound against what the task needs and get a fix before it, or refuse.

```cpp
if (dr.needs_fix(task_tolerance)) get_a_fix();
```
