## Symptom

The visual correction is accurate at the bench and not at the working range.

## Cause

The camera's sigma (2 mm) is for one range; it grows with distance and the budget 2.0 mm is for that range only.

## Fix

Take the camera's error at the range the hand will be at when it looks, and test it there.

```cpp
sigma_after_servo(camera_sigma_at_range, arm_sigma);
```
