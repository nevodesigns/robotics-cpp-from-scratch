## Symptom

A point inside the quoted reach cannot be reached the way the task needs.

## Cause

Reach depends on the tool's orientation: the yardstick asks for a level or downward tool and a different answer would come from a tilted one.

## Fix

State the orientation with every reach, and put the question for the task's own orientation.

```cpp
tool_down_reachable(table, x, y, z, tool);
```
