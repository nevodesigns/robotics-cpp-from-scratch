## Symptom

A step the planner says is reachable cannot be done because the knee reaches its stop.

## Cause

The inverse kinematics has a solution with the knee folded flat; a knee limit of 2.6 rad gave 0.229 m and a tighter one less.

## Fix

Check the joint limits as well as the reachability when planning a foot place.

```cpp
leg_ik(g, x, y, z, -1, q) && q[2] >= knee_lo;
```
