## Symptom

The vehicle turns back at what it thinks is the right moment and runs out of charge on the way home. The robot crossed sand on the way out and turned back by distance.

## Cause

The way home was taken as a straight line over flat ground. It turned back 16.8 m out and was 25 J short, because the sand in between cost more than tarmac.

The energy needed to get home depends on the ground in between, and a straight line over flat ground underestimates a way that crosses sand or climbs. The decision to turn back is only as good as that estimate.

## Fix

Plan the way home over the terrain and take its energy, at every decision to go on. Planned that way it turned back at 12.0 m with 71 J to spare.

```cpp
const double home_wh = rc::drive::route_wh(terrain, rc::drive::plan_offroad(terrain, risk, x, y, dock_x, dock_y, w).points, power);
```
