## Symptom

In a cluttered place the ground plane jumps between scans, though the ground does not.

## Cause

Too few draws for the share of ground. A draw is clean only if all three points are ground, and that gets rare fast as the share falls: 5 draws are enough at 85 percent for 99 percent confidence, and about a hundred times as many at 30.

## Fix

Choose the number of draws from the share of ground you expect in the worst place and the confidence you want.

```cpp
const int draws = rc::perceive::ransac_samples(expected_ground_share, 0.99);
```
