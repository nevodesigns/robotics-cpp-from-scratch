## Symptom

The check says no, or prints many numbers, and nobody knows which is the reason.

## Cause

Only the first failure names the requirement to fix: a speed of 2.0 m/s failed at 1, a route of 5000 m at 5.

## Fix

Report the number of the first failed requirement and keep the figures of the others.

```cpp
v.first_fail
```
