id: E-BUS-0029
title: A cancel taken for a stop
match: a cancel moves a running goal to canceling first and only the server's confirmation makes it canceled
match: a cancel that comes too late does not undo a goal that has finished, and one that races the finish still ends as succeeded
match: progress is reported while a goal runs, and is dropped and counted once it is anywhere else
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 17-10-goals-that-take-time
---

## Symptom

A client believes a goal was cancelled and the arm completed it, or it shows a move in progress after the move was stopped.

## Cause

A cancel is a request, and the arm takes time to stop. It puts a goal in canceling, and only a confirmation makes it canceled. A cancel that raced the finish ended succeeded. Feedback after the goal was no longer executing was dropped and counted: 2 kept, 3 dropped.

## Fix

Treat canceling as a state of its own, and read the final state for what happened.

```cpp
if (goal->state() == GoalState::succeeded) /* the cancel lost */;
```
