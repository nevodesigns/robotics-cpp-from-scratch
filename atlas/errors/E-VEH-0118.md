title: A field that forgets the latency
match: the vehicle stops too late for the thing in its protective field

match: a field worked out with no latency lets the vehicle through a thing
match: points behind, beside or past the end of the field are not in it
match: a thing in the warning field slows the vehicle to a creep and one in the stop field stops it
platforms: linux, windows

## Symptom

A thing enters the protective field and the vehicle stops, but not before it reaches it.

## Cause

The field was worked out without the latency (0.3 s): the vehicle stopped 0.25 m past where the thing was. With the latency it stopped 0.20 m short.

## Fix

Make the field the distance in the latency, plus the braking distance, plus a margin, at the speed the vehicle has now.

```cpp
field_length(speed, cfg);
```
