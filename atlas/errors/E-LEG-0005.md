## Symptom

A robot that stands low, to look stable or to fit under something, runs its motors hot and its battery down while doing nothing.

## Cause

A bent knee carries the weight on its motor, not on its bones. The knee's torque is the foot's share of the weight times how far the shank leans from the vertical: with 29.4 N on each foot of a 12 kg robot, the knee gave 5.43 N m with the foot 0.20 m below the hip, 4.33 at 0.30 m, and 1.88 with the leg nearly straight at 0.40 m.

## Fix

Stand as straight as control allows when nothing needs a crouch, and count the crouch's torque in the heat budget (34-06).

```cpp
double j[3][3], tau[3];
rc::legged::leg_jacobian(leg, q0, q1, q2, j);
const double ground[3] = {0.0, 0.0, 29.4};
rc::legged::stand_torques(j, ground, tau);   // tau[2] 5.43 N m crouched, 1.88 nearly straight
```
