## Symptom

A planner sees a wall that slopes, or the floor as a wall, depending on the mount of the sensor.

## Cause

The points were used in the sensor's frame. Tipped down 5 degrees, a flat wall had heights from -0.19 to 0.69 m in the vehicle's frame.

## Fix

Transform the points into the vehicle's frame with the mount, before anything uses them.

```cpp
const auto in_vehicle = transform_points(in_sensor, mount);
```
