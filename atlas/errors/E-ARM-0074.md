## Symptom

The sensor sees the thing coming and the arm goes on along its plan. The prediction was logged as a warning, and nothing acted on it.

## Cause

The arm acted on the plan and not on the prediction. It ended -0.112 m from the obstacle, that is through it, where one that stopped kept 0.031 m.

A monitor that only warns leaves the arm doing what the plan says. If the prediction is to protect anything, it has to change the command: stop, or slow.

## Fix

Stop when a meeting is predicted within the horizon (at least the time it takes to stop), and look again next tick.

```cpp
if (ttc >= 0.0) hold = true;
```
