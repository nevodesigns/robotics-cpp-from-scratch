## Symptom

The arm lags every move, by an amount that grows with the acceleration of the move.

## Cause

The wanted acceleration was not in the torque, so the error had to build up to supply it. The reference acceleration is part of the computed torque.

## Fix

Give the controller the reference velocity and acceleration, from a trajectory smooth to the second derivative (a quintic).

```cpp
quintic_at(move, t, q_ref, qd_ref, qdd_ref);
```
