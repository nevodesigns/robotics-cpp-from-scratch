## Symptom

The guard either takes over so often that the learned policy hardly drives, or so seldom that the policy fails as if it were not there.

## Cause

The threshold decides how much of the driving the network does. With 1 spread the guard handed over on 20 percent of the steps (success 98 percent); at the usual 3 on 12 percent; with 1000, a guard that never acts, on 0 percent, and the success fell back to the clone's 87 percent.

## Fix

Choose it by measurement: the success and the handover share from a spread of starts, at several thresholds, and say which was chosen and why.

```cpp
for (double z : {1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0}) { guard.z_max = z; measure(guard); }
```

A box round the data misses corners it never reached; record the threshold's limits with the policy.
