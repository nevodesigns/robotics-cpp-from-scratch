## Symptom

A balance check refuses every trot, or a trot controller is judged unsafe at every instant by a criterion that a walking robot passes.

## Cause

A static criterion was applied to a dynamic gait. With two diagonal feet down the support is a line, its margin is zero at best, and no acceleration is "safe" in the static sense. A trot is not balanced; it is caught by the next pair of feet.

## Fix

Use the static criterion for gaits with three or more feet down, and the dynamic ones for a trot: the time to fall (35-02) and the capture point (35-07).

```cpp
if (feet_down >= 3) ok = rc::legged::support_margin(feet, on_ground, zmp) >= 0.0;
else ok = rc::legged::trot_stance_ok(stance, offset, rate, 0.06, h, 0.1);
```
