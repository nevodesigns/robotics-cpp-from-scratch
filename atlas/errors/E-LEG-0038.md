## Symptom

Raising the balance gains makes the robot recover from small pushes faster and does nothing for large ones, which still roll it over.

## Cause

The most that can be answered is set by the feet, not the gains. With a roll inertia of 0.05 kg m2 and gains of 100 N m/rad and 4 N m s/rad, a push of 10 N m for 0.3 s peaked at 5.7 degrees and the body was level 0.12 s after the push ended. A push of 25 N m, more than the 17.7 N m the feet can supply, rolled the body over whatever the gains.

## Fix

Choose the gains for the inertia, and plan for pushes beyond the feet's limit with a step, not with gain.

```cpp
const double mx = rc::legged::balance_moment(roll, roll_rate, 100.0, 4.0);
rc::legged::foot_forces(feet, on_ground, W, mx, my, f);
```
