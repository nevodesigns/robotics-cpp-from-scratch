## Symptom

After the robot is moved, its position is wrong for the rest of the run and nothing says so.

## Cause

The cloud stayed on the old place; normalising the weights hid how badly the scans were explained. A plain cloud was 8.3 m out after the carry; the monitor found the robot again after 21 steps.

## Fix

Watch the average likelihood of the scans with a fast and a slow average and scatter new guesses when the fast falls below the slow.

```cpp
const double share = monitor.update(mc.correct(angles, ranges, measure));
```
