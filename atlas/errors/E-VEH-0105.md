## Symptom

The vehicle backs up, drives into the same wall, backs up again, until the battery is flat. The wall did not move.

## Cause

There was no limit on the attempts or on a behaviour. Recovery has to end.

A recovery that resets after each try never runs out of tries. Some obstacles cannot be got past by backing up, and only a count that survives the attempts can tell.

## Fix

Count attempts, forget them only after real progress (1 m here), end every behaviour by a time limit, and give up in an orderly way. A wall that never moved was given up on after 3 attempts and 31.2 s.

```cpp
if (b == rc::drive::kGiveUp) return rc::drive::DriveCmd();   // stop and report
```
