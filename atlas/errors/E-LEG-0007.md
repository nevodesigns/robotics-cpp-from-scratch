## Symptom

The estimated tilt jumps every time a foot leaves the ground.

## Cause

A foot in the air was put into the plane fit: only feet on the ground lie in the ground's plane, and three feet not in a line are needed.

## Fix

Fit the plane only through the feet that are in stance, and refuse a fit with fewer than three.

```cpp
body_pose_from_feet(feet, on_ground);
```
