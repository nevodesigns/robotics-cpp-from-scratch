title: Gains for one pose used for all
match: the arm is smooth in some poses and shaky in others
match: the controller was tuned with the arm folded
match: the inertia the shoulder sees changes several fold over the poses, and a gear makes it change less
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 29-03-a-gain-that-knows-the-pose
---

## Symptom

The arm moves well in the pose it was tuned in and rings or is sluggish in others.

## Cause

The inertia the shoulder sees changed by a factor of 3.0 over the poses (0.691 to 2.091 kg m2), and the gains were for one of them.

## Fix

Work the gains from the inertia of the present pose (or from the worst case, if the loop must stay simple).

```cpp
scheduled_gains(effective_inertia(model, q, joint, rotor, gear), wp, wv);
```
