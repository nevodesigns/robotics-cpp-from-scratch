title: A forward kinematics with no starting guess
match: the platform pose solver jumped to a different solution
match: the hexapod's solver did not converge
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 32-03-six-legs-and-a-table
---

## Symptom

The pose found from the leg lengths is far from the real one, or the solver does not converge.

## Cause

The iteration needs a start near the answer; from a guess some way off it took 4 steps, and a poor one may find another of the many solutions.

## Fix

Start from the last pose, and refuse a result that is far from it.

```cpp
stewart_pose(hexapod, lengths, last_pose, &iters);
```
