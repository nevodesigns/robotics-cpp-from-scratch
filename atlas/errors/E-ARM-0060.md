## Symptom

Given a goal that cannot be reached, the planner does not return.

## Cause

Nothing said when to stop. With a goal in the wall the pose check refused it at once (0 iterations), and a limit on iterations bounds the rest.

## Fix

Check the ends first, and give every plan a limit on iterations or time, and a result that says whether it found a path.

```cpp
p.max_iter = 3000;
```
