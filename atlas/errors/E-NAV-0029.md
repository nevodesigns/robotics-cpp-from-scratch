id: E-NAV-0029
title: A curvature that divides by a side of zero
match: three points on a circle give one over its radius, and points in a line give none
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 16-11-slow-down-for-the-corner
---

## Symptom

A path with a repeated point gives a speed limit of NaN, and the rover either stops or ignores every limit after it.

## Cause

Curvature divides by the product of the three sides, and two coinciding points make one of them zero. The function must give zero for that, as it does for three points in a line. Points on circles of radius 0.5, 1, 2 and 10 gave 1 / r to 1e-9.

## Fix

Guard the sides.

```cpp
if (ab == 0.0 || bc == 0.0 || ca == 0.0) return 0.0;
```
