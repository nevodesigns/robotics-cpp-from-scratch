## Symptom

The supervisor passes every test with a person standing still and gets too close to someone walking toward the robot.

## Cause

It was never tried against a moving person. A limit that falls continuously as the person nears asks the robot to slow faster than its brakes can follow. In a simulation with a person walking at 1.6 m/s, the supervised robot came to rest 0.14 m from them: clear, but 28 mm inside the 0.17 m of intrusion and uncertainty the distance was meant to keep. Unsupervised, it met them, closing at 3.1 m/s.

## Fix

Test against people who move, and keep more margin than the formula alone (a larger reaction time or uncertainty, or a lower speed asked).

```cpp
rc::arm::SsmParams ssm;
ssm.t_r = 0.15;   // more than the typical 0.10 s, for a limit that falls as the person nears
```
