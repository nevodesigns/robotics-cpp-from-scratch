title: A gap judged by its width and not the vehicle's
match: the planner finds a route through a gap that the vehicle does not fit
match: a gap of 0.40 m for a vehicle of 0.5
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 24-04-costmap-layers
---

## Symptom

A route goes between two posts, and the vehicle stops against one.

## Cause

The gap was wider than a point. With an inscribed radius of 0.25 m, 0.60 m left 2 free cells and 0.40 m left 0.

## Fix

Let the inflation decide; never compare a gap with a number the planner has.

```cpp
const bool open = master.at(i, j) < kCostInscribed;
```
