## Symptom

Each of the base and the hand is within the limit for the person's distance and together they close too fast.

## Cause

The budget of 1.23 m/s at 1.0 m is for both; given to each, the robot would have gone at 1.0 plus 0.5 m/s.

## Fix

Count the speeds toward the person together and share the one budget.

```cpp
share_budget(base_ask, hand_ask, base_cap, total);
```
