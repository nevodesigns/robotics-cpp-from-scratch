## Symptom

The tool is in the right orientation and the wrong place after a pose has been inverted.

## Cause

The inverse transposed the rotation and left the translation alone: a pose 0.5 m from the origin came back 0.35 m out. The correct inverse is accurate to 0.0000000000000002.

## Fix

Transpose the rotation, and take the translation as minus that rotation times the old translation.

```cpp
inverse(pose);
```
