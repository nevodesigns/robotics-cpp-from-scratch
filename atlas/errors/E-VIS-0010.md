title: A local mean that does not know where the picture ends
match: the sum of any rectangle from the table is the sum of its pixels, at the edges too
match: a mean near the edge uses only the pixels that exist, so a flat picture has the same mean everywhere
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 15-14-light-that-is-not-even
---

## Symptom

Marks near the border of the picture are missed, while the same marks in the middle are found.

## Cause

A box centred near the edge sticks out, and a mean that divides by the full box area counts pixels that do not exist as if they were black, which pulls the mean down so a mark there is no longer darker than it. On a flat picture of 90 the correct mean was 90.0 at the corner, where a 17 by 17 box holds 81 pixels of the picture. 2000 random boxes, partly or wholly outside, matched a pixel by pixel sum with none wrong.

## Fix

Clip the box to the picture and divide by the pixels that are inside.

```cpp
const double mean = table.box_mean(x - r, y - r, x + r, y + r);
```
