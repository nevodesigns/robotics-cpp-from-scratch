## Symptom

A margin check on the support polygon fails at every moment of a trot.

## Cause

With two diagonal feet down the support is a line: the margin is 0.000 m at best and no acceleration is safe; the trot is caught, not balanced (35-02).

## Fix

Use the timing of 35-02 and the step of 35-07 for a trot, and the polygon check for gaits with three or more feet down.

```cpp
support_margin(feet, on_ground, p) <= 0 with two feet
```
