## Symptom

The labels on the terminal chart say 5, 10, 15 and the marks on the window are at 6, 12, 18.

## Cause

Ticks computed from different ranges, or a range that was rounded in one place and not in another. Measured: the range of the model was -0.99919 to 20.9999 with **5** ticks, all **5** marked in the window, and the **3** labels of the text were all ticks of the same range.

## Fix

Take the range from the model and the ticks from the range, in both views:

```cpp
const Ticks ticks = ticks_for(v.range.low, v.range.high, kTickTarget);
```
