match: a delay makes the reading old by that many samples, and until it has history it gives the first reading
match: a controller that settles with a perfect sensor overshoots three times as far and is still swinging with a delayed one, which is what a sensor model is for
platforms: linux, windows
teaches: 18-04-a-sensor-that-lies-like-the-real-one

## Symptom

A controller is tuned in simulation and rings on the real machine, because nothing in the simulation was ever late.

## Cause

A perfect sensor gives the truth at once. A delay of 3 read 0 0 0 0 1 2 3 4 on a ramp. A proportional controller overshot by 0.1945 with a perfect sensor and 0.6543 with one 60 ms old, and was still swinging by 0.0325 in the last half second against 0.0000. Noise and a coarse resolution added almost nothing (0.6574), which says that for this controller the delay is what matters.

## Fix

Give the simulated sensor the delay of the real one.

```cpp
SensorOptions o;
o.delay = 12;
```
