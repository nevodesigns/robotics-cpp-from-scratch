## Symptom

The robot gets to a step it can reach and drags its foot on the edge, or lags.

## Cause

The geometry allowed 0.229 m but the servos, at 8 rad/s in a swing of 0.20 s, allowed only 0.033 m.

## Fix

Work out the servo-limited step for the swing time and slow down (a longer swing) for higher steps.

```cpp
max_step_servo(g, x, h, t_sw, clear, rate);
```
